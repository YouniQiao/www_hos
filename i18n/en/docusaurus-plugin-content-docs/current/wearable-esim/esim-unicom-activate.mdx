# Activate eSIM One Number for China Unicom Users

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

   Activate eSIM One Number for China Unicom Users


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Carriers have recently been upgrading their services. For the latest carrier support policies, see: HUAWEI WATCH Series eSIM Activation Methods.
>
> - The activation requirements are for reference only. Contact your carrier to confirm the details.
>
> - eSIM services cannot be activated when the watch is paired with a tablet.


## Activation Requirements


- Make sure the primary number is available or in normal use, and is not in an abnormal state such as overdue payment or suspension.
- The primary number has completed real-name verification and was registered with a personal second-generation ID card. However, the ID card type cannot be: military ID (such as an officer's card or soldier's card), or armed police ID (such as a police officer's card).
- Under the ID document associated with the primary number, no more than 4 SIM card numbers of this carrier have been registered.
- The user of the ID document associated with the primary number cannot be on the carrier's blacklist.
- The primary card number cannot have the ringback tone service activated.
- The number cannot belong to a corporate ivpn/short number user. If corporate ivpn/short number is activated, the One Number service cannot be activated.
- Only 4G and 5G users are eligible.
- Before activating the eSIM One Number service, we recommend consulting your local carrier to confirm whether activation is supported in your area.


## Before You Begin


- Pair the watch with a phone running Android 9.0 or later, HarmonyOS 2 or later, or iOS 9.0 or later. For an iOS phone, you can first activate the service online using a Huawei phone or an Android phone, and then connect it to your original phone. Note that you must sign in to the Huawei Health app with the same HUAWEI ID.
- After your phone is upgraded to HarmonyOS 5 or later, update the watch and **Huawei Health** to the latest versions.
- Turn off your phone's WLAN, insert the China Unicom primary card to be activated into the paired phone, and set this card as the default mobile data card.
- The latest version of the China Unicom app has been installed on your phone.
- Enable the **Phone** and **Location** permissions for the "China Unicom" mobile business hall app. Taking a HarmonyOS 2 phone as an example: In **Settings > Apps and services > App management** on your phone, search for "China Unicom" > **Permissions** > **Phone** > **Allow**.
- In **Settings > Apps and services > App management** on your phone, search for "China Unicom" > **Permissions** > **Location** > **Allow**. For other phones, consult the customer service of the corresponding phone brand.

      The watch supports activating only one One Number service at a time. If a One Number service is already active, you need to cancel it in advance.
      The battery level of HUAWEI WATCH 3 series watches must be above 30%, and that of other watches above 20%.
      The primary card must be inserted into the paired phone.


## Activation at an Offline Business Hall

     I: **You have never visited a carrier business hall**

1. Bring your ID card to a China Unicom offline self-operated business hall (click here to search). Cross-region activation may not be possible. Before you go, call 10010 to make sure the business hall you plan to visit can activate the service offline for you and provide an **eSIM activation QR code**.
2. Tell the staff that you need to activate eSIM offline by scanning a QR code at the counter, and ask them to help you with the activation. After the activation is successful, the carrier's staff will provide you with an eSIM activation QR code.
3. After you get the QR code, do not leave the business hall yet. Open the Huawei Health app on site, go to the device details page, and tap **eSIM management > One Number** > **Activate now** > **Activate at offline business hall** to enter the QR code scanning page, then scan the QR code provided by the carrier.

     **II: You visited a carrier business hall and the staff said it is not supported**

1. Confirm whether the carrier business hall you visited is a China Unicom self-operated business hall (click here to search for China Unicom self-operated business halls). If not, please bring your ID card to a China Unicom self-operated business hall. Before you go, call 10010 to make sure the business hall you plan to visit can activate the service offline for you and provide an eSIM activation QR code.
2. If the business hall you visited is a China Unicom self-operated business hall, remind the staff that you need to activate the eSIM service by scanning the **eSIM activation QR code** provided by the carrier, and that this is supported. If the staff are still unsure, since the eSIM service is a carrier service and Huawei cannot look up the relevant information, we recommend calling China Unicom at 10010 to file a technical ticket about this issue, and try to have the carrier's technical department verify or look up a nearby self-operated business hall that supports the activation.


## Online Activation on a Huawei Phone


1. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **One Number** > **Activate now**. > 💡 **Tips:** > > - On a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, the path is: Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **One Number** > **Continue**. > > - The path varies by Huawei Health version. For other paths, refer to: Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **eSIM management > One Number** > **Activate now**. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547039233.png" width="560" />
2. Select the number to activate and tap **Continue**.
3. Select the online activation method and tap **Continue**. On the prompt screen, tap **Confirm**, then tap **Next** on the carrier screen. After completing identity verification as prompted, tap **Next**.
4. On the activation screen, keep the Bluetooth connection between your phone and watch. The watch will start downloading and installing the eSIM profile. Check the download status and result on the watch. When the watch shows that it is enabled successfully, the activation is complete.
5. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management**. If the screen shows that the eSIM service is activated, the activation is successful.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547079223.png" width="560" />


## Online Activation on an Android Phone


> 💡 **Tips:** eSIM services cannot be activated when the watch is paired with a tablet.


1. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **One Number** > **Activate now** > **Online activation**.
2. Select the number to activate and tap **Continue**, then follow the on-screen instructions to jump to the China Unicom app.
3. Complete identity verification as prompted. On the confirmation screen, tap **Confirm activation**. Keep the Bluetooth connection between your phone and watch. The watch will start downloading and installing the eSIM profile. Check the download status and result on the watch. When the watch shows that it is enabled successfully, the activation is complete.
4. Open the Huawei Health app, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management**. If the screen shows that the eSIM service is activated, the activation is successful.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002515559296.png" width="560" /><img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002515399388.png" width="560" /><img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547039235.png" width="560" />

# Activate One Number, Two Devices as a China Telecom User

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate and WATCH 5**.

   Activate One Number, Two Devices as a China Telecom User


> 💡 **Note:** Activate from the Huawei Health app by jumping to the China Telecom app (Note: **New user applications have been suspended since 0:00 on March 21, 2024. The service for users who have already activated One Number, Two Devices is not affected.** For details, please consult 10000).


## Activation requirements


- Make sure the primary number is available or in normal use, and is not in an abnormal state such as arrears or suspension.
- The primary number has completed real-name verification and was registered with a personal second-generation ID card, but the ID card type cannot be: military ID card (such as officer's card or soldier's card), or armed police ID card (such as police officer's card).
- Under the certificate corresponding to the primary number, no more than 4 SIM card numbers have been registered.
- The certificate user corresponding to the primary number cannot be on the carrier's blacklist.
- It cannot be a corporate ivpn/short number user. If corporate ivpn/short number has been activated, One Number, Two Devices cannot be activated.
- Only available to 4G and 5G users.


## Preparations before activation


- Use a phone running Android 9.0 or later / HarmonyOS 2 or later / iOS 13.0 or later to complete pairing with the watch.
- The watch supports activating only one One Number, Two Devices service at a time. If a One Number, Two Devices service is already active, it must be canceled in advance.
- HUAWEI WATCH 3 series watches must have more than 30% battery, and other watches more than 20%.
- The primary card must be inserted in the paired phone.
- The latest version of the China Telecom app must be installed on the phone.
- When activating on a Huawei phone, password-free verification is required, and the phone's mobile data must be used.
- On an iOS phone, connect the watch to WLAN first.


## Activate eSIM


> 💡 **Note:** When paired and connected to a tablet, eSIM activation is not supported.


1. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **One Number, Two Devices** > **Activate now**. > 💡 **Note:** > > - On phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later, the path is: Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **One Number, Two Devices** > **Continue**. > > - The path varies by Huawei Health version. For other paths, refer to: Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **eSIM management > One Number, Two Devices** > **Activate now**. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547082577.png" width="560" />


1. Select the number to activate, tap **Continue**, follow the on-screen instructions to jump to the **China Telecom** app and log in with the primary number, obtain the device information, and tap **Agree**.
2. Select the watch brand and device model to enter the **eSIM service handling** page.
3. Tap **Service handling** to enter the **One Number, Two Devices activation** screen.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547042579.png" width="560" />
4. Tap **Add secondary device** to enter the real-name verification screen.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002515402736.png" width="560" />
5. Complete identity verification by following the on-screen instructions. After verification is complete, you will enter the **Add device** page. Confirm the device to add and tap **Confirm** to enter the **One Number, Two Devices activation** screen.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002515562648.png" width="560" />
6. After the order is submitted successfully, tap **View device status** to enter the **One Number, Two Devices activation** screen, where you can view the current service handling status.
7. After the service is handled successfully, tap **eSIM card data download**. Keep the Bluetooth connection between the phone and the watch. The watch will start downloading and installing the eSIM file. Wait for the process to complete, and check the download status and result on the watch. The watch displaying activation successful means the process is complete. > 💡 **Note:** Be sure to complete the manual eSIM file download. Otherwise, the eSIM cannot be used even if activation succeeds. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547082579.png" width="560" />
8. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management**. If the screen shows that the eSIM service is activated, the process is successful.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547042583.png" width="560" />


> 💡 **Note:** When the watch is connected to the phone via Bluetooth but not connected to the eSIM network, Bluetooth calling is used. When the watch is connected to the phone via Bluetooth and also connected to the eSIM network, you can choose to dial via the phone card or the eSIM card. When the watch is not connected to the phone via Bluetooth but is connected to the eSIM network, you can make calls using the watch's eSIM.

# Replace Your Watch or Change Your eSIM Number

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.


To replace your watch or change your eSIM number, you must first cancel the activated eSIM service, perform a reset in the watch's eSIM management screen, and then activate the service again.
1. Contact your carrier or use the method below to cancel the activated eSIM number. Otherwise, the number cannot be activated again and you will continue to be charged.
2. Activate the eSIM service using a new phone number or on a new watch.
> 💡 **Tip:** The reset operation only deletes the eSIM information from the watch; it does not automatically cancel the carrier's eSIM service. If the carrier's eSIM service is not canceled, you will continue to be charged. For cancellation methods, contact your carrier's hotline: China Mobile: 10086 China Unicom: 10010 China Telecom: 10000

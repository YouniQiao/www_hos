# Cancel the One Number, Two Devices service as a China Mobile user

> This article applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>

|  | Cancel the One Number, Two Devices service as a China Mobile user |
| --- | --- |


   Cancel the One Number, Two Devices service as a China Mobile user

    After activating the eSIM One Number, Two Devices service, you can manage it through the **Multi-SIM** app. To cancel the service, you must do so in the **Multi-SIM** app and delete the eSIM profile on your watch. On a Huawei phone, you can also cancel the One Number, Two Devices service through **Huawei Health**.


## Cancel the One Number, Two Devices service through the **Multi-SIM** app:


1. Sign in to the **Multi-SIM** app with your primary number.
2. In the **Multi-SIM** app, tap **One Number, Two Devices > Cancel service**, then follow the onscreen instructions to cancel the activated One Number, Two Devices service.
3. Wait for the cancellation to complete. Once it is successful, you will receive an SMS notification.


## Delete the eSIM profile on your watch:


1. Press the watch's upper button, then in the app list, go to **Settings > Mobile network** > **eSIM management** > **Settings**.
2. On the Settings screen, tap **Reset**, then follow the onscreen instructions to reset and delete the eSIM profile.


## Cancel the One Number, Two Devices service through **Huawei Health**


> 💡 **Tip:** Only Huawei phones support canceling the One Number, Two Devices service through Huawei Health.


1. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details screen, and tap **General settings > eSIM management**.
2. On the activated screen, tap **eSIM account management**, then tap **Unsubscribe**, and follow the onscreen instructions to cancel the activated One Number, Two Devices service.
3. Wait for the cancellation to complete. Once it is successful, you will receive an SMS notification.

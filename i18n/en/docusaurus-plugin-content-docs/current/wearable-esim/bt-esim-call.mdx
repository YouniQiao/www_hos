# Bluetooth calls and standalone eSIM calls

   Bluetooth calls and standalone eSIM calls


    The watch supports Bluetooth calls and standalone eSIM calls, meeting your calling needs in different scenarios so you can stay connected at all times.


> 💡 **Note:** Support for the eSIM one-number-two-devices call forwarding service varies by carrier and region. For details, consult your local carrier's customer service.


## Sync/delete contacts

     **Sync contacts:**

      The watch does not support adding contacts on its own. When the watch and phone are connected via Bluetooth, the watch automatically syncs contacts from your phone's contacts list to the watch.


> 💡 **Note:**
>
> - If contacts are not synced automatically, go to the app list on your watch, tap **Contacts > Sync contacts** > **Sync now**.
>
> - If you don't need to sync contacts, go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Contacts**, and turn off the **Sync data from phone to watch** switch.
>
> - The HUAWEI Health app on iOS phones does not have a contact sync switch. iOS 18 supports contact authorization. Go to **Settings > Apps > HUAWEI Health > Contacts** on your phone and configure contact syncing as needed**.**


     **Delete contacts:**
     After the watch syncs contacts from your phone, if you need to delete contacts from the watch, follow the steps below:

1. In the app list on your watch, tap **Settings > Apps** > **App management** > **System processes**.
2. Tap **Contacts storage > Storage** > **Delete data**. After deletion, the watch's contacts will be empty. You can manually sync them in the **Contacts** app on your watch.


> 💡 **Note:** The watch does not support deleting individual contacts. To delete a contact, select and delete it from your phone's contacts list. Once the watch connects to your phone, the corresponding contact will be deleted from the watch as well.


## Answer/end calls on your watch


> 💡 **Note:**
>
> - When using the watch standalone, wear the watch, and go to **Settings > Battery** on your watch, swipe up on the screen, tap **Power saving settings > Smart network off**, and turn off the corresponding switch as needed.
>
> - When using the watch standalone, features such as spam blocking and blacklist blocking are not supported. When the watch is not used standalone, blocks set on your phone will also be applied on the watch.


     **Answer:** When you receive an incoming call, the watch will alert you and display the caller's information. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001517962730.png" width="24" /> to answer.
     **End:** Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001569242281.png" width="24" /> to end the call.
     **Return to the call screen:**
     If you are not on the call screen, tap the call icon at the top of the watch face to return to the call screen.


## Make calls on your watch


      Press the watch's upper button and tap **Phone** in the app list. You can make calls in a variety of ways as needed:

- Tap **Call history** to view the watch's call history. Tap a specific entry to call back.
- Tap **Dialer**, enter the number you want to call, and tap the call button to make the call.
- Tap **Contacts** to call a contact as needed.


| Bluetooth connected/disconnected | No standalone number activated | Standalone number activated | One-number-two-devices activated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Watch connected to phone via Bluetooth | Bluetooth calls | Watch connected to eSIM network: You can choose Bluetooth calls or standalone eSIM calls. Watch not connected to eSIM network: Bluetooth calls | On your watch, go to **Settings > Mobile network** > **Smart network off** and turn off the **Auto turn off when Bluetooth connected** switch. When connected to the eSIM network, Bluetooth calls are preferred, and incoming eSIM calls are also supported. On your watch, go to **Settings > Mobile network** > **Smart network off** and turn off the **Auto turn off when Bluetooth connected** switch. When not connected to the eSIM network, only Bluetooth calls are supported. On your watch, go to **Settings > Mobile network** > **Smart network off** and turn on the **Auto turn off when Bluetooth connected** switch. Only Bluetooth calls are supported. To check whether the eSIM network is connected: On the watch face screen, swipe down from the top to open the Control Panel. If the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002408181341.png" width="24" /> icon appears at the bottom, the eSIM network is connected. |
| Watch disconnected from phone via Bluetooth | / (Calls unavailable) | Standalone eSIM calls | Standalone eSIM calls |


## In-call settings


- Adjust the call volume: Rotate the upper button to adjust the volume. Tap the mute icon <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001703172718.png" width="560" /> to mute your call.
- Enter numbers: Tap the more icon <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001704095130.png" width="560" />, select the keypad, and enter numbers.


## Switch calls between your phone and watch


> 💡 **Note:** eSIM calls do not support switching calls between your phone and watch.


1. Make sure your watch and phone are connected via Bluetooth.
2. On the call screen on your phone, tap the Bluetooth button in the lower right corner and select to switch the audio to your phone, watch, or earbuds, etc.


## View/delete call history


1. Press the watch's upper button and select **Phone > Call history** in the app list to view the watch's call history (including incoming, outgoing, and missed calls).
2. Swipe left on the screen and tap the delete icon to delete that call record. > 💡 **Note:** > > - Selecting and deleting multiple call records at once is not supported. > > - When using standalone eSIM calls to make or answer calls on your watch, no records will appear in your phone's contacts list.

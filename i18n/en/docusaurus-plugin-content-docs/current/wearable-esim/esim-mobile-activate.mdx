# Activate eSIM One Number Two Devices as a China Mobile User

> This article applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

   Activate eSIM One Number Two Devices as a China Mobile User


> 💡 **Note:**
>
> - Activating eSIM at a physical store is not supported at this time.
>
> - eSIM services cannot be activated when the watch is paired with a tablet.
>
> - Carriers have recently been upgrading their services. For the latest carrier support policies, see: HUAWEI WATCH Series eSIM Activation Methods.
>
> - Activating online via Huawei Health is not supported on Android/iOS phones at this time. Activate using a Huawei phone first, then connect the watch to your original phone. Note that you must sign in to **Huawei Health** with the same HUAWEI ID.


## Activation requirements

     For reference only. Contact your carrier to confirm the details:

- The primary number must be available or in normal use, with VoLTE activated. It cannot be in an abnormal state such as overdue payment or suspended service.
- The primary number must have completed real-name verification and be registered with a personal second-generation ID card. However, the ID card type cannot be: military ID (such as an officer's card or soldier's card), or armed police ID (such as a police officer's card).
- Under the ID document associated with the primary number, no more than 4 SIM cards from this carrier have been registered.
- The user associated with the primary number's ID document cannot be on the carrier's blacklist.
- A balance of more than 20 yuan on the card is recommended. Both prepaid and postpaid users need to top up first.


## Before you begin


- The watch supports only one One Number Two Devices service. If a One Number Two Devices service already exists, you must cancel it first.
- HUAWEI WATCH 3 series watches must have more than 30% battery, and other watches more than 20%.
- The primary SIM card must be inserted in the paired phone.


## Activation on a Huawei phone


1. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **One Number Two Devices** > **Activate now**. > 💡 **Note:** > > - On phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later, the path is: Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **One Number Two Devices** > **Continue**. > > - The path varies by Huawei Health version. For other paths, refer to: Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **eSIM management > One Number Two Devices** > **Activate now**. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002547076927.png" width="560" />
2. Select the number to activate, tap **Continue** to jump to the carrier page, tap **OK > Continue > Device activation**, fill in the verification information as prompted, and tap **Next**.
3. On the **Device activation** screen, keep the Bluetooth connection between the phone and watch active. The watch starts downloading and installing the eSIM profile. You can check the download status and result on the watch. If the watch shows activation successful, the process is complete.
4. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management**. If the screen shows that the eSIM service is activated, the process is complete.

# China Unicom Users: Cancel the One Number, Two Devices Service

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

   China Unicom Users: Cancel the One Number, Two Devices Service

    After activating the eSIM One Number, Two Devices service, you can manage it through the China Unicom app. On a HUAWEI phone, you can also cancel the service through Huawei Health.


> 💡 **Tip:** Due to China Unicom's service restrictions, once the eSIM is canceled, it cannot be reactivated immediately. You must wait 24 hours before you can activate it again.


## Cancel the One Number, Two Devices Service Through Huawei Health


> 💡 **Tip:** Only HUAWEI phones support canceling the One Number, Two Devices service through Huawei Health.


1. Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management**.
2. On the activated screen, tap **eSIM account management**, tap **Unsubscribe**, and follow the on-screen instructions to cancel the activated One Number, Two Devices service.
3. Wait for the cancellation to complete. Once it is successful, you will receive an SMS notification.


## Cancel the One Number, Two Devices Service Through the China Unicom App


> 💡 **Tip:** Both HUAWEI phones and Android phones can cancel the service through the China Unicom app.


     To cancel the One Number, Two Devices service, you need to complete the cancellation in the China Unicom app and delete the eSIM profile on your watch.

1. Log in to the China Unicom app with your primary number, search for **eSIM Zone** in the search bar at the top, and open **eSIM Zone**.
2. On the **eSIM Zone** page, tap **Unsubscribe**, and follow the on-screen instructions to cancel the activated One Number, Two Devices service.
3. Wait for the cancellation to complete. Once it is successful, you will receive an SMS notification.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002515561764.png" width="560" />


      **Delete the eSIM profile on your watch:**

1. Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, select **Settings > Mobile network** > **eSIM management** > **Settings**.
2. On the Settings page, tap **Reset** and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the reset and delete the eSIM profile.

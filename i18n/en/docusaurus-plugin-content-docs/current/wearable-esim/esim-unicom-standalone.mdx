# China Unicom Users: Activating a Standalone Number

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

   China Unicom Users: Activating a Standalone Number


> 💡 **Tip:** Carriers have recently been upgrading their services. For the latest carrier support policies, see: HUAWEI WATCH Series eSIM Activation Methods. A physical China Unicom SIM card is required; without one, you cannot activate a standalone number for your watch.


    An eSIM standalone number gives your watch a brand-new number and cellular mobile network, with its own separate rate plan and services such as SMS, calls, and network connectivity. After activation, when your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth, you can choose to make calls using either your phone's SIM card or the eSIM card; when your watch is disconnected from your phone via Bluetooth, calls are made through the watch's eSIM card.
    **Activating online in the Huawei Health app:**
    Open **Huawei Health**, go to the device details page, and tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **Standalone number** > **Activate online**, then follow the onscreen instructions to activate.
    **Activating in the China Unicom app (mobile business hall):**
    Phones running HarmonyOS 4.3 or earlier/Android phones: Activate in the China Unicom app (mobile business hall). Follow the onscreen instructions in the China Unicom app to complete the process.
    Phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later/iOS phones: First use a phone running HarmonyOS 4.3 or earlier/an Android phone to activate in the China Unicom app (mobile business hall), then connect to your original phone for use. Note that you must sign in to **Huawei Health** with the same HUAWEI ID.
    **Activating at a China Unicom offline business hall:**
    The China Unicom standalone number service must be handled at a China Unicom business hall. After arriving at the business hall, refer to the following steps or follow the on-site staff's instructions to complete activation.

1. On the device details page in **Huawei Health**, tap **General settings > eSIM management** > **Standalone number** > **Activate now**, and select activation at an offline business hall.
2. Follow the onscreen instructions to complete the following: Bring your valid ID and your watch to the carrier's business hall to handle the eSIM standalone number service.
3. Tap **Scan**, and use your phone to scan the QR code provided by the carrier to download the eSIM information.

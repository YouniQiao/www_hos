# Deactivate a Standalone Number as a China Unicom User

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

   Deactivate a Standalone Number as a China Unicom User


> 💡 **Note:** This feature is not supported after your phone is upgraded to HarmonyOS 5 or later. Please wait patiently for feature adaptation. Thank you for your understanding! Activation is not supported in Guangzhou, Guangdong for the time being. For details, call 10010.


    To deactivate a standalone number, you must first go to a China Unicom service center to cancel the service, and then delete the eSIM profile on your watch.

1. Go to a China Unicom service center to cancel the eSIM standalone number service.
2. Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, go to **Settings > Mobile network** > **eSIM management** > **Settings**.
3. On the Settings screen, tap **Reset** and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the reset and delete the eSIM profile.

# Create a to-do item

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_notepad_todo.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can create to-do items and set reminders to track your work project progress, daily shopping, and anything else you want to keep track of.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open App Center, then click to open Notepad.
2.  In the left sidebar, go to To-dos > All to-dos, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png) New at the top, enter the to-do item, and set a time reminder. If needed, you can also select a repeat reminder (such as daily, weekly, or monthly), and your PC will send reminder notifications at the specified time according to the repeat cycle you have set.
3.  Once you have finished setting it up, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) Save.

# Share Events for Easy Collaboration

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_ShareSchedule.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Share events, such as meetings and travel itineraries, with colleagues, and benefit from real-time changes.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Before using this feature, log in to **HUAWEI ID**, enable **Calendar** sync in Cloud, and enable the **MeeTime Communications** service.

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click to open Calendar.

2.  When creating or editing an event, click Add a member, select the contacts to be added, and click Done.

3.  After editing the event, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) to share the event.

4.  On the receiving device, click the notification to open the View shared event screen, and click Accept.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

# Share schedules for more efficient collaboration

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_ShareSchedule.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can share schedules such as meeting notifications and business trip plans with colleagues, so that one person creates them and multiple people can view them, with changes synced in real time.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before using this feature, sign in to your HUAWEI ID, turn on Calendar sync in Cloud, and enable the MeeTime communication service.

1.  Tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open App Center, then tap Calendar to open it.
2.  When creating or editing a schedule, tap Add shared members, select the contacts you want to add, and tap Done.
3.  After editing the schedule, tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) to save and start sharing the schedule.
4.  On the recipient's device, tap the notification to view the shared schedule, then tap Accept.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

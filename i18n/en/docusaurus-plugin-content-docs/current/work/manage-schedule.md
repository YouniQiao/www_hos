# Use multiple calendars to manage schedules by category

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_calendar_accounts.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>


Create multiple calendars and assign each a different color to manage different types of schedule activities, such as work, family, and personal, by category. The color of a schedule tells you at a glance which calendar it belongs to, making it easy to view.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On the home screen, tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the dock at the bottom to open the app drawer, then tap Calendar to open it.
2.  Tap Add > Add calendar, enter a calendar name and set a color, then tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) to save.
3.  Tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png) in the upper right corner to create a schedule, or edit an existing one. Tap Calendar (you may need to scroll down to the bottom of the page), select the calendar you want to move it to, then tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) in the upper right corner to save.

# Convert images to PPT for efficient creation

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_ImageTransfer_PPT.png" alt="Image to PPT" width="720" height="540" />

Send your favorite PPT images to Celia, and let Celia convert them into editable PPT documents to help you create efficiently.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![Celia](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia side window.
2. In an app (such as Files or Gallery), select an image in a PPT content style, press and hold the touchpad with one finger (or the left mouse button) and move it, drag it to the Celia side window area, and then release your finger.
3. Click the Tips prompt: **Image to PPT**. If the Tips prompt is not displayed, you can send text or voice to **Celia**, such as "convert to PPT".
4. After the conversion is complete, you can select the PPT document, right-click, and choose **Copy** or **Save** to save the generated document to your device.

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

Not all content in an image can be converted into an editable content format. The actual result may vary.

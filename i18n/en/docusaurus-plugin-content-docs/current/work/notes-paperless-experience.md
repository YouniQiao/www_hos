# Digital Note-Taking That Feels So Real

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_notes_edit.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**Notes** supports a wide range of brush styles and tools, allowing you to write and draw freely. Enjoy the creative freedom of a paper notebook on your computer.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The following example shows how to create and edit a note:

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click to open Notes.

2.  Click All notes, go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_public_add.png" width="24" height="24"/> > **New note** on the right, enter the note name, set the cover, content ratio, and content background, and click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_todo_checkmark.png) to save the settings and enter the editing screen.

3.  On the toolbar at the top, use various brushes, text, and images to create your note.

    +   Brush: Click the brush icons such as![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_ballpointpen.png),![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_pencil_2b.png), and![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_markerpen_fill.png) to use the brush directly. Click the icon again to access the brush settings screen, where you can adjust the brush thickness, opacity, and color as needed.

    +   Eraser: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_eraser_scribble.png) to erase part of the doodle or handwritten content. Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_eraser_scribble.png) again to access the eraser settings screen, where you can adjust the settings as needed.

    +   Instant shape: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_instantshape.png) and draw a desired shape (such as a circle, rectangle, or heart) in one stroke. After a 0.5-second pause, the shape will be replaced with a perfect one.

    +   Lasso: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_lasso.png) or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_square_dashed.png) to select the content you want to edit. You can then drag the selected content freely on the screen. Alternatively, click the selected content to copy, paste, or share it.

    +   Image: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_picture.png) to insert an image. You can also click the image to crop, copy, or share it.

    +   Sticker: Go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_stickers.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_stickers_store.png" width="24" height="24"/> to select your favorite stickers, covers, and paper from Resource Center and insert them into your note.

    +   Handwriting enhancement: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_lasso.png) or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_square_dashed.png), circle the handwriting you want to edit, select Neaten handwriting, choose your preferred font from the Font drop-down list, and adjust Level to enhance your handwriting. You can also go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_AIsummary_color.png" width="24" height="24"/> > **Handwriting enhance**, enable Handwriting enhance, and select Font and Level. The handwritten content will be automatically adjusted based on the target font and enhancement strength.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Only horizontally-written Chinese characters, English letters, numbers, and common punctuation marks are supported by **Neaten handwriting**. Actual usage shall prevail.

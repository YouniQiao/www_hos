# Extract Text from Images

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_gallery_extract_text.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Extract text from images to work, study, browse, and communicate with greater ease, whether the content you want to extract is a phone number, address, or study note.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Gallery to open it.

2.  Double-click the target image to go to the details screen, where text can be automatically recognized and extracted. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_AI_viewfinder_2.png) in the lower right corner of the image to highlight the recognized text.

3.  Select some or all of the text as needed, right-click the selected text, and copy, translate, or share it by choosing Copy, Translate, or Search.

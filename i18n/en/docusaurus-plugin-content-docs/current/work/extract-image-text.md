# Extract text from images intelligently

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_gallery_extract_text.png" width="720" height=""/>

Easily extract text from images to boost your work and study efficiency.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to enter App Center, then click Gallery to open it.
2.  Double-tap the image containing the text you want to extract to open its details page, and the text will be automatically recognized and extracted. You can also click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_AI_viewfinder_2.png) in the lower right corner of the image, and the recognized and extracted text will be highlighted.
3.  As needed, select part or all of the recognized text, then right-click to copy, translate, search, and more.

# A Smart Notepad

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_notepad_AI_tools.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Notepad comes with **AI tools**, which allows you to summarize, tidy up, or rewrite your notes with great ease.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Before using this feature, remember to log in to your **HUAWEI ID**.

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_notepad.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Notepad. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Notepad to open it.

2.  In the left pane, go to **Note** > **All notes**. Click a note in the note list in the middle pane, and click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM6_celia_AI_retouch.png) in the right pane to select a text processing mode (such as Summarize, Tidy up, or Rewrite):

3.  After the generation is complete, you can click Replace to replace the original note content with the rewritten result, or click Add to add the rewritten result to the original note.

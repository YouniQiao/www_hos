# Intelligently organize memo content

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_notepad_AI_tools.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Use the AI tools in Notepad to summarize the original text in a memo, intelligently format it, or polish and rewrite it, making text creation smarter and easier.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before using this feature, sign in to your HUAWEI ID first.

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to enter App Center, then click to open Notepad.
2.  In the left sidebar, go to Memos > All memos, click a memo in the memo list in the middle, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_celia_AI_retouch_colour.png) in the right pane and select the text processing method you want:
    +   **Smart summary:** Generates a full-text summary or a one-sentence topic to help you grasp the key points of the memo quickly.
    +   **Smart formatting:** Choose paragraph summary or meeting layout to intelligently format the text in the memo and generate the corresponding layout.
    +   **Polish and rewrite:** Choose a style such as formal, literary, or casual to polish and rewrite the text in the memo, meeting the needs of different scenarios.
3.  Once generation is complete, you can choose to replace the original text, or choose Add to save the result to a new memo.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Some features require the text to reach a certain length before they can be used. The actual situation shall prevail.

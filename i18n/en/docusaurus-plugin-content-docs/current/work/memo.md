# Notes, capture every flash of inspiration

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_notepad_note_edit.png" width="720" height=""/>

Use Notes to hold on to those fleeting thoughts and ideas.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open App Center, then click Notes to open it.
2.  In the left sidebar, go to Notes > All notes, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png) above the middle area to create a note. Enter a title and content, and add checklists, images, attachments, and more as needed.
    +   **Add a checklist:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_todo.png), enter text after the hollow circle, and press Enter to start a new line. Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_todo_empty.png) to mark the item as complete (the text is grayed out), and click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_todo_checkmark.png) to restore it.
    +   **Add an image:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_picture.png), then take a photo or select an image to insert. You can also drag or copy an image from Files into Notes.
    +   **Add an attachment:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_edit_more.png) > ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_attachment.png) , then select a document, audio file, video, or other attachment to insert.
    +   **Voice input:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_edit_more.png) > ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_AI_recorder.png) , say what you want to record, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_stop_circle.png) to stop recording. The recording is automatically saved in the note. Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_play_circle.png) to play it back.
3.  To change the text style, color, and more, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_textstyle_2.png) or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_textstyle.png) to configure the settings.
4.  Once you're done editing, move the cursor away or click another note to switch to it, and your changes will be saved automatically.

# Lift the Subject from the Background

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_gallery_extract_mainbody.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Hold down on an image to isolate the subject's profile from the image background, and then paste the profile to Notepad or add or share it in fun new contexts.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Gallery to open it.

2.  Double-click the target image to go to the details screen, move the cursor to the subject (such as a portrait or pet), and hold down on the touchpad (or left mouse button) with one finger until the subject recognition effect displays.

3.  Once the subject is recognized, you can select Copy or Share as prompted.

# Quickly extract the subject of an image

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_gallery_extract_mainbody.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

With a gentle press, you can quickly extract subjects such as people and pets from an image, then paste them into Notes or share them with friends.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to enter App Center, then click Gallery to open it.
2.  Double-click the image containing the subject you want to extract to open the details screen, move the cursor over the subject area (such as a person or pet), and press and hold the touchpad with one finger (or the left mouse button) until the subject recognition animation appears.
3.  Once the subject is recognized, you can choose to copy or share it as prompted on the screen.

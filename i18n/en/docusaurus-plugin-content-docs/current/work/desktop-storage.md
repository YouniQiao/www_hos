# Binder for Flexible Home Screen Layout

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_desktop_zone.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**Binder** allows you to fit more content on a single page of your home screen. You can also adjust the size of the binder or click an icon in the binder to quickly open an app, folder, or file.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Right-click a blank area on the home screen and select **New** > **Binder**. Click the default name text below the binder to edit the binder name.

2.  Hold down on the icon you wish to move to the binder, drag it to the binder area, and lift your finger.

3.  You can also perform the following operations as needed:

    +   Relocate a binder: Move the cursor to the binder blank area, press and hold the touchpad (or left mouse button) with one finger, and drag the binder to any position on the home screen. (The binder cannot overlap with home screen widgets or other binders. It is normal if the binder rebounds after being dragged.)

    +   Adjust the binder size: Move the cursor to the edge of the binder. When the cursor changes to a double-headed arrow (such as![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_cursor_NorthSouth.png) or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_cursor_NorthWest_SouthEast.png)), press and hold the touchpad (or left mouse button) with one finger and move it to drag the edge and adjust the size.

    +   Adjust the position of an icon in the binder: Move the cursor to an icon in the binder, press and hold the touchpad (or left mouse button) with one finger, and move it within the binder. Release when a rectangle displays.

    +   Sort icons in a binder: Right-click the blank area of the binder, select Sort by, and select a sorting rule, such as by name, modification date, or creation date.

    +   Quick access: Double-click an icon in the binder to open an app, folder, or file.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Home screen widgets cannot be moved to a binder. Please refer to the actual usage.

# Seamless Task Continuity Across Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/20005815_f001_AppContinuation.png" width="560" height=""/>

With continuity, you can seamlessly pick up a task from your current device on another device. For example, a video you're watching or a novel you're reading on your phone can be smoothly switched to your tablet to continue without interruption.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

The following steps use a phone and a tablet as an example:

1.  Turn on Bluetooth and WLAN on both the phone and the tablet, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On both the phone and the tablet, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Continuity, and turn on the Continuity switch.
3.  Open the content you want to continue on your phone (such as a video you're watching). The corresponding icon (such as HUAWEI Video) will pop up in the app bar on the tablet's home screen.
4.  Tap the app icon that pops up on the tablet to continue the unfinished task from your phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   This feature currently supports some apps, such as Notepad, HUAWEI Books, and HUAWEI Video. The actual support is subject to your device.
+   This feature is currently only supported between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

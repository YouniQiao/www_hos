# Seamless Image Editing Between Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20005815_f001_AppContinuation.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Continue tasks seamlessly on a nearby device. For example, edit a phone image on your tablet with a stylus, or switch back to your phone to save and share.

**Try it out**

1.  Make sure that both devices have logged in to the same **HUAWEI ID** and **Meitu** has been installed.

2.  On both your phone and tablet, go to Settings > More connectivity options > Handover and enable **Handover**.

3.  Open **Meitu** on your phone to start editing an image. The **Meitu** icon will be displayed on the shortcut bar of your tablet. Tap the icon to continue the editing task on your tablet.

4.  Tap the Meitu icon on the shortcut bar of your phone to switch the editing task back to your phone.

**Note**

+   Certain features of Meitu (such as collage and Video Editor) do not support Handover.

+   This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

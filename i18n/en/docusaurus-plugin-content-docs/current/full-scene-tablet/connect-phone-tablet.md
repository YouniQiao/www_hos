# Multi-Screen Collaboration Between Your Phone and Tablet

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20008669_f001_MultiCollaboration.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Connect your phone and tablet to use Multi-Screen Collaboration for cross-device operations, such as to display your phone screen on the tablet, use your phone apps on the tablet, drag files across the two devices, and edit a file from your phone on the tablet.

**Try it out**

1.  Enable **NearLink & Bluetooth** and **WLAN** on your phone and tablet, and log in to the same **HUAWEI ID** on both devices.

2.  Swipe down from the upper right corner of your phone to display Control Panel, and tap the name of the collaboration tablet to initiate a connection in **Super Device**.

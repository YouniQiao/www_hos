# Seamless Cross-Device Image Editing Continuation

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/20005815_f001_AppContinuation.png" alt="Seamless Cross-Device Image Editing Continuation" width="560" height="" />

With continuation, you can seamlessly pick up a task from your current device on another device. For example, you can smoothly move a photo editing task in Meitu from your phone to a tablet, then use the large screen with a stylus for more creative editing; when you're done, you can switch back to your phone to save and share.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Make sure your phone and tablet are signed in to the same **HUAWEI ID** and both have **Meitu** installed.
2. On both your phone and tablet, go to **Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Continuation** and turn on the **Continuation** switch.
3. Open **Meitu** on your phone and start editing a photo. The **Meitu icon** will **pop up** in the tablet's shortcut bar. Tap the Meitu icon that pops up to continue the editing task from your phone on the tablet.
4. Tap the Meitu icon that pops up in the phone's shortcut bar to move the editing task back to your phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

* Some features in Meitu (such as collage and video editing) do not currently support continuation. The actual experience prevails.
* This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

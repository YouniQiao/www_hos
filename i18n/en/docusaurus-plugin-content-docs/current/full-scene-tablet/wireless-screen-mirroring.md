# Wireless screen mirroring for a big-screen experience

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10045857_f001_WirelessProjection.png" width="560" height=""/>

No cables needed. Mirror your phone screen to a large display in seconds and enjoy a big-screen audio and video experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Connect your phone and the large display to the same LAN.
2.  Swipe down from the upper right of your phone to open the Control Panel, tap Wireless Projection, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the projection.

When you're done, tap the projection in progress capsule at the top of your phone<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202412090c4qmu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_projection_mirror.png" width="24" height="24"/>, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202412090c4qmu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_Multiwindow_close.png" width="24" height="24"/> to exit projection.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Wireless screen mirroring is supported only on some large display devices. Actual support may vary.

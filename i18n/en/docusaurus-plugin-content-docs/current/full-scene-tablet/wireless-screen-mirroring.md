# ScreenCast, for an Immersive Experience

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10045857_f001_WirelessProjection.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Project your tablet screen to a HUAWEI Vision in a heartbeat, and without having to run a cable between the two devices!

**Try it out**

1.  On the Vision home screen, go to Settings > More connectivity options/Connections/Remote & connections > Projection, and make sure that **Projection** is enabled.

2.  Swipe down from the upper right corner of the tablet screen to display **Control Panel**, tap **ScreenCast**, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the projection.

To exit projection, tap the screen projection capsule <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_projection_mirror.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of your tablet, and then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_project_close.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Close icon"/>.

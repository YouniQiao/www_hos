# Super Device: Quickly Collaborate Across Multiple Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10099735_f001_SuperDevice.png" width="560" height=""/>


Manage multiple devices in Super Device and quickly start collaboration to experience a wide range of multi-device scenarios. For example, connect your phone with a tablet for easier cross-screen editing, or connect your phone with earbuds to easily stream music.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Using phone-tablet collaboration as an example:

1.  Enable Bluetooth and WLAN on both the phone and tablet, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  Swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open the Control Panel, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_super_device.png" width="24" height="24"/> in Super Device.

3.  Tap the device you want to connect to start collaboration.

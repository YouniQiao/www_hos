# Copy and Paste Contents Between Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044822_f002_CrossDeviceClipboard.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Copy and paste images, text, and other materials between devices with the shared clipboard, to craft stunning works that put the world on notice.

**Try it out**

1.  Enable **NearLink & Bluetooth** and **WLAN** on your phone and tablet, and log in to the same **HUAWEI ID** on both devices.

2.  On both your phone and tablet, go to Settings > More connectivity options > Cross-device clipboard and enable **Cross-device clipboard**.

3.  On your phone, hold down to select the desired text or images, and tap **Copy**.

4.  On the editing screen of a tablet app (such as Notepad), hold down on the screen, and tap **Paste**.

**Note**

This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

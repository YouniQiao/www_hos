# Share Phone Communications with Nearby Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044640_f020_shareMobilenetwork.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Share the mobile network connection on your phone with your tablet, so that you can use your phone's communications, to connect to the Internet, make or answer calls, and send or receive SMS messages on your tablet.

**Try it out**

1.  Before you get started, apply the following settings:**Phone**: Enable **WLAN**, **NearLink & Bluetooth**, and **Mobile data**, and log in to **HUAWEI ID**. **Tablet**: Enable **WLAN** and **NearLink & Bluetooth**, and log in to the same **HUAWEI ID** as the phone.

2.  On the tablet, go to Settings > More connectivity options > Communication sharing, enable **Phone calls** and **Messages**, and select **Auto-connect** for **Internet**.

3.  Place the phone close to the tablet. When <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/status/ic_notification_hotspot.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Communication Sharing icon"/> is displayed in the status bar of the tablet, it indicates that the communication capability is successfully shared.

**Note**

If the phone uses dual SIM cards, the number selected for **Mobile data** will be used by default.

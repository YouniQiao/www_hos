# Large folders for flexible home screen layouts

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10044757_f002_LargeFolder.png" width="560" height=""/>


With large folders, you can fit more apps on a single home screen and resize folders to lay out your home screen flexibly. Tap an icon to open an app right away — convenient and practical.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Methods

**Switch the display mode**: Touch and hold a small folder, then tap Show as large folder in the menu that appears.

**Resize a folder**: Touch and hold a large folder until the menu appears, then release and drag its edge to resize it.

**Open apps directly**: Tap an icon in a large folder to open the app directly. You can also tap the stacked icon in the lower right corner of a large folder to open the folder and view more apps.

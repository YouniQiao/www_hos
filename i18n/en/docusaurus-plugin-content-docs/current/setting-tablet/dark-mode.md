# Dark mode, easier on the eyes

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044818_f001_DarkMode.png" width="560" height=""/>


Switch the screen background to a dark tone to reduce the harsh glare of an overly bright screen — it's both comfortable and power-efficient.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Settings > Display & brightness > Dark mode, then follow the onscreen instructions to configure it.

# Lock the home screen layout

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10044757_f007_locklayout.png" alt="Lock the home screen layout" width="560" height="" />

Worried that accidental touches might scramble your home screen icons? Try locking the home screen layout.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Go to **Settings** > **Home screen & personalization** > **Home screen settings**, and turn on the **Lock layout** switch.
2. Once enabled, elements on the home screen such as app icons, folders, and cards will be locked and cannot be moved or deleted.

To unlock the home screen, turn off the **Lock layout** switch.

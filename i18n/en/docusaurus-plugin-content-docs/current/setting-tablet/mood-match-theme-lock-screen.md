# Mood Match theme: playful stress relief on the lock screen

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10044753_f011_MoodMatch.png" alt="Mood Match theme" width="560" height="" />

Set the Mood Match theme to enjoy fun interactions with 3D emojis on the lock screen. Tap and press them for a playful, stress-relieving experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Go to **Settings** > **Home screen & personalization**, tap **More themes**, and under **Mood Box**, select **Mood Match**.
2. Tap **Customize** to open the editing screen, where you can change the **background style** and **visual style**.
3. Tap **Apply** to try the Mood Match theme, then wake the screen on the lock screen:
   - Each time you wake the screen, the system automatically switches to a new fun animation.
   - Tap or touch and hold an emoji to trigger a fun interaction.
   - A dedicated charging animation appears while charging, along with a charging sound effect when the system is in **Ring** mode.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

- The first time you use this theme, tap **Go to apply** to open the theme editing screen.
- You can adjust the volume of the theme's interaction sound effects by adjusting the system ringtone volume.

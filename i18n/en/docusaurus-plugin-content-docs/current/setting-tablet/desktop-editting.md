# Personalize and Streamline Your Home Screen

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20005692_f002_HomeScreen.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Use Home Screen Editor to switch up your wallpaper and theme, adjust icons, add widgets, and set the main home screen, for even greater efficiency and convenience.

**Try it out**

Pinch two fingers together on the home screen to enter Home Screen Editor. From there, feel free to:

+   Tap **Wallpapers**, **Widgets**, **Icons**, or **Themes** to select and apply the desired settings, for a more personalized home screen. For example, tap **Icons** and drag the **Icon size** slider to adjust the icon size.

+   Swipe left or right on the screen, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_public_home.png" width="24" height="24"/> above the most frequently used screen to set it as the main home screen.

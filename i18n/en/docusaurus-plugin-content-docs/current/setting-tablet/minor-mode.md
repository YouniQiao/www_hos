# Turn on Minor Mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202512310dkjZd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044767_f001_ChildrenMode.png" width="560" height=""/>


Turn on Minor Mode on your child's device, and some apps will automatically switch to Minor Mode to guide your child toward suitable content. You can also set device usage time for your child.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Digital Balance, tap Turn on Minor Mode, and set a password and security questions.

2.  On the Digital Balance screen, you can manage screen time and content access.

3.  When you open certain video apps, Minor Mode is enabled by default.

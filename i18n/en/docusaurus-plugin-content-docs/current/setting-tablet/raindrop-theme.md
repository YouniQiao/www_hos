# Interactive Raindrops: A Brand-New Theme Experience

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044753_f006_Raindrops.png" width="560" height=""/>

Set the Interactive Raindrops theme, and on the lock screen, tilt your phone to change the direction the raindrops flow. Tap a raindrop to flatten it — there's plenty of fun to be had.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & personalization > More themes, then under **Weather**, select **Interactive Raindrops**.
2.  Tap Customize to adjust the wallpaper pairing and rain density to your liking.
3.  Tap Apply to try out the Interactive Raindrops theme.

# Senior Mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20006188_f001_SeniorMode.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Senior mode applies elderly-friendly settings, like a magnified view.

**Try it out**

Go to Settings > Accessibility > Senior mode, and enable **Senior mode**.

Drag the slider in **Display magnification** to adjust to your desired level.

# Shake your tablet to bring your mood to life

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044753_f004_Theme.png" width="560" height=""/>


Set a Mood Bubble theme for a fun, interactive spatial experience. Rotate your tablet to reveal a new glasses-free 3D visual effect, or give it a shake and watch the ball's expression change with the force of your movement.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & personalization, tap More themes, and select **Mood Bubble** under **Mood Bubble**.
2.  Tap Customize to open the editing screen, where you can change the ball and background color.
3.  Tap Apply to try out the Mood Bubble theme.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   On the lock screen, keep your face facing the tablet and hold your gaze for a moment. When the ball at the bottom bounces up, you can unlock the fun spatial interaction.
+   Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

# Dark mode: easier on the eyes

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10044818_f001_DarkMode.png" alt="Dark mode" width="560" height="" />

Switch the screen background to a dark tone to reduce the strain that an overly bright screen puts on your eyes. It's more comfortable and saves power.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to **Settings** > **Display & brightness** > **Dark mode**, then follow the onscreen instructions to configure it.

# Cute Chubby Hands: Win Hand Accessories Blind Boxes in Rock-Paper-Scissors

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10044753_f010_fathands.png" alt="Cute chubby hands" width="560" height="" />

The cute chubby hands theme lets you play rock-paper-scissors and dress up. A little tap brings out the charm, and you can give your chubby hands a different look every day.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Open the **Themes** app, enter "chubby hands" in the search box, open the chubby hands theme, and tap **Apply**.
2. Wake the screen. On the chubby hands lock screen, tap **GAME** to enter the rock-paper-scissors game.
3. Tap the gesture buttons at the bottom to play your hand. Win 3 rounds in a row to earn hand accessories, which you can wear right away.
4. You can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_fatdress2.png" width="24" height="24"/> to view and switch between the hand accessory sets you have won.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If you use face unlock, for a better interactive experience, go to **Settings > Biometrics & passwords > Face recognition > Unlock screen** and use **Swipe to unlock**.

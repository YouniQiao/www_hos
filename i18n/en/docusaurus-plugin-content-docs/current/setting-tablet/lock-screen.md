# Lock Screen Interface, Personalized Style


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/20005688_f002_lockScreen.png" width="560" height=""/>

Pinch with two fingers on the lock screen to change the clock style, add lock screen cards such as alarms and weather, set a lock screen signature, and quickly switch wallpapers and themes to make your lock screen more attractive and practical.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Enter the lock screen editing interface:
    +   If you use password or fingerprint verification, turn on the screen, pinch with two fingers on the lock screen while it is locked, then unlock to enter.
    +   If you use face recognition, it is recommended that you select swipe to unlock. Turn on the screen, unlock with face recognition, then pinch with two fingers to enter.
2.  Tap the clock card to adjust the lock screen clock style, font, and color. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_lockscreen_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> to set a lock screen signature, then tap to go back.
3.  On the lock screen editing interface, tap the circle below the clock card to add your favorite cards. Touch and hold a card and drag it to adjust its position.
4.  Once you are done, tap Apply.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Currently, only some themes support this feature, such as **Recommended themes**, **Mood Box**, and **Weather**. You can go to Settings > Home screen & personalization > More themes to set it up.

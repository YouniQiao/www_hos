# Make a Lock Screen of Your Own

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20005688_f002_lockScreen.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

On the lock screen, you can create a lock screen that stands out, by selecting a clock style, adding widgets (such as alarm or weather), adding a lock screen signature, and changing the wallpaper and theme.

**Try it out**

1.  Enter the lock screen editing screen in either of the following ways:

    +   Turn the lock screen on, pinch two fingers together on the lock screen, and unlock the screen. If you have set up facial recognition to unlock the screen, it is recommended that you select **Swipe to unlock**, and pinch two fingers together after unlocking the screen.

    +   Go to Settings > Home screen & style > Lock screen.

2.  Tap the clock widget, adjust its style, font, and color, enter the signature in the text box under **Lock screen signature**, and tap **Confirm**.

3.  Tap the clock widget again, and tap the circle under the clock widget to add more widgets. You can also relocate a widget by holding and dragging it.

4.  After the setting is complete, tap **Apply**.

**Note**

This feature is only available in some themes, such as **Suggested** and **Space & Weather** themes. You can go to Settings > Home screen & style > More themes to select a theme.

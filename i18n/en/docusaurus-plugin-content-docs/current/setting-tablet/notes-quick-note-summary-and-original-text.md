# Notes quick note, summary and original text at a glance

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/20014428_f001_QuickNote.png" alt="Notes quick note" width="560" height="" />

While browsing content in an app, use the quick note feature to add a link to Notes. It not only generates a summary automatically, but also lets you revisit the original text with a single tap, making it easy to consolidate fragmented information.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Add the Quick Note shortcut switch:**

1. The first time you use it, swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open **Control Panel**, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_controlpanel_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/>, then tap the second <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the shortcut switch panel.
2. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add1.png" width="24" height="24"/> on **Quick Note** to add it to the panel, tap the gray area above to exit the add screen, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_choose_done.png" width="24" height="24"/> to save your settings.

**Use the quick note feature:**

1. For a better experience, sign in to your **HUAWEI ID** before use.
2. Open the content you want to save, swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open **Control Panel**, and tap **Quick Note**.
3. Tap **Add link** > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_choose_done.png" width="24" height="24"/> to save the original link to Notes.
4. Tap the notification at the top of the screen to open **Notes**, then open the corresponding note. A summary is generated automatically below the link; tap the link to quickly return to the original text.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Some apps do not support adding links, and summaries may not be generated for certain sensitive information. Your actual experience may vary.

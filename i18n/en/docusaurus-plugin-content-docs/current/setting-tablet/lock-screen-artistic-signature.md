# Personalized Lock Screen Signature

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044758_f013_Dynamic.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Tired of a dull, lifeless lock screen? Bring it to life with a personalized signature. Select a font that reflects your personal style while also harmonizing perfectly with your wallpaper.

**Try it out**

1.  Open **Gallery**, select an image, and go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_more.png" width="24" height="24" alt="More icon"/> > Set as wallpaper.

2.  Tap the clock area and then **Personalized signature**.

3.  Tap the signature for more personalized settings:

    +   Tap the text box under **Personalized signature** and enter a signature.

    +   Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/textformat_size_square_fill-01.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Font icon"/> to change the font style. You can also tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_ai_font_12-01.png" width="24" height="24" alt="AI Create icon"/> to select an image with characters. After the AI font is generated, tap to apply it to your signature.

    +   Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_desktop_color-01.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Color icon"/> to change the font color.

4.  Go to Apply > OK to complete the settings.

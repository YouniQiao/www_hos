# Getting to know the home screen

Learn about the home screen features on your HUAWEI Vision.

## Getting to know the new home screen

The new HarmonyOS home screen offers cards, large folders, and more, bringing you richer information and faster operations.

+   Status bar: Check your HUAWEI Vision's status, Control Panel, notifications, and more from the status bar at the top.
+   Large folder: Open apps in a folder in one step, without expanding the folder first.
+   Card: Quickly preview app information or use frequently used features.
+   Screen switch indicator: Swipe left or right to view home screen apps and cards. The screen switch indicator shows which screen you are currently on.
+   Quick action bar: Icons in the left area can be set to your frequently used apps, while the right area automatically adjusts to show your recently opened apps.
+   Organize home screen: Manage your device's home screen layout, adjust icon positions, and more.

## Organizing home screen icons

Manage your home screen layout to make your HUAWEI Vision home screen better suit your habits.

* Drag an icon to move it: Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on the app icon, drag it to any position on the home screen as needed, and then release the button.
* Use folders to organize icons: Group apps into folders by category and name the folders to make managing your home screen icons easier.

## Using cards

With cards, you can:

* Quickly preview or use apps, such as checking the weather or adding MeeTime contacts.
* Add cards to the home screen and choose different styles to create a personalized home screen.

## Adding more cards to the home screen

You can add cards to the home screen in any of the following ways:

* Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on the app icon, release the button when the menu appears, tap Card, switch left or right to select a card, and then tap Add to home screen.
* Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on an empty area of the home screen, release the button after entering the editing page, tap Card, tap the desired app, select a card, and then tap Add to home screen.
* Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on any card on the home screen, release the button when the menu appears, tap More cards, tap the desired app, select a card, and then tap Add to home screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>If there is no space on the current screen, your HUAWEI Vision will look for an empty spot on the next screen. If there is no space on either the current screen or the next screen, your HUAWEI Vision will automatically create a new screen to the right of the current screen to place the card. Cards cannot be pinned in folders.


## Removing cards

You can remove cards from the home screen in any of the following ways:

* Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on the card, release the button when the menu appears, and select Remove to delete the card from the home screen.
* Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on an empty area of the home screen, release the button after entering the editing page, press and hold the card you want to remove, and drag it to the delete icon in the upper right corner to delete the card from the home screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>When some apps are uninstalled, their corresponding cards will also be removed.

## Using large folders

You can use large folders to manage home screen app icons by category. Without expanding a folder, you can tap an app icon directly to open the app, laying out your home screen flexibly.

## Creating and deleting large folders

1.  Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on the app icon, drag it onto another icon, and then release the button. The two icons will be grouped into a new folder.
2.  Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on the new folder, release the button when the menu appears, and select Display as large folder.
3.  Tap the lower right corner of the large folder to open it, tap the folder name, and enter a new name.

You can also press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on the large folder, release the button when the menu appears, select Rename, and enter a new name.

To delete a folder you have created, move all apps in the folder to the home screen. When only one app remains in the folder, the system will automatically dissolve and delete the folder.


## Common large folder operations

You can perform the following operations on large folders:

* Open or exit: Tap the lower right corner of the large folder to enter it. Once inside, tap an empty area of the home screen to exit. When there are more than 9 app icons, a stacked icon appears in the lower right corner of the large folder. You can tap the stacked icon to enter the folder.
* Add apps: Tap the lower right corner of the large folder to enter it. Tap +, select the app icons you want to add, and tap Confirm.
* Remove apps: Tap the lower right corner of the large folder to enter it. Tap +, deselect the app icons you want to remove, and tap Confirm. When only one app remains in the folder, the system will automatically dissolve and delete the folder.
* Switch display mode: Press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on the folder to freely switch between small and large folders. For example, press and hold the confirm button on the remote control on a small folder, release the button when the menu appears, and select Display as large folder.

# Change the wallpaper

Keep your wallpaper up to date and give your HUAWEI Vision home screen a fresh new look.

## Change the wallpaper

Pick an image you like and set it as your home screen wallpaper to give your HUAWEI Vision home screen a fresh new look.

**Open the wallpaper settings**

You can open the wallpaper settings page in either of the following ways:

+   On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, press and hold the confirm button on the remote in an empty area. When the editing page appears, release the button and tap Wallpaper.
+   On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Home screen & personalization > Wallpaper.

**Set the wallpaper**

1.  On the wallpaper settings page, select a preset wallpaper, or tap Select from Gallery and choose an image you like. A polished, attractive wallpaper combination is then generated automatically.
2.  Adjust the wallpaper style. You can press and hold the confirm button on the remote and drag the image to reposition it.
3.  Tap Apply and follow the onscreen instructions to finish setting it up.

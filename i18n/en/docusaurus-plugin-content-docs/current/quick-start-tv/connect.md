# Connect and configure your HUAWEI Vision

How to connect and configure your HUAWEI Vision after receiving it.

## Connect the power supply

Connect the HUAWEI Vision to a power supply. It will power on automatically the first time it is connected. In daily use, you can turn the HUAWEI Vision on or off using either the power button at the bottom of the HUAWEI Vision or the power button on the remote control.

## Connect external devices

The HUAWEI Vision supports a variety of ports for connecting your devices. Refer to the following figure to connect devices and enjoy a range of entertainment experiences.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If you have activated broadband and TV services with your carrier, use an HDMI cable to connect the set-top box to the HDMI port on the HUAWEI Vision.
+   If you want to watch internet audio and video content, you can connect to the network via a wired or wireless connection. If you use a wired connection, use a network cable to connect the LAN port on the HUAWEI Vision to the LAN port on your router.

## Pair the remote control

Press and hold both the Home button ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002375930828.png) and the Menu button ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002376090800.png) on the remote control at the same time. Follow the on-screen prompts to press either the directional button or the OK button to confirm. Once confirmed, pairing is complete.

## Quick setup

During the initial power-on process, after the remote control is paired, follow the on-screen prompts to go to the quick setup page.

If you have a Huawei phone (running HarmonyOS 5.1.0.200 or later), you can follow the on-screen prompts to scan the pattern with your phone or verify using a connection code. The HUAWEI Vision will then automatically sync the WLAN and HUAWEI ID information from your phone, saving you the trouble of entering them manually. Otherwise, tap Skip and continue with the steps below.

## Configure the network

On the network configuration screen of the startup wizard, if you are using a wireless connection, select the WLAN you want to connect to and enter the password to connect. If you are using a wired connection, this step is not required.

If you choose to set it up later, you can also configure the network in the HUAWEI Vision Settings. Here's how:

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Network > Wireless network to access the network configuration screen.
2.  Select the wireless network you want to configure, enter the password, and tap Connect.

## Log in to your HUAWEI ID

On the startup wizard screen, you can log in by scanning the QR code with your phone, or use another method (SMS verification code or password). If you choose to skip this step, refer to the following for logging in to your HUAWEI ID later: [How do I log in to my HUAWEI ID on the HUAWEI Vision](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn00744811/).

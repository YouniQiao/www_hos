## Using the playback control center

In the playback control center, you can quickly control audio and video apps (for example, switching songs, playing or pausing, and more).

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002456010117.png)

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

**Control audio apps on this device**

When an audio app is running (such as Music), you can control it through the playback control center, for example, to play or pause.

1.  Open the Control Panel and tap the blank area at the top of the playback control center to enter the playback control center screen.
2.  The playback control center displays the audio apps that are currently running. You can tap the buttons to play or pause, adjust playback progress, switch to the previous or next track, and more.

The playback control center also supports controlling some video apps. For example, after you open HUAWEI Video and start playing a video, you can play or pause, fast-forward, or rewind in the playback control center.

## Notification Panel

You can choose to enable notifications for important apps so you never miss an important alert.

**Pin app notifications**

You can pin app notifications in either of the following ways to display notifications from apps you care about at the top of the Notification Panel:

**Manage notifications for a single app**

You can set different notification alert styles for each app.

Go to Settings > Apps, find and tap the target app, tap Notifications to enter the notification management details screen, and turn notifications for that app on or off.

You can set different notification alert styles as needed:

Banner notifications: Notification messages appear as a banner at the top of the screen.

# Customize the Control Panel

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_ControlCenter_edit.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**Control Panel** allows you to edit features, such as relocate, hide, and add shortcut switches, so that you can manage your device with greater ease.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen, or press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C (on the keyboard) to open Control Panel.

2.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png) to access the editing screen, where you can perform the following operations:

    +   Relocate shortcut switches: Move the cursor to the shortcut switch icon area, press and hold the touchpad (or left mouse button) with one finger, and drag the shortcut switch to your desired position (inside the white border under the volume bar on the editing screen), and then release the finger.

    +   Hide a shortcut switch: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_public_remove_red.png) on the shortcut switch.

    +   Add a shortcut switch: Among available controls, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add_norm_green.png) on your desired shortcut switch to add it to Control Panel.

3.  After the editing is complete, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) to save the changes and return to Control Panel.

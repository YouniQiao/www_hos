# Customize the Control Panel

<video controls src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/video/vid_ControlCenter_edit.mp4" width="720" height="540"></video>

You can customize the **Control Panel** as needed — for example, rearrange quick switches, hide the ones you rarely use, or add new ones — to make the Control Panel work better for you.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click the middle area of the **status bar** in the lower-right corner of the home screen (where the battery icon ![Battery](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png) is), or press the **HarmonyOS key** ![HarmonyOS key](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + **C** on the keyboard to open the **Control Panel**.
2. Click ![Edit](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png) to enter the editing screen, where you can do the following:
    *   **Rearrange quick switches:** Move the cursor over the quick switch you want to move, press and hold the touchpad with one finger (or hold the left mouse button), drag it to the desired position (within the white border on the Control Panel editing screen), and then release.
    *   **Hide a quick switch:** Click ![Remove](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_remove_red.png) on the quick switch to hide it.
    *   **Add a quick switch:** Click ![Add](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add_norm_green.png) on a hidden quick switch below to add it to the **Control Panel**.
3. When you're done, click ![OK](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) to save your changes and automatically return to the **Control Panel**.

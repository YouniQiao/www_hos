# Immersive Light for Stunning Lighting Effects

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig__Immersive_light.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Immersive light gives the home screen, Control Panel, Notification Panel, and other interfaces a transparent, luminous quality. Some interaction interfaces will also trigger subtle halos and dynamic particle effects, providing you with a refined and immersive visual experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

By default, your device provides a moderate sense of transparency and light effects. You can also adjust the intensity of **Immersive light**.

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Home screen & style in the left pane.

2.  In the Immersive light area on the right, select the desired level. The stronger the effect, the more prominent the lighting effects.

3.  When performing certain operations on the screen, you can see subtle halo and particle effects. For example, dragging content on the home screen, removing widgets from the home screen, or dragging the brightness or volume slider in Control Panel.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Only certain operations support lighting effects. Actual display may vary.

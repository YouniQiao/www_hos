# Stay on Top of Your Courses

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_calendar_CourseSchedule.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Effortlessly design your course schedule in Calendar by importing an image and pin it directly to your home screen for seamless access and streamlined organization.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click to open Calendar.

2.  Go to **Add** > **Add course schedule** > **Add from Gallery** > **Select from Gallery**, select the course schedule image, and click Done to import the course schedule.

3.  After the parsing is complete, check and modify the import result if needed, and tap Next.

4.  Adjust the schedule name, start and end time, and schedule time as required, and click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png).

5.  Right-click on the home screen, choose Add widgets, click Calendar, swipe down to the schedule card in the right pane, and click the card to add it to the home screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

If the information of a course in an existing schedule is incorrect, you can click the course and click **Edit** to modify the information.

# Calendar course schedule: check your class schedule anytime

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_calendar_CourseSchedule.png" alt="Calendar course schedule" width="720" height="540" />

In Calendar, you can quickly create a course schedule by importing an image, and add the course schedule card to the home screen to easily manage and check your classes.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![App Center](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to open **App Center**, then click **Calendar** to open it.
2. Click **Add > Add schedule > Import from Gallery > Start importing from Gallery**, select the course schedule image, and click **Done** to quickly import the schedule.
3. Once parsing is complete, check and edit the imported result, then click **Next**.
4. Adjust the schedule name, term start and end dates, and class times as needed, then click ![OK](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) to finish creating it.
5. If needed, right-click the PC home screen and select **Add card**, click **Calendar**, then swipe down in the panel on the right to the course schedule card and click the card to add it to the home screen.

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

For a course schedule you've already created, if any course information is incorrect, click the course's position, then click **Edit** to make changes.

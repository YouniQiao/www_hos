# Adjust the Volume of a Single App

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_ControlCenter_volume.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Adjust the playback volume for each app individually to enjoy an immersive sound effect.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/figure/en-us_image_0000002659166812.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  When opening an app such as Music or Video, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/figure/en-us_image_0000002659168000.png) in the Status bar in the lower right corner of the home screen to access the details screen.

2.  In the upper part of the sound screen, drag the system sound (![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/figure/en-us_image_0000002659168000.png)) or app volume slider to adjust the playback volume.

3.  By default, the sound screen displays the volume sliders of a maximum of three recently used apps. To adjust the playback volume of more apps, click Manage app audio at the bottom to access the details screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/figure/en-us_image_0000002659167084.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   Please make sure your device has been updated to the latest system version.

+   The playback volume of an app is dependent on the system volume. It is recommended that you adjust the system sound (![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/figure/en-us_image_0000002659168000.png)) to a suitable volume before adjusting the app volume.

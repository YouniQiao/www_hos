# Per-App Volume Adjustment

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_ControlCenter_volume.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can adjust the playback volume of individual apps, so you can enjoy clean sound without interference from other audio.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. When you open an app such as Music or Videos, click the middle area of the status bar (which contains the battery icon) in the lower right corner of the home screen, or press the HarmonyOS key + C on the keyboard, to open Control Panel.

2. Move the cursor over the volume bar area and click the arrow that appears on the right to open the sound details screen.

3. At the top of the sound screen, drag the slider for system sound or an app to adjust the playback volume.

4. By default, the sound screen displays volume sliders for up to 3 recently used apps. To adjust the playback volume of more apps, click Sound settings at the bottom to open Settings, then click App sound management on the right to open the details screen and make your adjustments.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

+ An app's playback volume is limited by the system volume. It is recommended that you first set the system sound to a suitable level, and then adjust the app volume separately.

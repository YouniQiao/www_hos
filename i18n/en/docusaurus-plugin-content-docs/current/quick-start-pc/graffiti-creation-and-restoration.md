# Doodle and One-Tap Undo

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_gallery_graffiti.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Doodle on your images and pause to turn scribbles into standard shapes. You can also tap at any time to restore the original image, even after your doodles have been saved.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Gallery to open it.

2.  Double-tap the image to be edited to enter the details screen, and then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_penkit.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enter the doodle screen. You can perform the following operations:

    +   Start doodling: Select the desired brushes and colors, and use your finger to start doodling. Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_more_1.png), enable Lift to form shape and draw your desired shape (such as a circle, heart, or triangle), in a single stroke with your finger. Once you've finished drawing the shape, pause for 1 second to have the hand-drawn shape replaced with a standardized shape.

    +   Add mosaic: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_mosaic.png) to add mosaic to the image.

    +   Erase strokes: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_rubber.png) to erase doodles or mosaics. Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_rubber.png) again. In the dialog box that is displayed, select an erase mode (such as Standard or Stroke), Size, or Only erase highlighter.

3.  After the editing is complete, go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png" width="24" height="24"/> to save the image as a new one or overwrite the original one.

After the image is saved, if you wish to modify or redo the image, double-click the image to go to the details screen, and then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_public_reset.png" width="24" height="24"/> to restore the image to the original one and edit it again.

# Doodle on Your Photos and Restore Them with One Tap

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_gallery_graffiti.png" alt="Doodle creation" width="720" height="540" />

You can draw standard shapes on a photo with ease using a variety of doodle brushes. After saving the photo, you can also restore the original with one tap.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![Gallery](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to open **Gallery**. You can also click ![App Center](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to enter **App Center**, then click **Gallery** to open it.
2. Double-click the photo you want to edit to open its details screen, then click ![Edit](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png) > ![Doodle](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_penkit.png) to enter the doodle editing screen, where you can do the following:
   - **Add doodles:** Select different brushes and colors, and draw on the screen with your finger. To quickly draw standard shapes, click ![More](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_more_1.png), turn on **Shape recognition**, then draw the shape in one stroke with your finger on the photo area and pause for 1 s. The shape will then snap into a standard shape (such as a circle, heart, or triangle).
   - **Add a mosaic:** Click ![Mosaic](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_mosaic.png) to add a mosaic to the photo.
   - **Erase brush strokes:** Click ![Eraser](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_rubber.png) to select the eraser, which can erase doodles or mosaics. Click ![Eraser](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_gallery_edit_rubber.png) again to choose the erase mode (such as **Tap to erase** or **Stroke erase**), **Erase diameter**, or **Erase highlighter only** in the pop-up box.
3. When you're done editing, click ![Confirm](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) > ![Save](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png), and choose to save as a new photo or overwrite the original.

After saving the photo, if you want to make changes or redo it, double-click the photo to open its details screen, then click ![Edit](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png) > ![Reset](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_reset.png) to restore the original, and edit it again.

# Screen Recording Made Easy

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_ScreenRecord.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Easily record your screen to capture tutorials, meetings, or any other on-screen activity, and share your recordings with friends or refer back to them later.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_public_screenrecording.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the lower right corner of the home screen or press![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_public_screenrecording.png) in the first row on the keyboard (when the Fn indicator is off) in hotkey mode to start recording.

2.  During the recording, you can perform the following operations in the screen recording floating window menu in the upper left corner of the home screen (default position) as required:

    +   Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_mic_filled_mute.png) to enable the microphone and record the sound. If you do not want to record the sound, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_mic_filled.png) to disable the microphone.

    +   Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_pause.png) to pause the recording. To resume the recording, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_play.png).

3.  After the recording is complete, click the red button or press![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_public_screenrecording.png) on the keyboard to stop recording.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

You can go to **Gallery** > **Screen recordings** to view the screen recordings by default.

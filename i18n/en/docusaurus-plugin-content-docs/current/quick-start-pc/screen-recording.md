# Screen recording: capture your screen's best moments

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_ScreenRecord.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can record what happens on your PC screen and share it with friends, or record online courses, online meetings, and more for later playback.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_up.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen > ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_screenrecording.png), or in hotkey mode (when the Fn key indicator is off), press the F10 key in the top row of the keyboard (or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_screenrecording.png)) to start recording.
2.  During recording, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_mic_filled_mute.png) as needed to turn on the microphone and record audio at the same time. If you don't want to record audio, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_mic_filled.png) to turn off the microphone.
3.  Click the red timer button ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_screenrecording_stop.png) in the upper left corner of the home screen (the default position) to stop recording.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

By default, screen recordings are saved in Gallery under From apps > Screen recording.

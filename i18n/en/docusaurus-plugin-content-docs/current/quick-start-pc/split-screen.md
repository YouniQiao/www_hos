# Split the Screen, to Multi-Task Away

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_SplitScreen.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Split-screen displays two app windows at the same time. You can drag content such as images and text between the apps to enjoy effortless multi-tasking. For example, you can drag an image from **Files** to the note being edited.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  When multiple apps are open at the same time, move the cursor to![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_window_maxmize.png) (or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_window_norm.png)) in the upper right corner of the target app window and hover. Click Show on left or Show on right in the pop-up to enable split-screen display.

2.  On the other side of the home screen, click another app to display two app windows on one screen.

3.  You can then perform the following operations as required:

    +   Switch split-screen panes: Move the cursor to![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_window_norm.png) in the upper right corner of the target app window on the left and hover, and click Show on right in the pop-up.

    +   Adjust the screen split ratio: Drag the split-screen line between the two panes to the left or right.

    +   Exit split-screen mode: You can also drag the split-screen line left or right until the other split-screen pane disappears.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Different apps have different minimum widths. If you see the split-screen divider snapping back after dragging, it means either that app doesn't allow resizing in split-screen mode, or it's already resized with the maximum allowed adjustment. Actual usage shall prevail.

# Turn on split screen to handle multitasking with ease

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_SplitScreen.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Split screen displays two app windows at once, so you can drag images, text, and other content between apps and get more done, faster. For example, you can drag an image from Files into a note you're editing.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  When multiple apps are open, move the cursor to the upper right corner of the active app window![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_window_maxmize.png) (or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_window_norm.png)) and hover there, then click **Show window on left side of screen** or **Show window on right side of screen** in the pop-up to turn on split screen.
2.  From the list of available apps on the other side of the home screen, click the app you want to use in split screen to enter split screen.
3.  In split screen, perform any of the following as needed:
    +   **Swap split-screen windows:** Move the cursor to the upper right corner of the active app window on the left![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_window_norm.png) and hover there, then click **Show window on right side of screen** in the pop-up.
    +   **Adjust the split ratio:** Drag the split-screen divider left or right.
    +   **Exit split screen:** Click the upper right corner of the split-screen app window![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_window_norm.png) (or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_window_minimize.png), ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_window_close.png)), or drag the split-screen divider left or right until the other split-screen app window disappears.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Minimum widths vary by app. If the divider springs back after you drag it, the app doesn't support adjusting the split ratio in split screen, or the maximum adjustable split ratio has already been reached. The actual behavior prevails.

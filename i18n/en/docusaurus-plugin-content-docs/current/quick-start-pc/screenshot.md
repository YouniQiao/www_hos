# Capture Your Screen with Ease

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_ScreenShot.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Capture your screen with ease, and on your own terms.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Capture the entire screen

You can capture the entire screen in any of the following ways:

+   Home screen icon: Go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_fullscreen.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the lower right corner of the home screen.

+   Screenshot key: Press F11 (or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_fullscreen.png)) when the Fn indicator is off.

+   Knuckle screenshot: Knock twice swiftly at one place on the touchpad with a single knuckle. Before using this feature, go to **Settings** > **Touchpad** > **More gestures** and make sure that Capture screen is enabled.

Capture a part of the screen

1.  Press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + Shift + S on the keyboard or go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_partial.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the lower right corner of the home screen. When the cursor changes to![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_partial_cursor.png), Partial Screenshot mode is enabled.

2.  Position the cursor at the starting point of your screenshot. Then, hold down on the touchpad (or the left mouse button) with one finger, move the cursor, and release to capture the selected part of the screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

You can go to **Gallery** > **Screenshots** to view the screenshots by default.

# Capture every moment with easy screenshots

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_ScreenShot.png" width="720" height=""/>

Capture what's on your screen with a variety of screenshot methods.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Capture the full screen**

You can capture the full screen using any of the following methods:

+   **Home screen icon:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_up.png) > ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_fullscreen.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to capture what's currently on the screen.
+   **Keyboard shortcut:** When the Fn key indicator is off, press F11.
+   **Knuckle screenshot:** Tap the touchpad twice in the same spot with a single knuckle. Before using this feature, go to Settings > Touchpad > More gestures and make sure the knuckle gesture switch is turned on.

**Capture a selected area**

1.  Press the HarmonyOS key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + Shift + S on the keyboard, or click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_up.png) > ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_partial.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen. When the cursor changes to ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_partial_cursor.png), you are in selected-area screenshot mode.
2.  Move the cursor to where you want the screenshot to start, then press and hold the touchpad with one finger (or hold the left mouse button) and drag to define the area. Release to capture it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

By default, screenshots are saved in Gallery under From apps > Screenshots.

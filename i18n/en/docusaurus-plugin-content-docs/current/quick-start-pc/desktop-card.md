# Add Home Screen Widgets

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_desktop_ServiceCard.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Preview and operate apps straight from the home screen, by adding handy app widgets.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  On the home screen, right-click any blank area and select Add widgets.

2.  Click your desired app from the list on the left, such as Notepad or Calendar.

3.  In the widget style list on the right, click your desired widget style.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

This feature is not supported in certain apps. Actual usage shall prevail.

# Add a home screen card

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_AddWidgets.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Add app cards to the home screen to quickly preview or use apps, such as checking memos or calendar reminders.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On the PC home screen, right-click a blank area and select Add card.
2.  In the app list on the left, click the app whose card you want to add (such as Notepad or Calendar).
3.  In the card style list on the right, click the card style you want.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Some apps do not support cards. The actual behavior may vary.

# Use Control Panel

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_ControlCenter.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Manage and control audio playback and shortcuts with greater ease, in **Control Panel**.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Click <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the lower right corner of the home screen, or press HarmonyOS Key <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png" width="24" height="24"/> + C on the keyboard to open **Control Panel**. From there, feel free to:

+   Use Media Controller: Controls audio playback and allows you to adjust the playback progress and switch tracks. Alternatively, click any blank area in Media Controller to access the details screen, so that you can adjust the playback mode and progress, switch to another recently used audio app, and transfer the playback to another device.

+   Quickly enable or disable a feature: For example, you can click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/status/HM_public_wlan.png),![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_hwshare.png), or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_eyemode.png) to enable or disable WLAN, Huawei Share, or Eye comfort.

+   Click the text area or the more icon (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_arrow_down_right_filled.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_arrow_right_filled.png" width="24" height="24"/>) of a feature to display more options: For example, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_hwshare.png) to enable Huawei Share, click the text area (or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_arrow_down_right_filled.png" width="24" height="24"/>) to access the details screen, and select which devices can find you.

+   Drag the slider to raise/lower a value: For example, you can drag the![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_public_brightness_filled.png) or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_speaker_2.png) slider to adjust the screen brightness or volume.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

More options can only be displayed by clicking the text area or the more icon (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_arrow_down_right_filled.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_arrow_right_filled.png" width="24" height="24"/>) for certain features. Actual usage shall prevail.

# Use App chest

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_AppCenter.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**App chest** allows you to search for, find, and open apps from one easy place.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Click <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png" width="24" height="24"/> in Dock at the bottom of the home screen or press HarmonyOS Key <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png" width="24" height="24"/> + A on the keyboard to open **App chest**, where you'll see all installed apps. From there, feel free to:

+   Open an app: Click the app icon.

+   Search for an app: If there are a large number of apps running, enter the name of an app in the search box at the top of the screen and click the app icon to open the desired app.

+   Relocate an app: Move the cursor to the app icon, hold down on the touchpad (or the left mouse button) with one finger, and drag the icon to your desired position. Release when a rectangle displays.

+   Uninstall an app: Move the cursor to the app icon, right-click the icon, and select Uninstall.

+   Perform further operations: For example, right-click an app icon and select Add to dock to add the app to Dock.

To install a new app, click <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_appmarket.png" width="24" height="24"/> in **App chest** to open **AppGallery**, and enter the name of the app in the search box, to find and install your desired app. You can then find and open the installed app in **App chest**.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Apps are being updated and released in **AppGallery** on an ongoing basis. Actual usage shall prevail.

# Using App Center

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AppCenter.png" width="720" height=""/>

App Center brings together all the apps installed on your PC and provides a search feature, so you can quickly view and manage your apps.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen, or press the HarmonyOS key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png)\+ A on the keyboard to open App Center and view all installed apps. You can:

+   **Open an app:** Click the app icon.
+   **Search for an app:** If you have many apps, enter a keyword in the search box at the top to quickly find an app, then click its icon to open it.
+   **Move an app:** Move the cursor over the app icon area, press and hold with one finger on the touchpad (or press the left mouse button) and move to drag the app to the desired position. Release your finger when a rectangular outline appears.
+   **Uninstall an app:** Move the cursor over the app icon area, right-click, and select Uninstall to remove apps you no longer use.
+   **Perform more actions on an app:** For example, move the cursor over the app icon area, right-click, and select Add to shortcut bar to add the app to the shortcut bar for quick access.

To install a new app, open App Center, find and click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_appmarket.png) to open AppGallery, enter a keyword (such as the app name) in the search box at the top, and search for and install the app you need. Once installed, the app will appear in App Center, where you can view and open it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips
Apps available in AppGallery are continuously updated. Please refer to the actual situation.

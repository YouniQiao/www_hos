# Open the Lid to Power On

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_settings_OpenCover_start.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Lift the lid to power on your laptop and get taken straight to the home screen in one go.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Power & battery in the left pane.

2.  Click More power settings in the right pane and toggle on the Power on when lid is opened switch.

3.  When the computer is powered off and the lid is closed, you can get it automatically powered on by opening its lid, without having to press the Power button.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

This feature is disabled by default. New devices can't be powered on when the lid is opened for the first time.

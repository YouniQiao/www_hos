# Open the lid to power on and start your PC quickly

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_OpenCover_start.png" width="720" height=""/>

No need to press the power button — just open the lid to power on, cutting down your startup wait time.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Power and battery in the left sidebar.
2.  On the right, click More power settings, then turn on the Enable lid-open power-on switch.
3.  When the PC is powered off and the lid is closed, opening the lid will power it on automatically — no need to press the power button.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

This feature is turned off by default, and lid-open power-on is not supported the first time you power on a new PC.

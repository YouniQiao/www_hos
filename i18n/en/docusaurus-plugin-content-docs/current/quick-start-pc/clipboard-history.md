# Clipboard

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_clipboard_history.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Enable **Clipboard history** to automatically save multiple copied records. You can view the clipboard history and paste content.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click System in the left pane.

2.  Click to enable Clipboard history in the right pane.

3.  Open a document or app (such as Notepad), select the text, image, or table, and press Ctrl + C to copy it to the clipboard.

4.  Move the cursor to the editing position, press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + V to open Clipboard, and click the content to be pasted.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   Please make sure your device has been updated to the latest system version.

+   By default, Clipboard displays a maximum of 24 recent copy records. If the size of the content copied at a time exceeds 4 MB, the content cannot be displayed in the clipboard history.

+   After you disable Clipboard history, the clipboard history will be cleared.

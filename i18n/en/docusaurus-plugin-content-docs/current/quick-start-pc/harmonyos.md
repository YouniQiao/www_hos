# New HarmonyOS 5 Home Screen Experience

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_desktop.png" width="720" height=""/>

The HarmonyOS 5 home screen provides a dock, Celia Search, Control Panel, and other features that make operation easy and help you work efficiently.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Methods

+   **Dock:** Located at the bottom of the home screen, it displays frequently used apps and services for quick access. Click an app icon to open the app or minimize it.
+   **Celia Search:**![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_celia_AI_search.png), located in the lower-left corner of the home screen. Click it to bring up the search box, then enter keywords (such as file names or file contents) to search for files, apps, and system settings on your PC, as well as information on the Internet.
+   **Celia:**![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png), located in the lower-right corner of the home screen, is a smart assistant that understands what you ask. You can interact with Celia by voice or text to quickly get answers to your questions, create meeting schedules, and more.
+   **Control Panel**: Located in the WLAN and battery area in the lower-right corner of the home screen (including the battery icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png)), it contains commonly used shortcut switches and the audio playback control center for quick settings. For example, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_wlan.png), ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/ic_public_bluetooth_filled.png), or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_hwshare.png) to quickly turn WLAN, Bluetooth, or HUAWEI Share on or off.
+   **Cards:** Add cards for frequently used apps to the home screen to quickly preview app information or click to open the app.
+   **Home screen shortcuts:** Add shortcuts for frequently used apps to the home screen and double-click to quickly open the app.
+   **Notification Panel:** Located in the date and time area in the lower-right corner of the home screen (including the notification icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notice_bell_filled.png)), click it to view notification details, such as system notifications, schedule reminders, and to-dos.
+   **Start menu:**![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_system.png), located in the lower-left corner of the home screen. Click it to view recent files and apps, or to access user settings, restart your PC, shut down your PC, and more.

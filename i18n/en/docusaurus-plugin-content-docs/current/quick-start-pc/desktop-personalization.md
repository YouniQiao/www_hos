# Customize the Home Screen

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_Homescreen_custom.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Add shortcuts to the home screen and customize the home screen layout and icons to suit your usage habits.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Add shortcuts to the home screen

+   App shortcut: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to enter App chest. Move the cursor to the area where you want to add an app icon, right-click, and choose Add to home screen from the shortcut menu.

+   Folder/File shortcut: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_filemanager.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Files. Move the cursor to the area where you want to add a folder/file icon, right-click, and choose Send shortcut to "Home screen" from the shortcut menu.

Customize the home screen

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Home screen & style in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, make the following settings as required:

    +   Change icon style: In the Icon style area, click and select your desired icon style.

    +   Adjust the size of home screen icons: In the Home screen icon size area, drag the slider to adjust the icon size. This setting only takes effect for app icons on the home screen. The icons in Dock at the bottom cannot be resized.

    +   Immersive light: Gives widgets and controls a luminous, translucent quality. You can choose how strong you want this effect to be. The stronger the effect, the more prominent the lighting effects.

    +   Switch the home screen style: In the Home screen style area (you may need to scroll down the page), select the home screen style you want.

    +   Adjust the Dock height: In the Dock height area (you may need to scroll down the page), drag the slider to adjust the Dock height. As the height changes, icons in the Dock will also be scaled up or down proportionally.

    +   Automatically add apps to home screen: Toggle on Add new apps. Once enabled, app icons will be automatically added to the home screen after the app is downloaded.

    +   Hide shortcut bar: Toggle on Hide the dock when windows are maximized or split (you may need to scroll down the page). Once enabled, the shortcut bar will be automatically hidden when the app window is maximized or in split-screen mode.

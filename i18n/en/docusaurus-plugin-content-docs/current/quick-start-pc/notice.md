# Viewing Notifications

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_NotificationPanel.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

View recent events, to-do items, and system notifications in **Notification Panel**, so that you never miss out on anything important or time-sensitive.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Click the date and time area (where the notification icon <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notice_bell_filled.png" width="24" height="24"/> is) in Status bar in the lower right corner of the home screen or press HarmonyOS Key <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png" width="24" height="24"/> + N on the keyboard to open **Notification Panel**. From there, feel free to:

+   View notification details: Click a notification to view its details.

+   View all notifications of an app: Click the notification card of an app (such as Calendar or Notepad) to view all notifications of the app. Click a notification to view its details.

+   Modify notification settings: Right-click a notification to perform further operations, such as Disable notifications.

+   Manage notifications: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notice_bell_set.png) in the upper right corner of the Notification Panel card to go to Notifications & status bar. You can then enable or disable banner notifications and adjust the notification settings for individual apps (such as pinned notifications, notification modes, and ringtones).

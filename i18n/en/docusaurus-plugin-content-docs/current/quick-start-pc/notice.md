# View notifications

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_NotificationPanel.png" width="720" height=""/>

The Notification Panel keeps you informed of upcoming schedules or to-do reminders, system notifications, and more, so you never miss an important appointment or task.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Click the date and time area (which includes the notification icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notice_bell_filled.png)) in the status bar at the bottom right of the home screen, or press the HarmonyOS key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png)\+ N on the keyboard, to open the Notification Panel and view all notifications. You can:

+   **View notification details:** Click a notification to open its details screen.
+   **View all notifications from a single app:** Click an app's message card (such as Calendar or Notepad) to expand all of its notifications, then click a notification as needed to open its details screen.
+   **Notification settings:** Right-click a notification for more options, such as turning off notifications for that app.
+   **Notification management:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_notice_bell_set.png) in the upper right corner of a card in the Notification Panel to open the Notifications and status bar screen, where you can turn banner notifications on or off and set how individual apps notify you (such as pinning notifications, alert style, and ringtone).

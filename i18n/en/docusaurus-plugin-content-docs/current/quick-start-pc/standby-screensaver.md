# Fun, Interactive Standby Display

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_MonmentX_cutePet.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**Standby display** enriches the standby display of your device, turning it into an electronic clock, photo wall, music player, and more.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Home screen & style in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, go to **Standby display** > **Edit** to access the editing screen.

3.  Swipe up, down, left, or right with two fingers on the touchpad, or use the arrow keys on the keyboard to switch between different standby display styles. When you switch to the standby display you want to edit, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png) at the bottom to edit it.

    +   Personalized clock: Basic clock allows you to customize the font and color. Balloon clock allows you to customize the color and triggers animations such as swelling, squeezing, and bursting on the hour and half-hour. Cute pet clock comes equipped with different adorable animals. When the device is in standby display mode, you can interact with the pet using touchscreen gestures or air gestures.

    +   Photo wall: You can add multiple favorite images that will be displayed in a loop.

    +   Concert hall: Creates an immersive audiovisual atmosphere based on the melody.

4.  After all editing is complete, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_left.png) in the upper left corner to return to the Standby display details screen. Set the duration for Show Standby display when power is connected and Show Standby display when on battery power (the duration must be shorter than the screen-off duration). When the device is idle (no operations are performed) for the specified period of time, it will automatically enter the standby display mode.

5.  When the standby display is on, you can:

    +   Switch between standby display types: Swipe left or right on the screen or use the left and right arrow keys on the keyboard to switch between different standby display types.

    +   Switch between standby display styles: Swipe up or down on the screen or use the up and down arrow keys on the keyboard to switch between standby display styles of the same type.

    +   Interact with pets (only supported by the Cute pet clock): Tap the pet on the touchscreen to interact with it. Use air gestures: Open your palm and face it towards the camera and pause for a moment. When![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handup_blue.png) appears on the screen, flick your wrist down to feed the pet or throw a ball to the pet.

    +   Control playback: Control audio playback, such as playing, pausing, or switching between tracks.

# High altitude monitoring

High altitude monitoring provides real-time updates on your physical condition in high altitude areas, helping you decide whether to rest or continue exercising. This helps prevent accidents and altitude sickness.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   High altitude monitoring is applicable when you are in areas between 2,500 and 4,500 meters above sea level. When the altitude exceeds 4,500 meters, SpO2 measurements may become less accurate, potentially showing higher values than the actual reading.
+   High altitude monitoring is only available when paired with a phone logged in with a HUAWEI ID registered in the Chinese mainland.
+   This feature requires the Automatic SpO2 measurement feature to be enabled at the same time.

## Enabling High altitude monitoring on your phone

Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, go to Health monitoring > High altitude monitoring, and enable Auto-monitor.

Phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later: Go to Huawei Health > Devices > High altitude monitoring, and enable High altitude alert.

## Performing an altitude sickness evaluation on your watch

If your device detects that you have been in a high altitude area for 6 hours, it will prompt you whether to perform a self-test for altitude sickness. (No prompt will be displayed if the altitude is between 2,500 and 3,500 meters, the average SpO2 value is greater than 90, and the risk of altitude sickness is less than 3.) You can also start a test at any time with the following steps:

1.  Press the Up button and select High altitude monitoring from the app list.
2.  Tap Evaluate to start a self-test.

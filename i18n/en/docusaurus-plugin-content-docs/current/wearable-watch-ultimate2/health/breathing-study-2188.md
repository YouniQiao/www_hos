# Respiratory Study

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The HUAWEI Research app is not available on iPhones, but it can be used on your watch independently.
+   Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you experience any discomfort, please seek medical assistance.

## Starting the Respiratory Study

1.  Pair your device in the Huawei Health app and open the HUAWEI Research app.
2.  Tap HUAWEI Research on your wearable device. Agree to the privacy statement if you are using it for the first time.
3.  In the HUAWEI Research app, enter Respiratory Study, select I have read and agree, and tap Join.

## Measuring your respiratory health

+   On your phone Open the HUAWEI Research app, enter Respiratory Study, and tap Start. Follow the onscreen tutorial to complete a measurement on your wearable device.
+   On your wearable device Open the HUAWEI Research app and enter Respiratory Study. Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, select HUAWEI Research and then Respiratory Study, and tap Start. If you are measuring for the first time, tap Next to complete cough recording and physiological data collection according to the measurement tutorial. After the measurement is complete, the measurement result will be displayed on the device screen. You can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002344579578.png" width="24" height="24"/> to view the measurement result description.

## Enabling nighttime measurement

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is not supported after the phone is updated to HarmonyOS 5 or later. Please stay tuned for further updates.

1.  Open the HUAWEI Research app on your phone, enter Respiratory Study, and tap the menu icon in the upper right corner. Tap Privacy management and enable Nighttime measurement.
2.  Once enabled, your device will automatically evaluate your respiratory health when you're asleep. You can swipe down on the phone screen to sync respiratory health data from the wearable when it's connected to your phone.

## Viewing data

Open the HUAWEI Research app, and enter Respiratory Study to view your current and historical measurement data.

Swipe down on the phone screen to sync measurement data from the wearable. After syncing, you can view the measurement result and respiratory rate. Swipe up on the screen to view the statistics details and historical measurement results.

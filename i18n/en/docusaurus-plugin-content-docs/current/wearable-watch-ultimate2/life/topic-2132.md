# Setting modes

You can quickly enable or disable Do not disturb/Sleep mode in the Control Panel of your watch. You can also synchronize the modes set on your phone to your watch.

## Enabling Do not disturb

Enable:

When Do not disturb is enabled, your watch won't ring or vibrate when receiving incoming calls and notifications (except for Alarm).

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   When the watch is paired with a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later: When Do not disturb is enabled, the watch won't ring or vibrate for incoming calls and notifications (except for Alarm and settings where Allowed interruptions is applied).

1.  Swipe down from the top of the watch face to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and select Do not disturb.
2.  After the setting, Control Panel is displayed as Do not disturb.

To disable Do not disturb mode, go to Control Panel and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002194646884.png" width="24" height="24"/>. You can touch and hold to change the mode or perform other settings.

Scheduled Start:

1.  You can access the Do not disturb setting screen in either of the following ways: Swipe down from the top of the watch face to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002379196064.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to Do not disturb. Go to the app list and tap Settings > Focus > Do not disturb.
2.  Tap Add time and set Start, End, and Repeat.

To disable scheduled wakeup, turn off the switch next to the corresponding time period. (If the device is in Do not disturb mode, turn off the Do not disturb switch first.)

## Enabling Sleep mode

Enable:

When Sleep mode is enabled, the watch won't ring or vibrate for incoming calls and notifications (except Alarm). Raise to wake will also be disabled, and the watch will enter Simple mode.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   When the watch is paired with a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later: When Sleep mode is enabled, the watch won't ring or vibrate for incoming calls and notifications (except for Alarm and settings where Allowed interruptions is applied). Raise to wake will also be disabled, and the watch will enter Simple mode.

1.  Swipe down from the top of the watch face to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and select Sleep.
2.  After the setting, Sleep will be displayed in the Control Panel.

To disable Sleep mode, go to Control Panel and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002230012881.png" width="24" height="24"/>. You can touch and hold to change the mode or perform other settings.

Scheduled Start:

1.  You can access the Sleep setting screen in either of the following ways: Swipe down from the top of the watch face to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002412596701.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to Sleep. Go to the app list and tap Settings > Focus > Sleep.
2.  Tap Add time and set Bedtime, Wake up, and Repeat.

To disable scheduled start, turn off the switch next to the corresponding time period. (If the device is in Sleep mode, turn off the Sleep switch first.)

## Syncing modes between the watch and phone

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Currently, this feature is only available when your watch is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

Swipe down from the top of the watch face to access Control Panel, tap Focus > More settings, and enable Sync with phone.

When enabled, mode settings will be synced between the phone and watch.

In Do Not Disturb or Sleep mode, if you want to receive a certain type of notifications or answer a certain call, go to Settings > Focus, and tap Notifications and live status and Contacts for settings in the Allowed interruptions screen.

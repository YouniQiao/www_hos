# Message management

When your watch is linked with the Huawei Health app and message notifications are enabled, messages that are pushed to your phone's status bar can be synced to your watch.

## Enabling message notifications

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, go to the device details screen, tap Notifications, and toggle on the switch for Notifications.
2.  Go to the app list and toggle on the switches for the apps that you would like to receive notifications from.

## Viewing unread messages

Your watch will vibrate to notify you of new messages pushed from the phone status bar.

Unread messages will be kept on your watch. To view them, swipe up on the watch home screen to enter the message center.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If you haven't activated the eSIM standalone service and your watch is disconnected from the phone, you will not be able to receive or reply to SMS messages or messages on your watch.
+   Messages will be stored on your watch after you view them in the message center. You can swipe left on the message center or tap the delete button on the details screen to delete them.

## Replying to SMS messages

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The feature is not supported when your device is paired with an iPhone.
+   You can reply to SMS messages directly on your watch once the eSIM Standalone Number service is enabled.
+   The quick reply feature is only supported when replying to an SMS message using the SIM card that has received the message.

+   You can tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002227220746.png"/> emoticon button below the message to select an emoticon for quick reply.
+   You can tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002262174461.png" width="24" height="24"/> keyboard button below the message to customize the reply content.
+   You can also swipe up on the message notification screen and select a quick reply.

## Customizing quick replies

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The feature is not supported when your device is paired with an iPhone.

You can customize a quick reply as follows:

1.  Once your watch is linked with the Huawei Health app, open the app, go to the device details screen, and tap the Quick replies card.Phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later: Link your watch with the Huawei Health app, open the app, go to the device details screen, and tap Message.
2.  You can tap Add reply to add a reply, tap an existing reply to edit it, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000001613477161.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to a reply to delete it.

## Deleting unread messages

Swipe up on the home screen of your watch to enter the unread message center, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000001562558476.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the bottom of the message list to delete all unread messages.

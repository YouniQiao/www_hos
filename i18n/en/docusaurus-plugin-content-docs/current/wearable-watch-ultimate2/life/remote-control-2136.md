# Remote shutter

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 2 or later or an iPhone running iOS 13.0 or later. If you're using an iPhone, open the camera first.
+   This feature is unavailable when your device is paired with a third-party Android phone.

## Remote shutter

1.  Make sure your watch is linked with Huawei Health.Swipe down from the top of the watch home screen, and make sure that <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002248941212.png"/> is displayed on the Control Panel, indicating that your watch is connected to your phone.
2.  Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera. Tap the shutter icon on the watch screen to take a photo.

## Setting a timer for photo taking

Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002283838693.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Remote shutter screen to set a timer for photo taking.

If your watch is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002283920897.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Remote shutter screen to set a timer for photo taking. You can also select the front or rear camera to best suit your needs.

## Selecting a camera mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your watch is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

1.  Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera.
2.  Tap the text at the top of the Remote shutter screen and select the portrait, photo, or video mode to best suit your needs.
3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002283845929.png" width="24" height="24"/> to take a photo/portrait, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/en-us_image_0000002248966936.png" width="24" height="24"/> to start recording video.

## Viewing photos

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your watch is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

After a photo is taken, you can tap the thumbnail in the lower left corner to view the most recent photo on your watch. You can also double-tap the photo, pinch your fingers on the screen, or rotate the watch crown to zoom in or out on the photo. To view more photos, open Gallery on your phone.

## Using the scene card

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your watch is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.
+   This feature is only triggered when your phone is placed on a stable stand or against a stable object. If your phone is held in hand, the scene card will not be triggered.

On the watch home screen, when you are using the photo/portrait/video mode on your phone, and your phone is 60–120 degrees to the horizontal plane and still for 2–3 seconds, your watch will automatically display the Remote shutter scene card to remind you to use the feature.

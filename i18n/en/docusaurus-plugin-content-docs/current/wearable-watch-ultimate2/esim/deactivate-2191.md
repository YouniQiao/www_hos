# Deregistering from the eSIM Standalone Number service for China Unicom users

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is not supported after the phone is updated to HarmonyOS 5 or later. Please stay tuned for further updates.
+   Currently, activation is not supported in Guangzhou, Guangdong. For details, call the service hotline 10010.

You need to visit a China Unicom customer service center to deregister from the eSIM Standalone Number service first and then remove eSIM configuration files from the watch.

1.  Visit a China Unicom customer service center to deregister from the eSIM Standalone Number service.
2.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and go to Settings > Mobile network > eSIM > Settings.
3.  Touch Reset and follow the onscreen instructions to finish resetting. eSIM configuration files will then be removed.

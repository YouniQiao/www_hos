# Deregistering from the eSIM One Number service for China Telecom users

Please visit a China Telecom customer service center in the number's home area for assistance.

(Note: Due to service upgrade, activation for new subscribers is temporarily unavailable from March 21, 2024. Subscribers who have activated the service are not affected, but will not be able to re-activate the service after deregistration. For details, call the service hotline 10000.)

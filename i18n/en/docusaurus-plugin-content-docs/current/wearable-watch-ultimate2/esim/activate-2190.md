# Activating the eSIM Standalone Number service for China Unicom users

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is not supported after the phone is updated to HarmonyOS 5 or later. Please stay tuned for further updates.
+   Carriers are upgrading their services. For their latest support policies, please refer to Activating the eSIM service on the HUAWEI WATCH Series.
+   You can activate the service for your watch even if you don't have a physical SIM card of China Unicom.

eSIM Standalone Number service gives your watch a new number and abilities to access a cellular network on its own. Your watch will have an independent tariff package with allowances for SMS messages, calls, and data.

Watches that the service has been activated for: When your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth, you can use the SIM card of your phone or the eSIM card to make calls. When your watch is not connected to your phone via Bluetooth, you can use the eSIM card to make calls.

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and go to eSIM management > Standalone Number > Activate. Select the eSIM carrier and touch CONTINUE.
2.  Perform the following operations as prompted: Take your ID card and watch to a carrier's customer service center to activate the eSIM Standalone Number service. Touch Scan and scan the QR code provided by the carrier to download eSIM information.

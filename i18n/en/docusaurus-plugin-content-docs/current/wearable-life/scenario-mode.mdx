   Set a scenario mode

    As needed, you can quickly turn Do Not Disturb, Sleep mode, and more on or off from the Control Panel on your wearable device.


## Turn on Do Not Disturb

     After Do Not Disturb is turned on, incoming calls and notifications are muted and won't vibrate (**Alarm** excluded).
     **Turn on now**:

1. On the watch face screen, swipe down from the top to open the **Control Panel**, tap **Scenario mode**, and select **Do Not Disturb**.
2. Once set, the **Control Panel** will display **Do Not Disturb**.

     To turn it off, open the **Control Panel** and tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002194646884.png" width="560" />** icon to turn off Do Not Disturb. Touch and hold to switch modes or configure other settings.
     **Scheduled turn-on**:

1. You can open the **Do Not Disturb** settings screen in either of the following ways: On the watch face screen, swipe down from the top to open the **Control Panel**, tap **Scenario mode**, and then tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002379196064.png" width="24" />** next to **Do Not Disturb**.
2. Open the app list on the device, and go to **Settings > Scenario mode** > **Do Not Disturb**.
3. Tap **Add schedule**, and set the **Start time**, **End time**, and **Repeat** days.

     To turn off scheduled turn-on, simply turn off the switch next to the corresponding time period.


## Turn on Sleep mode

     After Sleep mode is turned on, incoming calls and notifications are muted and won't vibrate (**Alarm** excluded), the screen won't turn on when you raise your wrist, and the watch face switches to a simplified mode.
     **Turn on now**:

1. On the watch face screen, swipe down from the top to open the **Control Panel**, tap **Scenario mode**, and select **Sleep mode**.
2. Once set, the Control Panel will display **Sleep mode**.

     To turn it off, open the **Control Panel** and tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002230012881.png" width="560" />** icon to turn off Sleep mode. Touch and hold to switch modes or configure other settings.
     **Scheduled turn-on**:

1. You can open the **Sleep mode** settings screen in either of the following ways: On the watch face screen, swipe down from the top to open the **Control Panel**, tap **Scenario mode**, and then tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002412596701.png" width="24" />** next to **Sleep mode**.
2. Open the app list on the device, and go to **Settings > Scenario mode** > **Sleep mode**.
3. Tap **Add schedule**, and set the **Bedtime**, **Wake-up time**, and **Repeat** days.

     To turn off scheduled turn-on, simply turn off the switch next to the corresponding time period.


## Sync scenario modes with your phone


> 💡 **Note:** This feature is currently supported only when connected to a Huawei phone whose initial version is HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.


     On the watch face screen, swipe down from the top to open the **Control Panel**, tap **Scenario mode > More settings**, and turn on the **Sync with phone** switch.
     Once enabled, scenario mode settings will sync between your phone and wearable device.
     In **Do Not Disturb** or **Sleep mode**, if you need to receive certain notifications or answer a particular call, go to **Settings > Scenario mode** on your phone, and under Allowed interruptions, tap **Apps and atomic services** and **Contacts** to configure the settings.


## Turn on Study mode


> 💡 **Note:** This feature is currently supported only when connected to a Huawei phone whose initial version is HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.


     After Study mode is turned on, incoming calls and notifications are muted and won't vibrate (**Allowed interruptions** and **Alarm** excluded).

1. On the watch face screen, swipe down from the top to open the **Control Panel**, tap **Scenario mode > More settings**, and turn on the **Sync with phone** switch.
2. On the **Scenario mode** screen, select **Study mode**. You can also tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002517518692.png" width="24" />** next to this mode on the Scenario mode screen, select scheduled turn-on, and set the start time and repeat days.

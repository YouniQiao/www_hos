# Set a video watch face

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="ultimate" label="WATCH Ultimate">


> 💡 **Tip:** Before using this feature, update HUAWEI Health and your watch to the latest versions. Custom video watch faces are not supported when the watch is paired with an iOS phone.

1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **My watch faces**, select **Video watch face**, and go to the video watch face settings screen.
2. Select a built-in video. Or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002356307844.png" width="24" />, choose to record a video or upload a local video, and after uploading, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002389907749.png" width="24" />.
3. You can also select **Style**, **Time position**, and **Features** to set the style, position, and other attributes of the video watch face controls.
4. Tap **Set as current watch face**, and the device watch face will automatically switch to the one you set. > 💡 **Tip:** You can add and upload only one video watch face, and you can also choose a video watch face provided by the system. System-provided video watch faces cannot be deleted. When a video watch face is set, tap the watch face to switch videos.

</TabItem>

# Minus-One Screen

> This document applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

On the minus-one screen, you can check weather information, control music playback, add cards, and more, bringing more convenient services to your daily life.


Smart scenario-based push notifications are supported only when paired with a HUAWEI phone running EMUI 11.1/HarmonyOS 2.0 or later.

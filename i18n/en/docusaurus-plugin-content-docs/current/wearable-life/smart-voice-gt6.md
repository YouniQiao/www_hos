# Smart Voice

> This document applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

Convenient Life


|  | Smart Voice |
| --- | --- |


   Smart Voice


    After your watch is successfully connected to your phone via **HUAWEI Health**, you can use Smart Voice for quick queries, such as checking the weather, time, horoscope, jokes, and stories. You can also set alarms, make calls from your watch, and open apps.


> 💡 **Tip:** Before use, update your device and **HUAWEI Health** to the latest versions. Make sure the language and region settings on your phone and the region registered to your **HUAWEI Health** app account are consistent.


## Using Smart Voice


1. You can wake up Smart Voice in the following ways: **From the minus-one screen**: On the watch face, swipe right to open the minus-one screen, then tap the voice ball to open the voice input screen.
2. **Press the button**: This is enabled by default. Press and hold the watch's bottom button to wake up Smart Voice and open the voice input screen. > 💡 **Tip:** If it is not enabled, open the app list on your watch, go to **Settings > **, and enable the **Press the button** switch.
3. In the watch's app list, tap **Smart Voice** to open the voice input screen.
4. On the voice input screen, speak your voice command to the watch. For example, "What's the weather like today?" or "Set an alarm for 8:00 tomorrow morning."


## Voice Broadcast


> 💡 **Tip:** Voice broadcast is supported only when paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 4.3 or earlier. It is not currently supported on HUAWEI phones, Android phones, or iOS phones. Stay tuned.


     Voice broadcast is enabled by default and controls the watch's voice broadcast feature. Once disabled, the watch will not broadcast voice.
     **Enable voice broadcast**: Open the device's app list, go to **Settings > Smart Assistant** > **Smart Voice**, and enable the **Voice broadcast** switch.
     **Adjust the voice broadcast volume**: You can adjust the broadcast volume only during a broadcast by pressing the volume buttons on the phone paired with the device.

# Bluetooth Voice Calls

> This document applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

Everyday life


|  | Bluetooth Voice Calls |
| --- | --- |


   Bluetooth Voice Calls


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Bluetooth voice calls require your wearable to stay paired and connected with **HUAWEI Health**.
>
> - Before use, update your watch to the latest version.


## Syncing contacts


      The watch does not support adding contacts on its own. When the watch and phone are connected over Bluetooth, the watch automatically syncs contacts from your phone's contacts list.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - If contacts are not synced automatically, open the app list on your watch, tap **Phone > Contacts** > **Sync contacts** > **Sync now**.
>
> - If you don't need to sync contacts, go to the device details screen in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Contacts**, and turn off **Sync data from phone to watch**.
>
> - On iOS phones, **HUAWEI Health** does not have the **Sync data from phone to watch** switch. iOS 18 supports contact authorization. Go to **Settings > Apps > HUAWEI Health > Contacts** on your phone and configure contact syncing as needed**.**
>
> - To delete a contact, delete it from your phone's contacts list. The watch will sync the latest contacts list once it connects to your phone. Deleting contacts is not supported on the watch.


## Answering/declining calls on your watch

     When there is an incoming call on your phone, your wearable receives a call notification at the same time. On your wearable, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002523394300.png" width="24" /> to answer the call, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002523434282.png" width="24" /> to decline it, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002523408294.png" width="24" /> to decline with a text message.
     Once the call is connected, your wearable switches to speakerphone mode.


> 💡 **Tip:** When paired with an iOS phone, declining a call with a text message is not supported. The actual behavior may vary.


## Making calls on your watch


      In the app list on your watch, tap **Phone**. You can choose from several ways to make a call as needed:

- Tap **Call history** to view your watch's call history. Tap a specific entry to call back.
- Tap **Contacts** to call a contact as needed.
- Tap **Keypad**, enter the number you want to call, and tap the call button to make the call.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - When you make a call from your watch's contacts, which SIM card a dual-SIM phone uses is determined by the phone's own protocol. The watch controls the phone to place the call, but cannot decide which SIM card the phone uses. For details, consult your phone's customer service.
>
> - When you make a call from your watch, the watch plays the audio and the phone shows a Bluetooth call with no sound. If you want to use the phone for the call, switch the phone to earpiece mode; the watch will then be silent.

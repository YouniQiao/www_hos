# Live Window Management

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

   Live Window Management

    The watch's Live Window can sync and display Live Window information from your HUAWEI phone, providing you with thoughtful real-time reminders so you can stay on top of the current task at any time.


> 💡 **Note:** To use the Live Window feature, make sure your watch has been updated to the latest version. The Live Window feature is only supported when paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 4.0 or later. It is not supported on third-party Android or iOS phones.


## Enabling the Live Window


1. In the app list on your wearable device, go to **Settings > Watch face & home screen** > **Live Window** and turn on the Live Window switch.
2. On your phone, go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **General settings > Live Window**, and turn on the **Sync phone Live Window info to watch Live Window** switch.


## Deleting Live Window messages


- Delete the corresponding live message on your phone's lock screen, and the live message on your watch is deleted at the same time.
- Delete the corresponding live message in your phone's notification panel, and the live message on your watch is deleted at the same time.
- Swipe right on the Live Capsule on your phone and delete it, and the live message on your watch is deleted at the same time.
- Tap the live info on your watch's Live Window, swipe left in the Live Window message list, and tap the delete button to delete the live message on your watch.


## More operations


- Swipe up on the watch face to enter the message center, then tap a live card to open the live notification details page and view the details of the corresponding live notification.
- Swipe up on the watch face to enter the message center, then tap a stacked live card to open the live notification list and view all current Live Window cards.
- Swipe down on the lower half of the watch face screen to close the Live Window. After you manually swipe down to close and hide the Live Window on the watch face, swipe up on the watch face again to enter the message center, then return to the watch face and the Live Window will pop up again.

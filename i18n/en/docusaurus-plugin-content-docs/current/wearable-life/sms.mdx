# Sending and Receiving Messages

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.


After you activate a standalone number plan for your watch, you can send and receive messages independently on the watch.
> 💡 **Tip:** The eSIM one-number-two-devices plan does not support sending and receiving messages independently. To use this feature, update your watch to the latest version. The standalone number plan does not support MMS, message forwarding, group messaging, or blacklist blocking.
## Viewing messages
When the standalone number used by your watch receives a message, go to the watch's app list and tap the icon to view unread messages and the number of messages.
> 💡 **Tip:** On the message conversation details screen, swipe up to call the contact or view the contact's details.
## Sending messages
To create a message:
In the watch's app list, tap the icon, tap the icon at the top, select a recipient, enter your custom message, and then tap the icon.
To reply to a message:
In the message list, tap a conversation, or on the notification details screen, reply in either of the following ways:
- Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001511629714.png" width="24" /> at the bottom, then type your reply on the keyboard or select an emoji.
- Swipe up on the screen, select a quick reply, and tap it to send.
## Deleting messages
To delete a single message in a one-on-one conversation:
In the message list, tap a conversation, touch and hold a message, and then tap the icon.
To delete all messages from one contact:
In the message list, find the contact's conversation, swipe left on the screen, and tap the delete icon.
To delete messages from multiple contacts or all messages:
In the message list, touch and hold a conversation, select multiple or all conversations, and then tap the icon.

# Play music

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

You can use your watch to control music playback on your phone, or add music to your watch for standalone playback.
## Control music playback on your phone
1. On the device details page, tap , and enable the switch. > 💡 **Note:** No setup is required for iOS phones. When using this feature, keep your watch and properly connected.
2. Open the music app on your phone and start playing music.
3. On your watch's home screen, swipe right to the minus-one screen, tap the music card to open the playback screen, where you can pause, switch songs, and adjust the volume.
## Add music to your watch
> 💡 **Note:** Before adding music to your watch, make sure the songs in your phone's music app have finished downloading and meet the watch's music import format requirements.
1. Go to the device details page, and tap > > . For iOS phones: Go to the device details page, and tap > .
2. Select the music you want to add, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002003864029.png" width="24" /> in the upper corner of the screen to finish adding.
3. On the music management page, tap , enter a playlist name, and then select music to organize the music uploaded to your watch into categories. After creating a playlist, you can also tap > in the upper corner of the playlist screen.
4. Once music files are added to your watch, you can play music without an internet connection.
5. To delete added music, go to the device details page, tap > , and in the music list, tap the icon next to the song name and select .
## Control music playback on your watch
1. Press the watch's upper button to open the app list, select <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001602662018.png" width="24" /> , or open another music app and tap in the music screen.

2. Tap the play button to start playing music.
3. On the music playback screen, you can adjust the volume and set the playback order (such as sequential playback, shuffle, and more).
4. On the music playback screen, swipe up to view the playlist; swipe right to open the local songs screen, and tap to view all songs on your watch.

# Set Up Custom Cards

> This topic applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

Configure app cards, such as adding cards or
## Add a Card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to open the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002200805644.png" width="24" /> below the card.

3. Tap the Up button to save. On the watch face screen, swipe left to view the added card.
## Edit a Combo Card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to open the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Tap below the card to enter the customization screen, where you can perform the following operations as needed: Tap the target widget, then select the widget you want to replace it with on the feature screen.
3. Touch and hold the target widget and drag it to adjust its position. > 💡 **Tip:** Widgets in a combo card cannot be deleted, only replaced. To add a card for a workout, you must first add the workout to the workout list.
4. Tap the Up button to save.
## Move a Card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to open the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Touch and hold the card you want to move, and drag it left or right to the target position.
3. Tap the Up button to save.
## Remove a Card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to open the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Touch and hold the card you want to remove, and drag it up to.
3. Tap the Up button to save.

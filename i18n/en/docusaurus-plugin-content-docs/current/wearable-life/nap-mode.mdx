   Fall Detection and SOS Emergency Assistance

    After Fall Detection is enabled, the watch uses its built-in acceleration sensor to detect your posture. If a fall is detected and there is a risk of you becoming incapacitated, the watch will initiate SOS emergency calling for you.
    To disable Fall Detection, open the app list on your watch, go to **Settings > SOS Emergency Assistance**, and turn off **Fall Detection**.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - When paired with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, update your watch and **HUAWEI Health** to the latest versions.
>
> - Your device may not be able to detect all falls in every situation. High-impact movements may also cause the watch to trigger Fall Detection unintentionally.
>
> - When your phone and watch are connected via Bluetooth, if the phone uses dual SIM cards, set the default dialing card in advance.
>
> - If the watch does not have an independent eSIM number, it cannot send SOS messages when connected to an iOS phone.

1. In the **HUAWEI Health** app, go to **Me > Profile** > **Personal emergency info**, and add your personal information and medical information such as blood type.
2. Go to **Emergency contacts > Add emergency contact**, and select the contact to add on the contact selection screen. ## View personal emergency information

     **On the watch**:
     In the app list on your watch, go to **Settings > SOS Emergency Assistance** to view your personal emergency information.
     **In HUAWEI Health:**
     In **HUAWEI Health**, go to **Me > Profile** > **Personal emergency info** to view your personal emergency information.


## Fall Detection and automatic emergency calling


1. In the app list on your watch, go to **Settings > SOS Emergency Assistance**, and enable **Fall Detection**.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002363797630.png" width="560" />
2. After the watch detects a fall and there is a risk of you becoming incapacitated, a dialog box will appear asking whether you need to make an emergency call.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002395325553.jpg" width="560" />
3. If you select **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002395187029.png" width="24" />**, or if there is no action within 60s, emergency assistance will be initiated automatically: If emergency contacts have been set, the watch will automatically call the first emergency contact and send an SOS message containing your current location to all emergency contacts (in environments with a weak satellite positioning signal, the SOS message sent will not contain location information).
4. If no emergency contacts have been set, a public emergency number will be displayed. Select it to make the call as needed.


## Manually initiate emergency assistance


      Press the Up button (Power button) 5 times in a row to initiate SOS emergency assistance:

- If emergency contacts have been set, the watch will automatically call the first emergency contact and send an SOS message containing your current location to all emergency contacts (in environments with a weak satellite positioning signal, the SOS message sent will not contain location information).
- If no emergency contacts have been set, a public emergency number will be displayed. Select it to make the call as needed.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - If **Fall Detection** is disabled, the watch will not automatically initiate SOS emergency assistance after detecting a severe fall. You can press the Up button 5 times in a row to make an emergency call.
>
> - When the watch is properly connected to your phone via Bluetooth, pressing the Up button 5 times in a row makes the emergency call from the watch, and the call screen will also appear on your phone a few seconds later. When not connected, only a watch with an eSIM can make the call.

   Control Nap Mode

    After Nap Mode is enabled in the car, the watch will vibrate to remind you when your nap ends. Raise your wrist to display the nap card, and quickly dismiss or postpone the reminder on your watch.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Before using this feature, update your watch to the latest version.
>
> - To use this feature, WLAN and Bluetooth must be enabled on both your phone and the car's head unit. They only need to be enabled, not connected.
>
> - This feature is supported when paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 2 or later.
>
> - For supported car models, see: Car models that support controlling Nap Mode with a HUAWEI watch


## Dismiss the nap reminder


- Tap the dismiss icon on the watch screen.
- Press and hold the Up button on the watch.


## Postpone the nap reminder


- Tap the postpone icon on the watch screen.
- Press the Up button on the watch.
- If there is no action for 40s, the reminder is postponed automatically.

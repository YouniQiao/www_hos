# Remote Photo

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.


The Remote Photo feature lets you use your watch to remotely control your phone's camera.
> 💡 **Tip:** Before use, update your devices to the latest version. When paired with an iOS or Android phone, you need to open the camera on the phone first. When paired with an Android phone, the phone must have no more than seven Bluetooth connections. If there are more, delete some connections before using this feature.
## Remote Photo
1. Check the connection status between your watch and phone to ensure they are properly connected. You can swipe down from the top of the watch face and check in the Control Panel that the connection status between your watch and phone is <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002355442668.png" width="24" />
.
2. In the watch's app list, tap the app to open your phone's camera, then tap the camera icon on the watch screen to take a photo.
## Timed Photo
1. In the watch's app list, tap the app to open your phone's camera, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002355602604.png" width="24" />
 on the watch's Remote Photo screen to set a timer for the photo. Tap again to switch the time.
2. Tap the camera icon on the watch screen. The photo is taken when the countdown ends.

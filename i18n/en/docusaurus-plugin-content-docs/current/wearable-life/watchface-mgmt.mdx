# Watch Face Management

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

## Customizing a watch face

     You can customize the style and feature modules of a watch face to your liking.

1. On the watch face screen of your device, touch and hold the watch face to enter the watch face selection screen, then swipe left or right to select a customizable watch face.
2. Touch **Customize** below the watch face, then swipe left or right to set different styles, features, and more. On screens such as **Style** and **Color**, swipe up or down to select your preferred style, color, and more.
3. On the **Features** screen, touch a widget to change its feature.


## Changing a watch face

     **Changing a watch face on your device**:
     On the watch face screen of your device, touch and hold the watch face to enter the watch face selection screen, swipe left or right to select a watch face, then touch the watch face to set it as the current watch face.
     **Changing a watch face in the HUAWEI Health app**:

1. Go to the device details screen in **HUAWEI Health**, then touch **My watch faces**.
2. Select your preferred watch face, then touch **Set as current watch face**. If the watch face has not been installed, install it first. Once installed, it will automatically be set as the current watch face.


## Deleting a watch face

     **Removing a watch face on your device**

1. On the watch face screen of your device, touch and hold the watch face to enter the watch face selection screen.
2. Swipe up on the watch face, then touch **Remove** to remove it from the watch face list.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - On the watch face screen of your device, touch and hold the watch face to enter the watch face selection screen, swipe left to the last page, touch **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002412503437.png" width="24" />**, then touch the watch face to use it again.
>
> - Go to the device details screen in **HUAWEI Health** on your phone, touch **My watch faces**, then select and set a watch face.


     **Deleting a watch face in the HUAWEI Health app**

1. Go to the device details screen in **HUAWEI Health**, then touch **My watch faces > Manage local watch faces**.
2. Touch **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002360016258.png" width="560" />** in the upper right corner of the watch face to delete it. > 💡 **Tips:** > > - If there is no **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002393496329.png" width="560" />** in the upper right corner of the watch face, the watch face cannot be deleted, such as the **Gallery watch face**. > > - After a purchased watch face is deleted from **My watch faces > Manage local watch faces**, you do not need to purchase it again. Simply reinstall it.

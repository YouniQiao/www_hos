## Customize a watch face


You can customize the style and function modules of a watch face to your liking.

1. On the watch face screen of your device, touch and hold the watch face to enter the watch face selection screen, then swipe left or right to select a customizable watch face.
2. Touch **Customize** below the watch face, then swipe left or right to set different styles, functions, and more. On screens such as **Style** and **Color**, swipe up or down to select your preferred style, color, and more.
3. On the **Functions** screen, touch a component to change its function.

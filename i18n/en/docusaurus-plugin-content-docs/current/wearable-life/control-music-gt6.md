# Control music playback

> This article applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

Everyday life


|  | Control music playback |
| --- | --- |


   Control music playback

    You can use your watch to control music playback on your phone, or add music to your watch for standalone playback.


## Control music playback on your phone


1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Music** and enable **Watch controls phone music**. > 💡 **Note:** No setup is required for iOS phones. When using this feature, keep your watch properly connected to **HUAWEI Health**.
2. Open the music app on your phone and start playing music.
3. On your watch's home screen, swipe right to the minus-one screen, tap the music card to open the playback screen, where you can pause, switch songs, and adjust the volume.


## Add music to your watch


> 💡 **Note:** Before adding music to your watch, make sure the songs in the music app on your phone have been fully downloaded and meet the format requirements for importing music to your watch.


1. Go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, and tap **Music > Music management** > **Add music**. For iOS phones: Go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, and tap **Music > Import music from phone**.
2. Select the music you want to add, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002003864029.png" width="24" /> in the upper right corner of the screen to finish adding.
3. On the Music management page, tap **New playlist**, enter a playlist name, and then select music to organize the music uploaded to your watch into categories. After creating a playlist, you can also tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002005714677.png" width="24" /> > Add music** in the upper right corner of the playlist page.
4. Once music files are successfully added to your watch, you can play music without an internet connection.
5. To delete added music, go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Music > Music management**, and in the music list, tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002366067117.png" width="24" />** icon next to a song title, then select **Delete**.


## Control music playback on your watch


1. Press the watch's upper button to enter the app list, select <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001602662018.png" width="560" />**Music**, or open another music app and tap **Now playing** on the music screen.
2. Tap the play button to start playing music.
3. On the music playback screen, you can adjust the volume and set the playback order (such as sequential or shuffle).
4. On the music playback screen, swipe up to view the playlist; swipe right to open the local songs page, and tap **Local songs** to view all songs on your watch.

# Use your watch for standalone navigation

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.


When your watch is connected to the internet, it supports standalone navigation, so you can enjoy smart, interactive walking and cycling navigation without your phone — perfect for getting around and for everyday life.
> 💡 **Tip:** Currently, only walking and cycling navigation are supported.
## Use your watch for standalone navigation
1. On the watch's app list, tap to open the app.
2. Tap the search icon at the bottom, then enter your destination in the search bar by voice, handwriting, or pinyin. You can also tap a category icon to see nearby places and select one.
3. Once you've confirmed the place, choose walking or cycling navigation. After you select a route, a route preview will appear.
4. Tap to start. During navigation, swipe up on the screen to display text navigation. Tap the screen and tap the sound icon to adjust the navigation volume.
5. Tap the screen and tap the exit icon to end navigation.
> 💡 **Tip:** On the search screen, touch and hold a place name to delete that single search record. Pull down to the bottom and tap to clear all search records.

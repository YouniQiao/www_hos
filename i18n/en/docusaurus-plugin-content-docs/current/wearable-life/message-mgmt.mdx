# Message management

> This topic applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

When the wearable is properly connected, if you enable message notifications, messages from your phone's status bar can be synced to the device for easy viewing.
## Turn on message notifications
1. On the device details page, tap , and turn on the  switch.
2. In the list below, turn on the switches for the apps whose messages you want to receive.
## Set the message reminder method
If you want the reminder device to be selected intelligently based on your phone usage, go to the device details page, tap , and set the message reminder method.
- HUAWEI phone (HarmonyOS 4.3 and earlier): Turn on . When you are using your phone, the watch will not vibrate or ring, and messages will be received silently. When you are not using your phone and are wearing the watch, message reminders will be received on the watch, and your phone will not vibrate or ring.
- HUAWEI phone (HarmonyOS 5 and later): Select  or . : Both the phone and watch will remind you. : When you are using your phone with the screen on, the watch will not remind you; when you are wearing the watch, the phone will not remind you.
- Other Android phones: Turn on the  switch (this switch is off by default). When you are using your phone, the watch will not vibrate or ring, and messages will be received silently.
> 💡 **Tip:** When paired with an iOS phone,  and  are not supported.
## View/delete unread messages
1. When you are wearing the watch and a new message appears in your phone's status bar, the status bar message will be pushed to the watch with a vibration reminder.
2. If you don't check the message right away, swipe up on the watch's home screen to enter the message center and view unread messages.
3. To delete a message: Swipe left on the message you want to delete, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002389118309.png" width="24" />
 to delete it.
4. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002548191753.png" width="24" />
 at the bottom of the message list to clear all messages.
## Reply to text messages
1. When the wearable receives a text message notification, you can swipe up on the screen, select a quick reply, and tap it to reply.
2. To customize the quick reply content for messages, go to the device details page and tap the  card.
3. Tap  to add a new reply; tap an existing reply to edit it; tap the icon to the right of a reply to delete it.
> 💡 **Tip:** When paired with an iOS phone, this feature is not supported. For quick replies to text messages, you can only reply using the SIM card that received the text message.

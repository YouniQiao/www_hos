# Pair with a New Phone

> This article applies to **WATCH 5**.

Everyday Life


|  | Pair with a New Phone |
| --- | --- |


   Pair with a New Phone

    The watch cannot connect to multiple phones at the same time. A watch can only be connected to one phone. If you need to connect to another phone, enter the Connect to New Phone mode first.

1. In the watch's app list, go to **Settings > System** > **Connect to New Phone**.
2. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002201481022.png" width="24" />. If the watch is connected to an iOS phone, go to **Settings > Bluetooth** on the currently connected iOS phone and find the watch. Tap the **i** icon, tap Forget This Device, and then proceed to the next step.
3. The watch enters the pairing screen. Follow the onscreen instructions to complete pairing.


> 💡 **Tips:** When the watch is in Sleep mode, the **Connect to New Phone** option is unavailable. When you pair the watch with a phone that uses a different HUAWEI ID, the watch will be restored to its factory settings. After the factory reset, all cards in the account and HUAWEI Wallet, as well as system and app data, will be removed from the watch. Exercise caution. If you need to connect to a new phone, it is recommended that you go to **Settings > ** on the watch. When resetting the watch, do not select **Delete eSIM configuration information** below the prompt. After pairing with the new phone, you can use the eSIM directly, without having to contact your carrier to cancel and reactivate it.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002242403793.png" width="560" /> If you tap **Connect to New Phone** and a message appears during the onscreen steps indicating that the watch will be restored to its factory settings, and the watch already has an eSIM activated, do not tap **Restore factory settings** directly. If you do, you will not be able to choose to keep the eSIM information.

# Watch Music

> This document applies to **WATCH 5**.

Convenient Life


|  | Watch Music |
| --- | --- |


   Watch Music


> 💡 **Tip:** Some music requires a Super Music VIP subscription in the HUAWEI Music app on your phone before you can listen to it.


## Add music to your watch


1. On the device details page of the **HUAWEI Health** app, tap **Music > Music management** > **Add**, and select the music you want to add.
2. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002236677109.png" width="24" /> in the upper right corner of the screen to finish adding. > 💡 **Tip:** You must update your watch and the HUAWEI Health app to the latest versions before you can use this feature. Adding music to your watch is not supported on iOS phones. Using the HUAWEI Health app, you can add unencrypted music files already downloaded on your phone to your watch, where they can be played independently without an internet connection.


## Download watch music

     **Manual download:**
     On the watch's music playback screen, tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001782276361.png" width="24" /> > Download** in the lower right corner.
     **Automatic download:**

      When your watch is connected to WLAN and charging, it automatically downloads your favorites (you need to turn on **Music > Settings** > **Favorites** on your watch) and the songs in your playlists.

- The device automatically downloads recently added songs.
- When the device screen is off, Power Saving Elf in the background automatically disconnects WLAN, and songs cannot be downloaded in this case.


## Control phone music from your watch


1. Go to the device details page in the **HUAWEI Health** app, tap the **Music** card, and turn on the **Watch controls phone music** switch. > 💡 **Tip:** When your watch is paired with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, you don't need to turn on the **Watch controls phone music** switch in **HUAWEI Health**. When music plays on your phone, a music control card automatically appears on your watch, and this card cannot be closed.
2. When you play music using a music app on your phone (such as HUAWEI Music), you can control phone music playback from your watch.
3. On the watch face screen, swipe right to go to the minus-one screen. On the music card, you can tap pause, previous, or next to control phone music.
4. Tap the music card to enter the audio playback control screen, where you can also adjust the music volume or tap the device name at the bottom to switch to another controllable device.

# Set a fun sticker watch face

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="ultimate" label="WATCH Ultimate">


You can select or upload multiple stickers and combine them with a variety of personalized styles and functional controls to create your own personalized watch face. You can also tap the screen to interact with the stickers for a fun surprise.

1. Go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **My watch faces**, and select **Fun stickers**.
2. Go to pages such as **Geometric**, **Mood**, and **Cute pets**, and select your favorite stickers. You can also tap **Custom > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002219835673.png" width="24" />** to upload sticker images.
3. Select your preferred **Layout**, **Background color**, **Time font**, and more. You can also select **Functions** to change the data displayed on the watch face.
4. Tap **Set as current watch face**. The device watch face will automatically switch to the one you set. Tap the screen to interact with the stickers.

</TabItem>

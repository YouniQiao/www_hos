# Celia

> This document applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

When your watch is connected to the network, press and hold the bottom button to turn on Celia, and you can also set up the "Celia Celia" voice wakeup. Celia can help you set alarms, start outdoor runs, and more. It can also answer questions and provide analysis based on your personal workout and health data.


- Before use, update your watch to the latest version.
- Before use, make sure your watch is signed in to the same HUAWEI ID as your phone. On the watch's app list, go to **Settings > Sign in to HUAWEI ID** > **Sync account**.
- Before use, make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your watch and the watch is properly connected to your phone, or the watch is connected to a WLAN, or the eSIM network is enabled.
- When paired with an iOS phone, the watch cannot use the phone's network. Connect the watch to a WLAN or enable the eSIM network on the watch.

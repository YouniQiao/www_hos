# Control Bluetooth Earbuds

> This document applies to **WATCH 5**.

Everyday life


|  | Control Bluetooth Earbuds |
| --- | --- |


   Control Bluetooth Earbuds

    Once your watch is properly connected to Bluetooth earbuds, you can use the earbuds to answer calls, listen to music, and more.


## Pair your watch with Bluetooth earbuds


1. Put the Bluetooth earbuds into pairing mode.
2. On your watch, go to the app list and tap **Settings > Bluetooth**. Your watch automatically scans for nearby Bluetooth devices.
3. In the scan results, select the name of the Bluetooth earbuds you want to pair, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete pairing.


## Control Bluetooth earbuds from your watch


1. After your watch and earbuds are successfully connected, swipe right on the watch face to the minus-one screen.
2. On the earbuds card, you can quickly check the earbud battery level, adjust the volume, switch noise cancellation modes, and more. > 💡 **Tip:** The features displayed on the earbuds card depend on your earbuds' capabilities. The actual display prevails.


## Use Bluetooth earbuds to answer calls on your watch

     Before use, put on the connected Bluetooth earbuds.
     With eSIM activated on your watch and the watch paired and connected to the earbuds, when you answer or make a call on your watch, the sound comes from the Bluetooth earbuds.


> 💡 **Tip:**
>
> - When you answer or make a call on your phone, the sound comes from the Bluetooth earbuds.
>
> - When you answer or make a call on your watch, the sound comes from your watch.

# View content on external storage devices

After connecting an external storage device to your HUAWEI Vision, you can use the Files app to browse the file list and open files.

1.  Power on your HUAWEI Vision first, then plug in the external storage device.
2.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, open Files<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002421111288.png" width="24" height="24"/>.

3.  Select the USB drive name on the left side of the window. The folders and files on the USB drive are then displayed in the area on the right.
4.  Tap a file or folder icon to open the file directly or enter the next folder level.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Move the Smart Remote cursor over a file, then press and hold the confirm button or press the menu button on the remote to open a menu, where you can choose how to open the file, open its containing folder, rename it, view details, and more.
+   Move the Smart Remote cursor over a file, then press and hold the confirm button or press the menu button on the remote to display multi-select options, which let you delete or share files in batches.

# Distributed Home Theater

After you set up a distributed home theater with your HUAWEI Vision and speaker, both play audio at the same time when you play videos or music, for an immersive audiovisual experience.

1.  Connect your HUAWEI Vision and smart speaker to the same WLAN, and turn on Bluetooth on both.
2.  HUAWEI Vision scans for nearby speakers and displays a prompt to create a wireless home theater system. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the networking.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332288925.png)

    If the create wireless home theater system dialog box appears but you don't configure it at that time, you can configure it later as follows:

    1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Sound > Output, and select WLAN speaker.
    2.  Turn on Auto-discover speakers. A dialog box appears, prompting you to enter the networking mode options.
    3.  Select a mode in the dialog box and touch OK.

3.  Follow the on-screen instructions and configure the speakers based on their actual placement and number to complete the home theater connection.

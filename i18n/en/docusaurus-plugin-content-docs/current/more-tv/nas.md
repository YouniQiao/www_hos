# How to Access NAS Devices on HUAWEI Vision

HUAWEI Vision supports connecting to NAS (Network Attached Storage) devices, and you can use the Files app to view photos and videos on the NAS device.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Make sure the HUAWEI Vision and the NAS device are on the same LAN.

## Discovering NAS Devices

On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, open Files<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002475277852.png" width="24" height="24"/>, then tap Network Neighborhood to automatically discover NAS devices.

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002477079724.png)

## Connecting to a NAS Device

Tap the NAS device you discovered, then enter the username and password for the shared device in the dialog box that appears.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The username and password must be set on the NAS device. If the NAS device supports anonymous login, you can simply select Anonymous login and skip entering a username and password.
+   If you have questions about setting the username and password, contact the NAS device manufacturer.

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002508999699.png)


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>After the NAS device connects to the HUAWEI Vision for the first time, if the NAS restarts, you will need to reconnect it to the HUAWEI Vision.

+   A gray NAS device status indicates that it is not connected to the HUAWEI Vision.
+   A green NAS device status indicates that it is not connected to the HUAWEI Vision.

## Accessing a NAS Device

Once you enter the username and password, you will be logged in and can access the resources on the NAS device.

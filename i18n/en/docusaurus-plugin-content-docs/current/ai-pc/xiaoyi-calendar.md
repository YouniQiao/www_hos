# Drag into Celia to quickly create a schedule

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_AI_CreateSchedule.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>


You can select text that contains a time and to-do items in an app, drag it into Celia, and let Celia quickly create a schedule for you.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before using this feature, sign in to your HUAWEI ID first.

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia conversation panel.
2.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to enter App Center, then click to open an app (such as Notepad or Email).
3.  In the app, select the text you want to send, press and hold on the touchpad with one finger (or hold the left mouse button) and move to drag it to the Celia conversation panel area, then release your finger.
4.  Click the Tips notification: Extract schedule.

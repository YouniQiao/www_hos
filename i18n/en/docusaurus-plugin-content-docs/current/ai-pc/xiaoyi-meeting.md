# Celia Notes: Quickly Capture Meeting Highlights

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_XiaoyiHuiji.png" width="720" height=""/>

During online meetings, turn on Celia Notes to identify speakers by voice and convert speech to text in real time. After the meeting ends, a summary is generated from what was said, saving you time and helping you quickly grasp the key points.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click the middle area of the status bar in the lower-right corner of the home screen (which contains the battery icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png)), or press the HarmonyOS key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C on the keyboard to open Control Panel.
2.  Click Celia Notes, then follow the onscreen instructions to enter a name for the notes session and select an audio source. You can also click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_down.png) to expand the section and set the time, location, participants, and other information as needed.
3.  Click Start to enter the Celia Notes details screen, where recording begins automatically and speech is converted to text in real time. You can also perform the following operations as needed:
    +   **Pause recording:** During a break in the meeting, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_recorder_stop.png) to pause recording.
    +   **Resume recording:** When the meeting continues, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_mic.png) to resume recording.
    +   **Add an image:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_Celia_notetaker_screenshot.png) > Screenshot to capture the PC screen and add it to Celia Notes.
    +   **Perform more operations on transcribed text:** Select a section of transcribed text, then right-click to choose Modify, Copy, or Note, so you can revise the transcribed text, copy it and paste it where needed, or add a note to the transcription.
4.  When the meeting ends, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_window_close.png), then choose to save and exit or exit directly as needed. You can also click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png) to save the notes content to Notepad and end the current notes recording.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If you choose to save and exit, or click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png), the notes transcription and summary will be saved in Notepad under Notes > Celia Notes.

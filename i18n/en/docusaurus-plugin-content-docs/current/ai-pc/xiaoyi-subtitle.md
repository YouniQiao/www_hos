# Celia Subtitles: Turn Sound into Subtitles in Real Time

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_subtitles.png" width="720" height=""/>

When you listen to foreign-language music or watch foreign-language videos on your PC, you can turn on Celia Subtitles to convert the audio into your target language in real time and display it as subtitles on the PC home screen, so you can enjoy foreign-language audio and video with no language barriers.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before using this feature, sign in to your HUAWEI ID first.

1.  When you listen to foreign-language music or watch foreign-language videos on your PC, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia conversation panel.
2.  Give Celia a voice or text command, such as "Turn on Celia Subtitles".
3.  In the Celia Subtitles floating window, select the source language and target language from the drop-down lists. You can also click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_settings.png) to configure more settings as needed, such as alignment, font size, and background transparency.
4.  Once set up, subtitles will appear according to your settings. When you move the cursor to the top of the subtitles floating window and it changes to ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_cursor_move.png), press and hold the touchpad with one finger (or hold the left mouse button) and move it to reposition the floating window.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Celia Subtitles supports only some languages. The actual supported languages shall prevail.

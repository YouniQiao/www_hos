# Deep Research: for in-depth search and analysis

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_DeepSeek.png" alt="Deep Research" width="720" height="540" />

Use **Deep Research** to let **Celia** help you clarify your needs through conversation, then combine professional online knowledge with uploaded local files for in-depth analysis and research, and deliver the content you need.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![Celia](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia side window.
2. Click ![Maximize](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_window_xiaoyi_maxmize.png) to enter the Celia app window, then click **Deep Research** in the left sidebar.
3. In the dialog box, enter the topic, purpose, method, and other information for the research you want to conduct, then press Enter to send. For example: "Help me write a marketing strategy report on classic beauty product brands."
4. Follow Celia's prompts to refine your requirements and send them to **Celia**.
5. Once Deep Research is complete, you can view the report details in the right pane, or click **Illustrated** to view a visualized research report or **PPT** to view an intelligently generated PPT document.
6. As needed, click ![Download](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_download.png) in the upper right corner, select the document type you want, and save the AI-generated Deep Research report to your device.

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

**Deep Research** is suitable for scenarios such as copywriting, content analysis, and in-depth search Q&A. It may take a long time, so please refer to the actual situation.

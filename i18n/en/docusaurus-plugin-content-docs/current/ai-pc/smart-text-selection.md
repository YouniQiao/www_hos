# Smart Text Selection, Instant Answers

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_TextToolbar.png" alt="Smart Text Selection" width="720" height="540" />

Say goodbye to tedious steps. With text selection, you can quickly run AI search, AI expand, AI translate, and more, helping you work more efficiently.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![Settings](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to open **Settings**, then go to **System > Smart Text Selection** and make sure the **Smart Text Selection** switch is turned on.
2. Open an app (such as Notepad), select a passage of text, and press **Ctrl** to bring up the text selection menu. Click a menu item to perform the corresponding action, such as search, translate, or copy.

# Celia Circle to Search for quick extraction

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_CeliaCircle.png" alt="Celia Circle to Search" width="720" height="540" />

When you come across something interesting on screen, you can circle it to let Celia identify the image and search encyclopedias or find similar items.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. While viewing an image, click ![Celia icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia side window, then click the Circle to Search icon ![Circle to Search icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_celia_AI_screenshot.png). You can also press the **HarmonyOS key** ![HarmonyOS key](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + **Alt** shortcut to quickly enter Circle to Search mode.
2. Using a mouse or one-finger touch, draw a closed shape around the content you want to select on the screen. When the circled area contains a fairly complete object, **Celia** automatically recognizes the screenshot content and recommends the corresponding services, such as attraction introductions, finding similar items, or text extraction.
3. As needed, click the corresponding service, or click **Ask Celia**.


![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

**Ask Celia** shows different options depending on the recognized content. Follow the actual on-screen prompts.

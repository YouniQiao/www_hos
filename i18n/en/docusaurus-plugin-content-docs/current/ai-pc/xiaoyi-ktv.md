# AI Smart Vocal Accompaniment, Karaoke Anytime

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_SoundVocal.png" width="720" height=""/>

Use AI sound effects to separate vocals from background accompaniment in music in real time, intelligently highlighting either the accompaniment or the vocals, so you can enjoy a karaoke experience anytime.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  While music is playing, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Sound in the left sidebar.
2.  Choose whether to turn on AI Smart Vocal Accompaniment or AI Vocal Highlight based on your scenario.
    +   AI Smart Vocal Accompaniment: Intelligently lowers the vocals during music playback while keeping the background accompaniment.
    +   AI Vocal Highlight: Intelligently lowers the background accompaniment during music playback to highlight the vocals.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

You can only choose one of AI Smart Vocal Accompaniment and AI Vocal Highlight. It is recommended that you select the appropriate audio effect based on your actual needs.

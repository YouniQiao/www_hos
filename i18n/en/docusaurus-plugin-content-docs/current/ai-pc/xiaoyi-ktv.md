# AI-powered Singing

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_AI_SoundVocal.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Remove vocals or background music from songs to transform any moment into your personal karaoke stage effortlessly.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  When playing music, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Sounds in the left pane.

2.  Enable Vocal remover or Vocal isolator as needed.

    +   Vocal remover: Remove vocals from songs, keeping only background music.

    +   Vocal isolator: Remove background music from songs, keeping only vocals.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

You can select either **Vocal remover** or **Vocal isolator** as needed.

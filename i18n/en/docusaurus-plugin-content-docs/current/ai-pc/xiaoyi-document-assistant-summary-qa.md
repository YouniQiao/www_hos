# Document Assistant: quick summaries and Q&A

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_DocumentAssistant.png" alt="Document Assistant" width="720" height="540" />

Use **Document Assistant** to quickly understand all kinds of documents through summaries and Q&A. It can also extract key points and generate mind maps to help you read more efficiently.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![Celia](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower-right corner of the home screen to open the Celia side window.
2. Click ![Maximize](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_window_xiaoyi_maxmize.png) to enter the Celia app window, then click **Document Assistant** in the left sidebar.
3. Click **Select document** or drag a document into the parsing area, and **Celia** will automatically parse the document content and generate a summary. You can also click **Enter URL** and enter the web address to be parsed (only some URLs are supported).
4. Enter your question and press Enter to send it, so that **Celia** can answer based on the document content. Alternatively, click the menu on the right side of the screen to perform more operations, such as mind map, translation, and close reading.

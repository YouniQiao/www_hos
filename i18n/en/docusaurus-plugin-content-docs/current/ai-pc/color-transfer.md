# Apply Colors with One Tap

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_gallery_AIretouch_color.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Transform your photos instantly by applying your desired colors. Explore a spectrum of hues that bring depth and mood to your images.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Gallery to open it.

2.  Double-click the image to be edited to enter the details screen, and then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_AIsummary.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enter the AI retouch screen.

3.  Click Color to expand the options. You can:

    +   Apply recommended colors: Click Suggested to apply recommended colors. Click again to change the recommended colors.

    +   Apply the color of a reference image: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png), select a reference image, and click Extract. You can add multiple reference images for future use. Hold down on an added reference image to delete or rename the colors.

4.  After applying the colors, drag the slider left or right to adjust the color intensity.

5.  Once you're done editing, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png) and select Save as new or Replace original to save it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Please make sure your device has been updated to the latest system version.

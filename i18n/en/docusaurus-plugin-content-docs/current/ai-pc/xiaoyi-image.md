# Drag into Celia to extract image information

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_TableExtraction.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can drag an image containing a table or text into Celia and let Celia quickly generate a table file or extract the text for you.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before using this feature, sign in to your HUAWEI ID first.

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia chat panel.
2.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to enter App Center, then click to open an app (such as Files or Gallery).
3.  In the app, select an image containing table content or text, press and hold with one finger on the touchpad (or the left mouse button) and move to drag it to the Celia chat panel area, then release your finger.
4.  Click the Tips prompt: Table extraction or Text extraction.

# Celia Knowledge Base, your personal knowledge steward

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_KnowledgeBase.png" alt="Celia Knowledge Base" width="720" height="540" />

**Celia Knowledge Base** helps you efficiently manage local data on your PC, massive amounts of online information and knowledge, and more, to build a personalized knowledge base. The knowledge base offers services such as knowledge saving, intelligently categorized knowledge collections, and AI Q&A, so the knowledge you accumulate over time can deliver greater value.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![Celia icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia side window.
2. Click ![Maximize](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_window_xiaoyi_maxmize.png) to enter the Celia app window, then click **Celia Knowledge Base** in the left sidebar.
3. In the knowledge base screen on the right, you can save and categorize knowledge in any of the following ways:
    * Click **Add knowledge** in the upper right corner and select the content you want to add, such as files, links, or images. Once parsed, the added content is automatically categorized into an existing collection.
    * Click an existing collection to open its details screen, then click **Add knowledge**.
    * Click ![Add](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png) in the **Create collection** area to create a collection, enter a **Name** and **Description**, turn on the **Smart association** switch, then click **Confirm**. On the new collection's screen, click **Add knowledge**.
4. After the knowledge is added, enter a question as needed, then press Enter to send it, and let **Celia** answer based on the knowledge base content.

# Celia Assistant, answers to your curious questions

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_CeliaQA.png" width="720" height=""/>

Celia has a vast knowledge base covering general reference, news, sports events, device features, and more — ask anything and you'll get an answer.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Sign in to your HUAWEI ID before using this feature.

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn-dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open the Celia conversation panel.
2.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn-dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) below the conversation panel to send a voice message, or type your question and press Enter to send it to Celia. For example: "Beijing Universal Studios guide," "Open Music."

# Smart Recommendations, Related Documents One Tap Away

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_fileManager_SmartRecommend.png" alt="Smart recommendations" width="720" height="540" />

The system intelligently analyzes local files in **File Manager** and recommends related documents, helping you find what you need quickly.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![File Manager](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_filemanager.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to open **File Manager**.
2. Click to select the document you want to view, then click **Recommend** in the upper right corner. The system will recommend related documents based on **similar content** or **similar names**.
3. In the recommended documents list on the right, double-click any document to open and view it, or right-click a document to perform more operations (such as opening its folder, choosing how to open it, or adding it to a smart category).

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

Smart recommendations are supported only for certain file formats stored locally on the PC (files in paths such as cloud drives, network shares, and external devices are not supported). Actual results may vary.

# Move, for a Perfect Group Photo

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_gallery_AIretouch_move.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

With Move, quickly cut out a subject from another photo and intelligently paste the subject right into place. It automatically blends the lighting and shadows, creating a perfect group photo in just one tap.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Gallery to open it.

2.  Double-click the image to be edited to enter the details screen, and then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_edit_pencil.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notes_AIsummary.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enter the AI retouch screen.

3.  Click Move to expand the options, click Stickers, and select the target photo.

4.  In Auto mode, draw a line inside the desired object, and click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png). The object will then be automatically pasted to the recommended position. From there, feel free to:

    +   Move the object: Move the cursor to the object area, hold down on the touchpad (or the left mouse button) with one finger, and drag the object to the desired position.

    +   Adjust the object size: Move the cursor to the object's four corners, hold down on the touchpad (or the left mouse button) with one finger, and drag to adjust the object size.

5.  After the adjustment is complete, click Generate and wait for the image to be generated.

6.  After a new image is generated, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png) and choose Save as or Replace original to save it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Please make sure your device has been updated to the latest system version.

# The All-New Celia, Wake It Up However You Like

Wake Celia in a variety of ways to quickly get answers to your questions, create calendar events, change system settings, and more.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Methods

+   **Wake from the home screen icon:** Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to wake Celia.
+   **Wake with a shortcut key:** Press the Celia key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_celia_filled.png) (on the keyboard) to wake Celia. Press and hold the Celia key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_celia_filled.png) to wake Celia and enter voice input mode.
+   **Wake by asking a question:** In Files, select the file you want to ask about, right-click, and choose Ask Celia.
+   **Wake by voice:** Say the wake word ("Celia Celia") to wake Celia. Before using this feature, go to Settings > Celia > Wake, click to turn on the Voice wakeup switch, and follow the onscreen instructions to record the wake word: "Celia Celia".

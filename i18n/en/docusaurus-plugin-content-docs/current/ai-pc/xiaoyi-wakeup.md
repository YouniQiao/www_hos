# Celia at Your Beck and Call

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_AI_celia_wakeup.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Wake up Celia and initiate a dialog, create an event, or change system settings with great ease.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

+   Via the home screen icon: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_xiaoyi.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to wake up Celia.

+   Via the shortcut key: Press Smart Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_celia_filled.png) on the keyboard to wake up Celia. Press and hold Smart Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_celia_filled.png) to wake up Celia and enter the voice input mode.

+   Via Ask Celia: In Files, right-click the target file, and select Ask Celia.

+   Via wakeup phrase: Say the wakeup phrase ("Hey Celia") to wake up Celia. Before using this feature, go to **Settings** > **Celia** > **Wakeup & dialogue**, enable Voice wakeup, and follow the onscreen instructions to record the wakeup phrase "Hey, Celia".

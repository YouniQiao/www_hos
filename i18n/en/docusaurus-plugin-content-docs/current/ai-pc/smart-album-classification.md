# Smart Album Classification

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_gallery_SmartAlbum.png" alt="Smart Album Classification" width="720" height="540" />

When your PC's Gallery contains a certain number of images, the PC automatically analyzes them while charging with the screen off and intelligently sorts them into categories such as landscapes, food, and buildings, helping you organize and view your images quickly.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. On the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen, click ![Gallery](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) to open **Gallery**. You can also click ![App Center](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to go to **App Center**, then click **Gallery** to open it.
2. In **Smart Album** on the left sidebar, click **Categorized albums**, then on the right, click a category album to view the corresponding images.

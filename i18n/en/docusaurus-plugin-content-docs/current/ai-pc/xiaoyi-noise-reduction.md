# AI Intelligent Noise Reduction for Clearer Communication

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_AI_SoundEffects.png" width="720" height=""/>

When you're in a noisy environment attending online meetings or taking online classes, you can enable HUAWEI SOUND AI sound effects to reduce noise interference (such as nearby voices, keyboard typing, and more) and enhance voices, giving you a pure audio experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to go to Settings, then click Sound in the left sidebar.
2.  Click to switch to the Input tab, then choose to enable either Solo Quiet Space or Multi-person Pure Voice based on your usage scenario.
    +   **Solo Quiet Space:** When making calls with earbuds, this eliminates surrounding sound interference and identifies and highlights the voice of the speaker wearing the earbuds.
    +   **Multi-person Pure Voice:** During multi-person meetings, this eliminates ambient sound interference and enhances all voices.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Solo Quiet Space and Multi-person Pure Voice are mutually exclusive. It is recommended that you choose the appropriate audio effect based on your actual situation.

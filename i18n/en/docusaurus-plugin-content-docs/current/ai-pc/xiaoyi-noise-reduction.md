# Clear Communication with AI Noise Cancellation

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_AI_SoundEffects.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**HUAWEI SOUND AI Audio Effects** filters out ambient noise like background chatter and keyboard typing, to keep conferences and online courses crystal clear.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Sounds in the left pane.

2.  Click to switch to the Input tab page and enable either Voice isolation or Multi-voice isolation.

    +   **Voice isolation**: Reduces ambient noise and enhances the voice of the speaker during a call using earbuds.

    +   **Multi-voice isolation**: Eliminates ambient noise and enhances the voices of all participants during a multi-party conference call.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

You can choose to select either **Voice isolation** or **Multi-voice isolation** depending on which is more applicable to your call.

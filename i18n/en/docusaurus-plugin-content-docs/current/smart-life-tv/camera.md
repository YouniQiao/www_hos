# Camera and Gallery

# Taking photos

On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, select Camera.
On the Camera screen, you can do the following:
Take a photo or record a video: Tap the expand icon to switch modes, then tap the photo icon or the video icon to take a photo or record a video.
Preview: Tap Preview to quickly browse the photos or videos you have taken.

## Camera settings

When using the camera, adjust the camera settings to suit your habits for faster shooting.

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, select Camera<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002454788733.png" width="24" height="24"/>.
2.  Press the Menu button on the remote control to open the settings screen.
    +   Video resolution: The higher the resolution, the clearer the video, and the larger the resulting video file. Choose based on your actual needs.
    +   Photo mirroring: When Photo mirroring is enabled, the photo taken is the reverse of the real scene and matches the camera preview. When Selfie mirroring is disabled, the photo taken matches the real scene and is the reverse of the camera preview.
    +   Shutter sound off: When enabled, the shutter sound is turned off when taking photos and recording videos.
    +   Auto watermark: When enabled, watermarks are automatically added to photos and videos.

# Smart Recognition

By enrolling face and voice information, you can let HUAWEI Vision provide personalized services for you in interactive communication, voice commands, and more, improving your quality of life and strengthening emotional bonds among family members.

## Enrolling Face/Voice Information

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, tap Settings > Celia, and turn on Smart Recognition.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002508732065.png)

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.

2.  Follow the on-screen instructions to enroll family member information. When adding a family member, you can enroll face and voice information as needed for your situation, making it easier to use various features later.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

* When you open Smart Recognition for the first time, you need to sign in to your HUAWEI ID at the same time.
* When enrolling voice information, use the remote control and follow the on-screen instructions. Press and hold the voice button on the remote control to start enrolling. When you finish, release the voice button to submit the enrolled content. You need to enroll three sentences in total. Repeat the above steps three times.
* Enrolled face information can be used for boot greetings, smart history, birthday reminders, or congratulations.
* Enrolled voice information can be used for smart responses, viewing time management, and more.

## Personalized Interaction

After family member information is enrolled, HUAWEI Vision will proactively interact with you in the following scenarios.

+   Proactive greeting at startup: You can set it to once a day, every time the screen turns on, or off.
+   Smart response: When you press and hold the voice button on the remote control <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002468049249.png" width="24" height="24"/> and say "Celia Celia" or issue a management command, it recognizes your identity and responds with a form of address.
+   Supports advance reminders for birthdays/wedding anniversaries and congratulations on the day itself.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>If you need to turn off the startup greeting feature, go to Settings > Celia > Smart Recognition > Smart Recognition settings > Startup greeting and tap Off.

## Viewing History

HUAWEI Vision uses your viewing records. Tap the Viewing history card on the home screen to open the history details page.

The details page displays all history records by default and triggers face recognition at the same time:

* If member A is recognized, member A's history records and all history records are displayed.
* If multiple members A and B are recognized, the history records of members A and B and all history records are displayed at the same time.
* If no member is recognized, only all history records are displayed.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

* Smart history records rely on face recognition and can be affected by various external factors such as angle, lighting, distance, and obstructions (masks/sunglasses/long bangs), so there may be some degree of deviation. For a better experience, perform operations in a well-lit environment without obstructions.
* If there is no Viewing history card on the large screen home screen, point the remote control at a blank area of the large screen home screen, press and hold the confirm button, then tap Large screen cards, and add the Viewing history card in Smart Recognition.
* Smart history records rely on face recognition and may intermittently use the camera. You may hear a faint "click" sound. The specific trigger times are as follows:
    * Each time the screen turns on, face recognition is triggered.
    * Every 1 hour during video playback, face recognition is triggered.
    * Each time the history details page is opened, face recognition is triggered.
* If you need to turn off the smart history feature, go to Settings > Celia > Smart Recognition > Smart Recognition settings > Smart history and tap Off.

## Viewing Time Management

You can quickly manage viewing time by voice and arrange your daily schedule reasonably.

+   Command to check remaining time: You can use Smart Voice to say, "How much longer can I watch?"
+   Command to turn off management: You can use Smart Voice to say, "Remove time management."
+   The command to remove time management triggers the voice password verification process. Steps to enable the voice password are as follows:
    1.  Go to Settings > Celia > Smart Recognition to open the member information page.

        ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002476769682.png)

        <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.

    2.  Turn on the Support voice password switch.
    3.  Edit the member's birthday information to ensure the member is older than 18.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

* After a management command is issued, a time countdown is displayed on HUAWEI Vision. When the time runs out, a lock screen is displayed. You can use Smart Voice to unlock and remove time management.
* Time management commands rely on voice recognition, and recognition accuracy can be affected by factors such as speaking rate, volume, ambient noise, and distance.
* Recognition accuracy is higher when commands are issued using the voice button on the remote control. If you are not using the remote control, stay within 5 meters, speak at a slower rate, and use a higher volume when issuing commands to improve recognition accuracy.

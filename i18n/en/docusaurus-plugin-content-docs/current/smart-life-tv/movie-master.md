# HUAWEI Vision Movie Master Feature Introduction

You can search for movies in multiple ways by calling out "Celia, Celia" to your HUAWEI Vision, and chat while watching, so you're never watching alone and can enjoy a more immersive and richer viewing experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Currently only HUAWEI Video is supported.

## Movie Search

+   **Find a movie from a few details**

    Try saying: "Celia, Celia," then once your HUAWEI Vision responds, describe the clues about the show. For example: "Celia, Celia, find a movie about a kid who gets left home alone on a Christmas trip."


+   **Find a movie from a plot snippet**

    Try saying: "Celia, Celia," then once your HUAWEI Vision responds, describe the plot snippet. For example: "Celia, Celia, which episode of Kangxi Dynasty is it where Kangxi captures Oboi?"


+   **Find a movie from a line of dialogue**

    Try saying: "Celia, Celia," then once your HUAWEI Vision responds, say the line from the show. For example: "Celia, Celia, which movie is 'Let the bullets fly for a while' from?"


## Chat While Watching

+   **Plot introduction**

    Try saying: "Celia, Celia," then once your HUAWEI Vision responds, say which show's plot you'd like to hear about. For example: "Celia, Celia, what is this movie mainly about?"

+   **Character introduction**

    Try saying: "Celia, Celia," then once your HUAWEI Vision responds, say which actor in the show you want to identify. For example: "Celia, Celia, who plays the role of XXX, and what else have they been in?"

+   **Show extras**

    Try saying: "Celia, Celia," then once your HUAWEI Vision responds, say what you'd like to know about the show's related content. For example, after finishing a show, you can ask: "Celia, Celia, is there a sequel?" / "Celia, Celia, are there any similar shows?"

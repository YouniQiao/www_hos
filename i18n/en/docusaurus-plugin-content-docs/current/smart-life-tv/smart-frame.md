# Smart Frame

You can set artworks as a Smart Frame, turning your HUAWEI Vision into a scenic window, photo wall, or art gallery to create an elegant atmosphere at home.

## Photo wall

1.  Open Smart Frame on your HUAWEI Vision and tap Photo wall.
2.  Select your preferred photo wall style and tap to enter it. You can then customize the photo wall as you like, including adding photos and turning background music on or off.

    To add photos:

    +   **Gallery:** Tap Add photos, then tap Add or Add from local Gallery, select one or more images, and tap Done.
    +   **Huawei Share:** In Gallery on your Huawei phone, select the photos you want to upload, tap Share, and select the corresponding HUAWEI Vision. After the photos are transferred to your HUAWEI Vision, on the Select photo wall screen, select the corresponding photo wall and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002391852588.png" width="24" height="24"/> to add them to the photo wall.
    +   **Smart Life app**: Download and install the Smart Life app on your phone, add your HUAWEI Vision device, tap the HUAWEI Vision card, and go to Photo wall to upload photos.

    If there is a Huawei phone running HarmonyOS 5.0 or later and signed in to the same HUAWEI ID near your HUAWEI Vision, you can also add photos by taking pictures with the phone or from the phone's Gallery.

    +   Tap Add photos, then tap Add, tap Take photo, tap the shutter button on your phone to take a photo, and then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002392036254.png" width="24" height="24"/> to add the photo to the photo wall.
    +   Tap Add photos, then tap Add, tap Select images, select one or more images on your phone, and tap Done to add them to the photo wall.
3.  Once you're done, tap Apply in the upper right corner.

## Smart Frame settings

You can access Smart Frame settings in either of the following ways:

+   On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Home screen & personalization > Smart Frame settings.
+   Open Smart Frame on your HUAWEI Vision and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002418262250.png" width="24" height="24"/> to access Smart Frame settings.

    Table 1. Smart Frame settings
    | Setting| Sub-setting| Description|
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Enable Smart Frame| /| Once enabled, Smart Frame will automatically start by default after 2 minutes of inactivity on your HUAWEI Vision.|
    | Screen wakes when someone approaches| Display when someone approaches| Once enabled, your HUAWEI Vision will automatically display Smart Frame when someone enters the camera's field of view.|
    | Screen wakes when someone approaches| Do not disturb| Set a Do not disturb period, during which the Screen wakes when someone approaches feature will not be triggered.|
    | Screen turns off when people leave| /| Once enabled, your HUAWEI Vision will automatically turn off the screen when people leave the camera's field of view.|
    | Set newly shared images from the same account as Smart Frame| /| If new images are shared to your HUAWEI Vision from the same account, you can set them directly as Smart Frame.|

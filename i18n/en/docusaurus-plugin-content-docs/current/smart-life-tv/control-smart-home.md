# Control smart home devices

You can control smart home devices through the Smart Life app on your HUAWEI Vision.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The smart home devices supported are subject to actual conditions.

The specific steps are as follows:

1.  Add smart home devices in the Smart Life app on your phone. For details, see: [How do I add smart home devices through the HUAWEI Smart Life app?](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn00752585/)
2.  Sign in to the same HUAWEI ID on your HUAWEI Vision and phone. You can then view the smart home devices that have been added in the Smart Life app on your HUAWEI Vision.
3.  Tap the card of the smart home device you want to control to access its control screen, where you can control the smart home device.

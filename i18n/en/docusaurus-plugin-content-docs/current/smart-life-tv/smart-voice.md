# Using Smart Voice

You can use voice commands to search for audio and video content, control your HUAWEI Vision, make video calls with family and friends, check the weather, look up facts, and more.

## Speaking into the remote control

Press and hold the voice button on the remote control<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002462606129.png" width="24" height="24"/>. No wake word is needed — simply speak what you want directly into the remote control's microphone (from 5 cm to 30 cm away), then release the button. Your HUAWEI Vision will recognize your voice automatically.

For example: press and hold the voice button on the remote control<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002429127652.png" width="24" height="24"/> and try saying, "Turn up the volume."

## Speaking to your HUAWEI Vision

While your HUAWEI Vision is powered on, say "Celia Celia" within 5 meters of it to wake it up, then give your voice command.

For example: try saying "Celia Celia," and once your HUAWEI Vision responds, say "Turn up the volume." Your HUAWEI Vision will adjust the volume automatically.

## Enabling/disabling Smart Voice

Smart Voice is enabled by default on your HUAWEI Vision, but you can also enable or disable it as follows:

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Celia > Wake-up.
    +   Voice wake-up: Once this switch is turned on, your HUAWEI Vision will respond to Smart Voice wake-up.
    +   Collaborative wake-up: Choose the appropriate device to respond, so that multiple devices don't respond at the same time.
    +   Continuous conversation: Once enabled, you don't need to wake Celia again. After each command is completed, Celia will automatically start listening, and you just need to say your next command.
2.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Celia > Voice.

    You can choose Female/Child/Male/Young female, or customize the voice.

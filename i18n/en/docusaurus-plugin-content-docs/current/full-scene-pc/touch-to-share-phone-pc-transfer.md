# Tap to Share: Instant Phone-PC Transfer

<video controls src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/video/vid_pc_TouchShare.mp4" width="720" height="540"></video>

Tap the top edge of your phone against your PC screen to share pictures, videos, documents, and more with each other, and even share **WLAN**. Work more efficiently.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. On both your phone and PC, go to **Settings > Multi-device collaboration** and make sure the following settings are configured:
   - **Phone:** Tap **HUAWEI Share > More HUAWEI Share settings** and make sure **Enable HUAWEI Share service** is turned on (on by default). Tap **Advanced** and make sure **Multi-device collaboration enhanced service** is turned on (on by default).
   - **PC:** Click **Tap to Share** and make sure **Tap to Share** is turned on (on by default). Click **Advanced** and make sure **Multi-device collaboration enhanced service** is turned on (on by default).
2. On both your phone and PC, sign in with the same **HUAWEI ID**.
3. Choose the sharing method that fits your scenario:
   - **Share from phone to PC:** Open or select the content you want to share on your phone (for example, open a single picture/video in **Gallery** or select multiple ones), tap the top edge of your phone against your PC screen, and after the sharing animation appears, swipe up on your phone to share.
   - **Share from PC to phone:** Open or select the content you want to share on your PC (for example, select multiple documents in **Files**), tap the top edge of your phone against the app window on your PC screen, and after the sharing animation appears, tap **Receive** on your phone.

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

- This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 6 or later.
- A phone case that is too thick or irregularly shaped may affect the experience. Actual performance may vary.

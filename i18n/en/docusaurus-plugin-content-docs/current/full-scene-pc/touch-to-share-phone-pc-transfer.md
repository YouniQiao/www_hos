# Tap to Transfer, for Instant File Sharing Between Your Phone and Computer

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_pc_TouchShare.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Share images, videos, documents, and **WLAN** between your phone and HarmonyOS PC simply by tapping the top of your phone against the screen of your HarmonyOS PC.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  On your phone and computer, go to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** and make sure that the following settings have been completed:

    +   Phone: Go to **Huawei Share** > **More settings** and make sure that Huawei Share Service is enabled (enabled by default). Tap Advanced settings and make sure that Enhanced collaboration services is enabled (enabled by default).

    +   Computer: Click Teleport and make sure that Teleport is enabled (enabled by default). Click Advanced settings and make sure that Enhanced collaboration services is enabled (enabled by default).

2.  Log in to the same HUAWEI ID on your phone and computer.

3.  Select the corresponding sharing method based on your scenario:

    +   From phone to computer: Open or select the content to be shared on your phone (for example, open a single image or video, or select multiple images or videos to be shared in Gallery), and tap the top edge of your phone to the computer screen. When the sharing animation appears, swipe up on your phone to share the content.

    +   From computer to phone: Open or select the content to be shared on your computer (for example, select multiple documents to be shared in Files), and tap the top edge of your phone to the app window on the computer screen. When the sharing animation appears, tap Accept on your phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 6 or later.

+   Thick or irregularly shaped phone cases will affect your user experience.

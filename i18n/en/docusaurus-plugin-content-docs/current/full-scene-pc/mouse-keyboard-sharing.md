# Share Your Keyboard and Mouse with Another Device

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_KeyboardMouseSharing.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Use **Keyboard & mouse sharing** to navigate within your device with the computer's keyboard and mouse, and even drag and drop files between the two devices. For example, move the cursor past the edge of the computer screen to make it appear on the screen of your phone/tablet to operate an app using the computer's mouse or enter text on the editing screen using the computer's keyboard.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The following uses keyboard and mouse sharing between a phone and a computer as an example:

1.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2.  On your phone and computer, go to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** > **Keyboard & mouse sharing** and make sure that the following settings have been completed:

    +   Phone: Make sure that Keyboard & mouse sharing is enabled (enabled by default).

    +   Computer: Make sure that Keyboard & mouse sharing and Move pointer across screens to connect are enabled (enabled by default).

3.  Move your computer cursor to the left/right edge of the screen and keep moving it outwards or tap the screen's side edge twice until a transition animation appears. Continue moving the cursor outwards until the keyboard and mouse sharing prompt appears.

4.  You can then operate a phone app using the computer's mouse or enter text on the phone editing screen using the computer's keyboard.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

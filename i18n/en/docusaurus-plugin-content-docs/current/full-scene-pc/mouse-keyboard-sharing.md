# Cross-device keyboard and mouse sharing

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_KeyboardMouseSharing.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

With keyboard and mouse sharing, your PC mouse can move freely between devices, giving you cross-device keyboard and mouse sharing and file drag-and-drop. For example, after you move the cursor to the edge of your PC screen and it crosses over to your phone or tablet, you can use the PC mouse to operate apps on the phone or tablet, and use the PC keyboard to enter text in an editing screen on the phone or tablet.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take keyboard and mouse sharing between a phone and a PC as an example:

1.  On both the phone and the PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On the PC, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, click Multi-device collaboration > Keyboard and mouse sharing, and select the device to share with from the drop-down list to the right of Keyboard and mouse sharing.
3.  Move the PC cursor to the left or right edge of the screen and keep moving it until the cursor crosses over to the phone.
4.  Once the cursor has crossed over, you can use the PC mouse to operate the phone or use the PC keyboard to enter text in an editing screen on the phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

# Multi-screen collaboration: open multiple phone windows

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/video/vid_pc_connect_phone_MultiWindow.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

After your PC and phone are connected through collaboration, you can open multiple phone windows on your PC. One screen, many uses — get more done with greater efficiency.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. On both your phone and PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2. On your PC, click the middle area of the status bar in the lower-right corner of the home screen (the area with the battery icon), or press the HarmonyOS key + C to open Control Panel.

3. In Super Device, click it, and then click the phone you want to connect to in the device list to start a collaboration connection.

4. Once connected, open an app in the main phone window on your PC, and click the plus sign at the top to open a split sub-window for that app. You can continue to open other apps in the main phone window. Each window works independently without affecting the others.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

+ If the split button for an app is grayed out, the app does not support split windows, or split windows are not supported in certain scenarios (for example, apps with App Lock enabled). The actual behavior prevails.

+ There is a limit on the number of split sub-windows. When the limit is reached, the message "New window limit reached" is displayed.

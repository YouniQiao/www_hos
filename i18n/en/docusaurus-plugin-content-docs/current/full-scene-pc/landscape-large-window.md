# Landscape large window: display phone apps on a large screen

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_pc_connect_phone_LargeWindow.png" width="720" height=""/>

After your PC and phone are connected for collaboration and you open an app sub-window, you can display the phone app sub-window on your PC in a landscape large window, making it easy to view more content details and collaborate efficiently.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. On both your phone and PC, enable WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2. On your PC, click the middle area of the status bar in the lower right corner of the home screen (which contains the battery icon), or press the HarmonyOS key + C on the keyboard to open Control Panel.

3. In Super Device, click it, and then click the phone to be connected in the device list to initiate a collaboration connection.

4. After the collaboration connection is established, open an app in the phone main window on your PC, and click the plus sign at the top to open a split sub-window for that app.

5. Click More > Display in landscape in the sub-window title bar to display the phone app interface in a landscape large window. You can also click the maximize button in the sub-window title bar as needed to maximize the interface and display more content.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ This feature requires both devices to be upgraded to HarmonyOS 6.1 or later.

+ Some apps do not support landscape large window display. The actual behavior may vary.

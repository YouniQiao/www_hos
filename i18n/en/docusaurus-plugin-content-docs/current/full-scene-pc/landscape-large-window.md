# Display Phone Apps in Landscape Mode on a Large Screen

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_pc_connect_phone_LargeWindow.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Sync your phone with your computer to open app split windows in expanded landscape mode. View more content details at a glance and enjoy a more efficient collaborative experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2.  On the computer, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen, or press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C (on the keyboard) to open Control Panel.

3.  In Super Device, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_superdevice.png), and then click the phone you wish to connect to in the device list.

4.  After the connection is set up, open an app in the phone window on your computer, and click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png) at the top to open a split window for the app.

5.  Go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_more_1.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/screen_rotation.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the title bar of the split window to display the phone app screen in a large landscape window. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_window_maxmize.png) on the title bar of the split window to maximize the window and display more content.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   This feature requires that both devices run HarmonyOS 6.1 or later.

+   Large window display in landscape mode is only supported for certain apps. Actual experience shall prevail.

# Seamless Task Continuation Across Devices

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_pc_handover.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>


With Continuation, you can seamlessly pick up a task from your current device on a nearby device for an uninterrupted experience. For example, you can jot down interesting things you see in Notepad on your phone while out and about, then smoothly switch to your PC to keep editing when you get home.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Using Notepad continuation as an example:

1.  On both your phone and PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in with the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On your phone, open the content you want to continue, such as a note you're editing.
3.  On your PC, click the Notepad continuation icon![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_notepad_connection_phone.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to continue editing the note.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.
+   Only some apps support Continuation. The actual behavior may vary.

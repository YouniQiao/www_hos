# Transfer Tasks Between Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_pc_handover.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**Handover** allows you to transfer tasks on your current device to another nearby device, with absolutely zero hassle. For example, you could jot down some thoughts on your phone when you're on the go, and edit them further on your computer when you get home.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The following uses phone-computer collaboration as an example:

1.  Make sure that Handover is enabled (enabled by default) on your phone and computer by going to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** > **Handover**. The paths may vary depending on the device.

2.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

3.  On the phone, open the content you wish to edit (for example, a note you've created).

4.  On the computer, click the![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_notepad_connection_phone.png) icon of Notepad in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to edit the note. If Notepad is already opened on the computer, tap![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_notepad_connection_phone.png) and then tap the thumbnail of the note from the other device.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

+   Handover is only available for some apps.

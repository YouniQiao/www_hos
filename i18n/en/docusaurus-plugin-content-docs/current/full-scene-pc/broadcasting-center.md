# Broadcasting Center, one-tap audio casting

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_BroadcastingCenter_share.png" width="720" height=""/>

With a single tap in Broadcasting Center, you can quickly cast your PC's audio to other audio devices.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Connect your PC to an audio device (such as earbuds or a speaker). For details about how to connect, refer to the device's user guide.
2.  Click the middle area of the status bar in the lower right corner of the home screen (which contains the battery icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png)), or press the HarmonyOS key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C on the keyboard, to open Control Panel.
3.  Click an empty area in the Broadcasting Center section to open the details screen, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_shareplay.png) (or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_headphones_filled_blue.png)) in the upper right corner of the card of the audio app you want to cast, and click the name of the target device in the device list below to finish casting the audio.

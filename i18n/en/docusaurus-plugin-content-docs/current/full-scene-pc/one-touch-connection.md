# Connect Your Phone and Computer with One Tap

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_pc_TouchConnect.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Tap your phone against your computer to initiate a connection. You can even drag and drop files between the two devices, and navigate on your phone with the benefit of a mouse and keyboard.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2.  On your phone, swipe down from the upper right corner to display Control Panel and enable NFC.

3.  Tap the back of your phone against the middle area of the touchpad until a connection pop-up displays. Then, tap Connect as prompted on your phone.

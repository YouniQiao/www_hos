# Connect your PC and phone with one tap

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_pc_TouchConnect.png" width="720" height=""/>

With a gentle tap, quickly connect your PC and phone. Once connected for collaboration, you can use the keyboard and mouse on your PC to control your phone (for example, drag and drop files between them, copy and paste text, and edit files on your phone), helping you work more efficiently.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On both your phone and PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in with the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On your phone, swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open the Control Panel, and turn on NFC.
3.  Hold the back of your phone against the center of your PC's touchpad until a connection prompt appears on your phone, then tap to connect on your phone by following the onscreen instructions.

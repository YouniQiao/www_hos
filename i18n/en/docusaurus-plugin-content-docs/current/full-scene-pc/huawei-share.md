# HUAWEI Share: Fast File Transfers Across Devices

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_HuaweiShare.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

No data required — transfer files wirelessly at high speed between your PC, tablet, and phone with HUAWEI Share.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On your phone/tablet and PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and enable HUAWEI Share on both.
    +   **Phone/tablet:** Swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open the Control Panel, and turn on HUAWEI Share.
    +   **PC running HarmonyOS 5:** Click the middle area of the status bar in the lower right corner of the home screen (which contains the battery icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn-dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png)) to open the Control Panel, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_hwshare.png) to turn on HUAWEI Share.
    +   **HUAWEI Windows PC:** Double-click to open HUAWEI PC Manager, and click to turn on HUAWEI Share.
2.  On the sending device, select the file you want to share, choose Share > HUAWEI Share (or Show more options > HUAWEI Share), and select the receiving device from the device list.
3.  On the receiving device, after you receive the file sharing notification, click Accept or Save as:
    +   **Accept:** The shared file is automatically saved to a fixed location, such as the HUAWEI Share folder in Files or Gallery.
    +   **Save as:** You can specify a custom destination folder for the shared file (only supported on some devices; the actual behavior may vary).

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   When some devices are shared with for the first time, after you turn on HUAWEI Share, you need to select Visible to all (10 minutes) before they can be discovered.
+   For devices signed in to the same HUAWEI ID, after you share a file on the sending device, the receiving device automatically accepts and saves it.

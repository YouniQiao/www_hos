# Hand-eye coordination: drag files with your gaze

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_KeyboardMouseSharing_DragFiles.png" alt="Hand-eye coordination: drag files with your gaze" width="720" height="540" />

When using keyboard and mouse sharing, drag a document or image on the current device, then shift your gaze to the target device and press Ctrl to confirm. The document or image instantly moves to the target device. Wherever you look, your content arrives — simple and efficient.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

Take dragging an image from your phone to instantly move it to Notepad on your PC as an example:

1. On both your phone and PC, turn on **WLAN** and **Bluetooth**, and sign in to the same **HUAWEI ID**.
2. On both your phone and PC, go to **Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Keyboard and mouse sharing**, and confirm the following settings are complete:
   - **Phone:** Confirm that **Keyboard and mouse sharing** is enabled (it is enabled by default), and enable **Hand-eye coordination**.
   - **PC:** Confirm that **Keyboard and mouse sharing** and **Allow the mouse to move within the screen of a nearby device to connect to that device** are enabled (they are enabled by default), and enable **Hand-eye coordination**.
3. On your PC, open **Notepad** and click a note to enter edit mode. Then move the cursor to the edge of the screen and keep moving it outward, or tap the edge of the screen twice in quick succession with the cursor. When the crossing animation appears at the edge of the screen, keep moving the cursor off the screen until the keyboard and mouse sharing prompt appears on your phone.
4. After the mouse cursor crosses over to your phone, press and hold the touchpad with one finger or hold down the left mouse button to drag the image on your phone (keep holding without releasing). Then shift your gaze to the PC screen. When ![Hand-eye coordination icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_face.png) appears at the top, press the Ctrl key on the PC keyboard to confirm.
5. After the image crosses over to the PC along with the cursor, move the cursor to the position in the note where you want to insert the image. When ![Add icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add_norm_green.png) appears in the upper right corner of the image, release your finger.

# Share Files Between HarmonyOS and iOS Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_HuaweiShare_Apple.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Seamlessly transfer photos, videos, and files across HarmonyOS PCs and iPhones, iPads, or MacBooks.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The following uses file transfer between a HarmonyOS computer and an iPhone as an example:

1.  Before you get started, apply the following settings:

    +   HarmonyOS computer: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen or press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C on the keyboard to open Control Panel, and make sure that Huawei Share is enabled (![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_hwshare_blue.png)). For devices to accept a sharing for the first time, enable Huawei Share and select Everyone (10 min) first to get them discoverable.

    +   iPhone: Download HarmonyOS Interconnect from the App Store, and enable Bluetooth.

2.  Perform the following operations based on the sharing scenario:

    +   From your device to an iPhone: Select the files to be shared, go to **Share** > **Huawei Share**, and click the icon of the target iPhone.

    +   From an iPhone to your device: Open HarmonyOS Interconnect, tap the computer icon, select the files to be shared, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the sharing.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

To use this feature, the iPhone must run iOS 13 or later, and your HarmonyOS computer must run HarmonyOS 6.0.0.115 or later.

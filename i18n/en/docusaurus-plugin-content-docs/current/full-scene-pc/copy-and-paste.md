# Copy and paste across devices

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_CrossDevice_Clipboard.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Use the cross-device clipboard to copy and paste text, images, and other content between devices, making it easy to share content across multiple devices.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take copying content from your phone and pasting it into a note on your PC as an example:

1.  On both your phone and PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  In an app on your phone (such as Browser or Notes), touch and hold to select text or an image, then choose Copy.
3.  In Notes on your PC, move the cursor to where you want to insert the content, then right-click and choose Paste.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

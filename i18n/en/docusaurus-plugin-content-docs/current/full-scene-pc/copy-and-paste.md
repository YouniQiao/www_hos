# Copy and Paste Contents Between Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_CrossDevice_Clipboard.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Copy and paste images, text, and other materials between devices with **Cross-device clipboard**, to craft stunning works that put the world on notice.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The following describes how to copy content from your phone to computer **Notepad**.

1.  Make sure that Cross-device clipboard is enabled (enabled by default) on your phone and computer by going to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** > **Cross-device clipboard**. The paths may vary depending on the device.

2.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

3.  In an app on the phone (such as Browser or Notepad), hold down on the text or image you wish to copy, and select Copy.

4.  In Notepad on the computer, right-click the location where you wish to insert the content and select Paste.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

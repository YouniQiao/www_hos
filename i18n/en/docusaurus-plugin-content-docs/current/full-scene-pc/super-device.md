# Super Device for Effortless Device Collaboration

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_SuperDevice_trag.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Enjoy a rich and seamless multi-device collaboration experience with **Super Device**, to manage multiple devices and quickly initiate collaboration. For example, you can edit phone files on your computer, or connect earbuds to your computer for more immersive listening.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The following uses phone-computer collaboration as an example:

1.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2.  Enable Control Panel and click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_superdevice.png) in Super Device on the first device.

    +   Mobile phone: Swipe down from the upper right corner to display Control Panel.

    +   Computer: Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen, or press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C (on the keyboard) to open Control Panel.

3.  In the device list on the Super Device screen, click the name of the device to be connected to initiate a connection.

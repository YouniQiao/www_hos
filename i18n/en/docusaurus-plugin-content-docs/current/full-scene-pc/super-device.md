# Super Device: Quickly Collaborate Across Multiple Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_SuperDevice_trag.png" width="720" height=""/>

Use Super Device to collaborate quickly with a single touch and manage collaboration across multiple devices. For example, connect your PC with your phone to edit across screens more conveniently, or connect your PC with your earbuds to easily stream music.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take collaboration between a phone and a PC as an example:

1.  On both the phone and the PC, enable WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On the PC, click the middle area of the status bar in the lower-right corner of the screen (which contains the battery icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png)), or press the HarmonyOS key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C on the keyboard, to open Control Panel.
3.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_superdevice.png), then in the device list on the Super Device screen, click the name of the device you want to connect to and collaboration will begin.

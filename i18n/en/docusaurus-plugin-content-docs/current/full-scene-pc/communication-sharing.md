# Multi-device communication sharing


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_CommunicationSharing.png" width="720" height=""/>

Turn on communication sharing to share your phone's mobile network with your PC when it has no network or a poor connection, so you can get online anytime, anywhere. You can also share your phone's mobile communication capabilities to make and answer calls and send and receive text messages while using your PC.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  To share the network, you need to delete the WLAN your phone is currently connected to. On your phone, go to Settings > WLAN, tap the name of the connected WLAN, then tap Delete this network.
2.  On your phone and PC, complete the following settings:
    +   **Phone:** Turn on WLAN, Bluetooth, and Mobile data, and sign in to your HUAWEI ID.
    +   **PC:** Turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID as your phone.
3.  On your phone, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Communication sharing, and turn on Network sharing.
4.  On your PC, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Multi-device collaboration > Communication sharing > Connect to network, and select a connection method as needed:
    +   **Auto connect:** Select Auto connect, and your PC will automatically search for and connect to the mobile network of a nearby phone. For the first connection, go to Settings > WLAN on your PC and click the network name in the shared network list to initiate the connection.
    +   **Manual connect:** Select Manual connect, and turn on either the Make and answer calls or Send and receive messages switch. Then go to Settings > WLAN on your PC and click the network name in the shared network list to initiate the connection.
5.  As needed, turn on the following switches on your phone and PC so that your PC can share your phone's calls or text messages:
    +   **Call sharing:** Turn on Call sharing on your phone and Make and answer calls on your PC. Once enabled, you can answer or make calls on your PC.
    +   **Message sharing:** Turn on Message sharing on your phone and Send and receive messages on your PC. Once enabled, you can view or reply to text messages received on your phone from your PC.
6.  When ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_communicationsharing_connect.png) or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_communicationsharing.png) appears in the status bar in the lower right corner of your PC, the connection is successful.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Currently, only one-to-one communication sharing between devices is supported.

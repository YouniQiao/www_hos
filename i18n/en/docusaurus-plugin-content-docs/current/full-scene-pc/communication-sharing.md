# Multi-Device Mobile Network

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_CommunicationSharing.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

The **Communication sharing** feature allows your computer with no cellular capability to make use of your phone's communications, to connect to the Internet, make or answer calls, and send or receive SMS messages.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  On the phone or computer, perform the following operations:

    +   Phone: Enable WLAN, Bluetooth, and Mobile data, and log in to HUAWEI ID.

    +   Computer: Enable WLAN and Bluetooth, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID as your phone.

2.  On your phone, go to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** > **Communication sharing** and enable Internet.

3.  On your computer, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, go to **More connectivity options** > **Communication sharing** > **Internet**, and select a connection mode as required.

    +   Auto-connect: Your computer will automatically search for and connect to the mobile networks of nearby phones. Currently, only one-to-one communication sharing between devices is supported. If your phone has already shared its mobile network with another device, go to **Settings** > **WLAN** on your computer and click the network name in the Shared list to initiate a connection.

    +   Connect manually: Go to **Settings** > **WLAN** on your computer and click the network name in the Shared list to initiate a connection.

4.  Toggle on the following switches on your phone or computer to share your phone calls or messages:

    +   Call sharing: Enable Phone calls on your phone and Phone calls on your computer. Once enabled, you can answer or make phone calls on your computer.

    +   Message sharing: Enable Messages on your phone and Messages on your computer. Once enabled, you can view or reply to messages received by your phone on your computer.

5.  If![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/status/HM_communicationsharing_connect.png) or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/status/HM_communicationsharing.png) displays in the status bar in the lower right corner of your computer, the connection is successful.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Some phones need to disconnect from the WLAN when sharing the network. You can go to Settings > WLAN, tap the name of the connected WLAN, and tap **Delete this network**.

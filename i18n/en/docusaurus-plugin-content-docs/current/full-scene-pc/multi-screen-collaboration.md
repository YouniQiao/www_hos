# Multi-screen collaboration: connect your PC and phone

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_pc_connect_phone.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Once connected, a mirrored window of your phone appears on the PC desktop. You can use the keyboard and mouse on your PC to control your phone — for example, drag and drop files between them, copy and paste text, and edit files on your phone — for more efficient work.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On both your phone and PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On your PC, click the middle area of the status bar in the lower-right corner of the desktop (which includes the battery icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png)) to open Control Panel.
3.  In the Super Device area, click the phone icon (![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_phone_filled.png)) to start multi-screen collaboration.

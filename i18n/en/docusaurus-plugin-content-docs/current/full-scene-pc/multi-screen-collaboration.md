# Phone-Computer Multi-Screen Collaboration

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_pc_connect_phone.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Drag and drop files between the two devices, and navigate on your phone with the benefit of a mouse and keyboard.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Make sure that Super Device is enabled (enabled by default) on your phone and computer by going to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** > **Super Device**. The paths may vary depending on the device.

2.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

3.  On the computer, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen, or press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C (on the keyboard) to open Control Panel.

4.  In the Super Device window, click the phone icon (![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_phone_filled.png)) to initiate a connection. Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_superdevice.png) to go to the details page if there are multiple devices that can be connected. In the device list, click the name of the device you wish to connect to initiate a connection.

# Quickly Get Beautiful Images Across Devices

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_device_interconnectivity.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

With cross-device connectivity, you can use the powerful camera on your phone or tablet to take photos or scan documents, making it quick and easy to get clear, beautiful images and insert them into what you're editing on your PC.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take editing a note as an example:

1.  On your phone/tablet and PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On your PC, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open App Center, then click to open Notepad.
3.  Click Notes > All notes in the left sidebar, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png) above to create a note or open an existing one. In the note editing area on the right, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_picture.png) and choose to use your phone/tablet's camera to take a photo or scan a document.
4.  After you tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) or ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png) on your phone/tablet to save the photo or scan, it will be automatically inserted into the note you're editing.
5.  When you're done editing, move the cursor away or click to switch to another note, and your changes will be saved automatically.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

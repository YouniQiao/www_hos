# Access High-Quality Photos with Connected Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_device_interconnectivity.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Make use of the powerful photography and scanning capabilities on your phone/tablet, and quickly insert the photos or scanned copies on your phone/tablet into the content you are editing on your computer, with **Device interconnectivity**.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Take note editing as an example:

1.  Make sure that Device interconnectivity is enabled (enabled by default) on your phone by going to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** > **Device interconnectivity**. The paths may vary depending on the device.

2.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

3.  On your computer, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click to open Notepad.

4.  Go to **Note** > **All notes** in the left pane, and then click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_add.png) at the top of the screen to create a note or open an existing note. In the right pane of the note editing screen, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_picture.png) and select Take a photo or Scan document to use your phone camera.

5.  Tap![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_ok.png) or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_save.png) on your phone. The photo or scanned copy will then be inserted into the note that you are editing.

6.  After editing, tap![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_notepad_todo_checkmark.png) at the top to save the note. Alternatively, move the cursor away or tap to switch to another note, and the changes will be saved automatically.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

# Extend Computer Screen to Your Tablet

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_wireless_projection_pad.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Wirelessly connect your computer to your tablet to expand your workspace and interaction possibilities. Experience the seamless efficiency of dual-screen browsing and multitasking.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Log in to the same HUAWEI ID on your computer and tablet.

2.  On your computer, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen or press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C to open Control Panel.

3.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_wirelessprojection.png) to start the search for devices that support screen casting. You can also press![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_cast.png) (on the keyboard) when the Fn key indicator is off to enable wireless screen casting.

4.  Click the name of your desired tablet in the device list to cast the computer screen content to the tablet.

5.  Select a screen casting mode as required:

    +   Extend this device's screen: The tablet will be used as the extended screen of the computer. This allows you to open different app windows on each screen, making it more efficient for work and study with dual screens.

    +   Mirror this device's screen: The tablet and computer screens display the same content and are synchronized in real time.

6.  To end the screen casting session:

    +   Computer: Go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_chevron_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_wirelessprojection.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the lower right corner of the home screen, and click the name of the device that is in screen casting.

    +   Tablet: Swipe down from the top of the screen to open the status bar, tap![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_wirelessprojection_filled_blue.png), and then tap![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/link_slash.png) to exit screen casting.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

This feature requires that both devices run HarmonyOS 6.1 or later.

# Wireless projection: use a tablet as an extended screen

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/video/vid_wireless_projection_pad.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Connect to a tablet via wireless projection to expand how you use your PC and the space you work in. With two screens, it's easier to operate and browse.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Sign in to the same HUAWEI ID on both your PC and tablet.

2. On your PC, click the middle area of the status bar in the lower-right corner of the home screen (the area with the battery icon), or press the HarmonyOS key + C on the keyboard to open Control Panel.

3. Click the projection icon to start searching for devices you can project to. You can also press F8 on the keyboard (when the Fn key indicator is off) to turn on wireless projection.

4. Once the search is complete, click the name of the tablet you want to connect to in the device list to project your PC screen to the tablet.

5. Choose a projection display mode as needed:

   - **Extended display:** Use the tablet as an extended screen for your PC. You can open different app windows on your PC and tablet and work across both screens.

   - **Mirror this PC's screen:** The tablet and PC display the same content and stay in sync in real time.

6. After projection starts, exit it using either of the following methods.

   - **PC:** Click in the lower-right corner of the home screen > the projection icon, then click the name of the device currently being projected to to exit projection.

   - **Tablet:** Swipe down from the top of the screen to open the status bar, tap the projection icon, then tap Exit projection.

+ This feature requires both devices to be upgraded to HarmonyOS 6.1 or later.

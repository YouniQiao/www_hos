# Gaze Pointer

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_KeyboardMouseSharing_GazePointer.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

When you're using keyboard & mouse sharing, press Ctrl to move the mouse cursor to the screen you're currently viewing, with the help of **Gaze pointer**.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The following uses keyboard & mouse sharing between a computer and a phone as an example:

1.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2.  On your phone and computer, go to **Settings** > **More connectivity options** > **Keyboard & mouse sharing** and make sure that the following settings have been completed:

    +   On your phone, enable Keyboard & mouse sharing and Gaze pointer.

    +   On your computer, enable Keyboard & mouse sharing and Gaze pointer, and select the device you wish to share the keyboard and mouse with from the Share with drop-down list.

3.  When the mouse cursor is on the computer you are currently looking at, move your gaze to the phone. When![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_face.png) appears above the phone screen, press Ctrl on the computer to switch the mouse pointer to the phone screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

+   This feature is only available on certain devices. If the Gaze pointer switch is not available on your device, this feature is not supported on your device.

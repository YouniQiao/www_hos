# Eyes and hands together: quickly switch keyboards and mice across devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_KeyboardMouseSharing_GazePointer.png" width="720" height=""/>

With Eyes and hands together, when sharing a keyboard and mouse across multiple devices, you can look at a screen and press the Ctrl key to move the cursor to the screen you are looking at.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take keyboard and mouse sharing between a PC and a phone as an example:

1.  On both the phone and the PC, turn on WLAN and Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  On both the phone and the PC, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Keyboard and mouse sharing, and complete the following settings:
    +   Phone: Turn on the switches for Keyboard and mouse sharing and Eyes and hands together.
    +   PC: Turn on the switches for Keyboard and mouse sharing and Eyes and hands together, and select the device to share the keyboard and mouse with from the drop-down list to the right of the device under Keyboard and mouse sharing.
3.  When the mouse cursor is on the PC you are currently looking at, shift your gaze to the phone. When ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_face.png) appears at the top of the phone screen, press the Ctrl key on the PC, and the mouse cursor will automatically switch to the phone screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.
+   Only some devices support Eyes and hands together. If your device does not have the Eyes and hands together switch, it does not support this feature. The actual situation shall prevail.

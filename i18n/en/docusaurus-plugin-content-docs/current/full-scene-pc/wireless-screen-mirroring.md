# Wireless screen mirroring for a big-screen experience

No cables needed — cast your PC screen to a large-screen device in seconds for smoother work and play.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Turn on the large-screen device and make sure the switch for the Cast+/Miracast protocol or the screen mirroring switch is enabled. See the device's manual for how to enable it.
2.  Click the middle area of the status bar in the lower-right corner of the home screen (which contains the battery icon ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/status/HM_public_battery.png)) to open Control Panel, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_wirelessprojection.png) to search for large-screen devices available for casting. You can also press the F8 key (when the Fn key indicator is off) or click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_cast.png) to turn on wireless screen mirroring.
3.  Once the search is complete, click the name of the target large-screen device in the device list to cast your PC screen to it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Wireless screen mirroring is supported only on some large-screen devices. Actual support may vary.

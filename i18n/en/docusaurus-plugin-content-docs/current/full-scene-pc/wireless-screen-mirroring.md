# ScreenCast, for an Immersive Experience

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_wireless_projection.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Cast your computer wirelessly to stream movies and work on a large-screen device.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Turn on the large-screen device and make sure that the Cast+/Miracast protocol or screen casting switch is toggled on. For details about how to toggle on the switches, see the device manual.

2.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen to open Control Panel, and click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_wirelessprojection.png) to search for the large-screen devices that support screen casting. You can also press![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_cast.png) (on the keyboard) when the Fn key indicator is off to enable wireless screen casting.

3.  Click the name of your desired large-screen device in the device list to cast the computer screen content to the large-screen device.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

**ScreenCast** is only supported on certain large-screen devices.

# Unlock Your Phone on Other Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_pc_unlock_phone.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Connect your computer and phone, and unlock your phone on the computer using the phone's lock screen password or the facial or fingerprint data enrolled on the computer.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Enable WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone and computer, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID.

2.  On your computer, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png) in the lower right corner of the home screen or press HarmonyOS Key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + C to open Control Panel.

3.  In Super Device, click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_superdevice.png), and then click the phone you wish to connect to in the device list.

4.  After the connection is established, if the phone screen is locked, you can select an unlock method in the phone mirroring window on the computer:

    +   Password unlock: Click Use phone screen lock and enter the lock screen password to unlock your phone.

    +   Biometric unlock: Click Use the computer's biometrics and unlock your phone through facial recognition or fingerprint verification based on the input on the computer. For the first use, you need to enter the lock screen password for authorization. After authorization, your phone will be automatically unlocked.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   To use biometric unlock, the computer and phone must run HarmonyOS 7.0 or later. If no biometric information (facial and fingerprint data) has been enrolled on the computer, no lock screen password has been set on the phone, or PrivateSpace has been enabled on the phone, you will not be able to unlock your phone using biometric recognition.

+   Facial recognition will be prioritized if you have enrolled both your facial and fingerprint data on your computer.

+   Face recognition is only available on certain models.

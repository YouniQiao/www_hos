# Media Control Panel: Adjust Playback with Ease

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10169850_f004_Audio_Control.png" width="560" height=""/>


From the Media Control Panel, you can easily switch tracks and control playback progress.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open the Control Panel, then tap an empty area of the Media Control Panel.
2.  The Media Control Panel displays the audio and video apps currently running. You can pause or resume audio playback, drag the progress bar to quickly adjust playback progress, and switch between tracks.

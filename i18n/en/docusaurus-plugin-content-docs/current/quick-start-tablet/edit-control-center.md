# Edit the Control Panel

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10099735_f005_EditSwitches.png" alt="Edit the Control Panel" width="560" height="" />

Arrange your frequently used shortcuts where they're easy to reach, and adjust the layout of the brightness and volume sliders to create a Control Panel that's all your own.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe down from the top right of the screen to open the **Control Panel**, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_controlpanel_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper left corner to enter edit mode:

- **Remove a shortcut**: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_remove.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the shortcut to remove it from the panel.
- **Add a shortcut**: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enter the add screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add1.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the shortcut to add it to the panel, then tap the gray area at the top to exit the add screen.
- **Move a shortcut**: Touch and hold a shortcut, then drag it to reposition it on the panel.

When you're done, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_choose_done.png" width="24" height="24"/> to save your settings.

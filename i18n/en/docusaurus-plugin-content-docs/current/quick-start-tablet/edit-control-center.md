# Edit Control Panel

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10099735_f005_EditSwitches.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Relocating the shortcut switches and brightness and volume bars in Control Panel gives you direct access to commonly-used features.

**Try it out**

Swipe down from the upper right corner of the screen to display **Control Panel**, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_controlpanel_edit.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Edit icon"/> in the upper left corner to access the editing screen.

+   **Remove a switch**: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_remove.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Remove icon"/> on the shortcut switch to remove it from the panel.

+   **Add a switch**: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Add icon"/>. On the displayed screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add1.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Add icon"/> on the shortcut switch to add it to the panel, and tap the gray area at the top to exit the adding screen.

+   **Move a switch**: Hold and drag the shortcut switch to adjust its position on the panel.

Once you're done editing, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_choose_done.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Confirm icon"/> to save the settings.

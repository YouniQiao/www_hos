# Add a card to the home screen

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044757_f005_AddSnippet.png" width="560" height=""/>


You can pick your favorite cards (such as the weather clock) to create a personalized home screen, and quickly preview or use apps — it looks great and is easy to use.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Pinch in with two fingers on the home screen, touch Cards, then touch a recommended card or an app name.

2.  Swipe left or right to choose the card style you like, then touch Add to home screen.

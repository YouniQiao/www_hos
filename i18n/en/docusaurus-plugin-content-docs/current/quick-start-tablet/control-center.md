# Using the Control Panel

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10044640_f014_ControlCentre.png" width="560" height=""/>


Whether your screen is locked or unlocked, you can use the Control Panel to quickly perform actions such as adjusting screen brightness or switching the ringer mode.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe down from the upper right side of the screen to open the Control Panel:

+   Tap a shortcut switch to quickly turn a feature on or off.
+   Touch and hold a shortcut switch to view more settings for some features.

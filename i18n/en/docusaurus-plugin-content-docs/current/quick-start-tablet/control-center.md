# Control Panel for Quick Feature Access

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044640_f014_ControlCentre.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Access the Control Panel to quickly set up WLAN, Huawei Share, and screenshot modes, and swipe left or right to switch between the Control Panel and Notification Panel.

**Try it out**

**Control switches:** Swipe down from the upper right corner of the screen to display the Control Panel, and tap a switch to enable or disable the corresponding feature. You can hold down on a switch to view more settings.

**Switch to the Notification Panel:** Swipe right on any area (except the brightness and volume bars) in the Control Panel to switch to the Notification Panel. Swipe left on any blank area in the Notification Panel to switch back to the Control Panel.

**Note**

If you do not wish to access Control Panel from the lock screen, go to Settings > Biometrics & screen lock and disable **Control Panel**.

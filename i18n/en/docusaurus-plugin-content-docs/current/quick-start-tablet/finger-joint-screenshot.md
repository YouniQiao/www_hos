# Knuckle Screenshot

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044781_f001_Screenshot.png" width="560" height=""/>


Use your knuckle to quickly capture the current screen, or capture a floating window or split-screen window specifically, so you can freeze the moment with ease.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Capture the full screen**: Tap the same spot on the screen twice quickly with one knuckle. In floating window or split-screen mode, you can also tap the icon at the top of the thumbnail to capture different window content, for example: tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202507110unrw6/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_floatingwindow.png" width="24" height="24"/> to capture only the floating window content.

**Scroll screenshot**: Tap the screen with one knuckle and draw an S shape with a little force. The screen will automatically scroll down and take a screenshot. Tap the scrolling area to stop the screenshot.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

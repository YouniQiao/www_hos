# Knuckle Screenshot

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044781_f001_Screenshot.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Capture content on your screen, a floating window, or a split-screen window, simply by knocking on the screen with your knuckles!

**Try it out**

**Enable knuckle screenshot:** Go to Settings > System > Shortcuts & gestures > Screenshots and make sure that **Knuckle screenshots** is enabled.

**Capture the full screen:** Knock twice on the screen with a single knuckle. In the floating window or split-screen mode, you can also tap the icon at the top of the thumbnail to capture content from different windows. For example, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_floatingwindow.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Floating Window icon"/> to capture only the content from the floating window.

**Scrollshot:** Tap a single knuckle against the screen and hold to draw an "S". Your device will automatically scroll to the bottom of the screen to capture all of the content in a single screenshot. You can tap the screen at any time to stop the scrolling.

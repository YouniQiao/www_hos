# Digital Note-Taking that Feels so Real

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044681_f009_Notes.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Draw and take notes to your heart's content on your tablet, just like your would on paper!

**Try it out**

**Create new notes**: Open **Notes**, and go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_add_note_pad.png" width="24" height="24"/> > New note.

**Edit notes**: Open a new note. The toolbar at the top features a range of tools, including those for handwriting, drawing, inserting images, and using the laser pointer.

# Notes: A New Paperless Experience for Learning and Work

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10044681_f009_Notes.png" alt="Notes" width="560" height="" />

Write and draw on your tablet for a note-taking experience just like using a paper notebook.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Create a note:** Open **Notes**, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_add_note_pad.png" width="24" height="24"/> > **New standard note**.

**Edit a note:** Open the note you just created. From the toolbar at the top, you can handwrite, doodle, insert images, and use the laser pointer.

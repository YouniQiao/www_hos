# Floating windows make multitasking easier

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202512310dkjZd/zh-cn/image/figure/10045865_f001_SuspensionWindow.png" width="560" height=""/>

Display another app's window as a floating window on top of the current screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Restore full-screen display:** Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508061ZsslO/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_muti_window_maximize1.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of the floating window.

**Minimize the floating window:** Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508061ZsslO/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_muti_window_bar.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of the floating window, then tap Minimize to shrink it into a side bar. Tap the side bar, or swipe inward on the side bar hidden at the edge, to restore the floating window.

**Switch to split screen**: When you're not on the home screen, touch the status bar at the top of the floating window and drag it to the left or right edge of the screen (in landscape mode) or to the top or bottom edge (in portrait mode), then hold briefly before releasing.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Only some apps support floating windows in landscape mode. The actual behavior may vary.

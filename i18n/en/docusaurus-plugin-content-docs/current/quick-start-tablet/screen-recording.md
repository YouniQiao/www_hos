# Screen Recording Made Easy

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044780_f001_ScreenRecord.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Capture action onscreen to create sharable videos, to show off your gaming skills, compose tutorials, and do so much more.

**Try it out**

Swipe down from the upper right corner of the screen to display **Control Panel**, and tap **Screen recorder** to start a screen recording. When you're finished, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/stop_circle_fill.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Stop icon"/> in the Live window at the top of the screen.

Tap any area except for the buttons in the Live capsule to expand it into a Live card, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/mic_slash_fill-01.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Microphone icon"/> to turn on the microphone, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/hand_point_up_tap_fill-01.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Show touch trails icon"/> to hide touches.

**Note**

To view the recorded video, go to Gallery > Screen recordings.

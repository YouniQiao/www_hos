# Quick Screen Lock

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044757_f006_ScreenLock.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Quickly lock your screen without pressing the Power button.

**Try it out**

+   **Lock screen**: Pinch two fingers on the home screen, tap **Widgets**, swipe down, tap **Lock screen**, select the required widget style, and tap **Add to home screen**.

+   **Tap to lock screen**: Go to Settings > System > Shortcuts & gestures, enable **Tap to lock screen**, and double-tap the blank area on the home screen.

**Note**

You can drag a Screen lock widget to the shortcut bar at the bottom (Please ensure that there is enough space.)

# Write with a Pen, Create with a Stroke

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10045857_f003_pen.png" alt="Write with a Pen, Create with a Stroke" width="560" height="" />

Write and draw in apps that support the stylus. Enjoy a more precise and efficient note-taking experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Pairing:** Attach the stylus by its groove to the top right of the tablet, then follow the on-screen prompt to finish pairing the stylus.

**Writing:** Open an app that supports the stylus, and use the stylus to draw or write.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Attach the stylus by its groove to the top right of the tablet to charge it wirelessly.

# Stylize Your Scribbles, with Stylus

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10045857_f003_pen.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Stylus makes handwriting and drawing on your tablet an absolute breeze, and is supported on a broad range of different apps.

**Try it out**

**Pairing:**Attach the groove of your stylus to the top right edge of the tablet and follow the onscreen instructions to complete pairing.

**Edit text**: Revise your text within the app's input box, using the special editing tools supported on the stylus.

**Note**

Attach the groove of your stylus to the upper right edge of the tablet to wirelessly charge the stylus.

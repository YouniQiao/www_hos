# Scan to pay, even from a distance or with a damaged code

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/20013791_f001_Scan.png" alt="Scan" width="560" height="" />

With the Scan feature, you don't need to open an app first. It handles complex scanning scenarios with ease, including damaged, reflective, and distorted codes, so you can scan in one step and go straight to the service.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open **Control Panel**, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_scan.png" width="24" height="24"/>, select **Scan**, and adjust the position of your tablet so that the QR code appears in the viewfinder.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Scanning is currently supported in a variety of scenarios, including mobile payments, ordering food, bike sharing, parking fees, parcel pickup, and EV charging.

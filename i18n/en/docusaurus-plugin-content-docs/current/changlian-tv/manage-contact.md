# Add or delete MeeTime contacts

To use MeeTime on your HUAWEI Vision, you need to add MeeTime contacts first. Once added, you can start a call.

## Prerequisites

+   The phone number of the contact you want to add must be linked to a HUAWEI ID.
+   The phone number of the contact you want to add must be registered for the MeeTime service.

## Add or delete contacts in the MeeTime app on HUAWEI Vision

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, open the MeeTime app<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002421110712.png" width="24" height="24"/>.

2.  On the Contacts screen, select Add contact.
    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002415824068.png)
    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.

3.  Follow the onscreen instructions to enter the phone number and nickname, then tap Save.
4.  On the contact card, press the remote control's menu button<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002376253718.png" width="24" height="24"/> or press and hold the remote control's confirm button to edit, delete, or pin a contact.

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Devices under the same HUAWEI ID as the HUAWEI Vision cannot be deleted or modified.


## Add or delete contacts in the AI Life app on your phone

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>This feature is not supported on Huawei phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

1.  Add your HUAWEI Vision in the AI Life app on your phone. For details, see: [Add a HUAWEI Vision in the AI Life app](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064466/).
2.  On the AI Life app home screen, select the corresponding HUAWEI Vision card, then go to Features > MeeTime.

3.  On the Contacts screen, tap + to add a HUAWEI Vision contact in either of the following ways.

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298312620.jpg" width="360" height="486"/>

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.

    +   Select Phone contacts, then under Contacts with MeeTime enabled, tap Add to the right of the contact you want to add.
    +   Select New contact, follow the onscreen instructions to enter a nickname and phone number, select a role, then tap the icon in the upper right corner<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002392781184.png" width="24" height="24"/>.

        <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332312153.png" width="360" height="486"/> <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332192353.png" width="360" height="486"/>

        <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.


4.  On the Contacts screen, tap the contact you want to delete to open the details screen, then tap Delete contact in the lower left corner or Edit contact in the lower right corner.

# Calling a HUAWEI Vision from Your Phone Using MeeTime

You can use the MeeTime app on your phone or the MeeTime feature in AI Life to call a HUAWEI Vision and make a MeeTime call. The operations vary by phone. See the following for details:

+   Huawei phones support both AI Life and the MeeTime app.
+   Non-Huawei phones support only AI Life.

## Prerequisites

+   Register for the MeeTime service. For details, see: [How do I enable/sign in to MeeTime on a HUAWEI Vision?](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064456/).
+   Add MeeTime contacts. For details, see: [How do I add/delete MeeTime contacts on a HUAWEI Vision?](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064452/).

## Procedure

**MeeTime app**

+   Open the MeeTime app on your phone, go to the **Contacts** tab, select the target HUAWEI Vision, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002428714248.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002428874104.png" width="24" height="24"/> to make a video or voice call.
+   On the **Calls** tab, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298153668.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the end of a call record, and then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002462354025.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002428715424.png" width="24" height="24"/> to start a call.

**AI Life**

1.  Make sure a HUAWEI Vision has been added in AI Life on your phone. For details, see: [Adding a HUAWEI Vision in AI Life](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064466/).
2.  Open AI Life on your phone, select the card of the target HUAWEI Vision, and go to **Features** > **MeeTime**.

    Table 1. Calling a HUAWEI Vision from a phone
    | Phone version| Operation|
    | --- | --- |
    | HarmonyOS 5 and later| Directly calls the HUAWEI Vision bound to your phone.|
    | HarmonyOS 4.3 and earlier/iOS devices/Android phones| On the Contacts screen, select the contact or HUAWEI Vision you want to call, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002462473885.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002428875256.png" width="24" height="24"/> to make a call. On the Calls screen, select a contact or HUAWEI Vision you have called before to make a call.|

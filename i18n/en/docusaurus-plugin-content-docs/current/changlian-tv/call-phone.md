# Make a MeeTime Call to a Phone on HUAWEI Vision

You can use HUAWEI Vision to call a HUAWEI Vision, in-car HUAWEI Vision, or phone that has been added as a contact, and make a video or voice call.

## Prerequisites

+   The number you want to call must have a HUAWEI ID and be registered for MeeTime call services. To register for MeeTime call services, see: [How do I enable/sign in to MeeTime on a HUAWEI Vision?](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064456/).
+   For details about the devices that HUAWEI Vision supports calling, see: [Device types supported by MeeTime calls](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn15783055/).
+   MeeTime call quality depends on the environment, network, and volume settings during the call. In a normal home environment, clear calls can be maintained within 3 meters of HUAWEI Vision. A network bandwidth of 50M or higher is recommended.

## Procedure

+   **Voice call**

    Press and hold the voice button on the remote control and say "Call XX" into the microphone, or simply say "Celia, Celia, call XX" to HUAWEI Vision to call that contact.

+   **Manual call**
    1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, open MeeTime<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002420950888.png" width="24" height="24"/>. You can make a call in any of the following ways:
        +   Method 1: On the dialer screen, enter the complete phone number and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332312701.png" width="24" height="24"/>, then select a callable device to make a quick call.
        +   Method 2: On the call screen, select a call record from the recent calls list and call back directly.
            ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002415984236.png)

        +   Method 3: Tap Contacts, select a contact and a callable device, and start a MeeTime call.

        +   Method 4: On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002458074457.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002458154621.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the MeeTime call card to call a contact.     2.  During a call, press the confirm button on the remote control to:

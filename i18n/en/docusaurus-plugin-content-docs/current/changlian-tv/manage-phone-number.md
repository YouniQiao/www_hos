# Manage the phone number bound to MeeTime calls

To change the phone number bound to MeeTime calls, you can do the following:

## **On HUAWEI Vision**

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, open MeeTime <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002454500433.png" width="24" height="24"/>.
2.  After entering the MeeTime app, select the profile picture in the upper right corner to go to Me > Number settings.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002415823720.png)

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. Refer to the actual product.


3.  You can manage your MeeTime number in the following ways:
    +   **Set the calling number**: In the list of numbers on this device, select the number you want to set, and press the confirm button on the remote control.
    +   **Add a call number**: Press the menu button on the remote control <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002409612329.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and select Add new number. Enter the new number and verification code, and select Confirm.
    +   **Delete a call number**: Press the menu button on the remote control <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002375932984.png" width="24" height="24"/>, select the number you want to delete, press the menu button on the remote control again <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002428871640.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and select Delete in the dialog box.
    +   **Change the call number**: Press the menu button on the remote control <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002376092860.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and tap Change next to the bound number to bind another number.

## **On phone**

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>This feature is not supported on HUAWEI phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

1.  If HUAWEI Vision has been added in the AI Life app on your phone, refer to: [Add HUAWEI Vision in the AI Life app](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064466/).
2.  Open the AI Life app on your phone, select the corresponding HUAWEI Vision card, and go to Functions > MeeTime calls to enter the MeeTime calls details screen.
3.  After entering the MeeTime calls screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002300290958.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner.

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002300450666.jpg" width="360" height="486"/>

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. Refer to the actual product.


4.  On the "MeeTime call number" tab, tap "Add new number" at the bottom.

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002334210309.jpg" width="360" height="486"/>

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. Refer to the actual product.


5.  You can manage your MeeTime number in the following ways:
    +   **Add a call number**: Tap Number management at the bottom of the screen, select Add new number, and follow the onscreen instructions to enter the new number and verification code, then select Confirm.
    +   **Delete a call number**: Tap Number management at the bottom of the screen, select the number you want to delete, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002392941076.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the right.

        <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Adding or deleting a number will sync to all devices where you have enabled MeeTime calls.

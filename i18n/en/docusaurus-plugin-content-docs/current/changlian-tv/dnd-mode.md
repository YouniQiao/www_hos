# Set Do Not Disturb for MeeTime Calls

HUAWEI Vision supports Do Not Disturb mode, so you can choose to reject calls from unknown numbers or silence incoming calls when the screen is off.

## Reject calls from unknown numbers

When the **Reject calls from unknown numbers** switch under Do Not Disturb in the MeeTime app<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002454509713.png" width="24" height="24"/> on your HUAWEI Vision is enabled, unknown numbers cannot find or call your HUAWEI Vision. If you want to protect your personal information, you can enable the **Reject calls from unknown numbers** switch to allow only your HUAWEI Vision contacts to call.

You can set this up in either of the following ways:

+   On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > MeeTime > Do Not Disturb > Reject calls from unknown numbers, and press the confirm button on the remote control to turn it off or on.
+   You can also go to MeeTime > profile picture in the upper right corner > Do Not Disturb > Reject calls from unknown numbers on the HUAWEI Vision home screen, and press the confirm button on the remote control to turn it off or on.

## Set Do Not Disturb when the screen is off

The **Do Not Disturb when screen is off** switch for MeeTime on HUAWEI Vision is enabled by default, so no call notifications are allowed between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM.

You can also set your own Do Not Disturb when screen is off period for HUAWEI Vision:

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to MeeTime > profile picture in the upper right corner > Do Not Disturb.

    You can also go to Settings > MeeTime > Do Not Disturb on the HUAWEI Vision home screen.

2.  Under Do Not Disturb when screen is off, tap the Do Not Disturb when screen is off period to set the start and end times.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>When the **Do Not Disturb when screen is off** switch is turned off, MeeTime call notifications will be supported all day even when the screen is off.

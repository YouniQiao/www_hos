# Enable/Sign In to MeeTime

You can sign up for MeeTime calling services in the MeeTime app<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002454580345.png" width="24" height="24"/> on your HUAWEI Vision or in the AI Life app on your phone. The specific steps are as follows:


* MeeTime calling is currently free of charge, but this may change due to policy or other reasons, so please refer to the actual situation. When using MeeTime calling over mobile data, your carrier may charge data fees. For specific charges, consult your carrier.
* A network bandwidth of 50 Mbit/s or higher is recommended for MeeTime calling services.
* For the devices that HUAWEI Vision supports calling, see: [Device types supported by MeeTime calling](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn15783055/).
* HUAWEI Vision MeeTime calling currently supports Huawei phones/tablets running HarmonyOS 2 or later, and non-Huawei phones running Android 8.0 or later.
* If your HUAWEI Vision has already been added in the AI Life app on your phone, see: [Add HUAWEI Vision in the AI Life app](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064466/).

## On HUAWEI Vision

On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, open the MeeTime<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002421227214.png" width="24" height="24"/> app, tap Agree, and enter your HUAWEI ID and password as prompted to sign in to MeeTime.

If you are already signed in to your HUAWEI ID on HUAWEI Vision, opening the MeeTime app will automatically link to your HUAWEI ID and sign you in.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>If you are already signed in to your HUAWEI ID but still cannot sign in to the MeeTime app automatically, follow the on-screen instructions.

## On your phone


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>This feature is not currently supported on Huawei phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

1.  On the **Home** screen of the AI Life app on your phone, select the corresponding HUAWEI Vision card, and go to Features > MeeTime Call to access the MeeTime Call details screen.


    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>After you tap the HUAWEI Vision card, the HUAWEI Vision plug-in and the MeeTime Call plug-in will be downloaded automatically over Wi-Fi. Over mobile data, you will be prompted to confirm whether to download the app plug-ins.

2.  Follow the on-screen instructions and tap Enable Now. (If this is your first time registering, follow the guide page to register.)

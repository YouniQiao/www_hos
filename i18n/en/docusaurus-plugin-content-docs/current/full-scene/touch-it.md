# Tap to Share: Transfer Services Between Your Phone and Car

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202412090c4qmu/zh-cn/image/video/10044618_f001_TapToTransfer.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Tap your phone against your car's center display to transfer addresses, routes, and navigation tasks from your phone's map app to the HUAWEI smart cockpit center display, so great services are always with you.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before you start, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Super Desktop on your phone and make sure Super Desktop is enabled.

1.  Swipe down from the upper right of your phone's screen to open the Control Panel, then turn on Bluetooth and NFC.

2.  On the center display's status bar, tap the Bluetooth switch to turn on Bluetooth.

3.  Open the app you want to transfer on your phone (for example, open Petal Maps and select a location, route, or navigation), and while the center display is on, hold the NFC area on the back of your phone near the NFC detection area on the center display. Move your phone away once it vibrates.

4.  Follow the onscreen instructions on your phone and the center display to complete the transfer.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

The NFC detection area on the center display is located at the bottom edge of the display, in the middle but slightly to the left.

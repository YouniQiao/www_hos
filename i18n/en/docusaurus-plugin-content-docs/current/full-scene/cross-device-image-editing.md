# Seamless Cross-Device Image Editing Continuation

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/video/20005815_f001_AppContinuation.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

With continuation, you can seamlessly pick up a task from your current device on another device. For example, you can smoothly move an editing task in Meitu on your phone to a tablet, and use the large screen with a stylus for more creative editing; when you're done, you can switch back to your phone to save and share.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Make sure your phone and tablet are signed in to the same HUAWEI ID and both have Meitu installed.
2.  On both your phone and tablet, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Continuation and turn on the Continuation switch.
3.  Open Meitu on your phone and start editing a photo. The Meitu icon will appear in the tablet's shortcut bar. Tap the Meitu icon that appears to continue the editing task from your phone on the tablet.
4.  Tap the Meitu icon that appears in your phone's shortcut bar to move the editing task back to your phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Some features in Meitu (such as collage and video editing) do not currently support continuation. The actual experience prevails.
+   This feature is currently supported only between devices running HarmonyOS 5 or later.

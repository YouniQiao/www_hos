# Multi-screen collaboration: drag and drop files across devices

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/video/10045857_f004_DragFile.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Connect your phone to your laptop for collaboration, and you can drag and drop files between the phone window on your laptop and the laptop itself, making editing and sharing more convenient.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Connect to your laptop**:

1.  Turn on Bluetooth and WLAN on both your phone and laptop, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  Swipe down from the upper right of your phone's screen to open the Control Panel, then tap the laptop icon in Super Device to initiate a collaboration connection.

**Drag and drop files to share**:

In the phone window on your laptop, touch and hold a file in Gallery or Files until it floats, then drag it to the laptop's home screen or a folder. You can also drag files from your laptop into the phone window.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Before use, update PC Manager to the latest version. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_PCmenu.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Check for updates to upgrade.

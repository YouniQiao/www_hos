# Access High-Quality Photos with Connected Devices

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/20005778_f001_CollaborativeNotes.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Make use of the powerful photography and scanning capabilities on your phone, or access your images in phone Gallery, and quickly insert the photos or scanned copies into the note or email you are editing on your Huawei tablet.

**Try it out**

1.  Enable **NearLink & Bluetooth** and **WLAN** on your phone and tablet, and log in to the same **HUAWEI ID** on both devices.

2.  Open **Notepad** on your tablet, In the **All notes** list, go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_add_note_pad.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_notepad_picture.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and choose to use your phone's camera to take photos or scan documents, or obtain photos from your phone.

3.  When you're done, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_insertphoto_notepad.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_input_file.png" width="24" height="24" alt="保存图标"/>. The photo will be sent back to the note that is being edited on your tablet.

**Note**

This feature requires that both devices support HarmonyOS 5 or later.

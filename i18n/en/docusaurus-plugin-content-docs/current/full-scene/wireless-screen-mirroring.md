# Wireless screen mirroring for a big-screen experience

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202412090c4qmu/zh-cn/image/figure/10045857_f001_WirelessProjection.png" width="360" height="486"/>

No cables needed — mirror your phone screen to a large display in seconds and enjoy audio and video on the big screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Connect your phone and the large display to the same LAN.
2.  Swipe down from the upper right of your phone to open the Control Panel, tap Wireless Projection, then follow the onscreen instructions to finish mirroring.

When you're done, tap the mirroring Live Capsule<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202412090c4qmu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_projection_mirror.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of your phone, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202412090c4qmu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_Multiwindow_close.png" width="24" height="24"/> to exit mirroring.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Wireless screen mirroring is only supported on some large display devices. The actual experience may vary.

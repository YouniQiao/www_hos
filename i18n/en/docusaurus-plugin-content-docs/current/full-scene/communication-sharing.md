# Move Close to Connect and Share Your Phone's Communication Capabilities

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/video/10044640_f020_shareMobilenetwork.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Share your phone's communication capabilities with your tablet to browse the internet using your phone's mobile network, make and answer calls, and send and receive text messages — all without interruption.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Before you begin, complete the following settings:
   - **Phone**: Turn on WLAN, Star Flash | Bluetooth, and Mobile data, and sign in to your HUAWEI ID.
   - **Tablet**: Turn on WLAN and Star Flash | Bluetooth, and sign in to the same HUAWEI ID as your phone.

2. On your tablet, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Communication sharing, turn on the switches for Make and answer calls and Send and receive messages, and set Network connection to Auto-connect.

3. Bring your phone and tablet close together. When <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/status/ic_notification_hotspot.png" width="24" height="24"/> appears in the tablet's status bar, communication sharing is enabled.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ If your phone uses two SIM cards, communication sharing uses the number selected for mobile data by default.

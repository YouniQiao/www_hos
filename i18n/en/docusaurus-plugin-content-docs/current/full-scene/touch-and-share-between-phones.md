# Tap to Share: Fun Sharing Between Phones

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202503231uozzo/zh-cn/image/video/10044641_f019_TouchShare.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

With just a tap, you can share photos, videos, WLAN, music, themes, and more directly with friends. You can also tap multiple devices in succession to easily share with several people at once.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On the sender's phone, enter the sharing state (for example, open a single photo or video in Gallery, or select multiple photos or videos; for more sharing scenarios, [see the official website](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16056054/?forwardType=0)).
2.  Gently tap the top edges of the sender's phone and one or up to four recipient phones together until the sharing animation appears.
3.  Swipe up on the sender's phone to share, and tap Receive on the recipient's phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

This feature requires both phones to be upgraded to HarmonyOS 5 or later. Sharing with multiple people requires the sender's device to support HarmonyOS 6 or later.

# Tap to Transfer, for Encrypted Sharing Between Phones

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044641_f019_TouchShare.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Tap your phone against your friend's phone to share encrypted images, videos, and files, or tap your phone against multiple devices in quick succession to share encrypted content with multiple friends.

**Try it out**

1.  On the sending phone, open the content to be shared (for example, open one image or select multiple images in Gallery).

2.  **Tap the top** of the sending phone against one or more phones until the sharing card is displayed.

3.  Tap the card to encrypt the content. Tap the card again to cancel encryption.

4.  Swipe up on the sending phone and tap **Accept** on the receiving phone.

**Note**

+   This feature requires both phones to run HarmonyOS 5 or later. If the sending phone runs HarmonyOS 7 or later, the sender can share content with up to eight devices.

+   Encrypted sharing requires both phones to be logged in to a HUAWEI ID and run HarmonyOS 7 or later.

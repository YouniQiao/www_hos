# Exercise and Blood Pressure Study

This feature measures your physiological metrics throughout a workout session using a high-performing sensor, to evaluate your blood pressure (BP) response to exercise as well as cardiovascular health.

## Joining the Exercise and Blood Pressure Study

You can choose to join the Exercise and Blood Pressure Study from your wearable device or phone.

On your watch:

1.  Open the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Exercise and Blood Pressure Study, and follow the onscreen instructions to join the study and complete the questionnaire.
2.  Touch Start assessment and fill in your basic personal information.
3.  Go to Next > Start and follow the onscreen tutorial to complete a measurement.

On your phone:

Make sure that your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth and the Huawei Health app.

1.  Download and install the HUAWEI Research app on your phone from AppGallery.
2.  Go to HUAWEI Research > Exercise and Blood Pressure Study, follow the onscreen instructions, and touch Join.
3.  Go to Start Assessment > Next and follow the onscreen instructions to start the assessment on your watch.
4.  On your watch, go to HUAWEI Research > Exercise and Blood Pressure Study > Start assessment > Next > Start.
5.  Follow the onscreen tutorial to complete a measurement.

+   If you have paired your watch with an iPhone, this feature is available on your watch, but the measurement data cannot be synced to your phone.
+   Data and results are for personal reference only and shouldn't be used as a basis for medical diagnosis.

## Viewing the assessment result

Assessment result

+   On your wearable: Open the app list and go to HUAWEI Research > Exercise and Blood Pressure Study to view the measurement results.
+   On your phone: Open the HUAWEI Research app and select Exercise and Blood Pressure Study to view detailed measurement data.

Exercise and cardiovascular risks

During exercise, the circulatory system, especially the heart, is under significant pressure. If abnormal physiological metrics are detected during or after exercise, such as abnormal BP response, ECG readings, or recovery heart rate, it may indicate cardiovascular issues like myocardial ischemia, ventricular hypertrophy, or arrhythmia, suggesting a certain level of cardiovascular risk.

BP response

During aerobic exercise, the heart accelerates blood circulation to meet the body's increased demand. This physiological response causes systolic BP to rise to some extent, followed by a gradual recovery after the workout. Meanwhile, diastolic BP typically remains unchanged or slightly decreases. These fluctuations in BP are referred to as BP response.

Post-workout BP response

+   Normal BP response: During exercise, the heart accelerates blood circulation to meet the body's increased demand. This physiological response causes BP to rise to some extent, followed by a gradual recovery after the workout.
+   Slow BP recovery: Failure of BP to return to resting levels within 6 minutes upon the end of a workout indicates slow BP recovery.
+   Hypertensive response: A systolic BP that rises to 190 mmHg or above during high-intensity exercise, or to 170 mmHg or above during low- and medium-intensity exercise, indicates a hypertensive response.
+   Hypotensive response: A systolic BP decrease by 10 mmHg or above during exercise indicates a hypotensive response.

## Exiting the Exercise and Blood Pressure Study

On the HUAWEI Research home screen, select Exercise and Blood Pressure Study, and touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/en-us_image_0000002003426669.png"/> in the upper right corner and then Leave study to exit the study.

You will no longer be able to use Exercise and Blood Pressure Study services. To use them again, you will need to rejoin the study.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   After you leave the Exercise and Blood Pressure Study, your relevant data will be permanently deleted and cannot be restored. To ensure data clearing on your wearable device, make sure it's connected to your phone.

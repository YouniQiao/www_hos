# Blood pressure, hypertension, and hypotension

1.  Blood pressureBlood pressure is created inside the arteries as the heart beats (contracts or relaxes) and pumps blood into the arteries. Systolic pressure refers to the highest pressure in the arteries when the heart contracts and pumps blood into the body. Diastolic pressure, on the other hand, represents the lowest pressure when the heart is at rest between beats and blood returns to the heart after circulating throughout the body. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/en-us_image_0000001231779485.png"/>
2.  HypertensionBased on the Chinese Guidelines for the Prevention and Treatment of Hypertension (revised version 2024): Hypertension can be diagnosed if systolic pressure is 140 mmHg or higher and/or diastolic pressure is 90 mmHg or higher for three measurements on different days without antihypertensive medication. Alternatively, if blood pressure readings remain below 140/90 mmHg while antihypertensive drugs are taken, the diagnosis of hypertension is confirmed. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/en-us_image_0000001229071023.png"/>
3.  HypotensionThere is no clear definition of hypotension, but it is generally diagnosed based on the following indicators: Systolic pressure (highest pressure) ≤ 90 mmHg Diastolic pressure (lowest pressure) ≤ 60 mmHg, systolic pressure (highest pressure) &lt; 140 mmHg The content above is for reference only and should not be used as a basis for medical diagnosis or treatment.

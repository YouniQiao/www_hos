# High Altitude Study

When you have a travel plan to a high-altitude area, the High Altitude Study app can comprehensively evaluate risks of altitude sickness for you, based on your measurement results and feelings, and provide related health guidance and suggestions.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The HUAWEI Research app is not available on iPhones, but it can be used on your wearable device independently.

## Starting the High Altitude Study

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > High Altitude Study, select I have read and agree, and tap Join.

## Measuring High Altitude Health

+   On your phone Open HUAWEI Research app, enter High Altitude Study, and tap Start. Follow the onscreen tutorial to complete a measurement on your wearable device. After the measurement is complete, the measurement result will be displayed on your phone screen.
+   On your wearable device Open the HUAWEI Research app and enter High Altitude Study. Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, select HUAWEI Research and then High Altitude Study, and tap Start. If you are measuring for the first time, tap Next to fill in the questionnaire and complete physiological data collection according to the measurement tutorial. After the measurement is complete, the measurement result will be displayed on the device screen. You can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/en-us_image_0000002358363533.png" width="24" height="24"/> to view the measurement result description.

## Viewing data

Open the HUAWEI Research app, and enter High Altitude Study to view your current and historical measurement data.

You can view the measurement result on your device. Swipe up on the screen to view the details and historical measurement results.

# Managing messages

When your wearable device is linked with Huawei Health and the Notifications feature is enabled, new messages pushed to the status bar of your phone will be synced to your wearable.

## Enabling the Notifications feature

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, tap Notifications, and toggle on Sync notifications from phone to watch.
2.  In the app list below, toggle on the switches for the apps you wish to receive notifications from.

## Push notifications settings

To receive notifications on the wearable based on the phone usage status, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, tap Notifications, and configure the notification settings.

+   Notify on both devices: Get reminders simultaneously on the watch and phone.
+   Notify on one device: Get notifications on only one device at a time. Your phone won't get notifications when your wearable is worn unless the phone screen is on.

+   Push notifications settings are unavailable when paired with an iPhone.

## Viewing and deleting unread messages

1.  When being worn, your wearable device will vibrate to notify you of new messages pushed from the status bar of your phone.
2.  Unread messages will be kept on your wearable. To view them, swipe up on the home screen to enter the message center.
3.  To delete a message, do as follows: Swipe left on a message that you wish to delete, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/en-us_image_0000002389118309.png"/> to delete it. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/en-us_image_0000002548191753.png"/> at the bottom of the message list to clear all messages.

## Replying to SMS messages

1.  When receiving an SMS message on your wearable, you can swipe up on the screen and select a quick reply.
2.  To customize quick replies, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and tap Messages.
3.  You can tap Add reply to add a reply, tap an existing reply to edit it, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/en-us_image_0000002355438280.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to a reply to delete it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FG/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is unavailable when your wearable is paired with an iPhone.
+   Quick SMS replies only work with the SIM card that has received the SMS message.

# Starting a workout

Your watch supports multiple workout modes, including exercises such as running and biking, fitness courses, equipment training, and other activities like leisure sports. You can select a workout mode based on your needs.

## Starting a workout on your watch

1.  Enter the device app list and tap Workout.
2.  Swipe up or down on the screen and select a desired workout mode. You can also swipe up until you reach the end of the screen, and tap Add to add other workout modes.
3.  Tap the workout icon to start a workout session.
4.  During a workout, you can press and hold the Up button to end the exercise. Alternatively, press the Up button to pause, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002167346396.png" width="24" height="24"/> to resume, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002349229442.png" width="24" height="24"/> to end the workout.During a workout, you can swipe up or down on the screen to view fitness data, and swipe right to quickly access the shortcut settings screen. From there, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002551443975.png" width="24" height="24"/> to adjust the volume of workout reminders or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002551563967.png" width="24" height="24"/> to access the workout settings screen.

## Starting a workout in the Huawei Health app

1.  Double-check that your watch is linked with Huawei Health, and wear your device snugly on your wrist. Then in Huawei Health, for example, go to Exercise > Outdoor run and tap the running icon to start your run.
2.  During a workout, the fitness data will be displayed on both the Huawei Health app and your watch. If the workout distance or duration is too short, the record won't be saved.
3.  During a workout, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002382949909.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Huawei Health screen to pause, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002349389262.png" width="24" height="24"/> to resume, or hold down on <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002349229510.png" width="24" height="24"/> to end the workout. Your wearable device will pause, resume, or end the workout accordingly.

## Viewing workout records and workout status

After a workout, you can view your workout records and workout status as follows:

1.  Enter the device app list and tap Workout records.
2.  Select a record and view the corresponding details. (You'll see different types of data for different workout modes. Actual display shall prevail.)

Viewing workout records in Huawei Health

On the Huawei Health home screen, tap Exercise records to view your workout details. (You'll see different types of data for different workout modes. Actual display shall prevail.)

When you start a workout using Huawei Health and sync it to your watch, the workout records will only be available in Huawei Health.

1.  Enter the device app list and tap Workout status.
2.  Swipe up on the screen to view various workout data, such as Running Ability Index, Training load, Training index, Recovery, VO2Max, and Predicted times. Depending on the workout mode, the data displayed varies. Actual display shall prevail.

1.  To use the AI workout analysis feature, enter the device app list and select Workout records. Swipe up to view More, then tap About, and select App description to enable AI interpretation service.
2.  Once enabled, you can swipe up on the Workout records home screen to access the AI Summary screen. There, you'll find detailed analysis results and personalized suggestions based on your workout data.

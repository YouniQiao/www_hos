# Cycling

During cycling, you can calculate the virtual power based on the cycling speed and grade, and record each flat sprint or grade climb with the virtual power, grade linkage, and statistics features.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Make sure that you have updated the Huawei Health app and your watch to their latest versions.

## Pairing with cycling accessories

Pairing for the first time

1.  Activate the accessory.
2.  On your watch, enter the app list, go to Settings > More connections > Health & fitness devices, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002542458449.png" width="24" height="24"/> to add a device.
3.  Go to the next step or tap Add manually to select the motion sensor, heart rate strap, speedometers/pedometers, bike trainer, or power meter. Then, find the target device, and follow the instructions to complete the pairing.

Disconnecting from/Reconnecting to a paired accessory

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Settings > More connections > Health & fitness devices.
2.  Tap the name of a connected accessory to access the device management screen, and tap Disconnect. Tap the name of a paired but currently disconnected accessory to access the device management screen, and tap Reconnect.

## Virtual power settings

The virtual power is estimated based on the real-time cycling speed, grade, your weight, and more. After cycling, you can view the average virtual power of this session in your exercise records to help you analyze your cycling performance.

Measurements are based on your weight and age. Therefore, before using this feature, make sure your information in Huawei Health is correct in Me > Profile.

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, and tap Workout.
2.  Swipe up or down on the screen to find and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002359805390.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to Outdoor cycle to access the settings screen.
3.  Go to Cycling management > Manage bikes to fill in the vehicle information (such as the vehicle model and weight).

## Importing a cycling route

Before your cycling session, import a compatible route file into Huawei Health from your phone or one of your past workout records, and sync it to your watch.

1.  Open Huawei Health, go to Me > My route > Import route, select a downloaded route, and save it to My route.
2.  Select the target route. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002592999986.png"/> in the upper right corner, then choose Send to wearable to sync the route to your watch.

## Starting a cycling session

Starting a cycling session on your watch auto-links with your phone, for enriched real-time data display on both devices.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Before using this feature, make sure that Display on phone is enabled. To check or enable this features, tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002657455415.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon next to Outdoor cycle, and toggle Display on phone on.
+   To ensure real-time cycling data display on your phone, the two devices must remain connected over Bluetooth throughout the workout.
+   Cadence data will only be displayed on your phone when your watch is paired with a third-party cadence sensor.

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, and tap Workout.
2.  Swipe up or down on the screen to find and select Outdoor cycle or Indoor cycle.
3.  When using Outdoor cycle, you can customize your session directly from the preparation screen. Simply swipe up or down on the right side of the screen to select your vehicle type. Swipe up to access the More screen, select Routes, choose your route, and begin your workout.
4.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002521273486.png"/> to start the workout, and your cycling data will be displayed on your phone in real time. You can customize the displayed data type on the phone to suit your needs.

## Viewing cycling data

+   Heart rate straps: Connect a strap to your watch, start your workout, and view the heart rate strap's collected data on your watch. Such data is only available when you are exercising, and not supported for pool swims or open water swims. When you are not exercising, the watch's collected data will display.
+   Speedometers, pedometers, bike trainers, and power meters: Enter the app list, select Workout, tap Outdoor cycle or Indoor cycle, and swipe up or down on the screen to view cycling data.
+   If no power meter or cadence sensor is connected, go to Huawei Health > Exercise records after cycling, to view the virtual power, grade linkage, virtual cadence, and more.

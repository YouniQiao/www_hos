# Pairing with a new phone

1.  Enter the app list and go to Settings > System & updates > Disconnect. If the current connected phone is an iPhone, go to your phone Settings and select Bluetooth to find your wearable device. Tap the i icon and set to forget the device.
2.  Tap Disconnect to disconnect from the connected phone.
3.  When your wearable displays the pairing screen, follow the onscreen instructions to pair with a new phone.

# Pairing with HUAWEI Vision

After connecting your watch to a HUAWEI Vision, you can monitor your exercise data (such as heart rate, step count, and calories) in real time on the Vision, gaining a clear overview of your exercise.

1.  Link your watch to the Huawei Health app on your phone.
2.  Log in to the HUAWEI Vision and your phone using the same HUAWEI ID, for the Vision to automatically sync the wearables linked with your HUAWEI ID.
3.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Health > Me > Devices, and tap your watch.
4.  Tap Connect and follow the onscreen instructions to add your watch.
5.  In the pairing prompt displayed on your watch, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000001992555909.png" width="24" height="24"/> to complete the pairing.

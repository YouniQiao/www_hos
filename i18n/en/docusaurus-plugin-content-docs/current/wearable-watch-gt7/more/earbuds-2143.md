# Bluetooth earphone/speaker connection

You can use your Bluetooth earphones/speaker for music and audio listening after connecting the device to your watch.

## Via watch system Settings

1.  Link your watch with the Huawei Health app, and set your Bluetooth earphones/speaker to pairing mode.
2.  On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Settings > Bluetooth.
3.  Select the target Bluetooth earphones/speaker, and initiate the connection as prompted.

## Via the watch-based Music app

1.  Link your watch with the Huawei Health app, and set your Bluetooth earphones/speaker to pairing mode.
2.  On your watch, enter the app list, tap Music, and go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002432867644.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Pair on the playback screen.
3.  Select the target Bluetooth earphones/speaker, and initiate the connection as prompted.

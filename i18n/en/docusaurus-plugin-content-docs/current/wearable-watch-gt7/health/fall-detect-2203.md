# Fall detection and emergency SOS

After fall detection is enabled, the watch will detect your posture through the acceleration sensor on the watch. If the watch detects that you have fallen and may fall into a disabled state, it will enable the emergency SOS feature for you.

To disable fall detection, enter the watch's app list, go to Settings > Emergency SOS, and toggle off Fall detection.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   When your watch is paired with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, update your watch and Huawei Health to their latest versions.
+   Your watch cannot detect all falls. It can sometimes misinterpret high-impact activities as falls and trigger fall detection.
+   If your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth and your phone has two SIM cards, make sure to set in advance the default card for making emergency calls.
+   When paired with an iPhone, your watch cannot send help messages.

## Setting emergency information and emergency contacts

Emergency information can help first responders in an emergency. To set emergency information and emergency contacts, do as follows:

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and tap General settings > Emergency SOS.
2.  Tap Emergency information > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002533847339.png" width="24" height="24"/> to set information such as your address and blood type.
3.  Tap Emergency contacts. On the emergency contacts editing screen, you can add or delete contacts, and hold down and drag <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002533850053.png" width="24" height="24"/> to adjust the contact sequence. You can also open Huawei Health and tap Me > Profile > Emergency information to set your personal information. Make sure you set your emergency contacts in the correct order. If emergency SOS is triggered, your watch will call your first emergency contact and send help messages to all your emergency contacts. You can add up to three emergency contacts.

## Viewing emergency information

On your watch

Enter the watch's app list, and go to Settings > Emergency SOS to view your emergency information.

On your phone

Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and go to General settings > Emergency SOS > Emergency information to view your emergency information.

## Fall detection and auto-SOS

1.  Enter the watch's app list, go to Settings > Emergency SOS, and toggle on Fall detection.
2.  If the watch detects that you have fallen and may fall into a disabled state, it displays a pop-up, asking you whether to make emergency calls.
3.  If you choose <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002533806181.png" width="24" height="24"/> or don't perform any operations on the watch within 60 seconds, the watch triggers emergency SOS. If emergency contacts are set, the watch will automatically call the first emergency contact and send a help message with your current location to all emergency contacts. (If the satellite signal is weak, the message won't include your location information.) If the first emergency contact does not go through, you can select another emergency contact manually on your watch. If no emergency contact has been set, public emergency call numbers will be displayed for you to select.

## Manual emergency SOS initiation

+   If emergency contacts are set, the watch will automatically call the first emergency contact and send a help message with your current location to all emergency contacts. (If the satellite signal is weak, the message won't include your location information.) If the first emergency contact does not go through, you can select another emergency contact manually on your watch.
+   If no emergency contact has been set, public emergency call numbers will be displayed for you to select.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If Fall detection is disabled, your watch won't initiate an emergency SOS upon detecting a major fall. In this case, press the Up button five times in quick succession.

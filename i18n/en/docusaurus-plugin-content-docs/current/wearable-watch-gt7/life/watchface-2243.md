# Pet-themed watch faces

When the style is set to Emotions, the pet-themed watch face features a dynamic background and animation that changes based on your latest emotional state. The watch face adapts to reflect whether you're feeling Neutral (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002468699402.png" width="24" height="24"/>), Pleasant (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002468699406.png" width="24" height="24"/>), or Unpleasant (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FW/en-US/en-us_image_0000002501619399.png" width="24" height="24"/>). The watch face also supports 12 distinct emotional expressions, which are calculated based on both the emotional state and the stress state (such as Upset, Peaceful, and Satisfied).

1.  Touch and hold anywhere on the watch face to visit the watch face selection screen, and swipe left or right to select your desired pet-themed watch face.
2.  Tap Custom below the watch face and swipe left or right to choose the style, color, and features you like. On the Style or Color screen, swipe up or down to select your favorite style or color. If the style is set to Emotions, the watch face features a dynamic color that changes based on your emotional state. This style does not support color customization. If the style is set to Color, swipe up or down on the Color screen to choose your favorite color. On the Function screen, tap a component to replace it.

You can also open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to My faces > Fun to select more pet-themed watch faces.

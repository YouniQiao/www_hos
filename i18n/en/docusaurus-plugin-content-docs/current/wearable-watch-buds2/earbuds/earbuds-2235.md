# Earbuds control card

Once the earbuds are properly connected, the earbuds control card will appear on the Assistant·TODAY screen when you wear them, allowing intuitive and seamless control of your earbuds.

1.  Link your watch with Huawei Health and make sure that the watch is connected to the earbuds.
2.  After wearing your earbuds, swipe right on the watch home screen to access the Assistant·TODAY screen.
3.  Tap the earbuds control card to view the battery level, adjust the volume, and switch between noise cancellation modes with ease. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002503529037.png"/> The figure is for reference only. The actual product prevails.
4.  Tap on the earbuds control card to open the earbuds management screen for further settings.

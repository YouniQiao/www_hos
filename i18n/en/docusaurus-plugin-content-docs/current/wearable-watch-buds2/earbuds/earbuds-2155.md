# Managing your earbuds

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The following guidance applies when the watch cover is open or the earbuds are removed. (The cover opens with a gentle push on the cover-opening button.)

## Managing the earbuds from your phone

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and tap Earbuds.
2.  On this screen you can: Choose from Noise canceling, Off, or Awareness mode. Tap Sound quality & effects to choose from Default, Bass boost, Treble boost, and Voices. Tap Morning briefing. Once Morning briefing is enabled, your earbuds will automatically play selected content after 5 seconds of inactivity the first time you wear them each morning. Tap Quick play. Once Quick play is enabled, you can play the selected audio by simply triple-tapping the earbuds, without needing to unlock your phone. Tap Gestures to set gesture controls for the earbuds, such as double-tapping to answer/end a call. Tap Tip fit test to find out how well the ear tips fit your ears. Tap Settings to enable or disable Auto-switch left & right and Wear detection.

## Managing the earbuds from your watch

1.  Swipe down on the watch home screen to access the Control Panel, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000001344753494.png"/> to access the earbuds management screen.
2.  If you swipe up, you can: Choose from Noise canceling, Off, or Awareness mode. Tap EQ effects, then swipe up or down on the screen to select a sound effect (such as Treble boost). Tap More settings to enable or disable Auto-switch L/R, reset the earbuds, or check the system version of the earbuds.

# Connecting your earbuds to a new device

By default, both the earbuds and watch are connected to the same phone. You can effortlessly connect the earbuds to a new Bluetooth device, such as a phone or tablet.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Before you proceed, update your watch and earbuds to their latest versions.
+   When you disconnect the earbuds from a new Bluetooth device (such as a phone or tablet), the earbuds will not be able to automatically reconnect to it.

1.  Put the earbuds in Pairing mode. If the earbuds are inside the watch, gently push the cover-opening button. When the cover pops open, press and hold the side button for 2 seconds. If you have taken the earbuds out of the watch, swipe down on the watch home screen to access the Control Panel, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002500143925.png"/> to access the earbuds management screen, and tap More > Pair new device.
2.  Go to the new device's Bluetooth screen.Go to Settings > Bluetooth, select the Bluetooth name of the earbuds from the discovered devices, and complete the pairing.

# Finding your earbuds

## Using your phone to find your earbuds

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to Find device > Find.
2.  If the earbuds are connected to your phone, tap Ring left bud or Ring right bud to locate them. If the earbuds are out of range, you can tap Locate on map to find them. If you are unable to connect your earbuds to your phone, open the Find Device app on your phone, tap Devices to see the list of devices, and select the earbuds to view their last known connection location. (The location data is retained for up to seven days.) The location information will be updated when the earbuds are connected or disconnected again. If both earbuds are in the same location, their icons will appear together with the location indicated. If they are in different locations, each earbud icon will display separately, showing its respective location.

## Using your watch to find your earbuds

1.  Swipe down on the watch home screen to access the Control Panel, and go to Find Device > Earbuds. If both earbuds are connected to your watch, tap Ring left bud or Ring right bud to locate them. If an earbud is disconnected, tap Reconnect first. Once reconnected, tap Ring left bud or Ring right bud to locate the specific earbud.
2.  Once located, tap Stop ringing or wait until the ringing ends.

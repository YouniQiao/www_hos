# Using gestures to control your earbuds

You can control your earbuds with simple gestures. For example, a quick double-tap allows you to answer or end calls, or play, pause, or switch between songs when you're not on a call.

## Setting earbud gestures (for phones running HarmonyOS 4.3 or earlier)

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to Earbuds > Gestures.
2.  Tap Double-tap or Triple-tap to set gesture controls.

## Setting earbud gestures (for phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later)

1.  Open Huawei Health, tap Devices, and go to Earbuds > Gestures.
2.  Tap Double-tap or Triple-tap to set gesture controls.

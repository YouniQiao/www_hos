# Factory reset

## Restoring your watch to its factory settings

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Once your watch is restored to its factory settings, all your personal data will be cleared, transportation card migration will be triggered (if the transportation cards support migration), and access cards and bank cards will be removed. Please exercise caution before proceeding.

Method 1: Enter the watch's app list, and go to Settings > System & updates > Reset.

Method 2: Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and tap Restore factory settings.

## Resetting the earbuds

1.  Swipe down on the watch home screen to access the Control Panel, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000001407007506.png"/> to access the earbuds management screen.
2.  Go to More settings > Reset earbuds, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002465085158.png" width="24" height="24"/>.

# Taking out, wearing, and putting back your earbuds

## Taking out the earbuds

1.  Gently push the button which opens the cover as shown in the diagram below.
2.  When the cover pops open, lift it to open the cover fully.
3.  Take out the earbuds, wear them, and close the cover.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002476586452.png"/>

+   The figure is for reference only. The actual product prevails.

## Wearing the earbuds

Insert an earbud into your ear canal and gently rotate the tail of the earbud until it is sitting comfortably.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000001401836972.png"/>

## Putting the earbuds back

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Please do not put the earbuds directly into the charging compartments as you may damage the charging terminals.

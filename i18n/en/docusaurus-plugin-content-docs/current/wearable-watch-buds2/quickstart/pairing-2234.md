# Pairing with an iPhone

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Only available for iOS 13.0 and later.

## Preparations

1.  Install the HUAWEI Health app (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464972260.png" width="24" height="24"/>).If you have already installed the app, update it to its latest version. If you haven't yet installed the app, go to your phone's App Store and search for HUAWEI Health to download and install the app.
2.  Go to HUAWEI Health > Me > Log in with HUAWEI ID. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, please register for one, and then log in. It is recommended that an adult account be used for login, to ensure successful pairing and connection between your wearable device and your phone. Keep the cover closed and the earbuds in the device until pairing is finished.
3.  Power on your wearable device, select a language, and place the device close to your phone.

## Pairing and connection

Connect via Add device:

1.  Access the home screen of HUAWEI Health, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002497971673.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper rightcorner and then Add device. Find your wearable device in the search result and tap Connect.
2.  When a pairing request appears on your wearable device, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464812644.png" width="24" height="24"/>.When a pop-up window appears in HUAWEI Health requesting a Bluetooth pairing, tap Pair. Another pop-up window will appear, asking you whether to allow the wearable device to display notifications from your phone. Tap Allow and follow the onscreen instructions.
3.  Wait for a few seconds until HUAWEI Health displays a message indicating a successful pairing.
4.  Once your watch has been successfully paired with your phone, the earbuds will automatically start pairing with your phone.When a pop-up window appears in the HUAWEI Health app requesting a Bluetooth pairing, tap Pair. When another pop-up window appears indicating a successful pairing, tap Done.

Connect via Scan:

1.  Access the home screen of HUAWEI Health, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464812900.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper rightcorner and then Scan.
2.  When a pairing request appears on your wearable device, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464972520.png" width="24" height="24"/>.When a pop-up window appears in HUAWEI Health requesting a Bluetooth pairing, tap Pair. Another pop-up window will appear, asking you whether to allow the wearable device to display notifications from your phone. Tap Allow and follow the onscreen instructions.
3.  Wait for a few seconds until HUAWEI Health displays a message indicating a successful pairing.
4.  Once your watch has been successfully paired with your phone, the earbuds will automatically start pairing with your phone.When a pop-up window appears in the HUAWEI Health app requesting a Bluetooth pairing, tap Pair. When another pop-up window appears indicating a successful pairing, tap Done.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Keep HUAWEI Health running in the background (and don't swipe up to close the app), to ensure a more stable connection between your device and HUAWEI Health. This will help enable a better experience with push notifications of new messages and incoming calls from your phone.
+   To ensure an optimal experience with push notifications, check that the notifications settings on your phone are correct.

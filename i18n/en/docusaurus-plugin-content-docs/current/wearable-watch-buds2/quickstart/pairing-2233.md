# Pairing with an HarmonyOS/Android phone

## Preparations

1.  Install the Huawei Health app (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464805488.png" width="24" height="24"/>).If you have already installed the app, update it to its latest version.
2.  On your phone, go to Settings > Log in to HUAWEI ID. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, please register for one, and then log in. It is recommended that an adult account be used for login, to ensure successful pairing and connection between your wearable device and your phone. Keep the cover closed and the earbuds in the device until pairing is finished.
3.  Power on your wearable device, select a language, and start pairing.

## Pairing and connection

Connect via Add device:

1.  Place your wearable device close to your phone. Access the home screen of Huawei Health, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464970380.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper rightcorner and then Add device. Find your wearable device in the search result and tap Connect.
2.  When a pairing request displays on your wearable device, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464965144.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions on your phone to complete the pairing.
3.  Once your wearable device has been successfully paired with your phone, the earbuds will automatically start pairing with your phone.

Connect via Scan:

1.  Place your wearable device close to your phone. Go to Huawei Health > Devices, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002498084493.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner and then Scan, to scan the QR code displayed on the wearable device.
2.  When a pairing request displays on your wearable device, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002464805492.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions on your phone to complete the pairing.
3.  Once your wearable device has been successfully paired with your phone, the earbuds will automatically start pairing with your phone.

# Heart Study

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The HUAWEI Research app is not available on iPhones, but it can be used on your wearable device independently.
+   Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you experience any discomfort, please seek medical assistance.

## Arrhythmia detection

On your phone:

1.  Keep your device connected to the HUAWEI Research app. On the home screen of the app, go to Heart Study > Detection.
2.  Tap Start and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the measurement.
3.  Once the measurement is complete, view the results in the HUAWEI Research app.

On your watch:

1.  In the device's app list, tap HUAWEI Research > Heart Study, join the study, tap Start, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the measurement.
2.  Once the measurement is complete, you will be able to view the result in Heart Study on your phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   It is recommended that you check for arrhythmia twice a day for two weeks non-stop or once every two days for three months, to ensure optimal accuracy.
+   Make sure that the rear cover of your device is not blocked by any foreign objects or covered with grime.

## Enabling smart real-time measurement

Once enabled, your device will automatically evaluate your heart health when you are resting. You can swipe down on the Huawei Health home screen to sync data from the wearable device, when it's connected to your phone.

1.  On your phone, go to HUAWEI Research > Heart Study.
2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002498917249.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner, then select Privacy management, and enable Smart real-time measurement.
3.  Check your results by going to HUAWEI Research > Heart Study > Measure on your phone.

## A-fib analysis

Once enabled, your watch will automatically assess your A-fib load when you are resting. Data will be synced from the wearable device to your phone when it's connected to your phone.

1.  Open the HUAWEI Research app, and go to Heart Study > Measure.
2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002453524452.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner, then select Privacy management, and enable A-fib analysis.
3.  Check your results by going to HUAWEI Research > Heart Study > Measure on your phone.If the result shows "&lt;=2%", this represents a fixed value, meaning the risk of A-fib is extremely low or within a normal range.

## A-fib risk detection

1.  Open the HUAWEI Research app, and go to Heart Study > Prediction.
2.  Tap A-fib risk detection and follow the onscreen instructions to join the A-fib risk prediction study.
3.  Check your results by going to Heart Study > Prediction on your phone.

From there, you can view the results and risk trends to take any necessary steps to mitigate the risks.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is useful for individuals who have tested positive for arrhythmia. If you are feeling uncomfortable, be sure to consult with a doctor as soon as possible.
+   It is recommended that you perform this atrial fibrillation detection at least three times a day, and enable automatic measurements to ensure that there is sufficient data.

## Leaving the Heart Study

On the HUAWEI Research home screen, select Heart Study, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002358283717.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner and then Leave study.

You can re-join the study at any time you desire.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   After you leave the heart study, your heart study data will be permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

# Sounds & vibration

You can adjust the settings to suit your needs.

## Ringtone volume settings

1.  Enter the device app list and go to Settings > Sounds & vibration > Ringtone.
2.  Swipe up or down to adjust the volume.

## Vibration strength settings

1.  Enter the device app list and go to Settings > Sounds & vibration > Vibrations > Vibration.
2.  Select your desired vibration strength: Strong, Soft, or Off.

## Custom vibration settings

You can customize the vibration strength for features like alarms. Here's how to do it using alarms as an example:

1.  Enter the device app list and go to Settings > Sounds & vibration > Vibrations > Custom.
2.  Select Alarm, and choose between Crescendo, Continuous, or Pulse to match your preferences.

# Updates

## On your wearable device

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Updates are not supported on the wearable device when paired with an iPhone. Earbud updates can be performed on the HUAWEI WATCH Buds 2.

1.  Enter the device app list and go to Settings > System & updates > Software updates.
2.  Tap the update icon and follow the onscreen instructions to download the package and update your device.

## On your phone

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and tap Firmware update.On the Firmware update screen, you can tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002502902197.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the upper rightcorner and toggle on Auto-download over WLAN and Auto-install updates and restart the device when it's not in use, to have new versions automatically pushed to your wearable device for updates. For iPhones, you can go to the Firmware update screen, tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002505099895.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the upper rightcorner, and toggle on Auto-download updates via WLAN and Auto-install updates and restart the device when it's not in use.
2.  Your phone will check for any available updates. Then follow the onscreen instructions to update your wearable device.

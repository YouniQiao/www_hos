# Making and answering calls

## Syncing contacts

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If the contacts are not automatically synced, open the app list on your watch, and go to Contacts > Sync contacts > Sync.
+   If you do not want to sync contacts, open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, go to Device settings > App management > Contacts, and disable Sync data to watch.This option is not available in the HUAWEI Health app on iPhones. iOS 18 supports contact authorization. You can go to Settings > App > HUAWEI Health > Contacts, and sync contacts as required.

## If the earbuds have been connected to the watch, and they have both been connected to the same phone

Answering/Rejecting calls

+   If the earbuds are in the watch when you receive a call, the watch will prompt you to wear the earbuds. Once you are wearing the earbuds, the watch will automatically answer the call.
+   When wearing the earbuds and receiving a call, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002498851701.png" width="24" height="24"/> to answer, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002465739756.png" width="24" height="24"/> to reject, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002465899360.png" width="24" height="24"/> to reject with a message.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   During a call, if you remove the earbuds, put them back in the earbud compartments and close the cover, the call will automatically switch to your phone.
+   You can only reject calls and reply with an SMS message when your watch is paired with certain phone models. This feature is not available if <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002498856801.png" width="24" height="24"/> is not displayed on the watch when you receive a call.

Making calls

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, select Phone, and choose any of the following methods to make a call. Tap Call log and then a call log to call back. Tap Contacts and then a contact to make a call. Tap Keypad, enter the number you want to call, and tap the Dial icon to initiate a call.
2.  If the earbuds are currently worn, the call screen will appear immediately. If you are not wearing them, you need to put them on first.

## If the earbuds have been connected to the watch, but the watch is connected to phone A, and the earbuds are connected to phone B

Answering/Rejecting calls

+   If the earbuds are in the watch The watch will not respond when there is an incoming call on phone B. When there is an incoming call on phone A, the watch will prompt you to wear the earbuds to answer the call. Once you are wearing the earbuds, the watch will automatically answer the call, and the call on phone A will be heard through the earbuds.
+   If you are wearing the earbuds The watch will display the incoming call on phone B. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002498851705.png" width="24" height="24"/> to reject the call, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002465892322.png" width="24" height="24"/> to answer the call. The watch will display the incoming call on phone A. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002465732702.png" width="24" height="24"/> to reject the call, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002498931665.png" width="24" height="24"/> to first connect the earbuds to phone A and then answer the call. If the earbuds are unable to reconnect to phone A for some reason when an incoming call is received on the phone, you will need to answer the call directly on the phone.

Making calls

1.  Put on your earbuds. On your watch, enter the app list, select Phone, and choose any of the following methods to make a call. Tap Call log and then a call log to call back. Tap Contacts and then a contact to make a call. Tap Keypad, enter the number you want to call, and tap the Dial icon to initiate a call.
2.  The earbuds will connect to phone A, and then automatically make the call.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   When the call ends, the earbuds will remain connected to phone A. They will not automatically reconnect to phone B.
+   To reconnect the earbuds to phone B, swipe down on the watch home screen to access the Control Panel, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002498851709.png"/> to access the earbuds management screen, and tap phone B.

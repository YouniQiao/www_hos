# Controlling music playback

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, make sure that your device is connected to the Huawei Health app.
+   Your watch does not have a speaker, so you need to wear the earbuds to listen to music.

## Adding music to your watch

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Before syncing music to your watch, make sure that the tracks have already been downloaded to your phone from a music app and are in a format supported by your watch.

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to Music > Manage music > Add songs.iPhones: Open HUAWEI Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to Music > Import from phone.
2.  Select the tracks to be added and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002466238380.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner of the screen.
3.  Access the music management screen, tap New playlist, create a name for the playlist, and add tracks to suit your desires. You can also categorize tracks that have been uploaded to your watch for easier management. You can update an existing playlist at any time by tapping <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002466078764.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper rightcorner of the playlist screen and then Add songs.
4.  Music files synced to your watch can be played even without an internet connection.

To delete an added track, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to Music > Manage music. In the music list, tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002499382529.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon next to the track, and select Delete.

## If the earbuds have been connected to the watch, and they have both been connected to the same phone

+   Controlling music playback on the phone On your phone, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, tap Music, and toggle on Control phone music. This setting is not required if you're using an iPhone. Open the music app on your phone and play music. On your watch, swipe right on the home screen to enter the Assistant·TODAY screen, and tap the Music card. From there you can pause playback, switch between tracks, and adjust the volume.
+   Controlling music playback on the watch On your watch, enter the app list, and tap Music (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002466508730.png" width="24" height="24"/>). Alternatively, open another music app, and tap Playing. Tap the play button to play music. On the music playback screen, you can adjust the volume and set the playback sequence (such as Play in order and Shuffle). You can swipe up on the screen to view the playlist and switch between tracks.

## If the earbuds have been connected to the watch, but the watch is connected to phone A, and the earbuds are connected to phone B

+   Controlling music playback on phone A/BOn the Music card on the Assistant·TODAY screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002466574630.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner, and choose between phone A or phone B. Once selected, you can play, pause, skip to the previous or next track, and adjust the volume level of the music on the phone. <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F1/en-US/en-us_image_0000002470813982.png"/> The figure is for reference only. The actual product prevails.
+   Controlling music playback on the watchSwipe left on your watch home screen to go to the music playback screen, tap the phone icon in the upper right corner, select your watch, and tap the play button.

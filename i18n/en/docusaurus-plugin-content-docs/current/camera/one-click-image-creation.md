# One-Tap Movie: Turn Photos into Dynamic Videos

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/video/10044584_f010_InstantMovie.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Use One-Tap Movie to quickly turn your favorite photos into videos with background music and motion effects. You can also use AI Motion Effects to make people and scenery in still photos come to life.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Gallery, and on the Photos tab or in an album, tap Multi-select, select the photos, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_creat.png" width="24" height="24"/> > One-Tap Movie.
2.  Select a theme and duration. When the photos show a single person, plants, scenery, or similar content, AI Motion Effects is supported. Select the AI Motion Effects option and tap Generate.
3.  After generation, check the default template effect. You can also choose other templates with the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_instantmovie.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the lower left corner.
4.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24"/> to save the video, then view it in Gallery > Albums > Video Editing.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After you select the AI Motion Effects option, photos that support this effect will display the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_instantmovie.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon. Some photos may not support this effect due to the shooting angle. You can try replacing them with other photos.

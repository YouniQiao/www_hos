# Fine-tune Your Photo at Ease with Expression

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044584_f006_BestExpression.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Any unnatural expressions in your group photo? Use the Expression feature to reshoot expressions, and AI retouch will use them to generate a new photo that everyone likes.

**Try it out**

1.  Select a photo from **Gallery**, go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_edit.png" width="24" height="24" alt="编辑图标"/> > AI retouch > Expressions. After a face in the image is recognized, tap the face recognition box.

2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_retake.png" width="24" height="24"/>, take a new expression, and tap **Replace**. The new expression will then be used in the photo.

3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24"/> to save the photo.

**Note**

If you've taken burst photos, tap the face recognition box in one photo, select a proper expression from the rest photos, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24" alt="保存图标"/> to save the photo.

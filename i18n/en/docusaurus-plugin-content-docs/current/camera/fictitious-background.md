# Portrait Photography: Blur the Background

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/figure/20005879_f001_CameraPortrait.png" width="360" height="486"/>


When taking photos in Portrait mode, you can adjust the aperture to blur the background and make the subject stand out.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Open Camera, select Portrait, and touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_aperture_adjust_reverse.png" width="24" height="24"/> to adjust the aperture:

+   Touch Virtual aperture and drag the slider to adjust the virtual aperture value. The smaller the value, the more blurred the background.

+   Touch Physical aperture and select a lens aperture to achieve a finely detailed, physical-level blur effect.


Once you've finished adjusting, touch the shutter button to take a photo.

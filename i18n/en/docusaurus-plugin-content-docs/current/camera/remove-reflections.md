# Smart Reflection Removal

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202504042smqrg/zh-cn/image/video/20005895_f003_De.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

When you shoot a scene through a window, reflections often show up in the photo. Use Reflection Removal to intelligently detect and remove them, leaving your photo cleaner and sharper.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  In Gallery, select the photo you want to edit, then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202504042smqrg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/> > AI Retouch > Reflection Removal.

2.  Tap One-tap removal, then once it's generated, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202504042smqrg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen prompts to save the photo.

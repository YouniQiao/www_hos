# Refine Portrait Photos for a More Atmospheric Look

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/video/10044584_f007_Refine.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Ever edited a portrait only to find the lighting looks unnatural and the photo lacks texture? Let AI refine it for you in a single step. It intelligently analyzes the scene and optimizes the lighting, background, and color tones, giving your portrait a fresh new look.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  In Gallery, select the portrait photo you want to refine, then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/> > AI Retouch > Portrait Refine > One-tap Refine.
2.  Once the recommended result is generated, you can adjust the facial lighting, bokeh, color tones, and other effects to your liking, then tap Regenerate when you're done.
3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions to save the photo.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.
+   Use a clear portrait photo, and avoid cases where the face is too small or overexposed.
+   If the system detects that the photo's composition can be optimized, it will optimize it automatically and a composition button will appear. Tap it to restore the original composition.

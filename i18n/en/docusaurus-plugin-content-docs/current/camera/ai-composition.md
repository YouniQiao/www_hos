# Optimize Camera Angle with AI Composition

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/20005877_f006_AI_composition.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Use AI composition to take greater portrait, building, landscape, and food shots. Once a recommendable composition is identified, your phone will guide you to optimize your camera angle.

**Try it out**

1.  Open **Camera** and select **Photo**, **Portrait**, or **Night** mode.

2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_AI_composition.png" width="24" height="24" alt="AI辅助构图图标"/> at the top of the viewfinder or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_up.png" width="24" height="24" alt="展开图标"/> to display the Camera Quick Menu, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_AI_composition.png" width="24" height="24" alt="AI辅助构图图标"/> to enable AI composition.

3.  Aim towards what you're shooting and keep the focus length between 1x and 10x. Then, perform the following operations as prompted. For example:

    +   When your phone detects a recommended position adjustment: The viewfinder will display prompts to move your phone forward, backward, up, or down. Adjust the device's position according to the prompts to align the frames.

    +   When your phone detects a recommended viewpoint for the scene: A circle and a dot marking the recommended area to focus on will appear in the viewfinder. Move your phone to align the dot with the circle. Wait for the camera to automatically adjust the focus.

4.  After the adjustment is complete, tap the shutter to take a photo.

**Note**

+   AI composition may not be able to recommend a camera angle if the background is cluttered or no better composition is identified.

+   The supported focus length may vary depending on the device model.

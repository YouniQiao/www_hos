# AI-assisted composition, recommended shooting angles

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/video/20005877_f006_AI_composition.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

When shooting people, buildings, natural scenery, and other subjects, you can enable AI-assisted composition. When your phone detects a framing area or angle worth recommending, it will guide you to adjust your shot, helping you discover more great shooting perspectives.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Open Camera and select Photo, Portrait, or Night mode.

2.  Below the shutter button, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> to open the toolbox, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_AI_composition.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enable AI-assisted composition.

3.  Point the camera at your subject and proceed based on the subject:
    +   **People**: If a shooting angle is recommended, yellow and white circles will appear in the viewfinder. Adjust the phone's tilt angle as prompted until the two circles overlap. After adjusting the angle, if a framing area is recommended, a center ring and a recommended target point will appear in the viewfinder.
    +   **Scenery**: If a framing area is recommended, a center ring and a recommended target point will appear in the viewfinder.
4.  Move your phone so that the center ring overlaps the target point until the target point turns yellow. Hold your phone steady and still; the camera will then zoom automatically and adjust the composition.
5.  Once the adjustment is complete, tap the shutter button to take the photo.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   To use assisted composition when shooting scenery and portraits, it is recommended that you keep the camera focal length at 1x, 2x, or 4x, or 1x, 2x, or 3.7x (depending on your model). To get recommended angles when shooting portraits, keep the focal length at 1x.
+   If the background is cluttered or no better framing area is detected, AI-assisted composition may not make a recommendation. Please refer to the actual experience.

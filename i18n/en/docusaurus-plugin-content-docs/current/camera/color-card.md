# Custom color cards for more striking photos

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/video/20005877_f007_Custom_Styles.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Turn on Custom color cards to take photos with preset color cards, or fine-tune the colors yourself to make your shots more vibrant.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Open Camera, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the bottom of the screen to open the toolbox, tap XMAGE Styles, then swipe left to turn on Custom color cards. You can:

+   **Use a preset color card**: Swipe left or right in the viewfinder to switch between preset color cards. You can also tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_custom_styles_on.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of the screen to choose a preset card such as Natural, Film, Cinematic, or Anime.
+   **Customize a color card**: Swipe on the color wheel to adjust **color temperature** and **saturation**; swipe up or down on the light and shadow bar beside the color wheel to adjust **light and shadow.** When you're done, you can also tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/phone_ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Save as, enter a name for the color card, and save it for easy access next time.
+   **Turn off Custom color cards**: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_custom_styles_on.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of the screen and select Original.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

When using the front camera, tapping XMAGE Styles in the toolbox will turn on Custom color cards directly. To turn them off, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_custom_styles_on.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of the screen and select Off.

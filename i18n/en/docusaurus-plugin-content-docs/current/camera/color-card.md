# Stunning XMAGE Styles

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/figure/20005877_f007_Custom_Styles.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

When shooting photos, select a preset **XMAGE** style or adjust the color to your liking, for stunning results.

**Try it out**

Open **Camera**, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_up.png" width="24" height="24" alt="展开图标"/> at the bottom of the camera to display the Camera Quick Menu, tap **XMAGE**, and swipe left to enable **Custom styles**. From there, feel free to:

+   **Use a preset style**: Swipe left or right in the viewfinder or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_leica_colour.png" width="24" height="24" alt="XMAGE风格图标"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/common/buttons/ic_custom_styles_on.png" width="24" height="24" alt="个性风格图标"/> at the top of the screen to switch between preset styles.

+   **Customize a style**: After selecting a style, you can adjust the parameters and preview the effect in real time. Swipe on the palette to adjust **Temperature** and **Saturation**, or swipe up or down on the palette sidebar to adjust **Tone**. After the adjustment, you can go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/common/buttons/phone_ic_more.png" width="24" height="24" alt="更多图标"/> > Save as, customize the name, and save the name for future use.

**Note**

To disable styles, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_leica_colour.png" width="24" height="24" alt="XMAGE风格图标"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/common/buttons/ic_custom_styles_on.png" width="24" height="24" alt="个性风格图标"/> and select **Original**.

# AI Cloud Enhancement for Sharper Photos

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/video/10044586_f010_CloudCamera.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

AI Cloud Enhancement intelligently analyzes the photos you take to improve their clarity, making the subject stand out more and the details sharper.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before use, go to Gallery and make sure AI Cloud Enhancement is enabled under <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Settings.

You can take photos in modes such as Photo and Portrait, and then apply cloud enhancement.

Take shooting a landscape as an example:

1.  In sufficient light, open Camera, select Photo mode, zoom the rear camera to 10X, and tap the shutter button.
2.  In Gallery, select the photo and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_AIbeauty.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enhance it.

3.  Once done, you can view the enhanced photo in Gallery > Albums > AI Cloud Enhancement.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

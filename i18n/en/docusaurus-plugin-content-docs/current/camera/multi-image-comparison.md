# Compare multiple images to quickly pick your favorite

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/figure/10044586_f009_GalleryCompare.png" width="360" height="486"/>

With image comparison, you can display multiple similar images on the same screen, compare their differences, and quickly pick the ones you're happy with.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Gallery, and on the Photos tab or in an album, tap the multi-select icon in the upper right corner to enter selection mode.

2.  Select the images, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_compare.png" width="24" height="24"/>Compare in the lower left corner. The selected images will be displayed on the same screen.

3.  Spread or pinch with two fingers to zoom all images at the same time. You can also tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_comparison.png" width="24" height="24"/> to turn off syncing and zoom a single image on its own.

4.  After comparing the differences, you can delete or keep the images.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Some image formats are not supported for comparison. The actual behavior may vary.

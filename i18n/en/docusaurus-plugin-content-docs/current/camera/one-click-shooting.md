# One-Tap Burst or Video

Quickly enter Burst or Video mode to capture the best moments of moving subjects.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Camera > Photo, touch and hold the shutter button, then drag it to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508061ZsslO/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_mode.png" width="24" height="24"/> to start a burst, or drag it to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508061ZsslO/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_modes_video.png" width="24" height="24"/> to start recording. While dragging to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508061ZsslO/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_modes_video.png" width="24" height="24"/>, drag the shutter button toward or away from the screen to zoom quickly.

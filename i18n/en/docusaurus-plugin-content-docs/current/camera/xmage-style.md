# XMAGE Styles for More Striking Photos

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/video/20005877_f007_Custom_Styles.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

When taking photos, use XMAGE Styles to pick a preset color card you like, or fine-tune the colors yourself, so your shots come out richer and more vibrant.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Open Camera, touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> to open the toolbox, and touch XMAGE Styles. You can:

- **Use a preset color card**: Swipe left or right in the viewfinder, or touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_leica_colour.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_custom_styles_on.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of the screen to switch between preset color cards.

- **Customize a color card**: After selecting a color card, you can adjust parameters and preview the effect in real time. Swipe on the color wheel to adjust color temperature and saturation; swipe up or down on the light and shadow bar beside the color wheel to adjust light and shadow.

  Once you're done, touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/phone_ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Save as, give it a custom name, and save it for easy access next time.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ To turn off color cards, touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_leica_colour.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_custom_styles_on.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the top of the screen and select Original.

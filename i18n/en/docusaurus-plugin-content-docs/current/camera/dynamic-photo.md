# Dynamic photos, create more creative effects

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202510231Wd3vg/zh-cn/image/video/10044606_f028_LivePhoto.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


After taking a dynamic photo, you can create more creative dynamic effects, such as capturing the motion trail of a basketball shot or a running cat or dog, or generating an effect photo that highlights the subject and blurs the background.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Open Camera, and in modes such as Photo and Portrait, make sure Dynamic photo is enabled (displayed as <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202510231Wd3vg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_livephoto1.png" width="24" height="24"/>), then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202510231Wd3vg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_shutter_take_photo_live.png" width="24" height="24"/> to take a photo.
2.  In Gallery, open the dynamic photo and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202510231Wd3vg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_livephoto1.png" width="24" height="24"/>:
    +   When the subject of the photo is a moving person, cat, or dog, tap Motion trail to generate an effect photo with motion trail clones.
    +   When the subject of the photo is still and the background objects are moving (such as traffic), tap Hero moment to generate an effect photo with a blurred background.
3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202510231Wd3vg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_edit_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> to select a cover, adjust colors, and more.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.
+   When using Portrait mode, only the front camera supports taking dynamic photos.

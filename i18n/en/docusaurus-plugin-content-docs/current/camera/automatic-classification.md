# Portrait albums: automatic photo classification by person

Want to quickly find photos of a certain person in Gallery? Gallery automatically recognizes portrait photos and classifies them, making it easy to view and search.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Gallery > Albums, tap Portraits to view the generated portrait albums. You can also rename an album, change its cover, and more.

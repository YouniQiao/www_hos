# Get Multiple Motion Effects with One Shoot

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/20005877_f008_multipleeffects.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Tap the Shutter button once to generate photos with motion smearing and other motion effects in Gallery.

**Try it out**

1.  Open **Camera**, in **Photo** mode, make sure that Moving picture is enabled (with <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_livephoto1.png" width="24" height="24" alt="动态照片图标"/> displayed), and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_shutter_take_photo_live.png" width="24" height="24" alt="快门图标"/> to take a photo.

2.  Open **Gallery** and enable Moving picture. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_livephoto1.png" width="24" height="24" alt="动态照片图标"/> and select **Pan** to create a motion smearing effect.

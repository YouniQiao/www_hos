# Quickly Adjust Camera Settings

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/video/20005877_f002_CameraSetting.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


You can quickly adjust camera settings in the camera toolbox, such as changing the video resolution or turning on the level, to make shooting faster.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Open Camera, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the bottom of the camera screen, or swipe up near the shutter button to open the toolbox.
2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_settings.png" width="24" height="24"/> and adjust the settings as needed.

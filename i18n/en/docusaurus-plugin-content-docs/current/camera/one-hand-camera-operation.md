# Camera toolbox for easy one-handed operation

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202505221hzqiz/zh-cn/image/figure/10044606_f053_CameraUX.png" width="360" height="486"/>


With the camera toolbox, you can easily adjust camera settings with one hand.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Open Camera, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202505221hzqiz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_up.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the bottom of the screen, or swipe up near the shutter button to bring up the toolbox, then tap the setting you need with one hand.

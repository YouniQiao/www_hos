# All-New Zoom Ring for More Precise Zooming

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509050godTj/zh-cn/image/video/20005877_f001_ZoomRing.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

The all-new zoom ring mimics the design of professional DSLR cameras, delivering smoother control and more precise fixed-focal-length shooting.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

+   Tap a shortcut zoom factor to quickly switch focal lengths.

+   Touch and hold a shortcut zoom factor, then swipe left or right (in portrait mode) or up or down (in landscape mode) within the expanded zoom ring area to quickly adjust the focal length.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Not all camera modes support zooming, and the available zoom factors may vary by device model. The actual behavior on your device prevails.

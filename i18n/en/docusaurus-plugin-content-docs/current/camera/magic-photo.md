# Magic Move, create imaginative photos

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/video/10044584_f008_Move.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Is your photo too ordinary and lacking creativity? Use Magic Move to cut out the subject of a photo and move, rotate, scale, or copy it to easily create imaginative compositions that make your photos more artistic and visually appealing.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  In Gallery, select the photo you want to edit, then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/> > AI retouch > Magic Move.

2.  Select the object to move:
    +   Smart: Tap the object you want to move, or draw a line inside the object to complete the selection.

    +   Manual: Drag the slider to select the line thickness, then draw over the object you want to move to complete the selection. For better results, draw over the entire object in one stroke.

3.  Tap the selected object to move, rotate, scale, or copy it.
4.  Tap One-tap merge. After the new photo is generated, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions to save the photo.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

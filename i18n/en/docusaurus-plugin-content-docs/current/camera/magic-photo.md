# Move, for a Perfect Group Photo

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044584_f008_Move.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Missing someone in the family photo? With Move, quickly cut out a subject from another photo and intelligently paste the subject right into place. It automatically blends the lighting and shadows, creating a perfect group photo in just one tap.

**Try it out**

1.  In **Gallery**, tap the image you wish to edit, and go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/> > AI retouch > Move.

2.  Go to Stickers > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_add.png" width="24" height="24" alt="添加图标"/> and select the target photo. In **Auto** mode, draw a line inside the object to select the object you wish to cut out, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_comfirm.png" width="24" height="24" alt="确认图标"/>. The object will be automatically pasted to the recommended position. You can:

    +   Drag <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_move_tuiyi.png" width="24" height="24" alt="空间推移图标"/> to move the object in 3D space. The object will automatically adjust its size based on its position.

    +   Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_move_layer.png" width="24" height="24" alt="图层图标"/>, hold and drag a layer to adjust the sequence of the object's layers.

3.  Tap **Generate**, and save the generated image by tapping <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24" alt="保存图标"/> and following the onscreen instructions.

**Note**

To edit objects within the current photo, select the object you want to cut out using **Auto** or **Manual**. After the object is identified, you can move, rotate, scale, or copy it.

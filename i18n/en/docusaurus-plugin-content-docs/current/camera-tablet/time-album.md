# Highlights to Bring Out Your Best Moments

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044580_f001_Highlights.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Gallery turns your daily photos into themed highlights (such as portraits, birthdays, scenic spots), so that you can relive your best memories.

**Try it out**

You can perform the following operations to quickly generate a Highlights album:

Before taking a photo or video, open **Camera**, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_setting_normal.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Settings icon"/> to enable **Location tag**.

Multiple different photos in the same scene are required to generate a Highlights album. If there are too few photos, or if the photos were taken at similar times or have similar content, the album may fail to be generated.

Go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_hiden_more.png" width="24" height="24" alt="More icon"/> > Settings in **Gallery** and toggle on the **Allow Internet access**, **Media analysis**, and **Accelerated smart analysis** switches. Connect your tablet to a WLAN network, turn the screen off, and charge your device. When your device has sufficient power, it will analyze the photos and create a Highlights album. It may take a while for the albums to be created.

Go to Gallery > Highlights to view the photos. Swipe up during photo playback to enjoy the artistic layout of the Highlights album.

**Note**

If the **Accelerated smart analysis** switch is not available in Gallery settings, this feature is not supported on your device.

# Instant Movie: Turn Photos into Dynamic Videos

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10044584_f010_InstantMovie.png" alt="Instant Movie" width="560" height="" />

Use Instant Movie to quickly turn your favorite photos into videos with background music and motion effects. You can also use **AI Motion Effects** to bring the people and scenery in still photos to life.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Go to **Gallery**, and on the **Photos** tab or in an album, tap **Multi-select**, select photos, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_creat.png" width="24" height="24"/> > **Instant Movie**.
2. Select a theme and duration. When the photos are single-person portraits, plants, scenery, or similar content, **AI Motion Effects** is supported. Select the **AI Motion Effects** menu and tap **Generate**.
3. After generation, check the default template effect. You can also choose other templates marked with the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_instantmovie.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the lower left corner.
4. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24"/> to save the video.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After you select the **AI Motion Effects** menu, photos that support this effect will display the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_instantmovie.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon. Some photos may not support this effect due to the shooting angle. You can try replacing them with other photos.

# Make Movies Instantly

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044584_f010_InstantMovie.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Choose your best photos and instantly craft a stunning clip complete with dynamic effects and captivating music, and let people and scenery in static photos move with **Vitalize**.

**Try it out**

1.  Open **Gallery**, tap **Select multiple** on the **Photos** tab or in an album, select the photos, and go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_creat.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Create icon"/> > Instant movie.

2.  Select a theme and duration. If you select single-person portraits or plant or landscape photos, you can **Vitalize** the photos by selecting **Vitalize** and tapping **Generate**.

3.  View the result generated using the default template. You can also select another template with the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_instantmovie.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the lower left corner.

4.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_settings_save.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Save icon"/> to save the video.

**Note**

After you select **Vitalize**, the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_instantmovie.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon is displayed for images that support this effect. Some images may not support this effect due to the angle. You can replace them with other images.

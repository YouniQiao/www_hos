# Effortless Doodling and Restoration

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20005895_f002_galleryedit.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Doodle on your images and pause to turn scribbles into standard shapes, add text, or mask your private information. You can also tap at any time to restore the original image, even after your doodles have been saved.

**Try it out**

Select an image from Gallery and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_edit1.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Edit icon"/> to enter the editing screen.

+   Tap **Markup** and select a brush and color to draw. You can draw a shape with your finger and pause to turn the random lines into a standard shape.

+   Tap **Text**. Tap **Add text** to add text, and tap **Style** to adjust the text color and font size.

+   Tap **Mosaic** to add mosaics to the image.

To restore the original image, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_edit1.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Edit icon"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/common/buttons/ic_gallery_restore_edit.png" width="24" height="24" alt="Restore icon"/>.

**Note**

Please make sure your device has been updated to the latest system version.

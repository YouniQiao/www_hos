# Remote Control Pairing and Replacement

Learn how to pair and replace the HUAWEI Vision remote control.

## First Power-On

Unplug the HUAWEI Vision and plug it back in to restart it. Once the screen lights up, press and hold the Home button<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002440719312.png" width="24" height="24"/> + Menu button<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002440559400.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Smart Remote at the same time, then follow the on-screen prompts to briefly press either the directional buttons or the confirm button to complete pairing.

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002440715848.png)

## Replacing the Remote Control


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>HUAWEI Smart Remote 2 cannot be swapped with other remote controls.

If your remote control is damaged and no longer works properly due to liquid ingress (such as water, milk, or cola), battery leakage, or impact, you can buy a new remote control from HUAWEI Store at: [HUAWEI Store](https://www.vmall.com/). If the remote control is not available on HUAWEI Store, please [request an on-site service visit](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn15932196/).

Steps:

1.  Unplug the HUAWEI Vision power cord and plug it back in; the HUAWEI Vision will restart automatically. (If you have turned off the power-on automatically when plugged in switch on your HUAWEI Vision, try pressing the power button at the bottom of the HUAWEI Vision to turn it on.)
2.  Press and hold the Menu<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002441932308.png" width="24" height="24"/> button + Volume-down button on the old remote control at the same time for about 8s to restore the remote control to its factory settings.
3.  After restoring the old remote control to its factory settings, wait at least 1 minute on the HUAWEI Vision, then press and hold the Home button<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002440555948.png" width="24" height="24"/> + Menu button<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002440715852.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the new remote control at the same time to enter pairing mode, and follow the on-screen prompts to pair it.

# Using the remote control

Learn how to use the HUAWEI Vision remote control.

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002405662653.png)


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.





## Moving the cursor

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002417927849.png)


Point the remote control at the HUAWEI Vision screen, and a cursor will appear at the corresponding position on the screen. The cursor follows the remote control's movement to the target object.

## Clicking

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002418530909.png)

When the cursor moves to the item you want to select or open, gently press the confirm button on the remote control to complete the action.

For example: click to select a picture or a settings item. Click an app icon to open the app.

## Swiping in different directions

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002418611165.png)

The Smart Remote supports swiping **up/down/left/right**. Move the remote cursor to the desired position, then swipe in the corresponding direction on the touch area to trigger the matching swipe response on the HUAWEI Vision screen, and the cursor will change to reflect the swipe direction.

+   **Swiping up and down**: Commonly used for browsing pages with images and text.
+   **Swiping left and right**: Commonly used for fast-forwarding/rewinding long videos, switching between cards/home screens, and more.


## Continuous swiping in different directions

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002393050512.png)


The Smart Remote supports continuous swiping, which is ideal for **fast-forwarding/rewinding long videos to adjust the progress**, **reading text**, **viewing comments**, and more.

**Continuous swiping:** The Smart Remote supports **slowly swiping** on the touch area and then holding still (without lifting your finger). The page will then scroll continuously on its own. Swipe forward to **increase** the continuous scrolling speed, or swipe backward to **decrease** it.


## Dragging

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002385013010.png)

Point the remote cursor at an app or card, then press and hold the confirm button without lifting your finger to drag it.

For example: move the remote cursor to an app icon, press and hold the confirm button without lifting your finger, and you can move the icon to a new position.


## Pressing and holding to open a menu or enter edit mode

![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002385013472.png)

Point the remote cursor at an app, a card, or an empty area on the home screen, then press and hold the confirm button. Once the menu appears or edit mode opens, release the button.

For example: press and hold the confirm button on the remote control while pointing at the corresponding app icon. When the menu appears, release the button, click the card, swipe left or right to select a card, and then click Add to home screen.


## Adjusting remote control parameters

Smart Remote 2 lets you adjust cursor sensitivity and click anti-shake. You can adjust these settings by going to Settings > Remote control & connections > Smart Remote.

* Click anti-shake: Increasing the anti-shake value can effectively improve the click success rate and reduce the chance of a click being misread as a swipe or drag. If clicks are inaccurate, try increasing this value.
* Cursor sensitivity: The cursor sensitivity setting adjusts how fast the cursor moves, catering to different users' preferences for cursor responsiveness. If the cursor feels unresponsive or lags, try increasing this value.


## Finding the remote control by voice

If you can't find your Smart Remote during everyday use, try saying to your HUAWEI Vision: "Celia, Celia, find the remote control" (this is just an example — you can use other similar phrases). The Smart Remote will beep to help you locate it.

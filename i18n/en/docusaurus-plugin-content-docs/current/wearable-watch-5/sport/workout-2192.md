# Auto-detect workouts

1.  Enter the device app list, go to Settings > Workout, and toggle on Auto-detect workouts.
2.  After this feature is enabled, the device will remind you to start a workout when it detects increased activity. You can ignore the notification, choose to start the corresponding workout, or touch Ignore today or Disable Auto-detect.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The following workout types can be automatically identified: Indoor walk, Outdoor walk, Indoor run, Outdoor run, Elliptical, and Rower.
+   Your device can automatically identify a workout and send a reminder only when your posture and activity intensity meet the corresponding requirements and when you maintain similar active states for a certain period of time.
+   When your activity intensity remains below the corresponding requirements for a certain period of time, your device will remind you to end the workout. You can ignore the notification, continue the workout, or end the workout as prompted.

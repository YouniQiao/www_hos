# Timer

Your wearable device supports quick timers with preset durations and customized timers, to help you measure and control the duration of various activities and events.

## Setting a quick timer

1.  Enter the device app list and touch Timer.
2.  Swipe up on the screen and select a duration. Start the timer.
3.  Your device will notify you when the timer expires. You can touch the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002251130517.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon to repeat the last quick timer.

## Setting a customized timer

1.  Enter the device app list and touch Timer.
2.  Swipe up to reach the bottom of the screen and touch Custom.
3.  Set the hour, minute, and second, then touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002272128118.png" width="24" height="24"/> to start timing.

## Pausing, resetting, and exiting the timer

Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002251132737.png" width="24" height="24"/> to pause, touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002215932220.png" width="24" height="24"/> to reset, or touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002216093288.png" width="24" height="24"/> to exit after the timer expires.

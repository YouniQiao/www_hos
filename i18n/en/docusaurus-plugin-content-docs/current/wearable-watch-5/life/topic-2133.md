# Live view

Live view provides real-time updates on ongoing services and tasks.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available on HUAWEI phones running HarmonyOS 4.0 or later.

## Enabling Live view

1.  Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to Settings > Watch face & home > Live view, and toggle on the switch.
2.  Open the Huawei Health app on your phone, navigate to the device details screen, touch Notifications, and toggle on the switch for Notifications. Also, enable Live view for the apps that support this feature, such as food delivery and travel apps.

## More

+   Swipe up on the watch home screen to enter the message center, and touch the Live card to enter the live notification details screen and view the notification details.
+   Swipe up on the watch home screen to enter the message center, and touch the Live card stack to enter the live notification list and view all Live cards.
+   Swipe down from the middle of the watch home screen to close Live view. Then, swipe up on the watch home screen to enter the message center, and return to the watch home screen to display Live view again.

# Smart home device control on your HUAWEI watch

You can use your watch to control your smart home devices and run automated scenes in an instant. Before you use this feature, ensure that the network connection is normal and the location information can be accessed.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   You'll need to download the AI Life app on your phone, add the smart devices and scenes, and log in to the same HUAWEI ID on your watch, to view the added devices and scenes on the Assistant·TODAY screen.
+   It is recommended that you open the AI Life app on your phone before using the watch to control the devices for the first time.

1.  Swipe right on the watch home screen to enter Assistant·TODAY.
2.  Swipe to the last card and touch Edit > Add card at the bottom to add the Device control card.
3.  Once the card is added successfully, you can find the card on the Assistant·TODAY screen and add the device to be controlled.
4.  On the added device card, you can turn on or off the device with one touch.For some devices, you can also touch the device card to control the device features on the details screen.

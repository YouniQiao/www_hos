# Find Phone feature

1.  Swipe down on the device home screen to open the shortcut menu. Check that <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000001521080849.png"/> is displayed, indicating your wearable is properly connected to your phone over Bluetooth.
2.  On the shortcut menu, touch Find Phone (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002022155997.png"/>) and then <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002022173325.png" width="24" height="24"/>. The Find Phone animation will then play on the screen.
3.  Look for your phone following the ringtone played on your phone (even in Vibration or Silent mode), as long as your wearable and phone are within the Bluetooth connection range.
4.  Touch the wearable screen to end the search.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If you are using an iPhone and the phone screen is turned off, your phone may not respond to the Find Phone feature as ringtone playback is controlled by iOS.
+   The ringtone and volume are preset and cannot be changed.

# Remote shutter

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 2/EMUI 10.1 or later or an iPhone running iOS 13.0 or later. If you're using an iPhone, open the camera first. Camera rotation, camera preview, and video and photo viewing are supported on the watch. To use these features, you'll need to perform the operations on your phone.

## Remote shutter

1.  Make sure your watch is linked with Huawei Health.Swipe down from the top of the watch home screen, and make sure that <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002248941212.png"/> is displayed on the shortcut menu, indicating that your watch is connected to your phone.
2.  Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera. Touch the shutter icon on the watch screen to take a photo.

## Setting a timer for photo taking

Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera. Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002283838693.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Remote shutter screen to set a timer for photo taking.

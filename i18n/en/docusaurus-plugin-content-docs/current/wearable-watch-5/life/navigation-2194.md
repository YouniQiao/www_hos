# Independent navigation on your watch

When connected to the Internet, the watch provides intelligent, interactive navigation services on its own, allowing you to enjoy an outdoor walk or ride without having to reach for your phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is currently only available for walking and cycling.

## Independent watch-based navigation

1.  Enter the watch's app list and open the Petal Maps app.
2.  Touch the search icon at the bottom and enter the destination via voice, handwriting, or keyboard. You can also touch the category icon to have nearby locations displayed for your browsing and selection.
3.  After confirming your destination, specify if you're going for a walk or ride. Select your preferred route, and the route preview will be available on the screen.
4.  Touch Start. Anytime during the navigation, you can tap the screen and then touch the volume icon for broadcast volume adjustments, or swipe up on the screen for navigation instructions in text.
5.  Tap the screen and touch the exit icon to end the navigation.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   On the search screen, you can hold down on a place's name to delete the individual search record. Or you can swipe down to the bottom and touch Clear to delete all search records.

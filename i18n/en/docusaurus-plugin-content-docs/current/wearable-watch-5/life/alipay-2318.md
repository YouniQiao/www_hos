# Alipay

Once you have linked the watch with the Alipay app on your phone, you can use the QR code or barcode on the watch to make payments.

## Linking Alipay

1.  Select Alipay from the watch's app list and follow the onscreen instructions until a QR code is displayed on the screen for you to scan.
2.  Open the Alipay app on your phone and scan the QR code displayed on the watch.
3.  Follow the onscreen instructions to set the device name. Touch Complete and enable and enter the PIN to complete the linking.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The watch can make payments even if it has been disconnected from your phone or tablet, has no Internet connection, or is offline.
+   If you have not topped up your Alipay balance on your watch, the payment sequence will be the same as that set on Alipay.

## Scanning QR codes to make a payment or use public transportation

After the watch has been linked with the Alipay app, swipe on the watch to enter the Alipay screen and show the Alipay QR code or barcode to the vendor to make payments.

1.  Open the app list on your watch and touch Alipay.
2.  To make a payment or use public transportation: To make a payment, touch the payment code, touch Barcode or QR code at the bottom of the screen, and show it to the vendor for scanning. To use public transportation, touch the ride code and show it to a code reader. You can also manually switch between your ride codes by touching the ride code and touching Change card at the bottom of the screen. Before using the ride code, you'll need to link an electronic metro or bus card to Alipay on your phone. Ride code is only available in certain regions and depends on Alipay. For details, view the watch information or call the Alipay service hotline 95188.

## Tap to pay

1.  Enter the watch's app list, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > NFC, and toggle on the switch.
2.  You can use Tap to pay in one of the following methods: Enter the watch's app list, open Alipay, touch Tap to pay, and tap the watch screen against the payment device. Swipe down from the top of the watch face to access Control Panel, touch Tap to pay, and tap the watch screen against the payment device. Swipe left on the watch home screen until you find the Alipay card. On the payment code screen, tap the watch screen against the payment device.

## Unlinking on your watch

1.  Open the app list on the watch and touch Alipay.
2.  On the Alipay screen, go to Settings > Unlink > Confirm to unlink the watch from the Alipay app. After the watch is unlinked, the balance will not be automatically returned to your Alipay account. To do so, you need to unlink in the Alipay app. Once the watch is restored to its factory settings, the Alipay app will be unlinked from the watch automatically.

## Unlinking in the Alipay app

1.  Enter "smart device" in the search box of the Alipay app on your phone, and touch the linked device to enter the watch management screen.
2.  Select the target device and follow the onscreen instructions to unlink the watch from the Alipay app.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   After the watch is unlinked from the Alipay app, the balance will be returned to the top-up account automatically.
+   If you enable this feature, please take extra precautions when using the watch. If the watch gets lost, promptly unlink the watch from Alipay. You can also call the Alipay service hotline 95188 to freeze your Alipay account.
+   After the Alipay app is unlinked, your watch will still display a payment code, but it will be invalid. If you want to use Alipay again, make sure that unlinking has been completed on both the phone and wearable, and then try re-linking.

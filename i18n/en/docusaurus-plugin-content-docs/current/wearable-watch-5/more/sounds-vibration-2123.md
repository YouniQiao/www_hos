# Sounds & vibration

## Vibration strength settings

1.  Enter the device app list and go to Settings > Sounds & vibration > Vibration strength.
2.  Select your desired vibration strength: Strong, Weak, or None.

## Mute settings

1.  Enter the device app list and go to Settings > Sounds & vibration.
2.  Enable or disable Mute to suit your needs.

## Crown haptics

1.  Press the Up button to enter the device app list, and go to Settings > Sounds & vibration.
2.  Enable Crown haptics and you will feel the haptic feedback at each turn of the watch crown. You can also disable the feature to suit your needs.

## Other volume settings

1.  Press the Up button to enter the device app list, and go to Settings > Sounds & vibration.
2.  You can set the volume of ringtones (of incoming calls, messages, and notifications), alarm clocks, music and media playback, calls, AI Voice, and workout broadcasts.

# Workout route syncing

## Managing workout routes in the Huawei Health app

1.  Exporting a route Go to Huawei Health > Health > Exercise records, select an outdoor workout record that includes route data, touch the route icon on the right, and select Export route. Select an export method and touch Save.
2.  Importing a routeGo to Me > My route > Import route, select a downloaded route file, and save it to My route.
3.  Viewing/Deleting a saved routeGo to Me > My route and select a route to view its details or delete it.

## Syncing workout routes to your wearable

1.  Go to Huawei Health > Me > My route and select a target route. On the route details screen, touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000001973189952.png"/> in the upper right corner and then Send to wearable, to import the route to your watch.
2.  On your wearable device, enter the app list, touch Workout, swipe up or down on the screen to select a supported workout mode, and touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002009712877.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the right. On the settings screen, touch Routes, select the target route, and enable workout navigation. Then you can follow the onscreen instructions to navigate to the target location.

# Blood Sugar Study

The built-in high-performance sensor of your watch constantly collects your vital sign data (such as sleep data and heart rate) when you're in different states throughout the day and night. Wear your watch for 3 to 14 days, and the collected data helps assess hyperglycemia risk.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Target audience: adults concerned about blood glucose, including healthy people and people with type 2 pre-diabetes or type 2 diabetes. Those with type 1 diabetes, gestational diabetes, and other special types of diabetes are not included.
+   This feature is not intended for medical use. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you experience any discomfort, please seek medical assistance.

## Joining the study

1.  Press the Up button on the watch to enter the app list, and go to HUAWEI Research > Blood Sugar Study > Join.
2.  Wear your watch all day. It takes 3–14 days to complete a round of assessment. Once completed, a message will be displayed on your watch, and you can touch View details to view the result.
3.  Swipe up on the home screen of the Blood Sugar Study app to view the assessment records.
4.  You can view the hyperglycemia risk assessment results in the HUAWEI Research app on your phone as long as it's not an iPhone. Wear your watch day and night. In each round of assessment, your watch needs to obtain at least 20 hours of vital sign data in resting state in the daytime and 40 hours of vital sign data during sleep at night. A total of at least 2 hours of valid daytime and 4 hours of night time data is required everyday. During the assessment, please avoid high-sugar and high-fat foods, exercise moderately, and get enough rest.

## Ending the assessment

You can choose to end the assessment by touching End this round of assessment. You can start the assessment at any time you desire, by touching Start new assessment round.

## Assessment result

Assessment result description:

+   Low risk: There is no apparent risk of hyperglycemia.
+   Medium risk: There may be a risk of hyperglycemia.
+   High risk: There is a high risk of hyperglycemia.
+   No assessment result: Your vital sign data collected in the 14-day assessment period is insufficient to produce an assessment result. Please start the next round of assessment.

## Leaving the study

Swipe up on the home screen of Blood Sugar Study, and go to Settings > Leave study.

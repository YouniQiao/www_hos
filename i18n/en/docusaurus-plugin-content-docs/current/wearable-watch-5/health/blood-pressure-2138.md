# Hypertension Risk Study

Your watch's high-performance sensor will continuously collect data during the day and night to provide a hypertension risk assessment. Continuous participation in the study can help in detecting your risk for hypertension and cardiovascular disease.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   It takes 24 hours to complete an assessment. The following conditions must be met: Wearing time: Keep your wearable on for at least 7 hours during the day and 6 hours at night. Active measurement: Take at least two measurements with the X-TAP sensor.

## Hypertension Risk Study

1.  Press the Up button on your watch to enter the watch's app list, and go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study > Join.
2.  For first-time use, enable sensor data access and touch Allow.
3.  For first-time use, read the feature description carefully, touch Start, enter your personal information, and touch OK to start an assessment cycle.

Cycle progress

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the watch's app list, and go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study to view the progress.

Assessment result

1.  When the progress reaches 100%, touch Start. For first-time use, select a wearing mode, view the measurement instructions, and touch OK.
2.  Tap the X-TAP sensor to start the measurement and adjust the contact pressure during the measurement as prompted.
3.  After the measurement is complete, you can view the assessment result.

Historical records

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the watch's app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study, and swipe up to view historical assessment records. For details, open the HUAWEI Research app on your phone.

## Reminder settings

1.  Press the Up button on your watch to enter the watch's app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study, swipe up to the last screen, and touch Reminder settings.
2.  Notifications will be enabled by default to send you notifications about the study's progress, reminders to help when it's progressing slowly, and more. You can disable it to best suit your needs.

## Leaving the study

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the watch's app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study, swipe up to the last screen, and touch Leave study. You can re-join the study at any time you desire.

# Sleep apnea app

Sleep apnea is characterized by the total or partial obstruction of the airway during sleep. It may present itself as snoring, and can cause a drop in blood oxygen, wake you up repeatedly during the night, and affect your sleep architecture, making you feel drowsy during the day and causing memory issues. In extreme cases, sleep apnea could affect the autonomic nervous system (ANS) and cognitive abilities. Sleep apnea tests use metrics like heart rate and SpO2, instead of snoring sounds.

## Activating the app

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, make sure your wearable device is connected to Huawei Health.

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and select Sleep apnea.
2.  During first-time use, follow the onscreen instructions to authorize and sign in with your account, agree to the privacy agreement, and touch Activate to enable the Sleep apnea app.

## Viewing data

1.  Wear your device properly to bed and sleep for more than five hours, and sleep apnea data will be generated. Then you can enter the wearable app list, swipe up till you find Sleep apnea, and touch this option to view your latest sleep apnea data. Swipe up on the screen to view data of the last seven days.
2.  You can check out data records in detail and result interpretation on your phone, by opening Huawei Health, touching the Sleep card, swiping up on the screen, and touching Sleep apnea.

## Enabling/Disabling sleep apnea test

Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and touch Sleep apnea. Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000001862572181.png"/> in the upper right corner and then Settings, and toggle the switch for Sleep apnea on or off.

## Disabling the service

Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and touch Sleep apnea. Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000001815892346.png"/> in the upper right corner and then About > Disable service, and follow the onscreen instructions to disable the service.

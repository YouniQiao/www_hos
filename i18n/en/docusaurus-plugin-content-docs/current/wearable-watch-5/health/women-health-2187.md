# Women's Health Study

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The HUAWEI Research app is not available on iPhones, but it can be used on your wearable device.
+   When the gender is set to Male or Rather not say, the watch will display a message indicating that this feature is not supported.
+   This feature is not intended for medical use. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you experience any discomfort, please seek medical assistance.

Women's Health Study uses the built-in high-performance sensor of the watch to collect vital sign data (such as body temperature, heart rate, and respiratory rate) on a continual basis in different states at night, to help access ovarian health. It takes 14–35 days to complete an assessment cycle. It is recommended that you keep your watch on for at least five days a week to accelerate the assessment.

## Joining Women's Health Study

1.  Download and install the HUAWEI Research app on your phone.
2.  Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study, and touch Join study.
3.  For first-time use, you will need to start an assessment cycle before being able to enter your personal information (such as gender, date of birth, height, and weight). Touch OK, read the instructions, and touch Next.
4.  Fill in the questionnaire based on your physical conditions over the previous month, and touch Next.
5.  Keep wearing your watch in the correct manner and start the assessment.

## Viewing your data

It takes 14–35 days to complete an assessment cycle, and at least 14 days of valid nighttime data within 35 days (except for your period, predicted period, or pregnancy days) is required. Keeping your device on as much as possible can help accelerate the assessment.

Cycle progress

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, and go to HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study to find the cycle progress.

Assessment result

You'll receive a prompt from your watch about a complete round of assessment. Touch View details under the prompt to access Women's Health Study and view your result, including ovarian health risk and tips and how you're doing compared with your peers.

Historical records

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study, and swipe up to find historical data. You can select any record to view details.

For more details, open the HUAWEI Research app on your phone.

## Exiting Women's Health Study

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study, swipe up to the last screen, and touch Leave study. You can re-join the study at any time you desire.

## Ending the current cycle

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study, and touch End cycle at the bottom of the screen. When ended, the data of the cycle won't be retained.

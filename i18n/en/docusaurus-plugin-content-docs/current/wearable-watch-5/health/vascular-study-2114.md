# Vascular Study

Vascular Study uses the wearable devices' high-precision ECG and PPG sensors for arteriosclerosis screening, and provides personalized guidance accordingly.

## Vascular health measurement

On your phone

Make sure that your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth and the Huawei Health app.

1.  Download and install the HUAWEI Research app on your phone.
2.  Open the HUAWEI Research app, enter Vascular Study, and touch Start.
3.  Follow the onscreen tutorial to complete a measurement on your watch.
4.  Once the measurement is complete, you will be able to view the result in the Vascular Study app.

On your watch

1.  Press the Up button to open the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Vascular Study > Join study, and touch Start. If this is your first time using the app, complete the personal information as prompted.
2.  Follow the onscreen tutorial to complete a measurement.
3.  Once the measurement is complete, swipe down on the HUAWEI Research screen on your phone to sync measurement data.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The gender, age, height, and weight specified in your personal information will affect measurement results. Please enter your information correctly for optimal accuracy. On the home screen of the Vascular Study app, there is a trend line that compares your data with that of other people of the same age, as the parameters of the standard value, high value, and low value varies by age. Measurement results should not be used as a basis for medical diagnosis or treatment of vascular diseases.
+   Make sure that the electrode/HUAWEI X-TAP sensor and the rear cover of your device are not blocked by any foreign objects or covered with grime, and that the finger in contact with the electrode/HUAWEI X-TAP sensor is slightly moist. Otherwise, the measurement result may be inaccurate or the measurement may fail.

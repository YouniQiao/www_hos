# Pairing with a HarmonyOS 5 phone

1.  Install the Huawei Health app (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000001921162186.png"/>).
2.  On your phone, go to Settings > HUAWEI ID. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, please register for one, and then log in. It is recommended that an adult account be used for login, to ensure successful pairing and connection between your wearable device and your phone.
3.  Power on your wearable device, select a language, and start pairing.
4.  Place your wearable device close to your phone. On the home screen of the HUAWEI Health app, touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002165898396.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner and then Add device. Find your wearable device in the search result and touch Connect. You can also touch Scan this QR code to pair by scanning the QR code displayed on your wearable device.
5.  When a pairing request displays on your wearable device, touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/en-US/en-us_image_0000002174844497.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions on your phone to complete the pairing.

# Heart Study

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The Huawei Research app is not available on iPhones. If the app is available on your device, you can use it independently on the device.
+   Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you experience any discomfort, please seek medical assistance.

## Starting the Heart Study

1.  Pair your device in the Huawei Health app, and go to HUAWEI Research > Heart Study on your phone.
2.  Tap Agree, select I have read and agree, and go to Join study > Start.
3.  On the Heart Study screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000001549618158.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner and then Privacy management, and enable Smart real-time measurement. Once enabled, your device will automatically evaluate your heart health when you are resting. You can swipe down on the phone screen to sync data from the wearable device, when it's connected to your phone.

## Arrhythmia detection

On your phone:

Keep your device connected to the HUAWEI Research app. On the home screen of the app, go to Heart Study > Detection, and tap Start to start a reading.

On your watch:

In the device's app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Heart Study, join the study, and tap Start to start a reading.

## Atrial fibrillation (A-fib) risk detection

On the HUAWEI Research home screen, go to Heart Study > Prediction, and enable A-fib risk detection.

From there, you can view the results and risk trends to take any necessary steps to mitigate the risks.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is useful for individuals who have tested positive for arrhythmia. If you are feeling uncomfortable, be sure to consult with a doctor as soon as possible.
+   It is recommended that you perform this atrial fibrillation detection at least three times a day, and enable automatic measurements to ensure that there is sufficient data.

## Leaving the Heart Study

On the HUAWEI Research home screen, select Heart Study, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000001493684396.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner and then Leave study.

You can re-join the study at any time you desire.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   After you leave the heart study, your heart study data will be permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

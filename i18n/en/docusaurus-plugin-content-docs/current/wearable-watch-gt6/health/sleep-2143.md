# Sleep apnea app

Sleep apnea is characterized by the total or partial obstruction of the airway during sleep. It may present itself as snoring, and can cause a drop in blood oxygen, wake you up repeatedly during the night, and affect your sleep architecture, making you feel drowsy during the day and causing memory issues. In extreme cases, sleep apnea could affect the autonomic nervous system (ANS) and cognitive abilities. Sleep apnea tests use metrics like heart rate and SpO2, instead of snoring sounds.

## Activating the app

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, make sure your wearable device is connected to Huawei Health.

In the Huawei Health app, tap the Sleep card, swipe up, and tap Sleep apnea. If this is your first time using the feature, follow the onscreen instructions to log in to your account and agree to the privacy agreement, then tap Activate to enable the sleep apnea app.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To disable this feature, go to the Sleep apnea screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002384956592.png"/> in the upper right corner and then Settings, and disable Sleep apnea.

## Viewing data

1.  Wear your device properly to bed and sleep for more than five hours, and sleep apnea data will be generated. Then you can enter the wearable app list, swipe up till you find Sleep apnea, and tap this option to view your latest sleep apnea data. Swipe up on the screen to view data of the last seven days.
2.  You can check out data records in detail and result interpretation on your phone, by opening Huawei Health, tapping the Sleep card, swiping up on the screen, and tapping Sleep apnea.

## Disabling the service

Open Huawei Health, tap the Sleep card, swipe up on the screen, and tap Sleep apnea. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002418398301.png"/> in the upper right corner and then About > Disable service, and follow the onscreen instructions to disable the service.

# Multi-tasking

Multi-tasking makes it easy to switch between recently used apps and quickly clear all background apps.

1.  Double-press the Up button when your watch is unlocked with its screen turned on, to enter the multi-tasking screen. From there you can view all running apps.
2.  Swipe left or right on this screen and perform any of the following operations: Tap an app card to visit the app details screen. Hold down on an app card and swipe up, to clear the app. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000001628586178.png"/> to clear all running apps.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If you're amid a workout session, the Workout app can't be cleared by holding down on it and swiping up.
+   If you're amid a workout session, the Workout app can't be cleared by tapping <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000001828895562.png"/>.

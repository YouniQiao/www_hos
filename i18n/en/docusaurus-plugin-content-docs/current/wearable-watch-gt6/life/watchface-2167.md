# Video watch face

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Make sure that you have updated the Huawei Health app and your watch to their latest versions.
+   Customized video watch faces are unavailable when the watch is paired with an iPhone.

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to My faces > Video to access the Video watch face settings screen.
2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002356307844.png" width="24" height="24"/>, select an uploading method, record a new video or select an existing video to upload, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002389907749.png" width="24" height="24"/>.
3.  Tap Style, Time position, and Features to set the style and position of the widgets on the video watch face.
4.  Tap Apply and your watch will switch to the newly set watch face. Only one video watch face can be uploaded. You can also choose a preinstalled video watch face. Pre-installed video watch faces cannot be deleted. Video watch faces are designed to cycle through different videos. The watch will switch between different videos when you tap the watch face or turn on the screen.

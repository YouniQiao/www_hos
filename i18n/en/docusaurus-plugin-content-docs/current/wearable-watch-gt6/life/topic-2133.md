# Live view

Live view on your watch can display the live notifications on your HUAWEI phone, providing real-time updates on ongoing services and tasks.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, make sure that you have updated your watch to the latest version.
+   Live view is only available when paired with HUAWEI phones running HarmonyOS 4.0 or later.

## Enabling Live view

1.  Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to Settings > Watch face & home > Live view, and toggle on the switch.
2.  HUAWEI phones running HarmonyOS 4.0 or later but earlier than HarmonyOS 5.0: Open the Huawei Health app, enter the device details screen, tap Live notifications, and enable Sync to watch.HUAWEI phones running HarmonyOS 5.0 or later: Open the Huawei Health app, enter the device details screen, go to General settings > Live view, and enable Sync to watch.

## Deleting Live view messages

+   Delete the Live view message on the lock screen of your phone to delete it from your watch.
+   Delete the Live view message in the notification panel of your phone to delete it from your watch.
+   Tap the Live view capsule on your phone and swipe right to delete the Live view message from your watch.
+   Tap the Live view message on your watch to enter the Live view message list, and swipe right to delete the message.

## More

+   Swipe up on the watch home screen to enter the message center, and tap the Live card to enter the live notification details screen and view the notification details.
+   Swipe up on the watch home screen to enter the message center, and tap the Live card stack to enter the live notification list and view all Live cards.
+   Swipe down from the middle of the watch home screen to close Live view. Then, swipe up on the watch home screen to enter the message center, and return to the watch home screen to display Live view again.

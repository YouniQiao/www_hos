# Sticker watch faces

You can select or upload multiple stickers to create a unique watch face with personalized styles and widgets, and mix it up by touching the screen.

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to My faces > Sticker Fun.<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002448068321.png"/>
2.  Select your desired stickers on the Geometric, Emoji, and Pet screens, or upload stickers by touching Custom and then <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002219835673.png" width="24" height="24"/>.
3.  Customize the settings of Layout, Background color, and Font. You can also change the data displayed on the watch face by touching Features.
4.  Touch Set as default and your wearable device will switch to the newly set Sticker watch face. You can mix it up by touching the screen.

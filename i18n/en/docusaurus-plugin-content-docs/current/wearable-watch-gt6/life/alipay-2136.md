# Alipay

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

Once you've linked your wearable device to your Alipay account via the Alipay app, you can use QR codes or barcodes to make quick on-wrist payments.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available on devices purchased in the Chinese mainland.

## Linking Alipay

1.  Open Alipay on your phone, scan the QR code displayed on your wearable device, and follow the onscreen instructions to link it to your Alipay account.
2.  Follow the onscreen instructions in Alipay to set your device name, payment method, and daily transaction limit. Touch Complete and enable, and enter the payment password to complete the linking.
3.  Open Alipay on your phone, search for "smart device", select the mini-program, and touch your device card to manage on-wrist payments.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Your device can continue to make payments even when it is disconnected from your phone and from the Internet.
+   The order of the on-device payment methods is the same as that set in Alipay on your phone.

## Making quick payments with code scans

After successful linking with the Alipay app, add Alipay as a custom feature card on your wearable device. Then to make a payment, swipe left on the wearable home screen until you find the Alipay card (or access the app list and touch Alipay), and show your payment code (which can be switched between QR code and barcode) to the vendor to pay.

## Unlinking Alipay

Use either of the following methods to unlink Alipay.

On your wearable device:

On your phone:

Open Alipay, search for "smart device", and select the mini-program. Choose the target device, touch Unlink, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the unlinking.

After operations on your phone, access Alipay on your wearable device, swipe up on the screen, touch Unlink, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the unlinking on the wearable end.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If you have deposited money into your wearable device, any balance will not be automatically returned to your top-up account after unlinking on the wearable end alone. The balance will be returned after unlinking via Alipay on your phone.
+   If you enable this feature, be sure to take extra precautions when using your wearable device. If it gets lost, promptly unlink it from your Alipay account using your phone.
+   After unlinking on the phone end alone, when you try to use Alipay on your wearable device, a payment code will still display but it'll be invalid. If you want to use Alipay again, make sure that unlinking has been completed on both the phone and wearable ends, and then try re-linking.
+   Once your wearable device is restored to its factory settings, it will be automatically unlinked from your Alipay account.

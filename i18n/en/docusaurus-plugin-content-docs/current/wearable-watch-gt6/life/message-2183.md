# Managing messages

When your wearable device is linked with Huawei Health and the Notifications feature is enabled, new messages pushed to the status bar of your phone will be synced to your wearable.

## Enabling the Notifications feature

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, tap Notifications, and toggle on Notifications.
2.  In the app list below, toggle on the switches for the apps you wish to receive notifications from.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   On the Notifications screen, the APPS section shows the list of apps that you can receive notifications from.

## Push notifications settings

To receive notifications on the wearable based on the phone usage status, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, tap Notifications, and toggle on Smart notifications or Mute notifications when using phone (This switch is toggled off by default.).

+   HUAWEI phones: Enable Smart notifications.Notifications will be muted on your wearable while you are using your phone. The wearable will still receive the notification, but it won't vibrate or ring. When you aren't using your phone and wearing your wearable, you will be notified of messages via your wearable and your phone won't ring or vibrate.
+   Other Android phones: Once Mute notifications when using phone is enabled, notifications will be muted on your wearable while you are using your phone. The wearable will still receive the notification, but it won't vibrate or ring.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The Smart notifications or Mute notifications when using phone feature is not available when the wearable is paired with an iPhone.

## Viewing and deleting unread messages

1.  When being worn, your wearable device will vibrate to notify you of new messages pushed from the status bar of your phone.
2.  Unread messages will be kept on your wearable. To view them, swipe up on the home screen to enter the message center.
3.  To delete a message, do as follows: Swipe left on a message that you wish to delete, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002389118309.png"/> to delete it. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002355598224.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the bottom of the message list to clear all messages.

## Replying to SMS messages

1.  When receiving an SMS message on your wearable, you can swipe up on the screen and select a quick reply.
2.  To customize quick replies, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and tap Quick replies.
3.  You can tap Add reply to add a reply, tap an existing reply to edit it, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002355438280.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to a reply to delete it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is unavailable when your wearable is paired with an iPhone.
+   Quick SMS replies only work with the SIM card that has received the SMS message.

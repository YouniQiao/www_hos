# Remote shutter

Remote shutter allows you to launch your phone's camera and take photos from your wrist.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 2/EMUI 10.1 or later or an iPhone running iOS 13.0 or later. If you're using an iPhone, open the camera first.

## Remote shutter

1.  Make sure your watch is linked with Huawei Health.Swipe down from the top of the watch home screen, and make sure that <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002355442668.png"/> is displayed on the Control Panel, indicating that your watch is connected to your phone.
2.  Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone's camera. Tap the shutter icon on the watch screen to take a photo.

## Setting a timer for photo taking

1.  Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone's camera. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002355602604.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Remote shutter screen to set a timer for photo taking, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/en-US/en-us_image_0000002355602604.png" width="24" height="24"/> again to switch to another timer.
2.  Tap the shutter icon on the watch screen. The phone's camera will take a photo as soon as the countdown ends.

# HUAWEI Smart Remote Air Touch

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>HUAWEI Smart Remote Air Touch is available for the HUAWEI Vision MateTV series, where it comes standard on the Pro version. More compatible models will be added gradually, so stay tuned.

## What is HUAWEI Smart Remote Air Touch?

HUAWEI Smart Remote Air Touch is a brand-new multi-finger interaction solution launched alongside the HUAWEI Vision MateTV. It breaks free from the constraints of traditional button controls and brings the phone usage habits you're used to to your HUAWEI Vision.

The video is for reference only. Please refer to the actual product.

HUAWEI Smart Remote Air Touch accurately detects the 3D position of your left and right thumbs in the air touch area in real time, and then uses a virtual cursor on the HUAWEI Vision to let you control targets on the HUAWEI Vision with two fingers, delivering the same control logic and experience as a phone or tablet.

With Smart Remote Air Touch:

+   You can easily control the HUAWEI Vision MateTV, such as quickly turning pages, swiping, and browsing content.
+   You can use two fingers to smoothly zoom in on, rotate, and finely edit images, maps, and more.

| Button| Function|

|Left/right shoulder buttons|Can be customized during gameplay.|

| Power button| When connected, you can turn the HUAWEI Vision on or off. On first use or after a factory reset, press and hold the power button for 4 seconds to start pairing.|
| Indicator light| See the description below.|
| Charging port| The Type-C port is for charging only and cannot be used for data transfer. It is recommended to use a charger of 5V/2A or above (the charger is not included in the packing list). Common chargers on the market are supported, with a maximum of 22.5W (10V/2.25A).|

+   Powering on: colorful light effect
+   Working: ice-blue breathing light
+   Standby: no light effect
+   Battery level display while charging:
    +   0–33%: one light flashes green
    +   33%–66%: three lights flash green
    +   66%–99%: five lights flash green
    +   100%: five lights stay green
+   Low battery red light reminder:
    +   Below 20%: flashes once
    +   Below 10%: flashes three times
    +   Below 5%: flashes continuously
+   Waiting to pair: blue light flashes once when a button is pressed
+   Disconnected: white light flashes once when a button is pressed

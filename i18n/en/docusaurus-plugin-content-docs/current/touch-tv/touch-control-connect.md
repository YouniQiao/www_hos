# How to Pair with a HUAWEI Vision

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>HUAWEI Air Touch is available for the HUAWEI Vision MateTV series, with the Pro version including it as standard. More compatible models will be added gradually, so stay tuned.

## Pairing and Connecting

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>If you have multiple HUAWEI Vision MateTV devices, it is recommended that you turn on only the Vision you want to pair with and turn off the others. This prevents accidental pairing with another Vision.

1.  With the Vision powered on, bring the Air Touch close to the Vision.
2.  Press and hold the power button for 4 seconds, then follow the on-screen instructions on the Vision to complete pairing.

## Replacing the Air Touch

1. Unpair the original Air Touch: press and hold the function button<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002458473625.png" width="24" height="24"/>and the left shoulder button L at the same time until the Air Touch's green light turns on, and pairing is removed.

2. Refer to [Pairing and Connecting](#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002424249484__section18805119114310) to pair the new Air Touch.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   While the Air Touch is paired, it cannot be powered off; after 5 seconds of inactivity, it automatically enters standby mode.
+   When the Air Touch is not paired with a Vision or has been unpaired, it automatically powers off after 4 minutes of inactivity.

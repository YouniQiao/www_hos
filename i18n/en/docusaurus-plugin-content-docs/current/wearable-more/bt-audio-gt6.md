# Connect Bluetooth earbuds/speakers

> This article applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

More guidance


|  | Connect Bluetooth earbuds/speakers |
| --- | --- |


   Connect Bluetooth earbuds/speakers

    After the watch is properly connected to Bluetooth earbuds/speakers, you can listen to music and more through the connected device.


## Connect Bluetooth earbuds/speakers through watch Settings


1. After the watch is paired and connected to **HUAWEI Health**, set the Bluetooth earbuds/speakers to pairing mode.
2. Go to the watch's app list, swipe up and down on the screen, and select **Settings > Bluetooth**.
3. Select the target Bluetooth earbuds/speakers to connect.


## Connect Bluetooth earbuds/speakers through watch Music


1. After the watch is paired and connected to **HUAWEI Health**, set the Bluetooth earbuds/speakers to pairing mode.
2. Go to the watch's app list, swipe up and down on the screen, and select **Music**. On the music playback screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002432867644.png" width="24" /> in the lower right corner, then tap **Audio channel > New device**.
3. Select the target Bluetooth earbuds/speakers to connect.

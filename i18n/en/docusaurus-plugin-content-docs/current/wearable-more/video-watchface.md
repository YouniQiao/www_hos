# Set a Video Watch Face

> This article applies to **WATCH 5**.

> 💡 **Tip:** Before using this feature, update HUAWEI Health and your watch to the latest versions. Custom video watch faces are not supported when the watch is paired with an iOS phone.

1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **My Watch Faces**, select **Video Watch Face**, and go to the video watch face settings screen.
2. Select a built-in video. Or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002356307844.png" width="24" /> to record a video or upload a local video. After uploading the video, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002389907749.png" width="24" />.
3. You can also select **Style**, **Time Position**, and **Features** to set the style, position, and other attributes of the video watch face controls.
4. Tap **Set as Current Watch Face**, and the watch face on your device will automatically switch to the one you set. > 💡 **Tip:** You can add and upload only one video watch face. You can also choose from the video watch faces provided by the system. System-provided video watch faces cannot be deleted. When a video watch face is set, touch the watch face to switch videos.

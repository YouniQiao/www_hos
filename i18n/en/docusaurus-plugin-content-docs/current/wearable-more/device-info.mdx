# View device information

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

## Viewing on the watch

1. Press the watch's upper button, tap **Settings** in the app list, and go to the Settings screen.
2. Tap the **device name (for example, HUAWEI WATCH XXX)**, then swipe up on the **About** screen.
You can view the watch's **model name**, **model code**, **software version**, **serial number**, **Bluetooth address**, **IMEI**, and other information.


To check the watch's EID (available only in Full mode): press the watch's upper button, tap **Settings > Mobile network** > **eSIM management** > **Settings** in the device's app list. The EID is displayed on the Settings screen.


## Viewing on the phone


On the device details screen in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **General settings > Device information** to view the **device name**, **device model**, **device SN**, and **device MAC** (that is, the **Bluetooth address**).

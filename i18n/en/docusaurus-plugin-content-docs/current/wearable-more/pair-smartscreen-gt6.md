   Pair and connect with HUAWEI Vision

    After the watch and HUAWEI Vision are properly connected, you can easily monitor real-time workout data (such as heart rate, step count, and calories) on HUAWEI Vision while exercising, so you can better understand your fitness results.

1. Pair the watch with the **HUAWEI Health** app on your phone, and make sure it is properly connected.
2. Sign in to HUAWEI Vision and your phone with the same HUAWEI ID. HUAWEI Vision will automatically sync the wearables under that HUAWEI ID.
3. On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to **Health > Me**, and tap the watch on the **My devices** tab.
4. Tap **Connect device**, and add the watch by following the onscreen instructions.
5. In the pairing request dialog box that appears on the watch, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001992555909.png" width="24" /> to complete pairing.

## Upgrade on the device


> 💡 **Note:** When paired with an iOS phone, upgrading the device version on the device is not supported. The system version upgrade notification for wearables cannot be turned off, and version rollback is not supported.


1. In the device's app list, tap **Settings > System & updates** > **Software update**.
2. Tap the update icon and follow the prompts to download and install the update.


## Upgrade on the phone


1. On the device details page of **HUAWEI Health**, tap **General settings > Firmware update**.
2. On the **Firmware update** page, tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002502902197.png" width="24" />** icon in the upper right corner, and turn on **Auto-download over WLAN** and **Auto-install and restart when device is idle**. When a new version is available, it will be silently transferred to the device for the version update. On an iOS phone, the path is: On the **Firmware update** page, tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002505099895.png" width="24" />** icon in the upper right corner, and turn on **Auto-download over WLAN** and **Auto-install and restart when device is idle**.
3. The phone will check for version updates for the device. Follow the prompts to perform the update.

## Upgrade on the watch


> 💡 **Note:** Before upgrading, make sure the watch is connected to the network. The system version upgrade notification for wearables cannot be turned off, and version rollback is not supported.


1. Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, tap **Settings** to enter the settings page.
2. Tap the **device name (for example, HUAWEI WATCH XXX)** to enter the **About watch** page.
3. Tap **Check for updates** and follow the prompts to complete the software version update.


## Upgrade on the phone


> 💡 **Note:** iOS phones do not support upgrading on the phone, and there is no **Firmware update** option.


     On the device details page of **HUAWEI Health**, tap **General settings > Firmware update**. The phone will check for version updates for the watch. Follow the prompts to perform the update.

# Set an album watch face

> This topic applies to **WATCH 5**.

## Set an album watch face

1. Open the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **My watch faces > Album watch face** to open the album watch face settings screen.
2. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002356284902.png" width="24" />, select **Take photo** or **Select image**, choose a photo, then drag the photo to adjust the displayed area. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002356437526.png" width="24" /> in the upper right corner to finish uploading the photo.
3. On the **Album watch face** screen, you can set the watch face's **color**, **style**, **features**, and more.
4. Under **Other settings**, turn **Show new photo on raise** on or off.
When more than one photo has been uploaded, turning this on switches to a new photo each time you raise your wrist to wake the screen. When it is off, raising your wrist keeps the last displayed photo, and you need to tap the watch face screen to switch to a new photo.
5. Tap **Set as current watch face** to set the device's watch face to the album watch face.


> 💡 **Tip:** The album watch face supports the following image formats: *.gif, *.jpg, *.jpeg, *.png, *.bmp, *.psd, *.tif, *.tiff, *.webp, *.pcx. GIF images are displayed as still images, without animation.

## Installing apps


1. Go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health** and tap **AppGallery**.
2. In the **Apps** list, select the app you want to use and tap **Install**. After the installation is complete, the app will appear in the app list on your watch.


## Updating apps


1. Go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health** and tap **AppGallery**.
2. Tap **Manage > Update management** > **Update** to update the app to the latest version.


## Uninstalling apps

     **On your phone**

1. Go to the device details page in **HUAWEI Health** and tap **AppGallery**.
2. Tap **Manage > Installation management** > **Uninstall** to uninstall an installed app. After uninstalling, the app will be deleted from your device as well.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Only third-party apps can be uninstalled. Preinstalled apps on the wearable device cannot be uninstalled.
>
> - If you have set **Stop AppGallery service** in **HUAWEI Health**, you will need to tap **AppGallery** again the next time you use it, and follow the onscreen prompts to grant permissions and enable the **AppGallery** feature.


    **On your device:**
    Go to the app list on your device, touch and hold the app you want to uninstall until the uninstall icon appears, then release, tap the uninstall button, and tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002392501485.png" width="24" />**.


## Adjusting the app list order

     Go to the app list on your device, touch and hold an app and drag it to the desired position, then release once the other apps have moved aside.

## Installing/uninstalling apps

     Before installing an app, make sure your watch is signed in to the same HUAWEI ID as your phone. In the app list on your watch, tap **Settings > Sign in to HUAWEI ID** > **Sync account**.

1. Press the watch's upper button, and tap **AppGallery** in the app list.
2. Search for the app you need, open its details, and tap **Install**.
3. Once installed, tap the app to open it. To uninstall an app, touch and hold it in the app list to uninstall it.


## Managing background apps


1. Press the watch's upper button twice, and swipe left or right to view apps running in the background.
2. Perform the following as needed:


- Tap an app tab to open that app.
- Swipe up on an app tab to close that app.
- Tap the delete icon to close all apps running in the background.


## Managing installed apps


1. Press the watch's upper button, and tap **AppGallery** in the app list.
2. On the AppGallery home page, swipe right to access more options, then swipe up to update or view installed apps.


## Viewing or setting app status


1. Press the watch's upper button, and tap **Settings > Apps** > **App management** in the app list.
2. Tap the target app to view its permissions, storage usage, and other information.


## Adjusting the app list order

     Press the watch's upper button, then in the app list, touch and hold any app and drag it up or down, or touch and hold any app and rotate the crown to quickly adjust the app order.


## Clearing cache


1. Press the watch's upper button, and tap **Settings > Apps** > **App management** in the app list.
2. Tap the target app, then tap **Storage > Clear cache** to clear the app's cache.

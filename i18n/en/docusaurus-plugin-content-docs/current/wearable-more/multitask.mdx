# Multitasking

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="ultimate" label="WATCH Ultimate">


Switch freely between recent apps and quickly clear all background tasks.
1. While the watch screen is on and unlocked, double-tap the upper button to enter the multitasking screen, where you can view all currently open apps.
2. You can swipe left or right on the screen to perform the following operations: Tap any app card to enter the app details screen.
3. Touch and hold any app card and swipe up to remove the task.
4. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001628586178.png" width="24" />
to remove all tasks.
> 💡 **Tip:** Workout tasks in progress cannot be removed.

</TabItem>

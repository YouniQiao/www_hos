# Charging

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.


## Charging precautions
- It is recommended that you purchase a Huawei charging cradle and power adapter suitable for this product from official Huawei channels, or a charging cradle and power adapter that comply with the laws and regulations of the applicable country/region and international and regional safety standards, to charge the device. Other power adapters and portable power banks (power banks) may not meet applicable safety standards and may cause issues such as slow charging and overheating, so use them with caution. If the charging cradle is connected to the USB port of another device (such as a PC USB port, docking station, or multi-port charger), the watch may fail to charge or charge more slowly. Because the charging cradle contains magnets, it will inevitably attract metal and other foreign objects, so be sure to clean it thoroughly before use. Do not let the charging cradle come into contact with high temperatures for extended periods, as this may demagnetize the charging cradle and cause other abnormalities.
- Before charging, keep the charging port and the surface of the charging cradle dry and clean to prevent short circuits or other risks. During charging, make sure the watch is correctly placed on the charging cradle and that the watch screen properly displays the charging status. The device will not send a notification when fully charged; when the charging indicator shows 100%, the device will detect this and automatically stop charging. Please remove the device and unplug the power adapter. If charging continues for an extended period, the device will automatically resume charging if the battery level drops.
- The watch uses a lithium battery. Due to the characteristics of lithium batteries, there is no need to repeatedly charge and discharge a new watch to activate it before first use; simply charge it whenever needed.
- If the watch will not be used for an extended period, to extend battery life, it is recommended that you charge it regularly, once a month.
## Checking the battery level
Method 1: Swipe down on the watch face screen to open the Control Panel, where you can check the battery percentage.
Method 2: After the device is connected to a power source, check the battery percentage on the charging indicator screen.
Method 3: When the device is properly connected, you can check the battery percentage on the device details page.
> 💡 **Tip:** The battery level can only be viewed on certain watch faces. The actual display shall prevail. When the device battery level is too low, the device will vibrate to remind you of the low battery and prompt you to charge it as soon as possible.
## Charging the watch
1. Connect the charging cradle to the power adapter, and plug the power adapter into a power outlet.
2. Place the device on the charging cradle. Make the back of the watch fit against the charging cradle, and adjust the fit until the charging indicator appears on the watch screen.

# Control Bluetooth earbuds

> This document applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

Convenient life


|  | Control Bluetooth earbuds |
| --- | --- |


   Control Bluetooth earbuds

    After the watch is properly connected to Bluetooth earbuds, you can answer calls, listen to music, and more through the earbuds.


## Pair the watch with Bluetooth earbuds


1. Set the Bluetooth earbuds to pairing mode.
2. On the watch's app list, tap **Settings > Bluetooth**. The watch automatically scans for nearby Bluetooth devices.
3. In the scan results, select the name of the Bluetooth earbuds you want to pair, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete pairing.


## Control Bluetooth earbuds from the watch


1. After the watch and earbuds are connected, swipe right on the watch face to the minus-one screen.
2. On the earbuds card, you can quickly check the earbud battery level, adjust the volume, switch noise cancellation modes, and more. > 💡 **Tip:** The features displayed on the earbuds card depend on your earbuds. The actual display prevails.


## Answer watch calls using Bluetooth earbuds

     Wear the connected Bluetooth earbuds before use.
     With eSIM activated on the watch and the earbuds paired and connected, when you answer or make a call on the watch, the sound comes from the Bluetooth earbuds.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - When you answer or make a call on your phone, the sound comes from the Bluetooth earbuds.
>
> - When you answer or make a call on the watch, the sound comes from the watch.

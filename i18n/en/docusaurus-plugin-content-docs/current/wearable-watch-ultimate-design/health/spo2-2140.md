# SpO2 measurement

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To ensure the accuracy of the SpO2 measurement, wear the wearable device snugly and in the correct manner. Make sure that the monitoring module is in direct contact with your skin, free of any obstructions.

## Fingertip SpO2 measurement

1.  Double-check that your watch is worn properly, and keep still.
2.  Enter the watch's app list, and tap SpO2.
3.  Gently place a finger of your free hand on the X-TAP sensor, then a measurement will be automatically initiated. After the countdown ends, you can keep contact with the sensor to continue measuring, or lift your finger to end the SpO2 measurement and view the results. You can also view your heart rate waveform during the measurement.

## Automatic SpO2 measurement

To enable automatic SpO2 measurement, simply toggle on Automatic SpO₂. Once enabled, your device will automatically measure and record your SpO2 whenever it detects that you are at rest. This measurement is particularly important when you're asleep or in high-altitude areas.

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, tap SpO₂, and toggle on Automatic SpO₂.
2.  Tap Low SpO₂ alert and set the lower limit for you to receive an alert while you're awake.Limit value: Disabled/75%/80%/85%/90%

## Viewing data

Open Huawei Health, tap SpO₂, and select a date to view your data on that day.

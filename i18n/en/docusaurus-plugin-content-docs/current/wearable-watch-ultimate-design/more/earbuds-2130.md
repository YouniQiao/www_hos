# Controlling Bluetooth earbuds

After the watch is connected to your Bluetooth earbuds, you will be able to use the Bluetooth earbuds to answer calls or listen to music.

## Pairing your watch with Bluetooth earphones

1.  Put your Bluetooth earbuds in pairing mode.
2.  In the watch app list, tap Settings > Bluetooth. Your watch will then automatically scan for nearby Bluetooth devices.
3.  Select the name of the Bluetooth earbuds to be paired from the scan results and then follow the onscreen instructions to finish pairing.

## Controlling Bluetooth earphones using the watch

1.  With a pair of Bluetooth earbuds connected, you can swipe right on the watch home screen to access the Assistant·TODAY screen.
2.  The earphones card shows you the earphones' battery level and allows for volume adjustment and switching between noise cancellation modes. The features displayed on the earbuds card depend on the features of your earphones.

## Using Bluetooth earphones to answer calls on the watch

Wear connected Bluetooth earbuds before use.

When the watch has activated the eSIM service and is paired with the Bluetooth earbuds, the sound is played from the Bluetooth earphones when you answer or make a call on the watch.

+   If your HUAWEI phone's Bluetooth or your Android phone's Bluetooth supports simultaneous connection to two audio devices, you can connect your phone to both your watch and your Bluetooth earbuds. Upon receiving an incoming call, you can answer or reject it directly from either your phone or your watch. Once you answer the call on either device, the audio will automatically play through your Bluetooth earbuds.
+   If your iPhone's Bluetooth or Android phone's Bluetooth supports simultaneous connection to two audio devices, you can connect your phone to both your watch and your Bluetooth earbuds. Upon receiving an incoming call, you can choose to answer or reject it directly from either your phone or your watch. The audio output depends on which device you use to answer the call: If you answer or make a call on your phone, the audio will play through your Bluetooth earbuds. If you answer or make a call on your watch, the audio will come from your watch.

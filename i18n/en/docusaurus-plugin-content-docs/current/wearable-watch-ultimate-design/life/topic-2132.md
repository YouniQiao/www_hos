# Setting modes

You can quickly enable or disable Do Not Disturb/Sleep mode in the Control Panel of your wearable device.

## Enabling Do Not Disturb

When Do Not Disturb is enabled, your device won't ring or vibrate when receiving incoming calls and notifications (except for Alarm).

Enable:

1.  Swipe down from the top of the device home screen to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and select Do Not Disturb.
2.  After the setting, Control Panel is displayed as Do Not Disturb.

To disable Do Not Disturb mode, go to Control Panel and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000002194646884.png" width="24" height="24"/>. You can touch and hold to change the mode or perform other settings.

Scheduled Start:

1.  You can access the Do Not Disturb setting screen in either of the following ways: Swipe down from the top of the device home screen to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000002379196064.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to Do Not Disturb. Go to the app list and tap Settings > Focus > Do Not Disturb.
2.  Tap Add time and set Start, End, and Repeat.

To disable scheduled start, turn off the switch next to the corresponding time period.

## Enabling Sleep mode

When Sleep mode is enabled, the device won't ring or vibrate for incoming calls and notifications (except Alarm). Raise to wake will also be disabled, and the device will enter Simple mode.

Enable:

1.  Swipe down from the top of the device home screen to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and select Sleep mode.
2.  After the setting, Sleep mode will be displayed in the Control Panel.

To disable Sleep mode, go to Control Panel and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000002230012881.png" width="24" height="24"/>. You can touch and hold to change the mode or perform other settings.

Scheduled Start:

1.  You can access the Sleep mode setting screen in either of the following ways: Swipe down from the top of the device home screen to access Control Panel, tap Focus, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000002412596701.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to Sleep mode. Go to the app list and tap Settings > Focus > Sleep mode.
2.  Tap Add time and set Bedtime, Wake up, and Repeat.

To disable scheduled start, turn off the switch next to the corresponding time period.

## Syncing modes between the device and phone

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Currently, this feature is only available when your device is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

Swipe down from the top of the device home screen to access Control Panel, tap Focus > More settings, and enable Sync with phone.

When this feature is enabled, mode settings will be synced between the phone and wearable.

In Do Not Disturb or Sleep mode, if you want to receive a certain type of notifications or answer a certain call, go to Settings > Focus, and tap Apps & services and Contacts for settings in the Allowed interruptions section.

## Enabling Study mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Currently, this feature is only available when your device is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

When Study mode is enabled, your device won't ring or vibrate when receiving incoming calls and notifications (except for Alarm and settings where Allowed interruptions is applied).

1.  Swipe down from the top of the device home screen to access Control Panel, tap Focus > More settings, and enable Sync with phone.
2.  On the Focus screen, select Study mode.You can also tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000002517518692.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to the mode on the Focus screen, select Schedule, and set the start time and repeat date.

# Deregistering from the eSIM One Number service for China Mobile users

After activating the eSIM One Number service, you will be able to manage it in the China Mobile app.

## Deregistering from the eSIM One Number service

Deregister from the eSIM One Number service in the China Mobile app and remove eSIM configuration files from the watch.

Deregister from the eSIM One Number service in the China Mobile app:

1.  Use your primary number to sign in to the China Mobile app.
2.  In the China Mobile app, touch One Number, touch Deregister on the Watch screen, and follow the onscreen instructions.
3.  Wait until deregistration is complete. You will then receive an SMS message.

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and go to Settings > Mobile network > eSIM > Settings.
2.  Touch Reset and follow the onscreen instructions to finish resetting. eSIM configuration files will then be removed.

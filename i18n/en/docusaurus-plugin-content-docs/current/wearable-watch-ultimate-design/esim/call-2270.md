# Bluetooth calls and standalone eSIM calls

Your watch supports Bluetooth calls and standalone eSIM calls in different situations.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The support for call forwarding by the eSIM One Number service varies by region. For details, consult with the customer service of your local carrier.

## Syncing/Deleting contacts

Syncing contacts:

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If the contacts are not automatically synced, open the app list on your watch, and go to Contacts > Sync contacts > Sync.
+   If you do not want to sync contacts, open the Huawei Health app, access the device details screen, tap Contacts, and toggle off Sync data to watch.
+   There isn't an option to sync contacts to your watch within the HUAWEI Health app on iPhones. iOS 18 supports contact authorization. You can go to Settings > App > HUAWEI Health > Contacts, and sync contacts as required.

Deleting contacts:

For the contacts synced from your phone, you can delete them on the watch as follows:

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Settings > Apps > App management > System processes.
2.  Go to Contacts Storage > Storage > Clear data. Then, there will be no contacts on your watch. You can manually sync contacts from the Contacts app on your watch.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The watch does not support deleting a single contact. To delete a single contact, select the target contact from the contacts list on your phone and delete it. The contact on the watch will be deleted when the watch is connected to your phone.
+   With Power Saver mode enabled, you cannot delete contacts directly on your watch. Deleting a contact requires selecting it from your phone's contact list and deleting it there. Once your watch is connected to your phone, the contact will automatically be deleted from your watch.

## Answering/Ending a call on your watch

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use the eSIM service on your watch, you'll need to keep wearing your watch and disable Auto-disable network by going to Settings > Battery, swiping up on the screen, and going to Power saving > Auto-disable network.

Answering a call: When you receive an incoming call, your watch will notify you and display the caller information. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000001517962730.png" width="24" height="24"/> to answer the call.

Ending a call:Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000001569242281.png" width="24" height="24"/> to end the call.

Returning to the call screen:

If you're not on the call screen, tap the Phone icon at the top of the device home screen to return to the call screen.

## Making a call on your watch

+   Tap Call logs and then a call log to call back.
+   Tap Keypad, enter the number you want to call, and tap the Dial icon to initiate a call.
+   Tap Contacts and then a contact to make a call.

## Configuring settings during a call

+   Adjust the call volume by rotating the Up button. Mute a call by tapping the Mute icon (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000001703172718.png" width="24" height="24"/>).
+   Enter digits by tapping the More icon (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/en-us_image_0000001704095130.png" width="24" height="24"/>) and then typing in the keypad.

## Switching calls between your phone and watch

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   eSIM calls can't be switched between your phone and watch.

1.  Make sure that your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth.
2.  On the call screen of your phone, tap the Bluetooth button in the lower right corner and choose to switch audio to your phone, watch, or earbuds.

## Viewing/Deleting call logs

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list and then go to Phone > Call logs to view your call logs (including incoming, outgoing, and missed calls).
2.  Swipe left on the screen and tap the Delete icon to delete the call log. Deleting multiple call logs at once is not supported. When you use standalone eSIM calls, the contacts on your phone do not contain any logs of calls made or received on your watch. While in Power Saver mode, you cannot delete individual call logs. However, you can delete all call logs by accessing the call log history screen and tapping Clear at the bottom.

# Activating the eSIM One Number service for China Mobile users

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Currently, the eSIM service cannot be activated at service centers.
+   Carriers are upgrading their services. For their latest support policies, please refer to Activating the eSIM service on the HUAWEI WATCH Series.
+   If you are using an Android phone/iPhone, activate the service on a HUAWEI phone on the carrier's service screen redirected from the Huawei Health app, and then connect your wearable device to your Android phone/iPhone to use the service. Make sure that you use the same HUAWEI ID to log in to the Huawei Health app.

## Requirements

+   VoLTE has been activated for your primary phone number.
+   The primary number is active and without any abnormalities such as having overdue payments or being suspended.
+   The primary number has obtained real-name authentication and has been registered on the Internet using your second-generation ID card. The ID card cannot be a military ID card (such as an officer or soldier card) or an armed police ID card (such as a police officer card).
+   The number of the carrier's SIM cards that have been registered using the certificate for registering the primary number cannot exceed four.
+   The subscriber corresponding to the certificate for registering the primary number cannot be in the carrier's blocklist.
+   The balance on your card is more than 20 CNY. Prepaid and postpaid subscribers need to top up their accounts first.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The activation requirements are for reference only. For details, contact your carrier.

## Preparations

+   The watch only supports one eSIM. If you have activated an eSIM, you will need to deregister it first.
+   The battery level of a HUAWEI WATCH 3 Series watch is higher than 30%, and that of a HUAWEI WATCH 4 Series watch is higher than 20%.
+   The primary SIM card must be inserted into the paired phone.

+   Pair your watch with a HUAWEI phone.

## Activating using a HUAWEI phone

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and go to eSIM management > One number, two devices > Activate. The eSIM service is not available when the watch is paired with a tablet.
2.  Select the number that the service needs to be activated for and touch CONTINUE to be redirected to the carrier's service screen. Go to YES > Continue > Activate, follow the onscreen instructions to enter the authentication information, and touch Confirm.
3.  On the Activate screen, make sure that the watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth so the watch can start downloading and installing eSIM files. You will then be able to view the download status and result on the watch. A message will then be displayed indicating that the eSIM service has been activated on the watch.

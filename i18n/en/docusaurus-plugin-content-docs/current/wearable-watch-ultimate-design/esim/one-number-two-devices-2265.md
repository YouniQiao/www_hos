# Activating the eSIM One Number service for China Telecom users

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The activation requirements are for reference only. For details, contact your carrier.
+   The Huawei Health app will redirect you to the China Telecom app to activate the service. (Note: Activation for new subscribers is temporarily unavailable from 00:00 on March 21, 2024. Subscribers who have activated the service are not affected. For details, call the service hotline 10000.)

## Requirements

+   The primary number is active and without any abnormalities such as having overdue payments or being suspended.
+   The primary number has obtained real-name authentication and has been registered on the Internet using your second-generation ID card. The ID card cannot be a military ID card (such as an officer or soldier card) or an armed police ID card (such as a police officer card).
+   The number of SIM cards that have been registered using the certificate for registering the primary number cannot exceed four.
+   The subscriber corresponding to the certificate for registering the primary number cannot be in the carrier's blocklist.
+   The subscriber cannot be a group IVPN or short number subscriber.
+   This feature applies only to 4G and 5G subscribers.

## Preparations

+   Pair your watch with a phone running Android 9.0/HarmonyOS 2/iOS 13.0 or later.
+   The watch only supports one eSIM. If you have activated an eSIM, you will need to deregister it first.
+   The battery level of a HUAWEI WATCH 3 Series watch is higher than 30%, and that of a HUAWEI WATCH 4 Series watch is higher than 20%.
+   The primary SIM card must be inserted into the paired phone.
+   The latest version of the China Telecom app has been installed on your phone.
+   If you're using a HUAWEI phone, perform PIN-free verification and use mobile data.
+   If you're using an iPhone, connect your watch to a WLAN first.

## Activating eSIM

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and go to eSIM management > One number, two devices > Activate. The eSIM service is not available when the watch is paired with a tablet.

1.  Select the number that the service needs to be activated for, touch Continue, follow the onscreen instructions to be redirected to the China Telecom app, use your primary number to sign in to the app, obtain the device information, and touch AGREE.
2.  Select the watch and model and then enter the eSIM Activation screen.
3.  Touch Service and enter the eSIM Activation screen.
4.  Touch Add Auxiliary Device to enter the real-name authentication screen.
5.  Follow the onscreen instructions to finish identity authentication. Enter the Add device screen, confirm the device to be added, and then touch OK to enter the eSIM Activation screen.
6.  After the order is submitted, touch View device status and enter the eSIM Activation screen to view the current service processing status.
7.  After processing is complete, touch eSIM data download and ensure that the watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth so the watch can start downloading and installing eSIM files. You will then be able to view the download status and result on the watch. A message will then be displayed indicating that the eSIM service has been activated on the watch. Be sure to manually download eSIM files. Otherwise, the service cannot be used even if it is activated.
8.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and touch eSIM management. A message will then be displayed indicating that the eSIM service has been activated.

+   When your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth and is not connected to the eSIM network, you can use Bluetooth to make calls. When your watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth and is connected to the eSIM network, you can use the eSIM card or the SIM card of your phone to make calls. When your watch is not connected to your phone and is connected to the eSIM network, you can use the eSIM card to make calls.

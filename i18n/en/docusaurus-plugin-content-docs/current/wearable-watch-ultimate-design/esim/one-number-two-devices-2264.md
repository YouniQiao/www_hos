# Deregistering from the eSIM One Number service for China Unicom users

After activating the eSIM One Number service, you will be able to manage it in the China Unicom app. For HUAWEI phones, you can also deregister from the service in Huawei Health.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Due to service limitations of China Unicom, eSIM can be re-activated only 24 hours after deregistration.

## Deregistering from the eSIM One Number service in the Huawei Health app

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is available only on HUAWEI phones.

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and touch eSIM management.
2.  On the displayed screen, touch eSIM account management > Unsubscribe, and follow the onscreen instructions.
3.  Wait until deregistration is complete. You will then receive an SMS message.

## Deregistering from the eSIM One Number service in the China Unicom app

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is available only on HUAWEI and Android phones.

Deregister from the eSIM One Number service in the China Unicom app and remove eSIM configuration files from the watch.

1.  Use your primary number to sign in to the China Unicom app, search for "eSIM" in the search bar on the top, and touch eSIM.
2.  On the eSIM screen, touch Unsubscribe and follow the onscreen instructions.
3.  Wait until deregistration is complete. You will then receive an SMS message.

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and go to Settings > Mobile network > eSIM > Settings.
2.  Touch Reset and follow the onscreen instructions to finish resetting. eSIM configuration files will then be removed.

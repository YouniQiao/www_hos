# Activating the eSIM One Number service for China Unicom users

## Requirements

+   The primary number is active and without any abnormalities such as having overdue payments or being suspended.
+   The primary number has obtained real-name authentication and has been registered on the Internet using your second-generation ID card. The ID card cannot be a military ID card (such as an officer or soldier card) or an armed police ID card (such as a police officer card).
+   The number of the carrier's SIM cards that have been registered using the certificate for registering the primary number cannot exceed four.
+   The subscriber corresponding to the certificate for registering the primary number cannot be in the carrier's blocklist.
+   The primary number cannot be used to subscribe to the Ring Back Tone service.
+   The subscriber cannot be a group IVPN or short number subscriber.
+   This feature applies only to 4G and 5G subscribers.
+   Before activating the eSIM One Number service, you are advised to consult the local carrier to check whether the service is available in the specified region. Carriers are upgrading their services. For their latest support policies, please refer to Activating the eSIM service on the HUAWEI WATCH Series. The activation requirements are for reference only. For details, contact your carrier.

## Preparations

+   Pair your watch with a phone running Android 9.0/HarmonyOS 2/iOS 9.0 or later. If you are using an iPhone, activate the service on a HUAWEI/Android phone, and then connect your wearable device to your iPhone to use the service. Make sure that you use the same HUAWEI ID to log in to the Huawei Health app.
+   Disable WLAN on your phone, insert the primary China Unicom SIM card that the service needs to activate for into the paired phone, and set the SIM card as the default mobile data card.
+   The latest version of the China Unicom app has been installed on your phone.
+   Grant the China Unicom app the permissions to access the phone call and location information. The following uses a HarmonyOS 2 phone as an example: On your phone, go to Settings > Apps & services > Apps, search for "China Unicom", and touch Permissions > Phone > Allow. On your phone, go to Settings > Apps & services > Apps, search for "China Unicom", and touch Permissions > Location information > Allow. For phones of other brands, contact the phone customer service.
+   The watch only supports one eSIM. If you have activated an eSIM, you will need to deregister it first.
+   The battery level of a HUAWEI WATCH 3 Series watch is higher than 30%, and that of a HUAWEI WATCH 4 Series/HUAWEI WATCH H7546 watch is higher than 20%.
+   The primary SIM card must be inserted into the paired phone.

## Activating offline

If you have not visited a carrier's customer service center

1.  Please take your ID card to a self-operated customer service center of China Unicom. It is recommended that you call the service hotline 10010 in advance to confirm that the service center supports offline activation and can provide the eSIM Activation QR Code.
2.  Ask the staff to help you activate the eSIM service offline by scanning the QR code at the counter. Once this is done, the staff will provide the eSIM Activation QR Code to complete the activation.
3.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and go to eSIM management > One Number > Activate > Activate offline. On the displayed scanning screen, scan the QR code provided by the carrier. It is recommended that you complete the activation at the customer service center.

If you visit a carrier's customer service center, and the staff tells you that offline activation is not supported

1.  Check whether the customer service center you visit is a self-operated customer service center of China Unicom. If not, take your ID card to a China Unicom service center. It is recommended that you call the service hotline 10010 in advance to confirm that the service center supports offline activation and can provide the eSIM Activation QR Code.
2.  If you're visiting a self-operated customer service center of China Unicom, remind the staff that you need to scan the eSIM Activation QR code provided by the carrier to activate the eSIM service, which is supported.If the staff still don't know how to activate the service, it is recommended that you call the service hotline 10010 to report this issue.

## Activating online using a HUAWEI phone

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The eSIM service is not available when the watch is paired with a tablet.

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and go to eSIM management > One Number > Activate.
2.  Select the number that the service needs to be activated for and touch CONTINUE.
3.  Select the online activation mode and touch Continue. On the displayed screen, touch OK to be redirected to the carrier's service screen, touch Next, follow the onscreen instructions to finish identity authentication, and touch Next.
4.  On the displayed screen, make sure that the watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth so the watch can start downloading and installing eSIM files. You will then be able to view the download status and result on the watch. A message will then be displayed indicating that the eSIM service has been activated on the watch.
5.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and touch eSIM management. A message will then be displayed indicating that the eSIM service has been activated.

## Activating using an Android phone

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F7/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The eSIM service is not available when the watch is paired with a tablet.

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and go to eSIM management > One Number > Activate > Activate online.
2.  Select the number that the service needs to be activated for, touch Continue, and follow the onscreen instructions to be redirected to the China Unicom app.
3.  Follow the onscreen instructions to finish identity authentication. On the service confirmation screen, touch Confirm. Ensure that the watch is connected to your phone via Bluetooth so the watch can start downloading and installing eSIM files. You will then be able to view the download status and result on the watch. A message will then be displayed indicating that the eSIM service has been activated on the watch.
4.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, and touch eSIM management. A message will then be displayed indicating that the eSIM service has been activated.

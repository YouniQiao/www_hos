# Remote Home Monitoring

You can turn on the Remote Home Monitoring feature on your HUAWEI Vision to check on your home anytime, anywhere and keep an eye on things.

## Prerequisites

Add your HUAWEI Vision to the AI Life app on your phone. For details, see: [Add a HUAWEI Vision to the AI Life app](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064466/).

## Procedure

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > MeeTime > Remote Home Monitoring, and turn on the Remote Home Monitoring switch.
2.  Open the AI Life app on your phone, select the corresponding HUAWEI Vision card on the home page, and go to Features > Remote Home Monitoring. The HUAWEI Vision will automatically answer the video call so you can see what's happening at home.
3.  When Remote Home Monitoring is connected, a Remote Home Monitoring dialog box pops up on the HUAWEI Vision. The user in front of the HUAWEI Vision can select Don't allow them to view in the dialog box to decline remote viewing from the phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The HUAWEI Vision must be powered on or in screen-off mode to initiate Remote Home Monitoring. This feature is not supported on HUAWEI Vision models without a camera.

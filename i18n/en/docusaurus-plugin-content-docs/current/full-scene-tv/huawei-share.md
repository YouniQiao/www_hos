# HUAWEI Share

With HUAWEI Share, you can share images or videos from your phone/tablet/HUAWEI PC to HUAWEI Vision, or from HUAWEI Vision to your phone/tablet/HUAWEI PC, without a USB data cable. Transferring files between multiple devices is quick and easy.

## Enable HUAWEI Share

1.  On HUAWEI Vision, go to Settings > Multi-device Collaboration > HUAWEI Share, or press and hold the menu button on the remote control, tap HUAWEI Share in the Control Panel, and turn on the HUAWEI Share switch.

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>In some scenarios (such as version upgrade or rollback, factory reset, and so on), if the device cannot be discovered after you select Visible to shared devices only, it is recommended that you select Visible to everyone (10 minutes).

2.  On your phone/tablet, swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open the Control Panel, and turn on HUAWEI Share.

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>When some devices are shared for the first time, after you turn on HUAWEI Share, you need to select Visible to everyone (10 minutes) before they can be discovered.

3.  Turn on the HUAWEI Share switch on your HUAWEI PC.

## Transfer from HUAWEI Vision to phone/tablet/HUAWEI PC

1.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Gallery, select an album, and select the image/video you want to share.
2.  Press the menu button on the remote control, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002379146594.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and select the device you want to share to.
3.  On your phone/tablet/HUAWEI PC, confirm receipt as prompted.

## Transfer from phone/tablet/HUAWEI PC to HUAWEI Vision

1.  On the sending device, select the file you want to share, and choose Share (or Share > HUAWEI Share, Show more options > HUAWEI Share, <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002425014912.png" width="24" height="24"/>\> HUAWEI Share).
2.  In the device list, select the name of the corresponding HUAWEI Vision.
3.  In the dialog box on HUAWEI Vision, tap Accept. Once the transfer is complete, you can view the transferred images or videos.

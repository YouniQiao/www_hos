# App Continuation

With app continuation, you can seamlessly pick up a task from your current device on a nearby device for an uninterrupted experience. For example, a video you are watching or a novel you are reading on your phone or tablet can be smoothly transferred to HUAWEI Vision so you can continue the task there with a better experience.

## Prerequisites

+   HUAWEI Vision and your phone or tablet must be signed in to the same HUAWEI ID.
+   WLAN and Bluetooth must be turned on for both HUAWEI Vision and your phone or tablet. Connecting both to the same WLAN is recommended.
+   The apps that support app continuation will continue to grow. The actual supported apps shall prevail.
+   On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Continuation and turn on the Continuation switch. This switch is turned on by default. If it is turned off, continuation with other devices will no longer be supported.
+   Your phone or tablet must be running HarmonyOS 5.0 or later.

## How to Use

1.  Open the content you want to continue on your phone or tablet (for example, a video you are watching). A continuation icon will appear at the bottom of the home screen of the nearby HUAWEI Vision.
2.  Tap the continuation icon on HUAWEI Vision. The video app on your phone or tablet will smoothly continue on HUAWEI Vision, and you can keep watching the video there.
3.  To continue the video from HUAWEI Vision back to your phone or tablet, tap the continuation icon that appears on your phone or tablet, and the video will be continued back on your phone or tablet.

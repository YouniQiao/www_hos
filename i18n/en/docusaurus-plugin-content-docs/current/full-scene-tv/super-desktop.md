# Super Desktop

You can set up Super Desktop between your HUAWEI Vision and HUAWEI phone to use the rich apps on your phone directly on your HUAWEI Vision. For example, you can watch short videos on your HUAWEI Vision.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>This feature is only supported on HUAWEI phones running HarmonyOS 5.0 or later.

## Connect to Super Desktop

1.  Sign in to the same HUAWEI ID on your phone and HUAWEI Vision, and turn on WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone.
2.  On your phone, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Super Desktop and turn on the Super Desktop switch.
3.  Bring your phone close to your HUAWEI Vision, go to Super Desktop on the HUAWEI Vision home screen <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002455966633.png" width="24" height="24"/>. Super Desktop will automatically detect available phones. Tap the phone you want to connect to.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002415991388.png)

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.

4.  Once connected, tap an app icon on your HUAWEI Vision to open the app.

If your phone and HUAWEI Vision are signed in to different HUAWEI IDs, you can still use Super Desktop.

**Steps:**

1.  Turn on WLAN and Bluetooth on your phone.
2.  On your phone, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Super Desktop and turn on the Super Desktop switch.
3.  Bring your phone close to your HUAWEI Vision, go to Super Desktop on the HUAWEI Vision home screen <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002458566141.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and tap Connect other HUAWEI phone. A QR code will appear on your HUAWEI Vision.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002415831772.png)

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The images are for reference only. The actual product may vary.

4.  Swipe down from the upper right corner of your phone screen to open the Control Panel, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332160269.png" width="24" height="24"/> to scan the QR code. A dialog box will appear on your phone asking whether to allow the Super Desktop connection. Tap Allow to connect to Super Desktop.
5.  Once connected, tap an app icon on your HUAWEI Vision to open the app.

## Manage apps

After connecting to Super Desktop, you can manage Super Desktop apps.

1.  Open Super Desktop on your HUAWEI Vision and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002418690477.png" width="24" height="24"/>.
2.  Tap App management.
3.  Tap Allow on your phone.
4.  On the app management screen of your HUAWEI Vision, turn the corresponding apps on or off.

    Once turned off, the app will no longer appear in Super Desktop.


## Disconnect Super Desktop

+   Disconnect from your phone: Swipe down from the upper left corner of the screen to open the Notification Panel, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002385253528.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the Super Desktop notification.
+   Disconnect from your HUAWEI Vision: On Super Desktop on your HUAWEI Vision, tap the device name in the upper left corner and select Disconnect Super Desktop.

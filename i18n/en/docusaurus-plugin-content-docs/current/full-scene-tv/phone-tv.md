# Add a HUAWEI Vision to Your Phone

Sign in to the AI Life app on your phone and to your HUAWEI Vision with the same HUAWEI ID, and you can add your HUAWEI Vision to the AI Life app on your phone and use your phone to control it.

## How to

1.  Download and install the AI Life app on your phone, and register or sign in to your HUAWEI ID.
2.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings and select HUAWEI ID.
    +   Option 1: Follow the onscreen instructions to sign in with the same HUAWEI ID used in the AI Life app on your phone, and agree to the HUAWEI Vision enhanced service agreement.
    +   Option 2: Both Huawei and non-Huawei phones can scan a QR code to sign in to a HUAWEI ID on your HUAWEI Vision quickly.
        +   On a Huawei phone, go to Settings > HUAWEI ID, tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332162233.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the upper right corner, and scan the QR code on the HUAWEI Vision account sign-in screen to sign in quickly.
        +   On a non-Huawei phone, use the AI Life app to scan the QR code. Tap + in the upper right corner of the Home tab, select Scan, and scan the QR code on the HUAWEI Vision account sign-in screen to sign in quickly.
3.  On your HUAWEI Vision, open the AI Life app from All apps, tap Settings in the upper right corner > HUAWEI Smart Home Cloud, tap Add, select a home and a room, and tap Add again.
4.  Open the AI Life app on your phone**,** and the HUAWEI Vision under the same HUAWEI ID will be added automatically.

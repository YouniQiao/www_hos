# Remote Assistance

HUAWEI Vision supports remote assistance initiated from the Smart Life app on your phone, allowing you to remotely control your HUAWEI Vision from your phone.

## Prerequisites

Add your HUAWEI Vision to the Smart Life app on your phone. For details, see: [Adding HUAWEI Vision to the Smart Life app](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16064466/).

## Procedure

1.  Open the Smart Life app on your phone, select the corresponding HUAWEI Vision card on the app's home screen, and go to Features > Family Interaction > Remote Assistance. Your phone will then initiate remote assistance to that HUAWEI Vision.
2.  After HUAWEI Vision receives the remote assistance notification, select √ Agree, and then select Allow in the dialog box that appears on HUAWEI Vision. Once the operation is complete on HUAWEI Vision, your phone automatically switches to the remote assistance screen.

3.  You can operate HUAWEI Vision from your phone using touch controls, and perform operations such as turning the microphone on or off, opening the Control Panel, and exiting assistance.

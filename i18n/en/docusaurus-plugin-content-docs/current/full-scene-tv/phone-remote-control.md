# Turn Your Phone into a Remote Control

Using the remote control feature in the Smart Life app, you can control HUAWEI Vision navigation, fast-forward/rewind, volume adjustment, and more from your phone. It's convenient and quick to control at any time. The specific steps are as follows:

## Prerequisites

1.  Connect your phone and HUAWEI Vision to the same WLAN, and make sure the Smart Life app installed on your phone and HUAWEI Vision are signed in to the same HUAWEI ID.
2.  HUAWEI Vision has been added in the Smart Life app on your phone. For details, see: [Add HUAWEI Vision in the Smart Life App/HUAWEI Vision App](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn15783914/).

## Access the Phone Remote Control

1.  On the home screen of the Smart Life app on your phone, select the corresponding HUAWEI Vision card, tap Features > Remote control to access the phone remote control screen.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002460514158.png)

2.  Tap the icons on the screen to use the following features:

    +   Fast-forward/rewind: Swipe left or right with your finger on the sliding area to fast-forward or rewind.
    +   Volume adjustment: Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002493749613.png" width="24" height="24"/> button to display a drop-down box, then slide the volume bar to adjust the HUAWEI Vision volume.
    +   Screen off/on: Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002460790060.png" width="24" height="24"/> button to turn the HUAWEI Vision screen off or on.
    +   Move the cursor: Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002493869537.png" width="24" height="24"/> button to move the cursor and tap.
    +   Return to the home screen: Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002493752157.png" width="24" height="24"/> button to return to the system home screen

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002493633669.png)

# Phone-Aware Control Card

When a HUAWEI phone comes close to a HUAWEI Vision, you can open the HUAWEI Vision control card on the phone and use it to perform quick operations on the HUAWEI Vision.

## Prerequisites

+   The HUAWEI phone is close to the HUAWEI Vision, and the HUAWEI Vision is either off or on.
+   The HUAWEI phone is unlocked and on the home screen.
+   The HUAWEI phone and the HUAWEI Vision are signed in with the same HUAWEI ID.
+   The phone runs HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

## Procedure

1.  Once the prerequisites are met, a colorful flowing light notification appears at the bottom of the HUAWEI phone.
2.  Touch and hold the navigation bar to open the Celia dialog box, which displays a HUAWEI Vision control card prompt. Tap it to open the control card.
3.  Use the HUAWEI Vision control card to perform the corresponding operations on the HUAWEI Vision.

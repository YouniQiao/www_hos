# Use floating three-button navigation

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512240Vrnk1/zh-cn/image/video/10044802_f007_FloatingNavigation.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

You can enable floating three-button navigation to match your habits.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Settings > System > System navigation, turn on the Floating three-button navigation switch, and you can:

+   Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512240Vrnk1/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/navigation_back.png" width="24" height="24"/> to return to the previous level.
+   Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512240Vrnk1/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/navigation_home.png" width="24" height="24"/> to return to the home screen.
+   Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512240Vrnk1/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/navigation_task.png" width="24" height="24"/> to view recent tasks.

+   Touch and hold the three-button navigation bar and drag it horizontally to adjust its position.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After you enable floating three-button navigation, the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen (for example, touch and hold the navigation bar to wake up Celia) will no longer work.

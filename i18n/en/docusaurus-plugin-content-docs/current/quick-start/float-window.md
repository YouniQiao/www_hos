# Use the Floating Window

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10045865_f001_SuspensionWindow.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Keep an extra app on hand in a floating window, without exiting the current screen — and enjoy productive leisure on your own terms!

**Try it out**

**Switch to full-view display**: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_muti_window_maximize1.png" width="24" height="24" alt="菜单栏图标"/> at the top of the floating window.

**Minimize the floating window**: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_muti_window_minimize.png" width="24" height="24" alt="最小化图标"/> at the top of the floating window to minimize the window to a side bar. Tap the side bar or swipe inwards to expand the side bar to a floating window.

**Switch to Split-screen mode**: If you are not on the home screen, tap the status bar at the top of the floating window, drag it to the left or right side (in landscape mode) or the top or bottom side (in portrait mode) of the screen, hold for seconds, and release.

**Note**

The landscape floating window feature is only available for certain apps. Actual availability shall prevail.

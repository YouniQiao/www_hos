# Adjust the default volume

When playing audio or video, or when you receive a call or notification, you can quickly adjust the device's default volume.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Press a volume button, swipe left on the volume bar to expand the volume panel, then drag the sliders to adjust the default volume for media, ringtones, alarms, and calls.

# Smart Grip: Easier One-Handed Operation

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/video/10044835_f009_reachability.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Smart Grip intelligently recognizes how you're holding your phone, so buttons follow your hand and automatically move to the side that's easier to reach — making one-handed operation effortless.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take answering a call as an example:

1. Go to Phone > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_AITouchmore.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Settings > Incoming call notifications when unlocked, and select Banner.

2. Go to Settings > System > Smart Grip and turn on the Smart Grip switch.

3. When a call comes in, pick up your phone and the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_call.png" width="24" height="24"/> and <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_end_call.png" width="24" height="24"/> buttons appear on the side of the screen for easy one-handed operation:
   - Hold the phone in your right hand, and the buttons appear on the right side of the screen.
   - Hold the phone in your left hand, and the buttons appear on the left side of the screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ Smart Grip currently supports Phone, MeeTime calls, and some voice and video call apps. Unlock your phone and go to the home screen to use it.

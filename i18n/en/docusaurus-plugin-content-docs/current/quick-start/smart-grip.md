# Smart Reachability for Easier One-Handed Operations

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044835_f009_reachability.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Smart reachability can intelligently identify your holding gesture, and automatically adjust the onscreen buttons to the side that is easier for you to reach.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Phone > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_AITouchmore.png" width="24" height="24" alt="更多图标"/> > Settings > Notifications when unlocked and select **Banner**.

2.  Go to Settings > System > Smart reachability and enable **Incoming call banners**.

3.  When there is an incoming call, the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_call.png" width="24" height="24" alt="拨打图标"/> and <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_end_call.png" width="24" height="24" alt="挂断图标"/> buttons are displayed on the side of the screen, making it easier for you to reach.

    +   If you hold the phone with your right hand, the buttons will be displayed on the right side of the screen.

    +   If you hold the phone with your left hand, the buttons will be displayed on the left side of the screen.

**Note**

Currently, **Smart reachability** supports phone calls, MeeTime calls, as well as voice and video calls for certain apps. Unlock your phone and go to the home screen to use this feature.

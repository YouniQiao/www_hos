# Media Control Panel: Easily Adjust What's Playing

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/figure/10169850_f004_Audio_Control.png" width="360" height="486"/>


From the Media Control Panel, you can easily switch tracks and control playback progress.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Swipe down from the top right of the screen to open the Control Panel, then tap an empty area of the Media Control Panel.
2.  The Media Control Panel displays the audio and video apps currently running. You can pause or resume audio, drag the progress bar to quickly adjust playback progress, and switch between tracks.

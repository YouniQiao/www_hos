# Navigate with screen gestures

Make the most of your full-screen display with smooth, easy swipes.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Methods

**Go back to the previous level:** Swipe inward from the left or right edge of the screen.

**Return to the home screen**: Swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen.

**Open recent tasks**: Swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen and pause.

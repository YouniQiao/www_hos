# Direct Your Device with a Wave of the Hand

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044802_f003_GestureNavigate.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Navigate on your device with a few simple hand gestures.

**Try it out**

**Back**: Swipe inward from the left or right edge.

**Return to the home screen**: Swipe up from the bottom of the screen.

**Access the Recent tasks screen**: Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and hold.

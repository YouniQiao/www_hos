# Lock tasks to prevent one-tap cleanup

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/video/20005697_f001_LockingTasks.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


Lock tasks in the multitasking center to launch them quickly and prevent them from being cleared with one tap.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe up from the bottom of the phone screen and pause to open the multitasking center. You can:

+   Swipe down on a task card to lock the task. Once locked, <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_public_focus_lock.png" width="24" height="24"/> appears in the upper right corner of the card. Swipe down again or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_camera_public_focus_lock.png" width="24" height="24"/> to unlock it.
+   Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_MultiWindowdock_delete.png" width="24" height="24"/> to end all unlocked tasks with one tap.

# Screen recording: capture the best moments on your screen

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512031Znvtc/zh-cn/image/figure/10044780_f001_ScreenRecord.png" width="360" height="486"/>


You can record great content on your screen as a video and share it with family and friends.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe down from the upper right of the screen to open the Control Panel, touch Screen recording to start recording; touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512031Znvtc/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_record_stop.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the Live Window at the top of the screen to stop recording.

Touch the Live Window to expand it from the capsule to the card view. Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512031Znvtc/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/mic_slash_fill-01.png" width="24" height="24"/> to turn on the microphone, or touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512031Znvtc/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/hand_point_up_tap_fill-01.png" width="24" height="24"/> to turn off touch trails.

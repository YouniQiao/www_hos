# Scan Anything, Even from a Distance or with a Damaged Code

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/video/20013791_f001_Scan.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Use the Scan feature to scan codes without opening an app first. It handles complex scenarios with ease, including codes that are far away, damaged, or distorted, and takes you straight to the service in one step.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe down from the upper right side of the screen to open the Control Panel, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_scan.png" width="24" height="24"/>, select Scan, and adjust the position of your phone so that the QR code appears in the viewfinder.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Scan currently supports a wide range of scenarios, including mobile payments, ordering food, bike sharing, parking fees, package pickup, and EV charging.

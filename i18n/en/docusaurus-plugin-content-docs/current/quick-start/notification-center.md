# Use the Notification Panel

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/figure/10044804_f005_NotificationPanel.png" width="360" height="486"/>


When your device is unlocked, you can view or manage notifications in the Notification Panel.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe down from the upper left of the screen to open the Notification Panel and view all notifications. You can also swipe left on a notification and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_notification_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/> to pin or dismiss the notification, or access more settings.

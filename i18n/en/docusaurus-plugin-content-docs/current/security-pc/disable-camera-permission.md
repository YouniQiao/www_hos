# Turn Off Camera and Other Sensitive Permissions with One Tap

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_SuperPrivacy.png" width="720" height=""/>

Turn on Super Privacy Mode with one tap to block other apps and services from accessing the camera, microphone, and location information, giving you a higher level of privacy protection.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Slide the Super Privacy Mode switch on the right side of the PC keyboard toward the touchpad (the right side of the switch turns red) to revoke access to the camera, microphone, and location information.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After Super Privacy Mode is turned on, some apps and services may be affected. Actual behavior may vary.

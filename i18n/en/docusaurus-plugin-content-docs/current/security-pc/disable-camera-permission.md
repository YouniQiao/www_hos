# Keep a Low Profile, with Super Privacy Mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_SuperPrivacy.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**Super Privacy** bolsters privacy protections by preventing apps or services from accessing your **camera**, **microphone**, and **Location** information.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Flip the **Super Privacy** switch on the right side of the computer keyboard towards the touchpad (the switch displays in red) to disable the **camera**, **microphone**, and **Location** permissions.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

**Super Privacy** may affect the use of certain apps and services.

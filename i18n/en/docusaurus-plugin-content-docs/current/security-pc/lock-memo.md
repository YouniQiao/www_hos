# Lock Private Notes

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_notepad_lock.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Enabling **Add lock** protects your private notes from prying eyes.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Before using this feature, remember to log in to your **HUAWEI ID**.

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click to open Notepad.

2.  Go to **Note** > **All notes** in the left pane, and click your desired note in the note list.

3.  In the right pane, go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_more_1.png" width="24" height="24"/> > **Add lock**, enter the password, and click OK to verify.

4.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_unlock.png) to lock the note.

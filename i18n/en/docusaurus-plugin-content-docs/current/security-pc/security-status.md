# Check your device's security status

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_SafetyBoard.png" width="720" height=""/>

Through Privacy & Security Center, you can view security recommendations, privacy data, and more, and change your privacy and security settings as needed to keep your PC more secure.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Privacy & security in the left sidebar. From there, you can:

+   **View security recommendations:** When your PC detects a possible security risk, a security recommendation will appear at the top of the right pane, and you can follow the prompt to complete the setup. To ignore a security recommendation, click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_cancel.png) in the upper right corner of the prompt.
+   **View permission access:** In the Privacy data panel on the right, you can directly view how sensitive permissions such as location, camera, and microphone are being used, including the number of apps that have accessed them and the access duration. To manage access permissions, click the corresponding permission (such as location, camera, or microphone) to open its details page and configure the settings.
+   **Set up privacy and security features:** Configure more privacy and security features at the bottom of the right pane, such as Password Vault, Strong Shield mode, and Smart Fill.

# Smart Security Protection for Safer Calls

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/video/vid_FaceSwap_Check.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Smart Security Protection intelligently detects face-swap traces during video calls. If it detects a possible AI face-swap risk from the other party, a prompt appears to help you guard against potential fraud.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. Click the Settings icon in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Privacy & security in the left sidebar.

2. On the right, click Harassment and fraud interception > Face-swap detection, and turn on the switch for Detect during video calls. When you turn it on for the first time, follow the onscreen instructions to download the AI face-swap detection plug-in over the internet.

3. Once enabled, face information is automatically detected during video calls. If a possible AI face-swap risk from the other party is detected, a dialog box prompts you about the risk.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202602261wvwjj/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ Some apps may not support video call detection. The actual situation prevails.

+ Face detection is performed only locally on your device. Your personal information is not uploaded to the cloud.

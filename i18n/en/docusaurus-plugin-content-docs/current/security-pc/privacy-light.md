# Stay Informed and Take Control with Privacy Indicator

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_PrivacyIndicator.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Worry about apps secretly using permissions? **Privacy indicator** shows you which apps are using sensitive permissions, and makes it easy for you to adjust your privacy settings.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

The **Privacy indicator** in the Status bar in the lower right corner of the home screen will alert you whenever an app accesses a sensitive permission like **Location**, **Camera**, or **Microphone**.

+   Yellow dot (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_PrivacyIndicator_yellow_2.png" width="24" height="24"/>) displays on the right of the Control Panel icon: An app is using the Microphone permission.

+   Orange dot (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_PrivacyIndicator_orange_2.png" width="24" height="24"/>) displays on the right of the Control Panel icon: An app is using the Camera permission.

+   <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/ic_controlcenter_location_filled.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon displays in the Status bar in the lower right corner: An app is using the Location permission.

Click <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_controlcenter_icon.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the lower right corner of the home screen to open **Control Panel**. At the top of **Control Panel**, you can view the apps that are using the permission. Click the prompt to view details, or click the permission access record of an app to go to the permission management screen and set the permission scope of the app.

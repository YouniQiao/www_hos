# Secured App Data with App Lock

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_AppLock.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Secure your essential office apps, such as Files, Email, and Notepad, with App Lock, ensuring access is granted only after verifying your identity for maximum privacy and protection.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Privacy & security in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, go to **App Lock** > **Get started**. When enabling App Lock for the first time, set Screen lock or Privacy lock as the verification method. If your face or fingerprint cannot be recognized, you can enter the password to access the app.

3.  You can add App Lock in either of the following ways:

    +   Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to enter App chest, select the icon of the app to be locked, right-click the icon, and choose Add lock.

    +   Go to **Settings** > **Privacy & security** > **App Lock**. Once your identity is verified, toggle on the switch of the app that you wish to lock.

4.  After the lock is added, click the locked app and enter the unlock screen password or use your fingerprint by tapping Password, Fingerprint recognition, or Face recognition for verification. If your device supports fingerprint and facial recognition and you have enrolled a fingerprint or face, your device will preferentially use facial or fingerprint recognition for verification.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   By default, the app will be locked after being minimized. You need to verify your identity to open the app again. You can also go to **Settings** > **Privacy & security** > **App Lock** and select Lock apps on exit from the drop-down list of Locking preference.

+   Face recognition is only available on certain models.

# Store Images and Videos Safe from Prying Eyes

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_gallery_hide.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Enable **Hide** for images and videos in **Gallery** to keep them private.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Gallery to open it.

2.  Select a single image or video or press and hold Ctrl/Shift and select multiple images and videos. Then, right-click and choose Hide from the shortcut menu, and click OK.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

Click **Photos & videos** in the left pane of **Gallery** and go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_more_1.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Hidden items in the right pane. Once your identity is verified (via **Password**, **Fingerprint recognition**, or **Face recognition**), you'll be able to view all hidden images and videos.

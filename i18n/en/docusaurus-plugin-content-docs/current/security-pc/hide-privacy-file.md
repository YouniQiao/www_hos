# Hide private photos and videos

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_gallery_hide.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

You can set private photos or videos in Gallery as hidden and set access permissions to protect your privacy.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery. You can also click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) at the bottom of the home screen to enter App Center, and click Gallery to open it.
2.  Click to select a single photo/video, or press and hold Ctrl/Shift and click to select multiple photos and videos, right-click and select Hide, then click OK.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Click Photos and videos in the sidebar on the left of Gallery, then click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_more_1.png) > Hidden albums on the right. After identity verification (lock screen password or fingerprint), you can view all hidden photos and videos.

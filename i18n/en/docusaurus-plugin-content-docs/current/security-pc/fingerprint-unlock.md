# Quick Fingerprint Unlock

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_settings_fingerprints.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Unlock your computer quickly and safely, and get straight to the home screen, by tapping your finger against the fingerprint sensor (the Power button).

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Biometrics & screen lock in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, click Fingerprint recognition, enter the password, and click OK to verify.

3.  Go to **New fingerprint** > **Add fingerprint**. Place your finger against the fingerprint sensor (the Power button), lift and move your finger slightly, repeat this step until Enrollment successful is displayed, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the enrollment.

4.  Once your fingerprint is enrolled, you can place your finger against the fingerprint sensor (the Power button) to unlock the screen when the screen is locked or turned off.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

The position of the fingerprint sensor may vary depending on the model.

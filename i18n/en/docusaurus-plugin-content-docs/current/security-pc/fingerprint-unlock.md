# Use a fingerprint to unlock the screen quickly

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_settings_fingerprints.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

You can enroll a fingerprint, and then touch the fingerprint sensor (located on the power button) with the enrolled finger when the PC is locked or the screen is off to unlock the screen quickly.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Biometrics & passwords in the left sidebar.
2.  On the right, click Fingerprint, enter your password, and click OK to verify.
3.  Click New fingerprint > Start, place your finger on the fingerprint sensor (located on the power button), hold it there briefly, and then lift it. Repeat until you are prompted that enrollment is successful, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the remaining steps.
4.  When the PC is locked or the screen is off, touch the fingerprint sensor (located on the power button) with the enrolled finger to unlock the screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

The fingerprint sensor may be located in a different place on different models. The actual product prevails.

# Security protection, real-time protection against virus threats

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_VirusProtection.png" alt="Security protection" width="720" height="540" />

Use **Security protection** to scan your device for virus threats, protect against risks in real time, and keep your device safe.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy & security > Advanced > Security protection** and make sure the **Security protection** switch is turned on (it is on by default). Once enabled, it protects your device against risks in real time and notifies you when virus threats are detected.
2. You can also click ![File Manager](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_filemanager.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to open **File Manager**, select the folder or file you want to scan, right-click it, and choose **More > Scan with Security protection**.
3. After the scan is complete, if the Security protection screen shows a risk file alert, you can click **Delete** or **Ignore** to the right of the risk file in the **Unhandled risks** list.

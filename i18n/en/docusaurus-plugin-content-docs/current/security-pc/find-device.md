# How to find a lost device

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_FindDevice.png" width="720" height=""/>

Turn on Find Device to locate your PC, phone, tablet, and other devices, keeping your devices and data safe.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Turn on Find Device on your PC**

Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings. Sign in to your HUAWEI ID and complete identity verification as prompted. Find My PC will then be turned on automatically.

**Find your lost PC from another device**

1.  On another device, go to Settings, sign in to the same HUAWEI ID as the lost device, and complete identity verification as prompted.
2.  Find and open the Find Device app. In the device list, click the device you want to find. You can then locate it, turn on Lost Mode, erase data, and more.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If your PC is not connected to the network or is powered off, the device is offline. You can set an online notification, and you will be notified once the PC comes online.

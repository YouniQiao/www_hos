# Communication Shield, Safeguarding Call Security

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_FaceSwap_Check.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Your device intelligently detects risks such as fraudulent communication scripts, face swapping, or voice changing during video or voice calls, and displays a risk alert to help you avoid potential fraud.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Privacy & security in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, go to **Scam Guard** > **Communication shield**, and toggle on the Check video calls and Check voice calls switches.

3.  Once enabled, your device will automatically detect facial and voice information during video or voice calls, and display a pop-up message if the caller shows signs of AI-generated faces or voice.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   Some apps may not support Communication shield. Actual usage shall prevail.

+   This process is performed entirely on your device, and your personal information is not uploaded to the cloud.

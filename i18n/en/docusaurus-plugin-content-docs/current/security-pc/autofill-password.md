# Autofill accounts and passwords

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_PasswordVault.png" width="720" height=""/>

With Password Vault, you can save the account and password for an app or website the first time you sign in, making it easy to sign in quickly next time. No one else, including the system, can access your data.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Before using this feature, set up your identity authentication information (lock screen password, fingerprint).

1.  The first time you sign in to an app or website, enter your account and password, then tap Save password in the dialog box that appears.
2.  The next time you sign in to the app or website, select the corresponding account as prompted and verify your identity (lock screen password, fingerprint) to automatically fill in the account and password.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Some apps and websites do not support Password Vault. The actual situation shall prevail.

# Log In Faster with Password Vault

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_PasswordVault.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

**Password Vault** saves your app and website login accounts and passwords to facilitate quick and easy access upon the first login. It also prevents any outside actors, including the device system, from obtaining your data.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Before using this feature, set identity authentication information (**Password** and **Fingerprint recognition**).

1.  Enter the account and password, follow the onscreen instructions, and click Save, to log in to apps and websites with greater ease all future times.

2.  When you log in to the app or website the next time, follow the onscreen instructions, select the corresponding account, and authenticate the account (with Password or Fingerprint recognition) to have your account and password automatically filled.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

**Password Vault** is only supported by certain apps and websites.

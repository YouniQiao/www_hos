# Celia Subtitles: Real-Time Subtitles

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/figure/10104442_f005_AISubtitles.png" width="360" height="486"/>

When it's not convenient to listen to a video, your phone can display subtitles in real time, and it can also translate foreign languages into Chinese subtitles, making open courses easier to follow.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  While a video is playing, touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. Once Celia opens, say the command: "Turn on Celia Subtitles."

2.  In the floating window, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_stylus_settings.png" width="24" height="24"/> to open the settings screen, select Media sound as the sound source, and choose the subtitle language based on the video's language.

3.  While the video is playing, subtitles are displayed in real time.

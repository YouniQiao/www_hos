# One-tap skin beauty and smart cutout

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202509160qo5Sr/zh-cn/image/video/20005895_f001_Cutout.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

With a gentle press, you can quickly beautify the skin of a portrait. You can also intelligently extract subjects such as people and pets, then paste them into a diary or share them with friends.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Gallery and open an image containing a person or pet, then perform the following:

+   **One-tap skin beauty**: Press and hold a person in the image with one finger, then tap One-tap skin beauty in the menu.
+   **Smart cutout**: Press and hold a person, animal, or other subject in the image with one finger. Once it is recognized and extracted, keep pressing and holding to drag it to the SuperHub floating window, or follow the onscreen instructions to copy it or perform other operations.

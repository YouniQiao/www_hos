# Circle to select with your knuckle for quick search

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512031Znvtc/zh-cn/image/video/20005923_f001_Select_Content.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

When you come across an object that interests you, circle it on the screen with your knuckle and let Celia search for it by image to find encyclopedia entries or similar items. You can also save or share the selected area separately.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Tap the screen with one knuckle and press a little harder to draw a closed shape around the object. You can:

+   **Smart selection**: When the circled area covers a fairly complete object, your phone intelligently recognizes and selects the object's edges, and you can save or share the selected area.
+   **Search by image**: Tap Search by image below to have Celia look up encyclopedia entries or links to similar products for the object.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After circling, if you need to adjust the position and size of the selected area, or add doodles, mosaics, and more, tap the selection shape and choose a selection type other than Smart.

# Memo, one-tap smart formatting and rewriting

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/video/10044681_f014_Summary.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Let Memo intelligently adjust your text layout and polish and optimize your content with a single tap, making office work easier.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Memo > Notes, open a note, and tap anywhere in the body to enter editing mode.
2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_notepad_AIsummary.png" width="24" height="24"/> and choose as needed:
    +   Smart summary: Generate a one-sentence topic and a full-text summary.
    +   Smart formatting: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_spinner_small.png" width="24" height="24"/> and select a layout format, such as Meeting.
    +   Polish and rewrite: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_spinner_small.png" width="24" height="24"/> and select a language style, such as Formal.
3.  Once generation is complete, you can choose to replace the original text, or choose Add to and save the result to a new note.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

The text must be at least 100 characters long to use Smart summary.

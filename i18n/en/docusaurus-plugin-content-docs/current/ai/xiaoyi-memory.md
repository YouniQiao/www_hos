# Celia Memo, quickly record information

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202410131vcl7z/zh-cn/image/video/10044717_f014_highspeed.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


Let Celia Memo help you quickly record orders, important dates, and other information. Once recorded, you can look it up anytime — no matter how much information you have, you'll never forget it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take recording a high-speed rail order as an example:

1.  After purchasing your ticket, wake up Celia directly on the order screen, or drag an image containing the order information onto the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen.
2.  Give Celia a voice or text command, for example: "Help me remember the order on the screen" or "Remember the order in the image."
3.  After Celia records it, you can ask Celia anytime, for example: "Check my high-speed rail order."

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Celia Memo can record commonly used orders such as train tickets, flight tickets, and hotel bookings by recognizing the screen or sending an image.

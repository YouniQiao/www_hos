# Smart text extraction from images

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/figure/10091161_f001_RecognizeText.png" width="360" height="486"/>


Easily extract text from images to boost your productivity at work and in your studies.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Gallery, open an image, and touch and hold the text in the image with one finger to recognize and extract it.
2.  Select the recognized text and follow the onscreen instructions to copy it or perform other operations.

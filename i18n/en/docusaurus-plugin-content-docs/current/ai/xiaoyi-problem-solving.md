# Celia Deep Problem Solving, Your Gold-Medal AI Teaching Assistant

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202503231uozzo/zh-cn/image/video/10104441_f003_celiasolve.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Stuck on a tough problem, or keep getting the same question wrong? Let Celia Deep Problem Solving answer your questions. Its guided approach sparks independent thinking so you learn more efficiently.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On the home screen, open the Celia app, tap Discover > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202503231uozzo/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_search.png" width="24" height="24"/> , search for "**Celia Deep Problem Solving**", and open it.
2.  Choose a suitable solving mode as needed, such as Visual Problem Solving.
3.  Tap Visual Problem Solving, take a photo to recognize the problem, and the agent will generate an illustrated video explanation.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Celia Deep Problem Solving also supports other modes, such as AI interactive explanations and AI-assisted writing.

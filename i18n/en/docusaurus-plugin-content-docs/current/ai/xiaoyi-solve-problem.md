# Have a question about your phone? Try asking Celia

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025102603wmgm/zh-cn/image/video/10044717_f011_celiacontrol.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

When you have questions about how a feature works while using your phone, let Celia answer them for you. You can also have Celia quickly perform the operation for you.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen to wake Celia, then ask your question about how a feature works, for example: "How do I set HUAWEI Share so everyone can discover me?"

+   Tap the blue text in the answer to go straight to the setting.

+   Tap Celia Can Help below the answer to have Celia perform the operation for you.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If you use three-key navigation, you can wake Celia by touching and holding the power button, using voice wakeup, and other methods.

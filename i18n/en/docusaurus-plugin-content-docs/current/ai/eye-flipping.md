# Immersive reading with eye-controlled page turning

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/video/20006247_f001_turnpages.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

When reading an e-book, you don't need to turn pages manually — the book turns pages automatically as your gaze moves, freeing your hands for a fully immersive reading experience.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Open an e-book in HUAWEI Books, tap the middle of the screen to open the toolbar, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512040kZZow/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_flipping_page.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enable eye-controlled page turning.
2.  While reading, keep the sensing area at the top of the screen clean and your face directly facing the screen. When you reach the bottom of a page, your phone will show a page-turning prompt based on your gaze — simply look up and the book turns the page automatically.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

When using eye-controlled page turning, keep your face 20–50 cm from the screen, and avoid using it in dim light, backlight, or strong light.

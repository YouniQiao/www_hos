# Celia Answers Your Device Questions

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/figure/10044717_f011_celiacontrol.png" width="360" height="486"/>


When you have questions about how to use or operate your phone's features, you can ask Celia for answers, and it can also help you quickly perform operations.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. After Celia opens, say what you want to know, for example: "How do I set dark mode?"
2.  Tap the blue text (for example, Dark mode) to go straight to the setting.

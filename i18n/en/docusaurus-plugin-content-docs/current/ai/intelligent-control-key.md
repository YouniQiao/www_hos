# Direct Access, with Smart Controls

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044744_f001_SmartControls.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Activating Smart controls allows you to bypass the lock screen by opening a preset app or service, like Camera, Recorder, or a payment code.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Settings > System > Shortcuts & gestures > Smart Control.

2.  Tap **Double-tap** and select the app or service you wish to open by tapping the Smart controls button twice. If you have not enrolled your fingerprint, follow the onscreen instructions to enroll your fingerprint and enable the fingerprint unlock feature.

3.  Whenever the screen is locked, you can tap the Smart controls (Power) button twice to directly access your desired app or service.

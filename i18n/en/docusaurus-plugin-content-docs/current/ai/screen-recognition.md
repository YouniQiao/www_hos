# Celia Screen Recognition and Chat, Authoritative Encyclopedia Q&A

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/video/10229830_f001_celiascreen.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


Have a question about what's on your screen? Try asking Celia. Celia can automatically recognize content on the screen and provide answers from encyclopedic knowledge covering animals, plants, history, culture, science, geography, and more.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

When you see a picture of a bird and want to know its species, simply stay on the current screen:

1.  Touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. Once Celia opens, ask: "What species is the bird on the left?"
2.  Celia will recognize and understand the content on the screen and provide a professional answer.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Celia can also recognize visual information in videos and answer your questions about it.

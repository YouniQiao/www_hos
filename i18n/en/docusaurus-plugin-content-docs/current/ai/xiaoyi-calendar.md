# Drag into Celia to quickly create a schedule

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512031Znvtc/zh-cn/image/video/20005937_f001_AIschedule.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Drag text into Celia and let Celia help you quickly create a schedule.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Select text that contains time information, touch and hold until it floats, then drag it onto the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. After Celia opens, follow the onscreen instructions and tap Extract schedule.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After the schedule is created, tap the schedule card to make further edits.

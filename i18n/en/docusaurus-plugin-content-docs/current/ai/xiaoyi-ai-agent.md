# Celia AI Agents: Professional Skills in One Step

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202504042smqrg/zh-cn/image/video/10104441_f002_celiasamrt.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Celia AI agents provide professional services such as translation and in-depth problem solving. You can choose different agents based on your needs, and add agents to the home screen for quick access.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Using the Celia Translation Assistant agent as an example:

1.  Open the Celia app on the home screen, tap Plaza > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202504042smqrg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_search.png" width="24" height="24"/> , search for "Celia Translation Assistant", and open it.

2.  In Celia Translation Assistant, follow the onscreen instructions to translate the content you need.

3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202504042smqrg/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/phone_ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner > Add to home screen to create a home screen shortcut for Celia Translation Assistant.

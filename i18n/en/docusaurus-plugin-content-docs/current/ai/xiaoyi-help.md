# Celia Lends a Hand for Life's Big and Small Tasks

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508270frczV/zh-cn/image/video/10104441_f005_xiaoyihelp.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Rushed off your feet before a trip and no time to cache videos in advance? Just say the word and let Celia lend a hand. Celia can also book flights and buy products for you, making life that much easier.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen to wake Celia, then say your task, for example: "Cache episode 2 of the TV series XXX on HUAWEI Video for me." Celia will carry out the task for you.

While the task is running, swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen to hide it and let it continue in the background. Once hidden, tap the navigation bar > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508270frczV/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_xiaoyi_arrow_right.png" width="24" height="24"/> to jump to the task screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+ Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

+ When using three-key navigation, you can wake Celia by touching and holding the power button, using voice wakeup, and other methods. While Celia is carrying out a task, it switches to the navigation bar style, and the three-key navigation bar is hidden.

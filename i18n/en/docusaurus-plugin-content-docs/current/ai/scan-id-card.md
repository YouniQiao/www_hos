# Scan an ID card to combine both sides on one page

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/figure/10084631_f013_ScanNationalIDCard.png" width="360" height="486"/>


Scan an ID card with your phone, and the front and back sides are automatically displayed on the same page, making it easy to print both on a single A4 page. You can also add a custom watermark to the ID card image to prevent misuse.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Swipe down from the top right of your phone to open the Control Panel, then go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_scan.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Scan > ID card.

2.  Point the camera at the front and back of the ID card, and tap the shutter for each side. Once recognition is complete, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_comfirm.png" width="24" height="24"/> to generate the image. You can also tap Anti-theft watermark to add a watermark to the generated ID card image.
3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_save_AILEN.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions to save it.

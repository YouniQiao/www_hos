# Quickly extract the subject of an image

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/video/20005895_f001_Cutout.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

With a gentle press, you can quickly extract subjects such as people and pets from an image, then paste them into a diary or share them with friends.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Gallery and open the image from which you want to extract the subject.
2.  Touch and hold the subject in the image with one finger. Once it is recognized and extracted, follow the onscreen instructions to copy it or perform other operations.

# Drag into Celia to extract image information

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044717_f012_tuoruxiaoyi.png" width="560" height=""/>


Drag an image into Celia and let Celia quickly extract tables or text for you.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Gallery, drag an image containing a table or text onto the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen, open Celia, and follow the onscreen instructions to tap Table extraction or Text extraction.

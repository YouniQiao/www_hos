# Touch and hold the navigation bar to quickly wake Celia

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044717_f010_AIVoice.png" width="560" height=""/>


Wake Celia in one step anytime, anywhere, for more convenient operation.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen to quickly wake Celia. Give Celia a voice or text command, such as "Set an alarm for 5 PM."

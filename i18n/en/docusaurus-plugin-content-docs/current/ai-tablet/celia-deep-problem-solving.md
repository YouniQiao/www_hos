# Celia Deep Problem Solving, Your Gold-Standard AI Teaching Assistant

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10104441_f003_celiasolve.png" alt="Celia Deep Problem Solving" width="560" height="" />

Stuck on a tough problem, or keep making the same mistake on the same question? Let Celia Deep Problem Solving answer your questions. Its guided approach sparks independent thinking so you learn more efficiently.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. On the home screen, open the **Celia** app, tap **Explore** > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_find.png" width="24" height="24"/> , search for "Celia Deep Problem Solving", and open it.
2. Choose a suitable solving mode as needed, such as **Visual Problem Solving**.
3. Tap **Visual Problem Solving**, take a photo to recognize the problem, and the agent will generate a solution video with both illustrations and text.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Celia Deep Problem Solving also supports other solving modes, such as **AI Interactive Explanation** and **AI Writing Tutoring**.

# Quickly extract the subject of an image

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/20005895_f001_Cutout.png" width="560" height=""/>

With just a light press, you can quickly extract subjects such as people and pets from an image, then paste them into a diary or share them with friends.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Gallery and open the image you want to extract the subject from.
2.  Touch and hold the subject in the image with one finger. Once it is recognized and extracted, follow the onscreen instructions to copy it or perform other operations.

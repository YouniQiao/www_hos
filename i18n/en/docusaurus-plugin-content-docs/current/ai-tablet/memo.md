# Notepad: One-Tap Smart Formatting and Rewriting

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044681_f014_Summary.png" width="560" height=""/>

Let Notepad intelligently adjust your text formatting and polish and optimize your content with a single tap, making office work a breeze.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Notepad > Notes, open a note, and tap anywhere in the body to enter editing mode.
2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_notepad_AIsummary.png" width="24" height="24"/>, then choose as needed:
    +   Smart summary: Generates a one-sentence topic and a full-text summary.
    +   Smart formatting: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_spinner_small.png" width="24" height="24"/> and select a formatting style, such as meeting format.
    +   Polish and rewrite: Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202509181mmlk4/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_spinner_small.png" width="24" height="24"/> and select a language style, such as formal.
3.  Once generation is complete, you can replace the original text or select Add to to save the result to a new note.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

The text must contain at least 100 characters to use Smart summary.

# Celia Subtitles, real-time subtitle generation

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10104442_f005_AISubtitles.png" width="560" height=""/>

When it's not convenient to listen to a video, your phone can display subtitles in real time, and can also translate foreign languages into Chinese subtitles, making it easier to watch open online courses.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  While a video is playing, touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. After Celia opens, say the command: "Turn on Celia Subtitles."

2.  In the floating window, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512250Xzgpn/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_stylus_settings.png" width="24" height="24"/> to enter the settings screen, select Media sound as the sound source, and select the subtitle language based on the video's language.

3.  While the video is playing, subtitles are displayed in real time.

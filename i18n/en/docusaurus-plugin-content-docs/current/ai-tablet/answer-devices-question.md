# Celia Answers Your Device Questions

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn-dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10229830_f001_celiascreen.png" width="560" height=""/>

When you have questions about how to use or operate your phone's features, you can ask Celia to help you out, and it can also help you quickly perform actions.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn-dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Touch and hold the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. Once Celia opens, say what you'd like to know, for example: "How do I set up Dark mode".
2.  Tap the blue text (such as Dark mode) to go straight to the setting.

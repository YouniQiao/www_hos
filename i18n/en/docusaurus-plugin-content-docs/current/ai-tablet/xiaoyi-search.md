# Celia Search: Quickly Find Images and Documents

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/20005959_f001_HiSearch.png" width="560" height=""/>

With Celia Search, you can quickly find images, documents, apps, settings items, and more, and even jump straight to where a document is stored.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Swipe down from the middle of the phone screen to open Celia Search.
2.  In the search box, enter keywords such as image content, shooting location, or document name, for example, "beach trip" or "illustration courseware".
3.  Search results appear at the top. Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202505221hzqiz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_AISearch_direct.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the end of a document to jump straight to where it is stored.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If swiping down does not open Celia Search, go to Settings > Home screen & personalization > Home screen settings and make sure the Swipe down on home screen switch is turned on.

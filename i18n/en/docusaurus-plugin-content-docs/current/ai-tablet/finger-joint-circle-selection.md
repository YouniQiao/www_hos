# Circle to select with your knuckle for quick search

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/20005923_f001_Select_Content.png" width="560" height=""/>

When you come across an object that interests you, you can circle it on the screen with your knuckle and have Celia identify it and search for an encyclopedia entry or a matching product. You can also save or share the selected area separately.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Tap the screen with one knuckle and press a little harder to draw a closed shape around the object. You can:

+   **Smart selection**: When the circled area covers a fairly complete object, your phone intelligently recognizes it and selects the object's edges, and you can save or share the selected area.
+   **Image search**: Tap Image search below to have Celia search for an encyclopedia entry about the object or a link to a matching product.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After circling, if you need to adjust the position or size of the selected area, or add doodles, mosaics, and more, tap the selection shape and choose a selection type other than Smart.

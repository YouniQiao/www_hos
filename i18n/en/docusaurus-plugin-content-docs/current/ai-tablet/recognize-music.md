# Identify Music with One Tap

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10169850_f006_identifysong.png" width="560" height=""/>


Heard a great song in a video or playing nearby but don't know its name? Turn on music recognition with one tap to quickly and accurately identify the song.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Turn on music recognition with one tap**:

1.  Swipe down from the top right of the screen to open the Control Panel, then tap Music recognition to start identifying music automatically.
2.  Once the song is identified, the result — such as the song title and artist — is displayed in the Live Window at the top of the screen.
3.  Tap the Live Window to open the details page, where you can play or favorite the song. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202507061t4dlt/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_historyrecord.png" width="24" height="24"/> to view your recognition history.

**If there is no Music recognition shortcut, add one**:

1.  Swipe down to open the Control Panel, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202507061t4dlt/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_controlpanel_edit.png" width="24" height="24"/> > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202507061t4dlt/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add.png" width="24" height="24"/> to expand the shortcut list.
2.  Find Music recognition <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202507061t4dlt/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_identify_song.png" width="24" height="24"/>, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202507061t4dlt/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_quickswitch_add1.png" width="24" height="24"/> to add it to the Control Panel.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Touch and hold the Music recognition shortcut to view your recognition history.
+   Music recognition requires your phone to stay connected to the internet and uses the speaker to pick up sound.

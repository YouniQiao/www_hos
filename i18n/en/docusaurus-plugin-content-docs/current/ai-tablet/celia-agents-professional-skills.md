# Celia Agents: Unlock Professional Skills in One Step

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10104441_f002_celiasamrt.png" alt="Celia Agents" width="560" height="" />

Celia Agents provide professional services such as translation and in-depth problem solving. You can choose different agents based on your needs, and you can also add an agent to the home screen for quick access.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take the **Celia Translation Assistant** agent as an example:

1. On the home screen, open the **Celia** app, tap **Plaza** > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_find.png" width="24" height="24"/>, search for "Celia Translation Assistant", and open it.
2. In Celia Translation Assistant, follow the prompts to translate the content you need.
3. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/phone_ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner > **Add to home screen** to create a **Celia Translation Assistant** home screen shortcut.

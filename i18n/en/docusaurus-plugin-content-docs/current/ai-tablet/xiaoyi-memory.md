# Celia Memo, quickly record information


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044717_f014_highspeed.png
" width="560" height=""/>


Let Celia Memo help you quickly record orders, important dates, and other information. Once recorded, you can look it up anytime — no matter how much information you have, you won't forget it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take recording a high-speed rail order as an example:

1.  After purchasing your ticket, wake up Celia directly on the order screen; or drag an image containing the order information into the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen.
2.  Give Celia a voice or text command, for example: "Make a note of the order on the screen" or "Make a note of the order in the image."
3.  After Celia records it, you can ask Celia anytime, for example: "Check my high-speed rail order."

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Celia Memo can record common orders such as train tickets, flight tickets, and hotel bookings by recognizing the screen or sending an image.

# PrivateSpace, Hide Important Information

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512240Vrnk1/zh-cn/image/video/10044538_f001_PrivateSpace.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


Turn on PrivateSpace to store important files, photos, and more in a separate space, keeping them away from prying eyes and protecting your privacy.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Privacy & security > PrivateSpace, and touch Turn on.
2.  Follow the onscreen instructions to set a PrivateSpace password. You can also set a security question and then enroll a fingerprint.

Once PrivateSpace is turned on, enter the PrivateSpace password or use your PrivateSpace fingerprint on the lock screen to access it.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

To switch between spaces using fingerprints, enroll different fingerprints in the MainSpace and PrivateSpace, and enable fingerprint unlock for both.

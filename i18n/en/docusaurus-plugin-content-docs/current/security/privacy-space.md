# Store Important Data in PrivateSpace

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044538_f001_PrivateSpace.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

PrivateSpace allows you to create a completely separate space on your device for storing personal data and protecting your privacy.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Settings > Privacy & security > PrivateSpace, and tap **Enable**.

2.  Follow the onscreen instructions to set a PrivateSpace password. You can also set a security question and then enroll your fingerprint.

After **PrivateSpace** is enabled, you can then access it using your fingerprint or a password from the lock screen.

**Note**

You can enroll different fingerprints for PrivateSpace and MainSpace to quickly access different spaces.

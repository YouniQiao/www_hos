# Use Tiantong Satellite Communication

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406181xmkoc/zh-cn/image/video/10056664_f001_SatelliteCall.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


When there is no terrestrial network coverage, you can make or answer calls and send messages in an open area with a clear view of the sky.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Satellite network > Tiantong satellite communication, and enable the Tiantong satellite communication switch.

2.  In an open area facing south with a clear view of the sky, follow the onscreen instructions to adjust the angle of your device to align it with the satellite.

3.  Once connected, you can make calls or send messages.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Before using Tiantong satellite communication, you need to subscribe to the relevant service from your satellite service provider. This feature depends on your carrier's service deployment, so the actual service available may vary by carrier.
+   Only some models support this feature. The actual experience may vary.

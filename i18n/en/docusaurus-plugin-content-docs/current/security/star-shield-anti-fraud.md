# One-Tap Star Shield Anti-Fraud, Safeguarding Your Phone Security

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612210iZdjk/zh-cn/image/video/20012126_f003_SmartScamCheck.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Turn on Star Shield Anti-Fraud to intelligently identify fraud risks across communication, web pages, apps, and more, safeguarding your phone security from multiple angles.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612210iZdjk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Settings > Privacy & security > Star Shield Anti-Fraud, and follow the onscreen instructions to turn on Star Shield Anti-Fraud with one tap. The following protections will be enabled automatically:

+   **Communication protection**: Enhances call and SMS detection. For example, during a video call, it detects whether the other party is using AI face-swapping or similar techniques.

+   **Fraud tool protection**: Enhances web page, app, and WLAN detection. For example, it blocks the installation of fraudulent apps.

+   **Fraud-related scenario protection**: Enhances detection of everyday risk scenarios. Your phone automatically detects and identifies fraud-related scenarios, and promptly alerts you and blocks them.

On the Star Shield Anti-Fraud screen, tap the anti-fraud alert record icon to view protection records from the past 30 days.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612210iZdjk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

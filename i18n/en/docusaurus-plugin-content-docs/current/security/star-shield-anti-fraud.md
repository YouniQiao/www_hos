# Scam Guard, Safeguarding Your Device

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606150sYodg/en/image/figure/20012126_f003_SmartScamCheck.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

**Scam Guard** can intelligently identify fraud risks, such as AI voice changer and face swapping, and phishing links, to comprehensively protect your device.

**Try it out**

Go to Settings > Privacy & security > Scam Guard, tap **Scam Guard**, and agree to enable it. The following features will be automatically enabled:

+   **Communication shield**: Enhances call and SMS detection, such as identifying AI voice change during calls.

+   **Scam tool blocking**: Detects and blocks risky WLAN connections and websites accessed via the browser.

+   **Scam flagging**: Provides collaborative detection in multiple scenarios, such as calls and web browsing, to block and alert on behaviors that match fraud scripts.

**Note**

On the **Scam Guard** screen, tap the fraud alert record chart to view protection records from the past 30 days.

# Lost Device: Precise Finding with NearLink

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/figure/10044536_f001_FindPhone.png" width="360" height="486"/>

After you enable Find Device, NearLink finding delivers precise distance measurement and directional guidance, helping you quickly recover a lost HUAWEI device.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Here is an example where both the finding device and the device being found are HUAWEI phones:

1.  On the finding device, turn on NearLink | Bluetooth, open the Find Device app, and tap the device you want to find to open its details page.
2.  Tap Precise finding. Once you are within NearLink finding range (up to 50 meters), the distance and direction are displayed based on signal strength.
3.  Follow the distance and direction guidance. If the connection is successful, try playing a ringtone as a reminder until you find the lost device.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Both the finding phone and the phone being found must meet these requirements: signed in to the same HUAWEI ID, Find My Phone enabled, and security authentication completed.

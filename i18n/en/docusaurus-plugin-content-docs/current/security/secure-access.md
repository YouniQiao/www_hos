# Secure access, protecting your private data

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/2025033026fVij/zh-cn/image/figure/20005744_f002_PrivacyPicker.png" width="360" height="486"/>

Your phone supports Secure access by default. When an app needs to access sensitive data such as images, videos, and contacts, Secure access can limit the app to only the data you select, keeping your data safe.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take sending or uploading an image in an app as an example. Under the restrictions of Secure access, the app can only access the content you select. It cannot access any other data you haven't selected, nor can it keep accessing Gallery in the background.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After an app accesses your data, you can go to Settings > Privacy & security, tap options such as Images and videos, and view the app's access records.

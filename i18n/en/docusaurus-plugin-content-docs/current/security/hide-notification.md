# Smartly Hide Banner Notification Content

After you unlock the screen, if the system detects that someone other than the device owner is viewing the phone, or that multiple people are viewing it, banner notification content is automatically hidden to protect the owner's privacy and security. If you are wearing a HUAWEI watch, notifications are instead delivered on the watch.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Biometrics & passwords > Face recognition, and follow the onscreen prompts to enroll your facial information and enable Face unlock.

2.  Go to Settings > Notifications & status bar > Banner notifications, and under Show banner notification content, select Only when owner is looking.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

+   To have notifications delivered on your watch, the following conditions must be met: the watch you are wearing is paired with your phone via Bluetooth, and in the Health app on your phone, the switch for syncing phone message notifications to the watch and Single-device reminder mode are both enabled.

+   Only some watches support this feature. The actual product prevails.

+   For urgent messages such as earthquake alerts and calls, both the phone and the watch will receive reminders.

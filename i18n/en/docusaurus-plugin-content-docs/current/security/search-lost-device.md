# How to find a lost device

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508180uszcw/zh-cn/image/figure/10044757_f006_ScreenLock.png" width="360" height="486"/>


After you enable Find Device, you can locate your phone, tablet, and other devices to protect your devices and data.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Enable Find Device on your phone:

Go to Settings, sign in to your HUAWEI ID, go to Find Device, and turn on Find My Phone.

Find your lost phone on another device:

In the Utilities folder or on the device's home screen, tap Find Device, sign in to the same HUAWEI ID as the lost device, and tap the device you want to find. You can then locate it, lock it remotely, or erase its data.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If your phone is not connected to the network or is powered off, it is offline. You can set an online notification, and you will be notified once the phone comes online.

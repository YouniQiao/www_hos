# Family anti-fraud: Safeguard your family's device security

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/video/20012126_f002_SmartProtection.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Turn on Family anti-fraud, and when a family member may be at risk of fraud, their phone will promptly send you a notification so you can step in right away and keep them safe while using their device.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On your phone and your family member's device, sign in to each HUAWEI ID, and go to Settings > Digital Balance to bind the guardian.
2.  After binding, Family anti-fraud turns on automatically and protects the following scenarios:
    +   **Answering or making risky calls**: You'll receive a notification at the same time, and you can end the call for your family member.
    +   **Receiving risky text messages or visiting risky web pages**: You'll receive a risk record.
    +   **Installing risky apps**: You'll receive a notification at the same time.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

On your phone, go to Settings > Privacy & security > Harassment and fraud blocking > Family anti-fraud to view Family anti-fraud records.

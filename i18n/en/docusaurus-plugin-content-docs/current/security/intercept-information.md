# Block Unknown Calls and Messages

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202505110dgxvV/zh-cn/image/video/10044838_f001_blocking.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


Turn on spam and fraud blocking to screen out scam calls and spam messages, avoid unwanted interruptions, reduce the risk of fraud, and keep your phone safe.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Privacy & security > Spam and fraud blocking > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202505110dgxvV/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/phone_ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/>  .
2.  Tap Call blocking settings and Message blocking settings to set the types of calls and messages you want to block.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

You can also tap Keyword blocklist in Message blocking settings to block spam messages by adding keywords.

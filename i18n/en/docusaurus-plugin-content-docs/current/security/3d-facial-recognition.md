# 3D facial recognition for secure screen unlock

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/figure/10044531_f008_3DFaceUnlock.png" width="360" height="486"/>

3D facial recognition captures more facial information to improve recognition accuracy. Your phone uses TOF 3D sensing technology, so it can recognize your face even in low light.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Enroll facial data**: Go to Settings > Biometrics & passwords > Face recognition, and follow the onscreen prompts to enroll your facial data.

**Use face unlock**: Once enrollment is complete, pick up your phone and hold it up to your face. Turn on the screen and let it recognize your face to unlock.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   On the unlock screen, you can choose to unlock directly or swipe to unlock, as needed.
+   If you want to enable recognition while wearing a mask, you can also go to Face recognition and turn on the Recognize while wearing a mask switch.

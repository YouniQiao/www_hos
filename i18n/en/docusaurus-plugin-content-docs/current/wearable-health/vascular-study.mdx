   Vascular Health Study


     The Vascular Health Study is initiated by the China International Exchange and Promotive Association for Medical and Health Care (CPAM), with the participation of Peking University People's Hospital, Beijing Anzhen Hospital of Capital Medical University, and the Third Xiangya Hospital of Central South University. It uses the high-precision ECG and PPG sensors on wearables to screen for arteriosclerosis and provides personalized guidance and suggestions, helping you better understand your vascular health.


> 💡 **Note:** This feature is currently supported only on HUAWEI WATCH GT 6 Pro.


## Vascular health measurement

     **Measuring on your phone**
     Before use, make sure your watch is properly connected to your phone via **Bluetooth** and to **HUAWEI Health**.

1. Download and install the **HUAWEI Research** app from AppGallery on your phone.
2. Open the **HUAWEI Research** app, go to the **Vascular Health Study** project, and tap **Start measurement**.
3. On your watch, follow the on-screen measurement tutorial to complete the measurement.
4. When the measurement is complete, you can view the results in the Vascular Health Study project.

     **Measuring on your watch**

1. Press the watch's upper button, tap **HUAWEI Research > Vascular Health Study** > **Join the study** in the app list, and tap **Start measurement**. When using it for the first time, follow the prompts to fill in your personal information.
2. Follow the on-screen measurement tutorial to complete the measurement.
3. After a successful measurement, you can pull down to sync the measurement data in **HUAWEI Research** on your phone.


> 💡 **Note:** Your gender, age, height, and weight in your personal information affect the measurement results, so fill in accurate basic information and measure correctly. The home page of the Vascular Health Study project provides a "compared with peers" analysis line. As you age, the thresholds for the standard, high, and low values change accordingly. Measurement results are not a basis for professional clinical diagnosis of vascular diseases. For accurate measurements, make sure the electrodes and the back cover of the device are free of foreign objects and dirt, and that the fingers touching the electrodes are slightly moist. If they are too dry, measurements may be inaccurate or the signal quality may be poor.

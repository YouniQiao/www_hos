   Heart Health Study


> 💡 **Note:** The **HUAWEI Research** app is not available on iOS phones, but you can use this feature independently on the device. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly.


## Arrhythmia detection

     **On your phone:**

1. Keep the device paired and connected to the **HUAWEI Research** app. On the **HUAWEI Research** app home screen, go to **Heart Health Study > Detect**.
2. Tap **Start detection** and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the detection.
3. After the measurement is complete, you can view the results in **HUAWEI Research** on your phone.

     **On your device:**

1. On the device, tap **HUAWEI Research > Heart Health Study** in the app list. After joining the study, tap **Start detection** and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the detection.
2. Once the measurement is complete, you can view the results in the Heart Health Study section on your watch.


> 💡 **Note:** We recommend taking measurements twice a day for two weeks, or once every two days for a quarter, which will significantly increase the detection rate of arrhythmia. For accurate measurements, make sure the back of the device is free of obstructions and dirt.


## Enabling smart real-time measurement

     Once enabled, the device will automatically assess your heart health when you are at rest. When the device is properly connected to your phone, you can pull down on the **HUAWEI Research** home screen to sync the measurement data to your phone.

1. On your phone, tap **HUAWEI Research > Heart Health Study**.
2. Tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002498917249.png" width="24" /> > Privacy management** in the upper right corner, and enable **Smart timely measurement**.
3. You can view the measurement results on your phone under **HUAWEI Research > Heart Health Study** > **Detect**.


## AFib burden analysis

     Once AFib burden analysis is enabled, the device will automatically and intelligently assess your AFib burden when you are at rest, and sync your AFib burden status to your phone once the device is connected.

1. On the **HUAWEI Research** app home screen on your phone, go to **Heart Health Study > Detect**.
2. Tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002453524452.png" width="24" /> > Privacy management** in the upper right corner, and enable **AFib burden analysis**.
3. You can view the measurement results on your phone under **HUAWEI Research > Heart Health Study** > **Detect**. The results show


## AFib risk prediction


1. On the **HUAWEI Research** app home screen on your phone, go to **Heart Health Study > Predict**.
2. Tap **Enable AFib risk prediction** and follow the prompts to enter the AFib risk prediction study.
3. You can view the prediction results on your phone under **Heart Health Study > Predict**.

     Through detection, you can view prediction results and risk trends, anticipate risks in advance, and take appropriate measures.


> 💡 **Note:** The AFib risk prediction feature is intended for people with a history of AFib. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly. We recommend that you take the initiative to measure your heart rate more than three times a day and also enable automatic measurement, so that the risk system has enough data to analyze and predict your future risk of AFib episodes.


## Exiting the Heart Health Study

     On the **HUAWEI Research** app home screen on your phone, go to **Heart Health Study**, and tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002344378208.png" width="24" /> > Exit study** in the upper right corner.
     After exiting, you will need to rejoin the corresponding study to use or experience it again.


> 💡 **Note:** After you exit the Heart Health Study, all related data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

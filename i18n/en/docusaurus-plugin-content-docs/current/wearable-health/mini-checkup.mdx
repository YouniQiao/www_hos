# Health Check

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

Health management


|  | Health Check |
| --- | --- |


   Health Check

    Quickly measure multiple health metrics such as heart rate, blood oxygen, stress, body temperature, ECG, vascular health, and respiratory health, and view the generated health check report on your watch and in the app.


## Taking a health check


1. Wear your watch properly and stay still.
2. Select **Health Check** from the device's app list. For the first measurement, you need to grant privacy authorization and, as needed, activate services such as ECG and join programs such as the body temperature study.
3. On the watch face, press and hold the sensor window for 3s to quickly launch Health Check, or tap **Measure** on the Health Check home screen and follow the measurement guidance.
4. Once the health check report is generated, your watch will vibrate to notify you. Swipe up on the Health Check home screen to view past reports.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Health check results are for reference only and cannot be used as a basis for medical diagnosis. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly.
>
> - Go to the Health Check home screen, swipe up, and on the **More** tab, you can select the wearing method.
>
> - If the **Health records** card is not displayed on the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Edit cards** on the home screen to add it.
>
> - HarmonyOS 5 and later: If the **Health Check** card is not displayed on the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Customize dashboard** on the home screen to add it.
>
> - Your gender, age, height, and weight affect the measurement results. Before measuring, open the **HUAWEI Health** app on your phone and make sure your personal information matches your actual situation (if the **HUAWEI Research** app is installed on your phone, its personal information must also be consistent with that in **HUAWEI Health**).


## Viewing health check reports


- On your watch: In the watch's app list, tap **Health Check**, then swipe up on the Health Check home screen to view past reports.
- On your phone: Go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen and tap **Health records** to view details.

     A health check report can display: **Average heart rate**, **Blood oxygen**, **Stress, Mood, Body temperature, ECG, Vascular elasticity, Heart rate variability (HRV), Cardiovascular risk**, **Lung function assessment**, **COPD risk**, **Lung infection risk, Ovarian function assessment, Pulse wave arrhythmia analysis, High blood pressure risk assessment, High blood sugar risk assessment, Sleep apnea detection**, helping you conveniently keep track of your health.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - After your phone is upgraded to HarmonyOS 5 or later, some items are not supported. The items actually displayed shall prevail.
>
> - **Pulse wave arrhythmia analysis** and **Sleep apnea detection** in health check reports need to be activated separately; the **High blood sugar risk assessment study** needs to be joined in **HUAWEI Research**. Blood pressure is also included (data is synced from the blood pressure card on the HUAWEI Health app home screen, obtained either from manual input via the blood pressure card or from other blood pressure monitoring devices connected through the HUAWEI Health app).


## Deleting health check reports

     Open **HUAWEI Health**, go to the **Health Check report** page, tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002378488157.png" width="24" /> > Delete** in the upper right corner, select the reports to delete, and tap **Delete** at the bottom of the screen. Health check reports cannot be deleted on the watch.

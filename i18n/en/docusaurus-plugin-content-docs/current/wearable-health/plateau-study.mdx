> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

   High-Altitude Health Study

    When you are planning a trip to a high-altitude area, the High-Altitude Health Study app can comprehensively assess your risk of altitude sickness based on measurement results and subjective feedback, and provide health guidance and suggestions.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - This feature is not supported when paired with an iOS phone.


## Enable the High-Altitude Health Study project

     Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, tap **HUAWEI Innovation Research > High-Altitude Health Study**, then tap **Join Study**.


## Measure high-altitude health


- Phone-side measurement 1. Open the **HUAWEI Innovation Research** app, go to the High-Altitude Health Study project, and tap **Start Assessment**. 2. On the device, follow the on-screen measurement tutorial prompts to complete the measurement. 3. When the measurement is complete, the results are displayed on the phone.
- Device-side measurement 1. Open the **HUAWEI Innovation Research** app on your phone and go to the High-Altitude Health Study project. 2. Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, tap **HUAWEI Innovation Research > High-Altitude Health Study**, then tap **Start Assessment**. For the first measurement, tap **Next** and follow the measurement tutorial to complete the questionnaire and collect physiological data. 3. When the measurement is complete, the results are displayed on the device. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002358363533.png" width="24" /> to view the result description.


## View data

     Open the **HUAWEI Innovation Research** app and go to the High-Altitude Health Study project to view measurement data and historical data.
     After a successful measurement, you can view the results on the device. Swipe up on the page and tap to view statistical details and historical measurement results.

# Record Your Menstrual Cycle

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="ultimate" label="WATCH Ultimate">


## Activate the app
When using the app for the first time, make sure your watch and phone are properly connected, and complete activation.
1. Go to the home screen of the app, tap the card, download the feature package, set your menstrual cycle data, and tap. If the menstrual cycle card is not displayed on the home screen, tap on the home screen to add it. > 💡 **Note:** If the menstrual cycle card is not displayed on the home screen, tap on the home screen to add it.
2. After activation, the watch will display an activation reminder (it pops up directly when the watch is being worn, and is saved to the message center when it is not). Tap to go to the app home screen. Tap to return to the previous level.
## Record and view your menstrual cycle on the device
1. In the app list on your wearable device, select to go to the menstrual cycle home screen, then tap the switch icon on the side <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002520040368.png" width="24" />
to switch between the month view and the ring chart. In the month view, switch months to view the predicted period data for the previous cycle and the next three cycles.
2. Tap the edit icon <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002520360226.png" width="24" />
to record and time. Swipe up on the screen to view more period-related information.
## Record your menstrual cycle in HUAWEI Health
1. Go to the home screen of the app, tap the card, select the target date on the page, select or at the bottom, set the period start time and end time, and you can also record your physical condition and more in.
2. On the page, tap the icon in the upper right corner <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001701298789.png" width="24" />
to configure the following settings: Tap to enable/disable, and the switches for each reminder item you need to set, and you can also set and.
3. Tap, and after tapping the button to enable it, keep wearing your watch at night, and it will track your body temperature, heart rate, and respiratory rate data to improve prediction results, making predictions more accurate. This feature is only supported on some devices. If your device does not have this setting item, it means the feature is not supported. Please refer to the actual display.
4. Tap to view and more.
5. Tap to view historical data.

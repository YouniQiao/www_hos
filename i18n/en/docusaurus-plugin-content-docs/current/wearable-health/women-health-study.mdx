# Women's Health Study

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

   Women's Health Study


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - The **HUAWEI Research** app is not available on iOS phones, but you can use the feature independently on the watch.
>
> - If the gender of your account is **blank** or **undisclosed**, the **OK** button is dimmed. If the gender is **male**, tapping **OK** displays the message **This study is only available to female users**.
>
> - This product is not a medical device. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly.


    Women's Health Study uses the high-performance sensors built into the watch to continuously collect basic overnight vital sign data in different states (such as body temperature, heart rate, respiratory rate, and sleep) to assess ovarian function. One assessment cycle takes 14 to 35 days. Wearing the watch at least 5 days a week helps complete the assessment faster.


## Join the Women's Health Study


1. Press the watch's upper button, tap **HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study** in the app list, and tap **Join study**.
2. When you join the study for the first time and start a new assessment cycle, first enter your personal information (such as gender, date of birth, height, and weight), tap **OK**, read the measurement instructions, and then tap **Next**.
3. Answer the questionnaire truthfully based on your physical condition over the past month, and tap **Next**.
4. Wear the watch correctly to start the assessment.


## View data

     One assessment cycle takes 14 to 35 days. You need to wear the watch at night for at least 14 days within 35 days, excluding your period, predicted period, and pregnancy. Wearing it for longer helps complete the assessment faster.
     **View assessment progress**
     Press the watch's upper button, tap **HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study** in the app list, and you will see the assessment progress.

     **View assessment results**
     After the assessment is complete, the watch will remind you to view the results. Tap **View details** on the reminder page to enter **Women's Health Study** and view the detailed assessment results (ovarian function risk value and recommendations, and comparison with peers).
     **View historical assessment records**
     Press the watch's upper button, tap **HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study** in the app list, and swipe up to view historical assessment records. Tap a record to view its details.
     For more details, view them on your phone in the **HUAWEI Research** app.


## Quit the Women's Health Study

     Press the watch's upper button, tap **HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study** in the app list, swipe up to the last page, and tap **Quit study**. After you quit, you will need to rejoin the study if you want to use it again.


## End this assessment cycle

     Press the watch's upper button, tap **HUAWEI Research > Women's Health Study** in the app list, and tap **End this assessment cycle** at the bottom of the assessment page. After you end this assessment cycle, the data from this cycle will not be retained.

# ECG

> This document applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

Health management


|  | ECG |
| --- | --- |


   ECG


    An electrocardiogram (ECG) is a graphic record of the heart's electrical activity. By reading an ECG, doctors can gain a deeper understanding of your heart rhythm, identify signs of irregular heartbeats, and detect potential risks early. Single measurements are supported.


> 💡 **Note:** This feature is currently supported only on HUAWEI WATCH GT 6 Pro.


## Before you start


- Use of this product must comply with the laws and regulations governing medical devices and the relevant regulations of your location.
- This feature is not intended for users under 18 years of age.
- It is not recommended for users with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device.
- Strong electromagnetic fields (such as those from an MRI machine) can severely affect ECG waveform quality. Do not take measurements near strong electromagnetic fields.
- All data obtained while using this software is for reference only and must not be used for medical research, diagnosis, or treatment.


## Preparing for a measurement


> 💡 **Note:** Before using this feature, update the **HUAWEI Health** app to the latest version.


     Activate the app: If you are using **ECG** for the first time, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, select **Heart health > ECG**, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete activation.


## Measuring your ECG


1. Wear the device properly, select **ECG** from the app list, and for your first measurement, choose your wearing method (**Worn on left wrist** or **Worn on right wrist**). You can also swipe up on the screen to the **More** page to change the wearing method.
2. Rest your arm flat on a table or your knee, and relax your arm and fingers.
3. Use a finger on the hand that is not wearing the device to lightly touch the side electrode (the Down button). The measurement starts automatically. > 💡 **Note:** > > - During the measurement, stay still, breathe evenly and steadily, and do not talk. Keep your finger on the electrode without letting go, and apply moderate pressure — avoid pressing too hard or touching too lightly for insufficient contact. > > - Very dry skin may affect the measurement. Moisten your finger with a small amount of water to improve conductivity. > > - A single measurement lasts 30 seconds.
4. When the measurement is complete, the result is displayed on the device screen. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001248101770.png" width="24" /> to view an explanation of the result. For details, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen and tap **Heart health > ECG**. If you need to download an ECG report, tap the download icon in the upper right corner of the ECG details page. > 💡 **Note:** > > - Viewing historical data is not supported on the watch.


## Understanding your results


- Consult a healthcare professional about your measurement results. Do not interpret them yourself or take clinical action based on them.


- This feature cannot detect blood clots, stroke, heart failure, or other types of arrhythmia.
- This feature cannot detect a heart attack or the onset of related complications. If you experience chest pain, chest tightness, a feeling of pressure in the chest, or other discomfort, seek medical attention promptly.
- Do not adjust your medication based on the measurement results output by this software without consulting a doctor.

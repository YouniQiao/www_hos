# Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

   Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis

    Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis uses the high-precision PPG sensor on your wearable device to screen for abnormal heart rhythms.


> 💡 **Note:** Measurement results are for reference only and cannot be used as a basis for medical diagnosis. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly.


## Activate the app


1. Before use, update **HUAWEI Health** on your phone to the latest version.
2. On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Heart Health > Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis** to activate the **Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis** app.


## Measure on your device


1. Wear your wearable device properly, rest your arm flat on a table or your knee, and relax your arm and fingers.
2. In the app list on your device, tap **Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis > Measure**.
3. When the measurement is complete, the result appears on your wearable device. To view the result details, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen and tap **Heart Health > Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis**.


> 💡 **Note:** **Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis**<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002553555837.png" width="24" /> introduction.


## Enable automatic detection and reminders


1. On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Heart Health > Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001309830244.png" width="24" /> > Automatic detection** in the upper right corner.
2. Turn on the **Automatic arrhythmia detection** switch. Once enabled, detection is performed automatically while you are at rest and wearing your wearable device. Turn on the **Arrhythmia reminders** switch. Once enabled, you will receive a notification on your wearable device when an arrhythmia is detected.


> 💡 **Note:** During periodic measurement, if an abnormal heart rhythm is detected, a prompt will appear on your wearable device. If you are using a wearable device that supports ECG, you will be prompted to take an ECG measurement. To avoid causing unnecessary concern, reminders are only sent when abnormal results account for a relatively high proportion after multiple measurements. When you are awake, the device will vibrate, turn on the screen, and display a pop-up reminder. When you are asleep, the device will not vibrate or turn on the screen, and will only display a pop-up reminder.


## Deregister the service

     On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Heart Health > Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002553555339.png" width="24" /> > About** > **Deregister service**, and follow the on-screen instructions.


## Understand your results

     Measurement results may differ from ECG analysis results. Please consult a healthcare professional and do not interpret the results yourself or take clinical measures on your own.
     On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Heart Health > Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis**. On the **Pulse Wave Arrhythmia Analysis** page, view the pulse wave arrhythmia bar chart. You can also tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001647346040.png" width="24" /> > Description** in the upper right corner to view an explanation of the measurement results.
     Measurement results: No abnormal heart rhythm detected, suspected atrial fibrillation, suspected premature beat.

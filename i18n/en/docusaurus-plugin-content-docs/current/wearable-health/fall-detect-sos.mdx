# Fall Detection and SOS Emergency Assistance

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.

   Fall Detection and SOS Emergency Assistance

    After you enable Fall detection, the watch uses its built-in acceleration sensor to monitor your posture. If it detects a fall and determines that you may be unable to respond, it will start SOS emergency calling for you.
    To turn off Fall detection, open the app list on the watch, tap **Settings > SOS Emergency Assistance**, and turn off the **Fall detection** switch.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - When the watch is paired with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, update both the watch and **HUAWEI Health** to the latest versions.
>
> - Your device may not detect every fall in all situations. High-impact movements may also cause the watch to trigger Fall detection unintentionally.
>
> - When the phone and watch are connected over Bluetooth and the phone uses two SIM cards, set the default dialing card in advance.
>
> - When the watch is connected to an iOS phone, assistance text messages cannot be sent.


## Set up personal emergency information and emergency contacts

     Personal emergency information helps first responders provide assistance in an emergency. Before using it, complete the relevant configuration.

1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **General settings > SOS Emergency Assistance**.
2. Tap **Personal emergency information > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002533847339.png" width="24" />** and set information such as your address and blood type.
3. Tap **Emergency contacts**. On the Edit emergency contacts page, you can add or delete contacts, or touch and hold <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002533850053.png" width="560" /> and drag to change the order of contacts. > 💡 **Tips:** > > - You can also set your personal information in **HUAWEI Health** by tapping **Me > Profile** > **Personal emergency information**. > > - When the device starts emergency calling, it automatically calls the first emergency contact you have set and sends an assistance text message to all emergency contacts. Set your emergency contacts in order. > > - You can add up to 3 emergency contacts.


## View personal emergency information

     **On the watch**:
     In the app list on the watch, tap **Settings > SOS Emergency Assistance** to view your personal emergency information.
     **In HUAWEI Health:**
     On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **General settings > SOS Emergency Assistance** > **Personal emergency information** to view your personal emergency information.


## Fall detection and automatic emergency assistance


1. In the app list on the watch, tap **Settings > SOS Emergency Assistance** and turn on the **Fall detection** switch.
2. After the watch detects a fall and determines that you may be unable to respond, a dialog box appears asking whether you need to make an emergency call.
3. If you select **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002533806181.png" width="24" />**, or if there is no action within 60s, emergency assistance will be started automatically: If emergency contacts have been set, the watch automatically calls the first emergency contact and sends an assistance text message containing your current location to all emergency contacts (in environments with a weak satellite positioning signal, the assistance text message sent does not include location information). If the first emergency contact cannot be reached and you need to call another emergency contact, select the contact manually on the watch to make the call.
4. If no emergency contacts have been set, a public emergency number is displayed. Select it to make the call as needed.


## Manually start emergency assistance


      Press the upper button (power button) 5 times in a row to start SOS emergency assistance:

- If emergency contacts have been set, the watch automatically calls the first emergency contact and sends an assistance text message containing your current location to all emergency contacts (in environments with a weak satellite positioning signal, the assistance text message sent does not include location information). If the first emergency contact cannot be reached and you need to call another emergency contact, select the contact manually on the watch to make the call.
- If no emergency contacts have been set, a public emergency number is displayed. Select it to make the call as needed.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - If **Fall detection** is turned off, the watch will not automatically start SOS emergency assistance after detecting a severe fall. You can press the upper button 5 times in a row to make an emergency call.

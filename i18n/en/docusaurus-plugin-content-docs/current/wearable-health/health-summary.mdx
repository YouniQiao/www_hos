## Health Summary overview

     Health Summary is an app that tracks changes in your personal health. It analyzes your multidimensional personal data and notifies you when appropriate. It mainly tracks changes in data such as sleep quality, workout duration, and stress, and provides trend chart analysis.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Summary reports are not generated every day. If you wear the device for too short a time or do not wear it while sleeping, the relevant report may not be triggered.
>
> - You need to wear the device for two consecutive days before data becomes visible on the watch. Wearing it over the long term makes the analysis gradually more accurate.
>
> - Health changes can be caused by many factors. Summary information is based on a comprehensive analysis of your multidimensional personal health data and identifies correlations among multiple factors. Short-term data changes may be a sign of certain abnormalities or illnesses.
>
> - This app is not medical software, and its analysis results are not a basis for medical diagnosis. Health suggestions are for reference only. Please take your individual differences into account and arrange an improvement plan accordingly.


## Turn on the reminder switch

     Once enabled, if the Health Summary analysis results are updated and you have not checked Health Summary in time, the device will notify and remind you at the appropriate time.
     Go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, swipe down, tap the card below **Health Summary**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298138392.png" width="24" /> > Settings** in the upper right corner, and enable the **Health Summary notifications** switch.


## View Health Summary data

     **View on the device**
     In the app list on the device, tap **Health Summary** to view an overview of the Health Summary report. Swipe up to view each result. When using it for the first time, complete the relevant authorization as prompted.
     **View in HUAWEI Health**
     Go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, swipe up, and tap the card below **Health Summary** to view the following:

- Tap **Featured summaries**: view a summary report of workout and health data analysis.
- Tap **Trend list**: view trend changes for each type of data. Trend changes may indicate that your health status has changed or may affect your health status. You are advised to keep using your wearable device or the HUAWEI Health app on your phone to record workout and health data.

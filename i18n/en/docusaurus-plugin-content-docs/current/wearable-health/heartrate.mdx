# Measure Your Heart Rate

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.

   Measure Your Heart Rate


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - The measurement data is for reference only and cannot be used as a basis for medical diagnosis.
>
> - To ensure the accuracy of heart rate measurements, wear the device properly, avoiding the wrist bone, and not too loosely. Wear it as snugly as possible during exercise.
>
> - External factors (such as low blood perfusion, excessive arm hair, tattoos, darker skin tone, a lowered arm, arm movement, and low temperatures) may cause inaccurate results or no reading at all.


## Continuous heart rate measurement


1. On the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Heart health**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002441223990.png" width="24" /> > Settings** in the upper right corner, and make sure **Continuous heart rate measurement** is enabled.
2. You can set **Heart rate measurement mode** to **Smart** or **Real-time**. **Smart**: intelligently monitors your heart rate based on your activity level.
3. **Real-time**: monitors your heart rate in real time regardless of your activity level.
4. On the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Heart health**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002378667093.png" width="24" /> > All data** in the upper right corner to view or delete heart rate measurement data.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Monitoring your heart rate in Real-time mode increases power consumption. Smart mode adjusts the heart rate measurement interval based on activity intensity, which helps save power.
>
> - An icon value on the heart rate measurement screen of the device, such as <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002503704223.png" width="560" />, indicates the highest and lowest heart rate values measured that day.


## Set heart rate alerts


1. On the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Heart health**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002474463949.png" width="24" /> > Settings** in the upper right corner, enable **Continuous heart rate measurement**, and set **High heart rate alert** and **Low heart rate alert**.
2. Once set, when you are not active and your heart rate stays above or below the set value for more than 10 minutes, the device will vibrate and display a high or low heart rate notification on the screen.


## Single heart rate measurement


> 💡 **Tips:** Single heart rate measurement data is not synced to **HUAWEI Health**.


1. On the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Heart health**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002441064118.png" width="24" /> > Settings** in the upper right corner, and disable **Continuous heart rate measurement**.
2. In the app list on the device, tap **Heart rate** to take a single heart rate measurement. The result is displayed on the device.

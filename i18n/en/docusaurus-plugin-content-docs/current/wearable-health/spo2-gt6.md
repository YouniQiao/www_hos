# Measuring Blood Oxygen Saturation

> This document applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

> 💡 **Tip:** To ensure measurement accuracy, wear your watch normally when measuring blood oxygen saturation, avoiding your wrist bone, and wear it snugly but comfortably. Make sure the monitoring unit stays in contact with your skin and is not blocked by any foreign objects.
## Measuring a Single Blood Oxygen Reading
1. Wear the wearable device normally and stay still.
2. On the wearable device's app list, swipe up or down on the screen and select.
3. Tap. The actual operation on your product prevails.
4. Stay still during the measurement with the screen facing up to measure your current blood oxygen saturation.
## Automatic Blood Oxygen Detection
After automatic detection is enabled, the device automatically measures and records your blood oxygen saturation while you are still. This data can help you keep an eye on your health (for example, while you are asleep).
1. On the device details page, tap,.
2. Tap to set the device to alert you when your blood oxygen saturation drops below the set value while you are not asleep.
## Viewing Data
Go to the home screen, tap, and select a date to view the blood oxygen saturation measurements for that time.

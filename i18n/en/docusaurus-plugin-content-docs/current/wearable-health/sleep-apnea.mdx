# Sleep Apnea Detection

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

   Sleep Apnea Detection

    Sleep apnea detection combines heart rate, blood oxygen, and other measurements rather than relying on snoring. It is mainly characterized by pauses in breathing during sleep caused by complete or partial upper airway obstruction and/or reduced respiratory center drive, which leads to chronic intermittent hypoxia, repeated micro-arousals, abnormal sleep architecture, daytime sleepiness, and memory decline, and can cause autonomic nervous system disorders.


## Enabling the app


> 💡 **Note:** Before use, make sure **HUAWEI Health** is properly connected to your wearable device.


     On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap the **Sleep** card, swipe up, tap **Sleep Apnea Detection**, and tap **Activate** to activate the Sleep Apnea Detection app. Once activated, the **Sleep Apnea Detection** switch is turned on by default.

      You can also go to the device details screen in **HUAWEI Health**, tap the **Sleep Apnea Detection** card, and tap **Activate**.


> 💡 **Note:** To turn off **Sleep Apnea Detection**, go to the **Sleep Apnea Detection** screen, tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002384956592.png" width="24" />** icon in the upper right corner, and turn off the **Sleep Apnea Detection** switch.


## Viewing data


> 💡 **Note:** Sleep apnea detection results require wearing the device for more than 5 hours of sleep, during which your heart rate must be above 45 beats/min and blood oxygen must be measurable.


1. In the app list on your wearable device, swipe to select Sleep Apnea Detection to view your sleep apnea data. Swipe up to view data from the last 7 days.
2. On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap the **Sleep** card, swipe up, and tap **Sleep Apnea Detection** to view detailed data and result interpretations.


## Deregistering the service

     On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap the **Sleep** card, swipe up, tap **Sleep Apnea Detection**, tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002522596132.png" width="24" />** icon in the upper right corner, then go to **About** > **Deregister service** and follow the onscreen instructions.

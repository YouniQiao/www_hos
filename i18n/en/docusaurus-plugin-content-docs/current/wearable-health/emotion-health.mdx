# Emotional Wellbeing

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

   Emotional Wellbeing


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly.


## Enable emotion/stress tracking


1. In the device's app list, tap **Emotional Wellbeing**, follow the onscreen instructions to complete the relevant operations, and then go to the **Emotional Wellbeing** screen.
2. On the device details screen in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Emotional Wellbeing** and turn on the **Emotion/stress tracking** switch.


## View/modify emotion data

     **View on the device**
     In the device's app list, tap **Emotional Wellbeing**. The emotion animation shows your current emotional state. Swipe up to view more records.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - If you feel the emotion detection results are inaccurate, you can tap **Modify recent emotion** on the watch's emotion trend screen to modify them.
>
> - Emotional states are not recorded during sleep.


     **View in HUAWEI Health**
     Go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen and tap **Emotional Wellbeing** to view your emotion/stress data, along with analyses and interpretation suggestions for different time periods.
     To view all historical data, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Emotional Wellbeing**, then tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002329268364.png" width="24" /> > All data** in the upper right corner.


## Emotion reminders


1. In the device's app list, tap **Emotional Wellbeing**.
2. Swipe up to the **Settings** screen, tap **Reminder settings**, and turn on the **Emotion reminders** switch. The device will send you notifications based on your recent emotional state. If you have been in a low mood for a while, you can follow the suggestions in the reminder to help adjust your emotional state.

# Measure an ECG

   ECG


    An electrocardiogram (ECG) is a graphic record of the heart's electrical activity. By reading an ECG, doctors can gain a deeper understanding of your heart rhythm, identify signs of irregular heartbeats, and detect potential risks early. Single measurements are supported.


> 💡 **Tip:** This feature is currently supported only on HUAWEI WATCH GT 6 Pro.


## Before you start


- Use of this product must comply with the laws and regulations governing medical devices and the relevant regulations of your location.
- This feature is not intended for users under 18 years of age.
- It is not recommended for users with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device.
- Strong electromagnetic fields (such as an MRI machine) can seriously affect ECG waveform quality. Do not take a measurement when you are near a strong electromagnetic field.
- All data obtained while using this software is for reference only and cannot be used for medical research, diagnosis, or treatment.


## Before measuring


> 💡 **Tip:** Before using this feature, update the **HUAWEI Health** app to the latest version.


     Activate the app: If you are using **ECG** for the first time, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, select **Heart health > ECG**, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete activation.


## Measure an ECG


1. Wear the device properly, then select **ECG** from the app list. For your first measurement, select how you are wearing the device (**Worn on left hand** or **Worn on right hand**). You can also swipe up on the screen to the **More** page to change how you wear it.
2. Rest your arm flat on a table or your knee, and relax your arm and fingers.
3. Gently touch the side electrode (the Down button) with a finger on the hand that is not wearing the device. The measurement starts automatically. > 💡 **Tip:** > > - During the measurement, stay still and breathe evenly and steadily. Do not talk, and do not lift your finger off the electrode. Apply moderate pressure against the electrode — avoid pressing too hard or touching too lightly for insufficient contact. > > - Very dry skin may affect the measurement. Moisten your finger with a little water to improve conductivity. > > - A single measurement lasts 30 seconds.
4. When the measurement ends, the result appears on the device screen. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001248101770.png" width="24" /> to view an explanation of the result. For details, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen and tap **Heart health > ECG**. If you need to download an ECG report, tap the download icon in the upper right corner of the ECG details page. > 💡 **Tip:** > > - Viewing historical data is not supported on the watch.


## Understanding the results


- Consult a professional healthcare provider about your measurement results. Do not interpret them yourself or take clinical action based on them.


- This feature cannot detect blood clots, stroke, heart failure, or other types of arrhythmia.
- This feature cannot detect a heart attack or the onset of related complications. If you feel chest pain, chest tightness, pressure in your chest, or other discomfort, seek medical attention promptly.
- Do not adjust your medication based on the measurement results output by this software without consulting a doctor.

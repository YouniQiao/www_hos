# Fat Loss & Shaping

> This document applies to **WATCH 5**.

By combining data such as weight changes, calorie deficit, diet records, and workouts, you can set up reasonable personalized training to lose weight scientifically and maintain it.


## Setting Your Initial Goal

1. In the app list on your watch, tap **Fat Loss & Shaping**. The first time you use it, the user authorization page appears. Tap **Agree**.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the setup, and you'll be taken to the home screen of the **Fat Loss & Shaping** app.


> 💡 **Tip:** If you haven't set your weight information, follow the on-screen instructions to complete the relevant settings, and your watch will generate a scientific weight loss plan or weight maintenance plan. If you've already set your current weight and target weight in the **HUAWEI Health** app, they'll sync to your watch automatically, so you don't need to set them again on the watch.

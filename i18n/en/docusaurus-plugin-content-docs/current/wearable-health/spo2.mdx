## Measure a Single Blood Oxygen Saturation Reading
1. Wear the wearable device normally and stay still.
2. In the app list on the wearable device, swipe up and down on the screen and select.
3. Tap. The actual operations on your product prevail.
4. During the measurement, stay still with the screen facing up to measure your current blood oxygen saturation.
## Automatic Blood Oxygen Detection
Once automatic detection is enabled, the device will automatically measure and record your blood oxygen saturation while you are still. This data can help you keep an eye on your health (for example, while you are asleep).
1. On the device details page, tap,.
2. Tap to set an alert for when your blood oxygen saturation falls below the set value while you are not asleep.
## View Data
Go to the home screen, tap, and select a date to view the blood oxygen saturation measurements for that time.

## Fingertip Blood Oxygen Detection
1. Wear the watch normally and stay still.
2. In the watch's app list, select.
3. Gently touch the sensor window with a finger on the hand that is not wearing the watch to start the measurement. When the countdown ends, keep your finger lightly touching to take continuous measurements; remove your finger to end the measurement and view the result. The waveform shown during fingertip blood oxygen measurement represents the heart rate waveform.

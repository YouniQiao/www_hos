   Measure blood oxygen


> 💡 **Tip:** To ensure measurement accuracy, when measuring your blood oxygen saturation, wear your watch properly, avoid your wrist bone, and wear it snugly but comfortably. Make sure the monitoring unit stays in contact with your skin and is not blocked by any foreign objects.


## Fingertip blood oxygen measurement


1. Wear your watch properly and stay still.
2. In the watch's app list, select **Blood oxygen saturation**.
3. Lightly touch the sensor window with a finger on the hand that is not wearing the watch to start the measurement. When the countdown ends, keep your finger in place to measure continuously, or lift your finger to end the measurement and view the result. The waveform shown during fingertip blood oxygen measurement represents your heart rate waveform.


## Automatic blood oxygen measurement

     Automatic blood oxygen measurement requires the **Auto blood oxygen measurement** switch to be turned on first. Once enabled, the device will automatically measure and record your blood oxygen saturation while you are still, and the data can help you keep an eye on your health (for example, during sleep or at high altitudes).

1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Blood oxygen** and turn on the **Auto blood oxygen measurement** switch.
2. Tap **Low blood oxygen alert** to set an alert for when your blood oxygen saturation drops below the set value while you are not sleeping.


> 💡 **Tip:** **HUAWEI Health** **Blood oxygen** **Continuous blood oxygen measurement** Tap **Low blood oxygen alert** to set an alert for when your blood oxygen saturation drops below the set value while you are not sleeping. Available values: Off/75%/80%/85%/90%


## View data

     On the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Blood oxygen saturation** and select a date to view the blood oxygen saturation measurements for that time.

   Hypertension Risk Study

    This study uses the watch's built-in high-performance sensors to continuously collect physical sign data in different states during the day and at night, in order to assess hypertension risk. Taking part in this study on an ongoing basis is an effective way to assess the risk of hypertension and cardiovascular and cerebrovascular diseases.


> 💡 **Tip:** iOS phones do not have the **HUAWEI Research** app, but you can use this feature independently on the watch. This product is not a medical device. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly. One assessment takes 24 hours and requires the following: Wearing time: wear the watch continuously for at least 7 hours during the day and at least 6 hours at night; active measurements: at least 2 sensor window measurements.


## Hypertension Risk Study


1. Press the watch's upper button, and in the watch's app list, tap **HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study** > **Join study**.
2. When joining the study for the first time, enable sensor permissions as prompted and tap **Allow**.
3. When joining the study for the first time, read the feature introduction carefully and tap **Start now**. On the start assessment page, tap **Start assessment**; on the personal information page, fill in your personal information truthfully and tap **Confirm**; on the wearing method page, select left or right hand and tap **OK**; on the measurement instructions page, swipe up to read the information and tap **OK** to enter the measurement screen. Follow the onscreen instructions and lightly touch the sensor window with your finger to start the measurement. During the measurement, adjust the pressure of your finger as prompted on the screen. Once the measurement is complete, you will be taken to the assessment progress page.


    **View assessment progress**
    Press the watch's upper button, and in the watch's app list, tap **HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study**. Once you are on the home screen, you can view the assessment progress.
    **View assessment results**

1. When the assessment progress reaches 100%, tap **Measure now** to enter the measurement screen.
2. Follow the onscreen instructions and lightly touch the sensor window with your finger to start the measurement. During the measurement, adjust the pressure of your finger as prompted on the screen.
3. Once the measurement is complete, you can view the assessment result (for example, suspected hypertension or no abnormality found). Details of the assessment result are displayed on the latest assessment result page.

    **View historical risk records**
    Press the watch's upper button, and in the watch's app list, tap **HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study**. Once you are on the home screen, swipe up to view historical risk records. Tap a record to view the hypertension risk assessment results for the last 7 days, and tap the date you want to view to see the details of the hypertension assessment result for that date. For more details, go to the **HUAWEI Research** app on your phone.


## Alert settings


1. Press the watch's upper button, and in the watch's app list, tap **HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study**. Once you are on the home screen, swipe up to the last page and tap **Alert settings**.
2. This app turns on **Message alerts** by default and will send you related messages, such as progress achieved and progress behind schedule. If you do not need them, you can turn off this alert.


## Quit study

     Press the watch's upper button, and in the watch's app list, tap **HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study**. Once you are on the home screen, swipe up to the last page and tap **Quit study**. After you confirm, if you want to use or take part in the study again, you will need to join the project again.

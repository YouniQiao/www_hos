# Breathing Health Study

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<TabItem value="gt6" label="WATCH GT 6">

Health management


|  | Breathing Health Study |
| --- | --- |


   Breathing Health Study


> 💡 **Note:** This feature is not supported when paired with an iOS phone. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention promptly.


## Enable the Breathing Health Study project


1. After pairing and connecting the device in **HUAWEI Health**, open **HUAWEI Research** on your phone.
2. Tap **HUAWEI Research** on the device. When you open it for the first time, you need to agree to the privacy statement.
3. Go to **HUAWEI Research > Breathing Health Study** and tap **Join study**.


## Take a single breathing health measurement


- **Measurement on your phone** 1. On your phone, open **HUAWEI Research > Breathing Health Study** and tap **Start measurement**. 2. On the device, follow the on-screen measurement tutorial to complete the measurement.
- **Measurement on the device** 1. On your phone, open **HUAWEI Research** and enter the Breathing Health Study project. 2. Press the device's upper button, then in the app list tap **HUAWEI Research > Breathing Health Study** > **Start measurement**. For the first measurement, tap **Next** and follow the measurement tutorial to record your cough sounds and collect physiological data. 3. When the measurement is complete, the device screen displays the results. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002344579578.png" width="24" /> to view the result description.


## Measure during nighttime sleep


1. On your phone, open **HUAWEI Research > Breathing Health Study**, tap the menu in the upper right corner, tap **Privacy management**, and turn on **Measure during nighttime sleep**.
2. Once enabled, your breathing health is assessed while you sleep. After the device is connected to your phone, swipe down on the phone screen to sync your breathing health status to your phone.


## View data

     Open **HUAWEI Research > Breathing Health Study** to view measurement data and historical data.
     After a successful measurement, swipe down on your phone to sync the measurement data. Once synced, you can view the measurement results and respiratory rate. Swipe up on the screen and tap to view statistical details and historical measurement results.

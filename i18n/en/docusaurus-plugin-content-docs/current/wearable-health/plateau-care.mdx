# High-Altitude Care

> This topic applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="ultimate" label="WATCH Ultimate">


When you are in a high-altitude area, High-Altitude Care helps you keep track of your physical condition in real time, so you can decide whether to rest or continue exercising safely, greatly reducing the risk of accidents and altitude sickness.
> 💡 **Note:** Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version. High-Altitude Care is designed for high-altitude areas at 2,500 meters and above. This product is not a medical device. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. When using this feature, make sure to enable the feature as well.
## Enabling High-Altitude Care on your phone
After you enable High-Altitude Care on your phone, continuous health monitoring on your watch will start when you enter a high-altitude area, and you will be notified when a risk of altitude acclimatization is detected.
Go to the device details page, tap, and enable the feature.
## Performing an altitude sickness assessment on your watch
After you enter a high-altitude area, if the device detects a suspected risk of altitude acclimatization, a rapid change in altitude over a short period, or that you have entered an extremely high-altitude area (above 4,500 meters), it will ask whether you want to perform an altitude sickness assessment. You can choose to perform the assessment or ignore the notification.
> 💡 **Note:** Suspected altitude acclimatization risk notifications and rapid altitude change notifications are supported only at altitudes between 2,500 and 4,500 meters.
You can also start an altitude sickness assessment manually:
1. Press the watch's upper button, and tap in the app list.
2. Tap, and follow the onscreen instructions to complete the questionnaire self-check and blood oxygen measurement.
3. After the assessment is complete, you can view the results on the High-Altitude Care home screen.

# Monitor Sleep

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="gt6" label="WATCH GT 6">

Health management


|  | Monitor Sleep |
| --- | --- |


   Monitor Sleep

    Wear your wearable device correctly, and it can identify data such as when you fall asleep, when you wake up, and your sleep structure. Based on this data, it gives a sleep quality score and provides corresponding sleep suggestions.


## Sleep settings


1. In the app list on your wearable device, tap **Sleep**, swipe up on the screen to go to the **More** page, and tap **Sleep mode** to configure sleep mode settings.
2. You can select only one mode: **Sleep mode** or **Scheduled**. **Sleep mode**: Off by default. When Sleep mode is enabled, incoming calls and notifications are muted and do not vibrate (except alarms), the screen does not turn on when you raise your wrist, and the watch face switches to simplified mode.
3. **Scheduled**: Once enabled, the device enters and exits Sleep mode according to the times you set. **Add a schedule**: Tap **Add schedule**, set the **Bedtime**, **Wake-up time**, and **Repeat**, then tap **OK**. **Delete an added schedule**: Tap the added schedule to open it, then tap **Delete**. The wearable device comes with one preset schedule, which can only be edited, not deleted.


## Enable/disable Scientific sleep

     On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Sleep > Scientific sleep** to enable or disable the **Scientific sleep** switch.
     **Scientific sleep** is enabled by default. If you disable **Scientific sleep**, sleep data and analysis will be unavailable.


## View/delete sleep data

     **View sleep data**

- In the app list on your wearable device, tap **Sleep** to view data such as your nighttime sleep, naps, and sleep health for the day. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002238650645.png" width="24" /> to view explanations of the measurement results.
- On the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, tap the **Sleep** card to view detailed sleep data. You can also tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002203690636.jpg" width="560" /> > All data** in the upper right corner of the **Sleep** card page to view more historical sleep data.

     **Delete sleep data**

1. On the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, tap the **Sleep** card.
2. Tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002251712653.jpg" width="560" /> > All data** in the upper right corner, tap the delete icon in the upper right corner, then select and delete historical sleep data.

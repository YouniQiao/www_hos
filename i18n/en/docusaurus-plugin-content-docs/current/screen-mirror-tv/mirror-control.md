# Mirror Control

After mirroring your Huawei phone/tablet, you can use HUAWEI Smart Remote 2 to control the mirrored content.

## Prerequisites

The HUAWEI Vision and phone/tablet must meet the requirements in [HUAWEI Vision, phone, and tablet models that support mirror control](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16033111).

## Procedure

After mirroring, you can use the remote control to perform the following operations. The main usage scenarios are listed below:

+   Operations that can be performed with one finger on your phone/tablet, such as tapping, swiping, and dragging, can be performed on the mirrored screen on your HUAWEI Vision using the remote control.
+   When playing short videos, swipe up or down on the touch area to browse through them.
+   When playing a video, swipe left or right on the remote control's touch area within the video playback area to adjust the playback progress, and press the remote control's confirm button to pause/play the video.
+   When playing games, use the remote control's touchpad to control the game.
+   When playing a PPT, use the remote control's left and right buttons to turn pages.

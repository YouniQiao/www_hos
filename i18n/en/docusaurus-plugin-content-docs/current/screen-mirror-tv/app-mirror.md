# Single App Mirroring

You can wirelessly mirror the content of a single app on your phone/tablet to a HUAWEI Vision, and actions on your phone/tablet (for example, replying to messages or making and answering calls) won't affect the mirrored content. Mirroring and phone/tablet use don't interfere with each other.

## Prerequisites

+   If your phone runs HarmonyOS 5.X, single app mirroring is not supported yet.
+   Currently, only some Huawei phones/tablets support single app mirroring to a Huawei Vision (for the list of supported devices, see: [Huawei App Mirroring Supported Device List](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn15922089/)).
+   On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Multi-device Collaboration > Mirroring and make sure the mirroring switch is turned on.
+   WLAN and Bluetooth must be turned on on both your phone/tablet and the HUAWEI Vision.

## How to Use

Using a phone as an example:

1.  Swipe down from the status bar to open the notification panel, and tap Wireless Projection.
2.  In the list of available devices, select the HUAWEI Vision you want to connect to. When connecting for the first time, tap Always Trust/Trust This Time in the prompt on the HUAWEI Vision to ensure mirroring succeeds.
3.  On your phone, open the app you want to mirror (for example, HUAWEI Music).
4.  Tap the blue mirroring capsule at the top of your phone<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298084012.png" width="48" height="24"/>, and on the card that appears, choose to mirror a single app (the app is the one you opened on your phone to mirror). The app will then be mirrored to the HUAWEI Vision, and you can use your phone for other things without affecting what's displayed on the HUAWEI Vision.

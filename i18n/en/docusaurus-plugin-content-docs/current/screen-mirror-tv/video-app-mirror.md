# Cast Video Apps to Your HUAWEI Vision

You can cast video content from video apps to your HUAWEI Vision. Meanwhile, using your phone (for example, replying to messages or making and answering calls) won't affect the cast content, so casting and phone use don't interfere with each other.

## How to Cast

1.  Connect your phone and HUAWEI Vision to the same WLAN.
2.  On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Multi-device Collaboration > Cast, and make sure casting is enabled on the HUAWEI Vision.
3.  On your phone, open the video app and play any video.
4.  Tap the TV/cast/![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298074568.jpg) icon in the upper right corner (the exact icon depends on the video app), then select the name of the HUAWEI Vision you want to connect to.

# HDMI Screen Mirroring

HUAWEI Vision doesn't just support wireless screen mirroring from your phone — you can also use an HDMI adapter cable to mirror your phone's screen to the Vision.

## How to do it

1.  Get an HDMI adapter cable. One end has an HDMI connector; for the other end, choose a cable that fits your phone's port.
2.  Connect one end of the cable to your phone and the HDMI end to the HDMI port on the Vision. When prompted on your phone, agree to the operation.
3.  On the Vision home screen, open the input source<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002420941580.jpg" width="24" height="24"/>, press the menu button on the remote control<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002424490784.png" width="24" height="24"/> to open the menu, tap Switch input source, and select the corresponding HDMI port. Your screen is now mirrored successfully.

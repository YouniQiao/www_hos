# Wireless PC Screen Mirroring

You can wirelessly mirror your Windows PC to a HUAWEI Vision, displaying your PC screen on the Vision so you can enjoy the picture on the big screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Wireless screen mirroring from a PC to a Vision does not support using the Vision's camera.

## How to mirror

+   **Windows 10:**
    1.  Search for a screen mirroring device
        +   Open the ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002331998961.png) in the lower right corner of the screen, and tap Connect, or use the **Win+K** shortcut to search for nearby devices available for screen mirroring.

            ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298079784.png)

            <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The image is for reference only. Refer to the actual product.

        +   Open the ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002429125860.png) in the lower right corner of the screen, tap Project, or use the **Win+P** shortcut, then select Connect to a wireless display.

            ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332119177.png)

            <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The image is for reference only. Refer to the actual product.

    2.  Select the name of the Vision you want to connect to.
    3.  The first time you connect, you need to confirm the connection on the Vision. After that, no confirmation is needed when this PC and Vision connect again.

        <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>If you need to change the projection mode after mirroring, use the **Win+P** shortcut and select the projection mode you want.

+   **Windows 11:**
    1.  Search for a screen mirroring device
        +   Open the Settings page on your PC, and select System > Display > Multiple displays. Under Connect to a wireless display, tap Connect to open the list of devices available for screen mirroring.

            ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002331998965.png)

            <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The image is for reference only. Refer to the actual product.

        +   Use the **Win+K** shortcut to open the list of devices available for screen mirroring.
    2.  Select the name of the Vision you want to connect to.
    3.  The first time you connect, you need to confirm the connection on the Vision. After that, no confirmation is needed when this PC and Vision connect again.

## More operations

After your PC is successfully mirrored to the Vision, you can switch the audio output device on the PC. The specific operations are as follows:

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Whether this feature is supported depends on your actual Windows version. Different Windows versions may vary. Refer to the actual screen.

+   **Windows 10:** While mirroring, tap the speaker icon on the taskbar in the lower right corner of your PC. If there are multiple audio output devices, tap the expand arrow to expand the list of audio output devices and switch between them.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298079788.png)

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The image is for reference only. Refer to the actual product.

+   **Windows 11:** While mirroring, tap the speaker icon on the taskbar in the lower right corner of your PC, open <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298239424.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and select the audio output device you want to switch to.

    ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002332119181.png)

    <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The image is for reference only. Refer to the actual product.

# 4K Screen Mirroring

## Prerequisites

* The Huawei phone must be running HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.
* HUAWEI Video on the Huawei phone must be the latest version.
* To use 4K screen mirroring from HUAWEI Video on your phone, you need an active HUAWEI Video membership on the phone. The content must support AIMAX quality.
* On the HUAWEI Vision home screen, go to Settings > Multi-device Collaboration > Screen Mirroring and make sure screen mirroring is enabled on the HUAWEI Vision.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>Membership tier requirements for video apps are set by the app providers themselves. If you have any questions, please contact the video app provider.

## Version Requirements


| Device | System |HUAWEI Video |
|--|--|--|
| HUAWEI Vision |- |-|
| Phone | HarmonyOS 5.1 or later |1.0.70.300 or later |

## How to Use

1. Open HUAWEI Video on your phone and select content that supports AIMAX quality.
2. Tap the TV icon in the upper right corner to start screen mirroring, then select the HUAWEI Vision you want to mirror to from the device list.

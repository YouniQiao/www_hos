# One-tap projection

Tap the NFC area of your Huawei phone against the NFC area of the remote control to quickly project your phone screen to HUAWEI Vision. Move from a small screen to a big screen and enhance your viewing experience.

## Prerequisites

+   Currently, only some Huawei phones support one-tap projection to HUAWEI Vision. (For specific phone models, see: [List of phone models that support one-tap projection to HUAWEI Vision](https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn15942575/)).
+   WLAN and Bluetooth are enabled on both HUAWEI Vision and the phone.
+   On HUAWEI Vision, go to Settings > Multi-device collaboration > Projection, make sure the projection switch is turned on, and select the devices that are trusted by default for one-tap projection.

## Procedure

1.  With HUAWEI Vision turned on and unlocked, swipe down from the status bar of your Huawei phone to open the Control Panel, and turn on NFC.

2.  Gently tap the "**NFC** area on the back of the phone" against the "phone one-tap projection area" at the bottom front of the remote control <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002410480708.png" width="24" height="24"/>, and hold for 2–3 seconds.
    +   If your phone is running a system version earlier than HarmonyOS 5.1.0.200: wait for the list of available devices to appear on your phone, then select the HUAWEI Vision you want to connect to. Follow the on-screen prompts on HUAWEI Vision and your phone to complete projection.
    +   If your phone is running HarmonyOS 5.1.0.200 or later: you only need to tap confirm on your phone the first time you project. After that, you can project to HUAWEI Vision directly.

To disconnect, briefly press the menu button on the remote control <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374457124.png" width="24" height="24"/>, then tap ![Close icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002426454125.png) in the window that appears.

# Phone Mirroring

As long as your phone supports screen mirroring, you can mirror your phone screen to a HUAWEI Vision, which then displays your phone content.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>The steps for non-HUAWEI phones may vary slightly. Refer to the actual onscreen interface and prompts.

## Prerequisites

+   WLAN and Bluetooth must be enabled on both your phone and the Vision.
+   On the Vision's home screen, go to Settings > Multi-device Collaboration > Screen Mirroring and make sure screen mirroring is enabled on the Vision.

## Procedure

Using a HUAWEI phone as an example:

1.  Swipe down from the right side of the status bar to open the Control Panel, and tap Wireless Projection.
2.  In the list of available devices, select the Vision you want to connect to. When connecting for the first time, tap Always trust or Trust this time in the dialog box on the Vision.
3.  Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002298063720.png" width="60" height="230"/> icon at the top of the screen to open the settings dialog box and configure more settings.

    +   Privacy protection: During screen mirroring, notifications such as incoming call reminders, messages, and alarms are by default shown only on your phone, not on the Vision. To change this, turn off this switch.

    +   PC mode: The Vision displays a PC-style desktop, which you can use with a keyboard and mouse (connected to your phone) or your phone's touchpad for a PC-like experience.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   PC mode requires your HUAWEI phone to be running HarmonyOS 4.X. If your phone is running HarmonyOS 5 or later, PC mode is not supported.
+   While mirroring, press the remote control's Home button <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002451180065.png" width="24" height="24"/> (or press the remote control's Menu button <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002451484573.png" width="24" height="24"/> and then tap ![Close icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002417805630.png)) to exit the connection; press the remote control's Volume <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002407939649.png" width="24" height="24"/> and Volume <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/V0FM/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374460384.png" width="24" height="24"/> buttons to adjust the Vision's volume.

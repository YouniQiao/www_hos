# AI Voice

Once your watch is connected to your phone via Huawei Health, AI Voice on your watch makes it easy to check the weather, time, zodiac sign, jokes, and stories, and even set alarms, make calls, and open apps.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, first update your wearable device and Huawei Health on your phone to their latest versions.
+   Ensure the consistency between the following three settings: language and region in your phone system Settings, and the country/region selected when registering your HUAWEI ID (i.e. the HUAWEI ID used to log in to the Huawei Health app).

## Using AI Voice

1.  You can wake up AI Voice in either of the following ways: Using Assistant·TODAY: Swipe right on the watch home screen to enter the Assistant·TODAY screen, and tap the AI Voice icon to enter the voice input screen. Button wakeup: Press and hold the Down button to enter the voice input screen. This feature is enabled by default. If this feature is not enabled, go to Settings > Smart assistance > AI Voice, and toggle on the switch for Wake-up button.
2.  Give a voice command on the displayed screen, such as "What's the weather like today?", or "Set an alarm for 8:00 a.m. tomorrow".

## Voice broadcast

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 4.3 or earlier. It is currently unavailable when your watch is paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, an Android phone, or an iPhone.

Voice broadcast is enabled by default. Once disabled, the watch will no longer play voice messages.

Enabling voice broadcast: Enter the app list, go to Settings > Smart assistance > AI Voice, and toggle on the switch for Broadcast.

Adjusting the broadcast volume: The broadcast volume can be adjusted only during a broadcast, by pressing the volume buttons on the paired phone.

# Body temperature monitoring

This feature monitors the changes in your wrist skin temperature and body temperature after exercise. When the High temperature alert feature is enabled, your device will send an alert if body temperature is higher than the preset value for a certain period of time.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, first update your watch and Huawei Health to their latest versions.
+   The product is not a medical device. This feature is intended for change monitoring in wrist skin temperature and body temperature of healthy people over the age of 18 during and after exercise. Results are for reference only, and should not be used as a basis for medical diagnosis or treatment.
+   During the measurement, keep the device snugly on your wrist for at least 10 minutes and stay in a quiet, resting state in a relaxed environment at room temperature (about 25.0°C). Ensure there's no water, alcohol, or other liquids on your wrist skin. Avoid measuring in environments with direct sunlight, wind, or extreme temperatures.
+   After a workout, shower, or switching between outdoors and indoors, wait 30 minutes before starting a measurement.
+   Avoid measuring after drinking.

## Individual body temperature measurement

If you're using this feature for the first time, enter the watch's app list, find and select Body temperature, and touch Join.

On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Body temperature > Measure > Next > Measure now. Wait till the measurement is complete, and view your body and skin temperatures on the watch screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   When body temperature is lower than 37.2°C, the actual reading will display on the watch. When body temperature is between 37.2°C (included) and 38°C, ≥ 37.2 will display. When body temperature is 38°C or higher, ≥ 38.0 will display.

## Enabling/Disabling High temperature alert

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and go to Health monitoring > High temperature alert.
2.  Toggle High temperature alert on or off to suit your needs. When enabled, the device will continuously monitor body temperature and send an alert if temperature is higher than the preset value for a certain period of time.

## Changing the temperature unit

Go to Huawei Health > Me > Settings > Units > Temperature unit, and choose Celsius (°C) or Fahrenheit (°F).

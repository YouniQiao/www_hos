# Updates

## On your wearable device

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Updates cannot be performed on the wearable device when it's paired with an iPhone.
+   Notifications for wearable system updates cannot be disabled. Version rollback is not supported after an update.

1.  Enter the device app list and go to Settings > System & updates > Software updates.
2.  Tap the update icon and follow the onscreen instructions to download the package and update your device.

## On your phone

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and tap General settings > Firmware update.
2.  On the Firmware update screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/en-us_image_0000002531790625.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper rightcorner and toggle on Auto-download over WLAN and Auto-install updates and restart the device when it's not in use, to have new versions automatically pushed to your wearable device for updates.If you are using an iPhone, go to the Firmware update screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/en-us_image_0000002531710599.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper rightcorner, and toggle on Auto-download updates via WLAN and Auto-install updates and restart the device when it's not in use.
3.  Your phone will then check for any available updates. Follow the onscreen instructions to update your wearable device.

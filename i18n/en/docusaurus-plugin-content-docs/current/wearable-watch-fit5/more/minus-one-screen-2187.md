# Assistant·TODAY

Assistant·TODAY provides quicker, easier access to services including weather information display and music playback controls.

## Entering/Exiting Assistant·TODAY

Swipe right on the watch home screen to enter Assistant·TODAY. Then, swipe left on the screen to exit Assistant·TODAY.

## Quick access to apps

1.  Swipe right on the watch home screen to enter Assistant·TODAY.
2.  On the Assistant TODAY screen, tap any card to access the corresponding app details screen.

## Audio/Video control

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Phone audio/video controls via Assistant·TODAY on your watch are only available for ongoing audio/video apps displayed in the Media Controller on your phone. Certain apps are not compatible with your phone's Media Controller. If the Media Controller on your phone displays a message indicating that playback controls are not supported, the same applies to watch-based controls.

On the Assistant·TODAY screen, you can use the Audio/Video control card for audio/video playback controls on both your phone and watch. Note that phone audio/video controls will only work when your watch is linked with your phone normally and your phone is playing audio/video.

## Enabling AI Tips

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0F9/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   AI Tips card is only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running EMUI 11.1/HarmonyOS 2.0 or later. You need to enable the corresponding switch on your phone.

To display AI Tips (such as smart reminders for your flights, trains, and package deliveries), go to Settings > Smart assistance and toggle on AI Tips.

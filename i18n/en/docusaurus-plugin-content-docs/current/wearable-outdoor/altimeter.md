## Starting a dive


1. Press the watch's up button, then go to **Workout > Diving** in the app list.
2. Select a dive mode as needed (for example, Technical), then press the down button or tap the start icon on the dive preparation screen to enter dive mode. During a dive, you can perform the following operations: Press the down button or rotate the crown to turn pages. **Stopwatch**: When you turn to the stopwatch screen, press the up button to start/pause/resume timing. Touch and hold the up button to reset the stopwatch. **Compass**: When you turn to the compass screen, press the up button to mark/unmark a heading.
3. During a dive, press the assist button to enter the advanced menu. **Switch gas**: Press the up button to select Switch gas, press the down button to enter the gas list, select a gas, then press the down button to confirm and complete the gas switch. **Dolphin communication**: For details, see the Dolphin communication operation guide. **Reset average depth**: Press the up button to select Reset average depth. After you press the down button to confirm, the watch automatically switches to the dive data screen and refreshes the current average depth data. **Adjust GF value**: Press the up button to select Adjust GF value, press the down button to enter the GF value editing screen, adjust as needed, then press the down button to confirm and complete the GF value adjustment. **Edit gas**: Press the up button to select Edit gas, press the down button to enter the gas list, select a gas, edit the **oxygen concentration** and **helium concentration**, then press the down button on the confirmation screen to switch to the newly edited gas. **Add gas**: Press the up button to select Add gas, press the down button to enter the gas list, edit the **oxygen concentration** and **helium concentration**, press the down button to confirm, set the gas to off, on, or as the current gas type, then press the down button to finish setting it up.
4. At the surface, touch and hold the up button to end the dive.
5. After ending the dive, promptly rinse or soak the watch in fresh water for at least 15 min to prevent pollutants such as sea salt particles or sediment from accumulating on the watch. After soaking, swipe down on the watch face to enter the Control Panel, tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002450391348.png" width="560" />** to automatically drain water, then let the watch air dry. > 💡 **Tip:** After a dive, do not take a flight during the no-fly period.


## Diving examples

     **Recreational scuba**

1. On the preparation screen, you can set the water type and view the icon of the current dive type, the altitude, and the surface interval since the last dive.
2. Descent: During the descent, you can view dive data in real time, such as the dive mode, descent rate, depth, temperature, gas, and underwater time. You can also turn pages to view more dive data. On other screens, core data such as depth, NDL time, and underwater time remains displayed.
3. Bottom stay: The NDL time is initially displayed as 99 min. As depth and time increase, the NDL time gradually decreases. Meanwhile, PO2 increases with depth, and CNS gradually accumulates.
4. Low NDL: When the NDL time is less than 5 min, the NDL time is marked in yellow. To avoid entering decompression diving, ascend promptly.
5. Ascent: The NDL time gradually increases as you ascend. During the ascent, pay attention to ascent rate alerts. When the ascent rate stays above 9 m/min, the watch will remind you. When the ascent rate stays above 12 m/min, the watch will issue an alert. To reduce the risk of decompression sickness, slow down your ascent.
6. Safety stop: When you ascend to a depth of 6 m, a "Start safety stop" popup appears, and the countdown starts based on your preset safety stop time. When the safety stop ends, a "Safety stop complete" popup appears, and forward timing of the completed stop begins.

     **Technical diving**

1. Check the gas list: Check the gas list before each dive. The gas calculation in decompression calculation and the gas settings in technical diving can be shared. Make sure that every gas you carry is in the gas list, and avoid leaving gases you are not carrying turned on.
2. Decompression calculation: You can use Decompression calculation in Diving tools to create a decompression plan. Based on your maximum descent depth and bottom stay time, it estimates your total underwater time, decompression plan, gas switch timing, estimated gas consumption, and other information. These results from decompression calculation are estimates. Do not use this data as the sole basis for judgment. For complex diving activities, it is recommended to use multiple devices for comprehensive calculation and evaluation.
3. Preparation: You can set the water type and view the icon of the current dive type, the altitude, and the surface interval since the last dive. Make sure the remaining battery level is sufficient for this dive.
4. Descent: During the descent, you can view real-time changes in data such as depth, underwater time, PO2, and CNS, and you can see the NDL time gradually decrease.
5. Bottom stay: When the NDL time reaches 0, the NDL display field switches to the first decompression stop (including the decompression stop depth and time), and the TTS time gradually increases.
6. Gas switch: Throughout the dive, when the watch detects a gas better suited to the current stage, it will prompt you to switch gases. If you fail to switch in time, the corresponding reminder or alert may be triggered, and the accuracy of the decompression information cannot be guaranteed.
7. Ascent to the decompression stop: When you safely ascend to the first decompression stop, a decompression stop reminder is triggered. After completing the decompression stop for the current stage, you can continue ascending to the next decompression stop.
8. End of decompression: After completing all decompression obligations, the watch will display a "Decompression stop complete" prompt and begin forward timing.


## Reminders/alerts


| Reminder/alert | Content | Trigger condition | Notification method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reminder | **Ascent rate too fast** | Ascent rate above 9 m/min for 3 consecutive seconds | Rate (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Reminder | No-decompression countdown | No-decompression time less than or equal to 5 min for 3 consecutive seconds | NDL (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Reminder | **Start safety stop** | Safety stop required & ascending & depth less than or equal to 6 m | Popup **Start safety stop** |
| Reminder | **Safety stop complete** | Safety stop time counts down to 0 | Popup **Safety stop complete** |
| Reminder | Depth outside the safety stop range | Depth less than 3 m or greater than 7 m during the safety stop | Indicator arrow (yellow + animation) |
| Reminder | **Entering decompression diving** | NDL equals -1, entering decompression diving | Popup **Entering decompression diving** |
| Reminder | **Start decompression stop** | Decompression stop required & ascending & depth less than or equal to the decompression stop depth output by the algorithm | Popup **Start decompression stop** |
| Reminder | **Decompression stop complete** | Algorithm determines that the decompression stop is complete | Popup **Decompression stop complete**; XmYmin (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Reminder | Depth exceeds the preset value | Descent depth reaches the preset depth | Depth (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Reminder | Time exceeds the preset value | Dive time exceeds the preset time | Time (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Reminder | CNS reminder | CNS greater than or equal to 80% | CNS (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Reminder | **Recommended to switch gas to XX** | When the maximum PO2 set by the user is reached, the watch indicates that a gas turned on in the list is a better choice and recommends that the user switch | Popup **Recommended to switch gas to XX** |
| Reminder | Better gas available | Algorithm determines that a better gas is available | Gas (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Reminder | Surface interval time | Surface interval time reaches the preset value | Time (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Alert | **Oxygen partial pressure too low** | Oxygen partial pressure less than the preset minimum oxygen partial pressure | Popup **Oxygen partial pressure too low** Oxygen partial pressure (red + flashing) Gas (red + flashing) |
| Reminder | **Hover complete** | User stays within a certain depth range longer than the set threshold | Popup **Hover complete** |
| Alert | **Ascent rate too fast** | Ascent rate above 12 m/min for 3 consecutive seconds | Popup **Ascent rate too fast** Rate (red + flashing) |
| Reminder | Depth exceeds the decompression stop upper limit | Depth less than the decompression stop depth by 0.3 m (technical diving)/1.0 m (recreational scuba) | Descent indicator animation (yellow) |
| Alert | Depth less than CEIL alert | Depth less than the CEIL value | Descent indicator animation (red) |
| Alert | Missed decompression stop | Depth less than the CEIL value for 1 min | Popup "Missed decompression stop" Descent indicator animation (red) |
| Alert | **Oxygen partial pressure too high** | Oxygen partial pressure greater than the preset maximum oxygen partial pressure | Popup **Oxygen partial pressure too high** Oxygen partial pressure (red + flashing) Gas (red + flashing) |
| Reminder | **Water resistance depth exceeded** | Descent depth > 150 m | Popup **Water resistance depth exceeded** Depth (yellow + flashing for 5s) |
| Alert | **CNS too high** | CNS greater than or equal to 100%, then an alert every additional 5% | Popup **CNS too high** CNS (red + flashing) |
| Alert | Depth exceeds the preset value | Depth | Popup **Preset alert depth exceeded** Depth (red + flashing) |
| Alert | Time exceeds the preset value | Time | Popup **Preset alert time exceeded** Time (red + flashing) |
| Reminder | **Battery level low** | Battery level less than or equal to 5% | Popup **Battery level low** |

# Diving

> This document applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN supports 100-meter deep diving to meet professional diving needs.

## Using the diving feature

1. On the watch's app list, tap **Diving**.
2. Select a diving mode (scuba diving or freediving).
3. Follow the onscreen prompts to complete the pre-dive safety check.
4. Tap **Start diving**, and the watch will record diving data in real time.

## Diving data

During a dive, the watch records the following data:

- Current depth
- Underwater time
- Water temperature
- Ascent rate
- Safety stop reminder

:::tip Note
After diving, check the dive log on your watch for detailed dive data analysis.
:::

:::warning Caution
Diving is a high-risk sport. Make sure you have received professional training and follow diving safety rules. The watch is only an auxiliary tool and cannot replace professional diving equipment.
:::

# View the Outdoor Survival Manual

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

Outdoor exploration


|  | Outdoor Exploration |

   Outdoor Exploration

    During outdoor exploration, you can mark location points and navigate back along them, and quickly access key outdoor information such as altitude and blood oxygen.


## Settings


1. Press the watch's upper button, then tap **Outdoor Exploration** in the app list.
2. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002342864936.png" width="560" /> at the bottom to configure **Outdoor Exploration**. **User guide**: View information about the features supported by the Outdoor Exploration app to help you make better use of them.
3. **Battery settings**: Raise-to-wake is enabled by default for Outdoor Exploration. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002376822833.png" width="560" /> to disable it. Tap **Location recording interval** to set a **Time interval** or **Step interval**.

5. **Navigation routes**: Routes let you save tracks from workout records to My Routes, or import them from your local device into Huawei Health. You can also transfer routes directly from the popular routes list to your watch for use. In **Huawei Health**, go to **Me** > **My routes** > **Import route**, select a route file you've downloaded locally, and save it to **My routes**.
6. After selecting a target route, tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002394173505.png" width="24" /> > Transfer to wearable device** in the upper right corner to import the route file to your watch.

        **Exploration record name settings**: You can set a **Custom name** or a **Standard name**.


## Start outdoor exploration


1. Press the watch's upper button, then tap **Outdoor Exploration** in the app list.
2. Tap **Start exploration**. Once exploration begins, the exploration screen appears. During exploration, you can do the following: **Mark**: On any exploration screen, press the watch's lower button, or on the main exploration screen, tap **Mark** to manually mark a location. Tap **Mark count** to view or edit marked points. > 💡 **Tip:** Mark count only shows manually marked points. The maximum number of marked points is 500. Automatic and manual marked points are collectively called waypoints. Automatic marked points are marked once every 30 minutes by default. You can customize the marking method using a time interval or step interval.
3. **Low-light mode**: On the main exploration screen, press the watch's auxiliary button to enable or disable low-light mode.
4. **Outdoor tools**: On the main exploration screen, swipe up on the watch screen to open the **Outdoor tools** screen, where you can use exploration tools such as **Blood oxygen** and **Flashlight**.
5. On any exploration screen, press the watch's upper button to choose **End exploration**, **Lock screen**, or **Pause exploration**.
6. Navigate back. Method 1 for navigating back to a marked point: Tap **Mark count** to open the marked points list, select the marked point you want to navigate back to, then tap **Start return** once inside. Method 2: On the main exploration screen, swipe up twice on the watch screen to open the compass/map screen, tap the screen, tap **Return to marked point** to open the marked points list, rotate the upper button to switch between marked points, press the upper button to open a marked point, then tap **Start return** to navigate to the vicinity of the marked point.
7. Return along the original route: On the main exploration screen, swipe up twice on the watch screen to open the compass or map screen, tap the screen, then tap **Return along original route** to navigate to the vicinity of the starting point.
8. Press and hold the watch's upper button to end outdoor exploration.


## View exploration records

     **On the watch:**

1. Press the watch's upper button, then tap **Outdoor Exploration** in the app list.
2. Tap **Exploration records** to open the exploration records list.
3. Tap the exploration record you want to view, then swipe up or down on the screen to view details such as **Distance** and **Altitude curve**.

     **On the phone:**
     Open the **Huawei Health** app home screen, tap the **Workout records** card, then tap an outdoor exploration record to view its details.

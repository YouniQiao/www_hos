# BeiDou Satellite Communication

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

Dolphin sonar communication (Dolphin Communication for short). Once Dolphin Communication is enabled, it can be used for communication while diving.


Dolphin Communication lets dive buddies send preset text messages, emoji messages, and SOS messages to each other through their watches underwater. When a Dolphin Communication message is sent, the watch emits a continuous beeping sound for 25 seconds. Dolphin Communication has a maximum range of 30 meters. An underwater SOS message can be relayed once by a watch with Dolphin sonar communication enabled, for a maximum range of 60 meters.


Dolphin Communication uses water as the medium to transmit messages

- It can only be tested over short distances in air.
- Sending and receiving messages between air and water is not supported.

# Diving Tools

> This document applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

In addition to its core diving features, WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN also provides a variety of diving assistance tools.

## Compass

The underwater compass helps you find your bearings while diving:

1. On the diving screen, swipe left or right to switch to the **Compass** page.
2. The watch displays your current heading in real time.
3. After you set a target direction, you can quickly navigate back to that direction.

## Depth Gauge

The watch has a built-in high-precision depth sensor:

- Displays the current depth in real time
- Records the maximum depth
- Depth change curve

## Dive Log

After each dive, the watch automatically generates a dive log:

1. On the watch, open the **Diving** app.
2. Tap **Logs** to view past records.
3. Select any log to view detailed information, including:
   - Dive timeline
   - Depth curve
   - Water temperature changes
   - Heart rate data

## Dive Data Analysis

You can view more detailed dive data analysis in the HUAWEI Health app:

1. Open the **HUAWEI Health** app on your phone.
2. Go to **Devices** > **WATCH ULTIMATE** > **Diving records**.
3. View the complete dive report and trend analysis.

:::tip Note
Sync your watch with your phone regularly to ensure your dive logs are fully backed up.
:::

# Celia

With your watch connected to the Internet, you can press and hold the Down button to wake the voice assistant and enable Voice wakeup. Celia can set an alarm or start an outdoor run session for you, and deliver personalized analysis and answers based on your health and fitness data.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Before using the feature, make sure that the watch has been logged in to the same HUAWEI ID as your phone by entering the app list on your watch and tapping Settings > Log in to HUAWEI ID > Sync.
+   Before using this feature, make sure that your watch is properly connected to your phone via Bluetooth, to a WLAN, or to an eSIM cellular network.
+   The watch cannot use an iPhone's network via Bluetooth. If you're using an iPhone, connect your watch to a WLAN or enable the eSIM cellular network.

## Enabling Celia

1.  Enter the watch's app list and go to Settings > Celia. If you're using Celia for the first time, read the privacy statement carefully and tap OK to agree to the statement and enter the Settings screen. If you have selected Celia in Enhanced Services and user experience during pairing, the privacy authorization screen will not be displayed.
2.  You can wake up Celia in any of the following ways: Enter the watch's app list and tap Celia to enter the voice input screen. Button wakeup: Press and hold the Down button to enter the voice input screen. This feature is enabled by default. Voice wakeup: When enabled, say "Xiaoyi Xiaoyi" after the screen turns on to enter the voice input screen.
3.  Give a voice command on the displayed screen.For example, "Start a 3 km outdoor run", "What's the weather like today?", "Set an alarm for 8:00 tomorrow morning", "Measure my blood oxygen", or "How was my sleep yesterday?". To check the local weather via Celia, you'll need to grant it the following permissions to access location information: On your watch, enter the app list, go to Settings > Apps > Permission manager > Apps > Celia > Location, and enable Allow only while in use. On your watch, enter the app list, go to Settings > Privacy & security > Location, and enable Location Services. On your watch, enter the app list, go to Settings > Celia > Settings > Other, and enable Location-based services.

## More settings

Enter the watch's app list, go to Settings > Celia, and access the Settings screen to perform the following settings:

+   Button wakeup: When enabled, press and hold the Down button to enter voice input screen. You can enable or disable the Button wakeup feature to best suit your needs.
+   Voice wakeup: When enabled, say "Xiaoyi Xiaoyi" after the screen turns on to enter the voice input screen. You can enable or disable the Voice wakeup feature to best suit your needs.
+   Continuous conversation: When enabled, Celia will ask you for more information and automatically enter the sound pickup state after each response once it's awake and the command is not executed. In this case, you won't need to say the wakeup phrase for each conversation.
+   Voice: You can set the voice as required. This feature is supported only in Standard mode.

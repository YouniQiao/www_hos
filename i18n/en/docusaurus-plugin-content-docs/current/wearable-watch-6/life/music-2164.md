# Music playback

The watch supports playing online and offline music, so you can still enjoy listening to music even if you don't have your phone with you.

## Playing music online

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, make sure that your watch is connected to the Internet.
+   The search feature in Music on the watch is available only in certain languages.

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and tap Music.
2.  Select a song to play.

## Controlling phone music playback

Open a phone music app, such as HUAWEI Music, and start the playback.

1.  On your phone, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, tap Music, and toggle on Control phone music. Phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later: On your phone, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, tap Music and then <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002664280313.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner, and toggle on Control phone music.
2.  On your watch, swipe right on the home screen to enter the Assistant·TODAY screen, and you'll find the Music card. From there you can pause playback and switch to the previous/next track.
3.  Tap the Music card to enter the playback screen, where you can adjust the volume or tap the device name at the bottom of the screen to switch between audio output devices.

## Syncing music to your watch

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, update your watch and the Huawei Health app to their latest versions.
+   You can use the Huawei Health app to sync to your watch the unencrypted music files downloaded on your phone. These songs can then be played on your watch independently, not requiring Internet access.

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, go to Music > Manage music, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002380558656.png"/> at the bottom then to add music.
2.  When you're done, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002344097978.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner of the screen.

# Assistant·TODAY

Assistant·TODAY provides quicker, easier access to services including weather information display, music playback controls, and card adding.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   AI Tips push notifications are only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running EMUI 11.1/HarmonyOS 2.0 or later.

## Enabling/Disabling Assistant·TODAY

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and go to Settings > Watch face & home > Assistant∙TODAY.
2.  Enable or disable Assistant∙TODAY.Once Assistant∙TODAY is enabled, you can swipe right on the watch home screen to enter Assistant·TODAY. To exit, simply swipe left. If Assistant∙TODAY is disabled, swiping right on the watch home screen will not open Assistant·TODAY.

## Quick access to apps

1.  Swipe right on the watch home screen to enter Assistant·TODAY.
2.  On the Assistant TODAY screen, tap any card to access the corresponding app details screen.

## Audio/Video control

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Currently, video control is only available when your watch is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.
+   Phone audio/video controls via Assistant·TODAY on your watch are only available for ongoing audio/video apps displayed in the Media Controller on your phone. Certain apps are not compatible with your phone's Media Controller. If the Media Controller on your phone displays a message indicating that playback controls are not supported, the same applies to watch-based controls.

On the Assistant·TODAY screen, you can use the Audio/Video control card for audio/video playback controls on both your phone and watch. Note that phone audio/video controls will only work when your watch is linked with your phone normally and your phone is playing audio/video.

## Audio/Video scene awareness

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your watch is connected to a HUAWEI phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.
+   Scene cards cannot be disabled.

When you play audio/video on your phone, and the phone's Media Controller supports playback controls, your watch will automatically display a scene card for audio/video controls on the watch home screen.

## Assistant·TODAY Management

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The Edit button is only available when your watch is in Standard mode.

Adding a card

1.  Swipe right on the watch home screen to enter Assistant·TODAY.
2.  Swipe up to the last screen and tap Edit at the bottom to manage your sections.
3.  Swipe up to the last screen and tap Manage widgets to access the card management screen.
4.  Swipe up to the last screen and tap Add card to add cards as needed.

Pinning/Deleting a card

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The Music card is a built-in feature and cannot be deleted.

1.  Swipe right on the watch home screen to enter Assistant·TODAY.
2.  Touch and hold a card on the Assistant TODAY screen, and pin, unpin, or delete the card as required.You can also swipe up on the Assistant·TODAY screen to find the last screen and tap Edit at the bottom to manage your sections. From there, swipe up to the last screen again and tap Manage widgets to access the card management screen, where you can pin, unpin, or delete cards as needed.

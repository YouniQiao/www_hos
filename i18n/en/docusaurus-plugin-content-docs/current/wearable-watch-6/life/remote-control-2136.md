# Remote shutter

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 2/EMUI 8.1 or later, an Android phone, or an iPhone running iOS 13.0 or later. If you're using an iPhone, open the camera first.
+   When using the watch with an Android phone, you need to open the camera on the phone first. When your watch is paired with an Android phone, the number of Bluetooth connections on the phone must not exceed seven. If the number of connections exceeds seven, you are advised to delete some connections before using this feature.
+   If your device is connected to a phone with an initial version of HarmonyOS 5.1 or later (API version ≥ 18) or updated to HarmonyOS 6.0 or later, you can access the camera rotation, camera preview, and video and photo viewing features on your watch. Before using these features, make sure that the watch has been logged in to the same HUAWEI ID as your phone. To check your phone's API version, go to Settings, tap the search icon, and enter "API version". If your phone's API version is earlier than the required one, update your phone system. Otherwise, these features won't work.

## Remote shutter

1.  Make sure your watch is linked with Huawei Health.Swipe down from the top of the watch home screen, and make sure that <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002248941212.png"/> is displayed on the Control Panel, indicating that your watch is connected to your phone.
2.  Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera. Tap the shutter icon on the watch screen to take a photo.

## Setting a timer for photo taking

Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002283838693.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Remote shutter screen to set a timer for photo taking.

If your device is connected to a phone running HarmonyOS 5.1 or later (API version ≥ 18) or updated to HarmonyOS 6.0 or later, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002283920897.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Remote shutter screen to set a timer for photo taking. You can also select the front or rear camera to best suit your needs.

## Selecting a camera mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your watch is connected to a phone running HarmonyOS 5.1 or later (API version ≥ 18) or updated to HarmonyOS 6.0 or later.

1.  Enter the watch's app list and select Remote shutter, which will automatically launch your phone camera.
2.  Tap the text at the top of the Remote shutter screen and select the portrait, photo, or video mode to best suit your needs.
3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002283845929.png" width="24" height="24"/> to take a photo/portrait, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002248966936.png" width="24" height="24"/> to start recording video.

## Viewing photos

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your watch is connected to a phone running HarmonyOS 5.1 or later (API version ≥ 18) or updated to HarmonyOS 6.0 or later.

After a photo is taken, you can tap the thumbnail in the lower left corner to view the most recent photo on your watch. You can also double-tap the photo, pinch your fingers on the screen, or rotate the watch crown to zoom in or out on the photo. To view more photos, open Gallery on your phone.

## Using the scene card

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is only available when your watch is connected to a phone running HarmonyOS 5.1 or later (API version ≥ 18) or updated to HarmonyOS 6.0 or later.
+   This feature is only triggered when your phone is placed on a stable stand or against a stable object. If your phone is held in hand, the scene card will not be triggered.

On the watch home screen, when you are using the photo/portrait/video mode on your phone, and your phone is 60–120 degrees to the horizontal plane and still for 2–3 seconds, your watch will automatically display the Remote shutter scene card to remind you to use the feature.

# Managing messages

When your watch is linked with the Huawei Health app and message notifications are enabled, messages that are pushed to your phone's status bar can be synced to your watch.

## Enabling message notifications

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to the device details screen, tap Notifications, and toggle on Sync notifications from phone to watch.
2.  In the app list below, toggle on the switches for the apps you wish to receive notifications from.

## Push notifications settings

To receive notifications on the wearable based on the phone usage status, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, tap Notifications, and configure the notification settings.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Push notifications settings are unavailable when paired with an iPhone.

You can choose either Notify on both devices or Notify on one device as needed.

+   Notify on both devices: Get reminders simultaneously on the watch and phone.
+   Notify on one device: Get notifications on one device at a time. Your phone won't get notifications when your wearable is worn unless the phone screen is on.

## Viewing and deleting unread messages

1.  When being worn, your wearable will vibrate to notify you of new messages pushed from the status bar of your phone.
2.  Unread messages will be kept on your wearable. To view them, swipe up on the home screen to enter the message center.
3.  To delete a message, do as follows: Swipe left on a message that you wish to delete, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002525684067.png"/> to delete it. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002548191831.png"/> at the bottom of the message list to clear all messages.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   If you haven't activated the eSIM standalone service and your watch is disconnected from the phone, you will not be able to receive or reply to SMS messages or messages on your watch.
+   Messages that you've read on your wearable will still be kept in the message list on your wearable, and you'll need to tap the Deletion icon to remove them.

## Replying to SMS messages

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The feature is not supported when your device is paired with an iPhone.
+   You can reply to SMS messages directly on your watch once the eSIM Standalone Number service is enabled.
+   The quick reply feature is only supported when replying to an SMS message using the SIM card that has received the message.

1.  To reply to a new SMS message on your watch, use any of the following ways: You can tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002227220746.png"/> emoticon button below the message to select an emoticon for quick reply. You can tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002262174461.png" width="24" height="24"/> keyboard button below the message to customize the reply content. You can also swipe up on the message notification screen and select a quick reply.
2.  To customize quick replies, open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, and tap Messages.
3.  You can tap Add reply to add a reply, tap an existing reply to edit it, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002525568045.png" width="24" height="24"/> next to a reply to delete it.

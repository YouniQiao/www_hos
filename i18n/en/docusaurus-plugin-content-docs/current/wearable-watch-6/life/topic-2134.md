# Nap mode

After Nap mode is enabled on your vehicle, your watch will vibrate to remind you when the set duration ends. You can raise your wrist to display the Nap card, and stop or snooze the alarm on your watch.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, first update your watch to its latest version.
+   This feature is only available when your device is paired with a HUAWEI phone running HarmonyOS 2 or later.
+   Supported vehicle models: AITO M9 series, AITO M8 series, AITO new M5 series, AITO new M7 Ultra, and later.

## Stopping the alarm

+   Touch the stop icon on the watch screen.
+   Press and hold the Up button on the watch.

## Snoozing the alarm

+   Touch the snooze icon on the watch screen.
+   Press the Up button on the watch.
+   The alarm is automatically snoozed if no operation is performed within 40s.

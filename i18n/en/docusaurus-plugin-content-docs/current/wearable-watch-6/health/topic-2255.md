# Coronary Heart Disease Study

Join the study via the HUAWEI Research app on your phone. By simply wearing your device day and night for three to four days, you'll receive a personalized coronary heart disease risk assessment.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, first update your watch to its latest version.
+   If your device is paired with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, update the HUAWEI Research app to version 1.1.10.320 or later. For phones running a version earlier than HarmonyOS 5 or Android phones, update the app to version 16.0.10.320 or later.
+   The HUAWEI Research app is not supported on iPhones. Consequently, this feature is unavailable when your watch is paired with an iPhone.
+   Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you experience any discomfort, please seek medical assistance.
+   This study is only intended for individuals aged 18 and older.

## Joining the study

On the HUAWEI Research home screen of your phone, tap Coronary Heart Disease Study. If you're using the app for the first time, follow the onscreen instructions to grant the necessary permissions. Then, tap Join study to access the study.

## Starting risk assessment

You'll be prompted to take your ECG at the start of each assessment cycle, then you'll need to wear your watch for about three days both day and night. During the cycle, you will be prompted to measure your ECG again.

1.  Ensure that your wearable device is connected to Huawei Health via Bluetooth.
2.  Open the HUAWEI Research app on your phone, and go to Coronary Heart Disease Study > Start. If this is your first assessment, complete the questionnaire and enter your personal information by following the onscreen instructions.
3.  On the tutorial screen, tap Next to view the measurement guide.
4.  When using the HUAWEI Research app on your watch for the first time, follow the onscreen instructions on the device to complete the privacy authorization and specify which wrist you're wearing the device on.
5.  Open the ECG app on your watch. Tap the X-TAP sensor with your finger to start. Maintain a still and correct posture during the measurement.
6.  Once the ECG measurement is complete, the assessment will begin. Wear your watch day and night throughout the assessment. You can track the progress on the Coronary Heart Disease Study home screen within the HUAWEI Research app on your phone.
7.  Upon reaching 100% completion with sufficient wear time, the watch will display the assessment results and automatically sync them to your phone.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Wear your watch for about three days (maximum of four days), covering at least 23 hours during the daytime and 18 hours at night in total. Once these requirements are met, the watch will display your assessment results when the progress hits 100%.
+   During the assessment, your watch will provide progress reminders for day and night wear time, reminders for optional and post-exercise ECGs, and status updates on potential failure or completion.
+   If you haven't started an assessment in a long time, the system will send a reminder based on your last assessment results to encourage regular assessments.

## Viewing assessment results

+   On the HUAWEI Research home screen of your phone, tap Coronary Heart Disease Study to view your assessment progress and results.
+   Once an assessment is complete, your watch will prompt you to check the detailed results, which will also automatically sync to your phone.

## Leaving the study

On the HUAWEI Research home screen of your phone, tap Coronary Heart Disease Study. Then tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002579678672.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the upper right corner and select Leave study.

You can rejoin the study at any time you desire.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Please note that after leaving, all data associated with the study will be permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

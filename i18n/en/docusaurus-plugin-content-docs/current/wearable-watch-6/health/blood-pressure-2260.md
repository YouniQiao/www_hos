# Hypertension Risk Study

Your watch's high-performance sensor continuously collects data day and night to provide a hypertension risk assessment. Continuous participation in the study can help detect your risk for hypertension and cardiovascular disease.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   The HUAWEI Research app is not available on iPhones, but it can be used on your watch independently.
+   This product is not a medical device. Measurement data and results are for reference only and should not be used as a basis for diagnosis or treatment. If you experience any discomfort, please seek medical assistance.
+   It takes 24 hours to complete an assessment. The following conditions must be met:Wearing duration requires a minimum of 7 hours during the day and 6 hours overnight.

## Joining the Hypertension Risk Study

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study > Join study.
2.  If you are joining the study for the first time, you need to enable sensor permissions as prompted and tap Allow.
3.  If you are joining the study for the first time, read the overview carefully, fill in your personal information as prompted, and select the wearing mode of your watch.Before each round of assessment, you must complete the 5-step measurement. Gently touch the X-TAP with your index finger to start. During the measurement, follow the onscreen instructions to adjust your finger's contact pressure through 5 levels. The assessment can only begin after this measurement is complete.

Viewing the assessment progress and results

Press the Up button on the watch to enter the app list, and go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study to view your assessment progress on the home screen.

After the assessment is complete, you can view your results (such as "Suspected hypertension" or "No abnormality"). Detailed assessment results are displayed on the latest results page.

Viewing historical risk records

Press the Up button on your watch to enter the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study, and swipe up on the home screen to view historical risk records. Tap a record to view the hypertension risk assessment results over the past seven days. Tap a specific date to view the detailed hypertension assessment result for that day. For more details, check the HUAWEI Research app on your phone.

## Reminders

1.  Press the Up button on your watch to open the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study, then swipe up on the home screen to the final screen and tap Reminders.
2.  Notifications is enabled by default to send you notifications about the study's progress, lag alerts, and more. When the assessment task falls behind schedule, you can tap Measure as prompted to complete the 5-step measurement to get back on track.

## Leaving the study

Press the Up button on the watch to enter the app list, go to HUAWEI Research > Hypertension Risk Study, swipe up on the home screen to the final screen, and tap Leave study to exit. Doing so will permanently clear your historical assessment records. You can re-join the study at any time you desire.

# Expedition mode

When you're on an outdoor expedition, you can mark your locations and navigate back along them. You can also use the watch to get key data like altitude and SpO2.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   This feature is currently only available on HUAWEI WATCH 6 Pro.

## Settings

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and tap Expedition.
2.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002342864936.png" width="24" height="24"/> at the bottom for more Expedition related settings. Battery: When in Expedition mode, the Raise to wake feature is enabled by default. You can toggle off the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002376822833.png"/> switch to disable it. You can also set Positioning interval by Time or Steps. Storm warning: Enable or disable Warnings to suit your needs. When enabled, you can set Pressure drop. Routes: You can save routes from your workout records to My route, import routes from your phone into the Huawei Health app, or directly transfer routes from the popular routes list to your watch. Record at night is enabled by default, and you can toggle off the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002343024756.png"/> switch to disable it. Dim after sunset is enabled by default, and you can toggle off the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002376942741.png"/> switch to disable it.

## Starting an outdoor expedition

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and tap Expedition.
2.  Tap Start. During the expedition: Mark: On any screen of the Expedition app, press the Down button on the watch, or on the Expedition app home screen, tap Mark to manually mark a location. Tap Marked to view and edit the marked location. The Marked option only shows manually marked locations. You can mark up to 500 locations. Such marked locations, whether automatically or manually marked, are called waypoints. By default, a waypoint is marked automatically every 30 minutes. You can set positioning interval to by time or steps to better suit your needs. Tools: On the Expedition app home screen, swipe up to access Tools, like SpO2 and Flashlight. On any screen of the Expedition app, your can press the Up button on the watch and select to End or Pause the journey, or Lock screen.
3.  For your return journey: Navigate back to a waypointMethod 1: Tap Marked to enter the waypoint list, find and select your target waypoint, and tap Navigate. Method 2: On the Expedition app home screen, swipe up twice to access the compass/map screen, tap that screen, and tap Navigate to enter the waypoint list. Rotate the Up button to switch between waypoints, press the Up button to select a target waypoint, and tap Navigate to navigate to a place near the waypoint. Backtrack: On the Expedition app home screen, swipe up twice to access the compass or map screen, tap that screen, and tap Backtrack to navigate to a place near your starting point.
4.  Press and hold the Up button on the watch to end the expedition.

## Viewing expedition records

On your watch:

1.  Press the Up button to enter the app list, and tap Expedition.
2.  Tap Expedition records to display the record list.
3.  Select a target record, and swipe up or down on the screen to view the details like Distance and Altitude.

On your phone:

Access the home screen of Huawei Health, tap the Exercise records card, and select an expedition record to view the details.

## Deleting Expedition records

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Expedition records cannot be deleted directly from your watch. You can delete them on your phone.

1.  Open the Huawei Health app, navigate to Exercise records, and select Expedition records.
2.  Find the specific record you wish to remove, and touch and hold it to delete it.

# Starting a workout

Your watch supports multiple workout modes, including exercises such as running and biking, fitness courses, equipment training, and other activities like leisure sports. You can select a workout mode based on your needs.

## Starting a workout on your watch

1.  Enter the device app list and tap Workout.
2.  Swipe up or down on the screen and select a desired workout mode. You can also swipe up until you reach the end of the screen, and tap Add to add other workout modes.
3.  Tap the workout icon to start a workout session.
4.  During a workout, you can press and hold the Up button to end the exercise. Alternatively, press the Up button to pause, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002167346396.png" width="24" height="24"/> to resume, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002349229442.png" width="24" height="24"/> to end the workout.During a workout, you can swipe up or down on the screen to view fitness data, and swipe right to quickly access the shortcut settings screen. From there, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002551443975.png" width="24" height="24"/> to adjust the volume of workout reminders or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002551563967.png" width="24" height="24"/> to access the workout settings screen.

## Starting a workout in the Huawei Health app

1.  Double-check that your watch is linked with Huawei Health, and wear your device snugly on your wrist. Then in Huawei Health, for example, go to Exercise > Outdoor run and tap the running icon to start your run.Phones running HarmonyOS 5 or later: Go to Huawei Health > Workout, select a workout mode, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002572729512.png"/> to start the workout.
2.  During a workout, the fitness data will be displayed on both the Huawei Health app and your watch. If the workout distance or duration is too short, the record won't be saved.
3.  During a workout, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002382949909.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the Huawei Health screen to pause, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002349389262.png" width="24" height="24"/> to resume, or hold down on <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002349229510.png" width="24" height="24"/> to end the workout. Your wearable device will pause, resume, or end the workout accordingly.

## Viewing workout records and workout status

After a workout, you can view your workout records and workout status as follows:

1.  Enter the device app list and tap Workout records.
2.  Select a record and view the corresponding details. (You'll see different types of data for different workout modes. Actual display shall prevail.)

Viewing workout records in Huawei Health

On the Huawei Health home screen, tap Exercise records to view your workout details. (You'll see different types of data for different workout modes. Actual display shall prevail.)

When you start a workout using Huawei Health and sync it to your watch, the workout records will only be available in Huawei Health.

1.  Enter the device app list and tap Workout status.
2.  Swipe up on the screen to view various workout data, such as Running Ability Index, Training load, Training index, Recovery, VO2Max, and Predicted times. Depending on the workout mode, the data displayed varies. Actual display shall prevail.

+   When you access Workout records on your watch for the first time, tap Value-added services on the privacy statement authorization screen, select AI analysis, and confirm the authorization.
+   Your watch must maintain a stable network connection during the AI analysis process. If the network connection is interrupted, an error message will display on the screen. Once the network connection is restored, the AI analysis task for the corresponding workout will automatically resume.

1.  Enter the device app list, tap Workout records, swipe up on the screen to access the More screen, then go to About > App description, and toggle on AI interpretation service.
2.  After enabling this feature, simply navigate to the Workout records screen and tap AI analysis after you end a workout.
3.  On the analysis screen, you can either stay and wait for the analysis to complete, or exit the app and let it process in the background. A notification will display once the analysis is complete; you can then tap it to view the results.

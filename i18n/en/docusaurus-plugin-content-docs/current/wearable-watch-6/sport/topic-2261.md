# Snow sports

The watch supports both indoor and outdoor skiing and snowboarding, monitoring various fitness metrics to help you improve your performance with every run. It also supports ski resort maps, allowing you to view the resort layouts instantly simply by raising your wrist.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, first update your wearable device and Huawei Health on your phone to their latest versions.

## Downloading ski resort maps

Before starting outdoor skiing/snowboarding or cross-country skiing, download the corresponding ski resort map. Once downloaded, the watch will display the distribution of ski slopes at your current resort and track your workout routes in real time, enhancing both your snow sports experience and your safety.

1.  Open Huawei Health, navigate to the device details screen, then tap Offline maps to access the Regions screen.
2.  Download Global overview map, Ski resort maps (Global), and the offline map of your current region. Do not leave this screen during the download process.
3.  After the download is complete, you can view the download results on the Manage downloads screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Currently, more than 3,000 ski resort maps are supported, covering ski resorts across 59 countries and regions.
+   You can also enable the function to automatically download offline maps for your region.On the Manage downloads screen of Offline maps, enable Auto-cache over WLAN. Once this feature is enabled, when your watch detects that you have arrived in a new city or region and your phone is connected to a WLAN network, the offline map of your current location will be automatically cached and synced to your watch. (Note: This feature is not supported when the watch is paired with an iPhone.)

## Outdoor snowboarding or skiing

1.  Download the snow resort map.
2.  On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Workout > Skiing.
3.  Select Alpine skiing or Snowboarding as needed, then tap to enter the preparation screen. Once positioning is successful, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002594062378.png"/> to begin your session. On the workout screen, swipe up or down to view your data (such as activity time, distance, speed, turns, slope, total descent, G-Force, and route). On the workout screen, swipe right to access shortcut settings screen. From there, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002629051294.png" width="24" height="24"/> to adjust the volume of workout reminders or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002659530487.png" width="24" height="24"/> to access the workout settings screen.
4.  After your workout ends, you can view detailed records in Workout records on your watch or Exercise records on the Huawei Health home screen.The workout records include your workout duration, distance, slope, turns, runs, trail breakdown, time breakdown, maximum G-Force, and workout route. Note that the workout route and time breakdown can only be viewed within the Huawei Health app.

## Cross-country skiing

1.  Before starting, import the skiing route file into the Huawei Health app and transfer it to your watch. If the route is located in a new city, you must download that city's offline map as well. Go to Huawei Health > Me > My route > Import route, select a downloaded route, and save it to My route. Select the target route, then tap Transfer to wearable at the bottom of the screen to sync the route to your watch.
2.  In the app list on your watch, go to Workout > Skiing > Cross-country skiing to enter the skiing preparation screen. Swipe up to access the More screen, and tap Routes.
3.  Select your desired route and go to Go exercise > <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002625425715.png"/> to begin skiing. On the workout screen, swipe up or down to view your data (such as duration, speed, and route navigation). On the workout screen, swipe right to access shortcut settings screen. From there, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002659410541.png" width="24" height="24"/> to adjust the volume of workout reminders or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002629211186.png" width="24" height="24"/> to access the workout settings screen.
4.  After your workout ends, you can view detailed records in Workout records on your watch or Exercise records on the Huawei Health home screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Before starting cross-country skiing, download the offline map of your city.

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Workout > Skiing > Cross-country skiing to enter the skiing preparation screen. Once positioning is successful, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002594986204.png"/> to begin your skiing workout.
2.  After your workout ends, you can view detailed records in Workout records on your watch or Exercise records on the Huawei Health home screen.

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Workout > Skiing.
2.  Select Indoor snowboarding or Indoor skiing as needed to enter the preparation screen. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002625343043.png"/> to begin your session. On the workout screen, swipe up or down to view your workout data (such as skiing time, distance, slope, speed, turns, and total descent). On the workout screen, swipe right to access shortcut settings screen. From there, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002629051290.png" width="24" height="24"/> to adjust the volume of workout reminders or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002659530485.png" width="24" height="24"/> to access the workout settings screen.
3.  After your workout ends, you can view detailed records in Workout records on your watch or Exercise records on the Huawei Health home screen.

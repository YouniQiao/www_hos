# Pairing with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later

## Preparations

1.  Install the Huawei Health app (<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002374851905.png" width="24" height="24"/>).
2.  On your phone, go to Settings > Log in to HUAWEI ID. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, please register for one, and then log in. It is recommended that an adult account be used for login, to ensure successful pairing and connection between your wearable device and your phone.
3.  Power on your wearable device, select a language, and start pairing.

## Pairing and connection

Connect via Add device:

1.  Place your wearable device close to your phone. Access the home screen of Huawei Health, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002340894070.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner and then Add device. Find your wearable device in the search result and tap Connect.
2.  When a pairing request displays on your wearable device, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002341053842.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions on your phone to complete the pairing.

Connect via Scan:

1.  Place your wearable device close to your phone. Open Huawei Health and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002374732085.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upper right corner and then Scan, to scan the QR code displayed on the wearable device.
2.  When a pairing request displays on your wearable device, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0FO/en-US/en-us_image_0000002374851909.png" width="24" height="24"/> and follow the onscreen instructions on your phone to complete the pairing.

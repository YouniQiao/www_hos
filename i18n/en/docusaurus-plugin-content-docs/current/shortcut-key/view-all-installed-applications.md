Shortcut key to view all installed apps

Press the HarmonyOS key ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + A on the keyboard to enter App Center, where you can view all apps installed on your PC. Click an app icon to open it. Press the keys again to exit.

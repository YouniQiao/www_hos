Use a shortcut key to open Celia Search

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Press the HarmonyOS key on the keyboard ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + S to open Celia Search. Enter a keyword (such as a file name, content, or attribute) to search for files, apps, and system settings on your PC, as well as information on the Internet. Press the shortcut again to exit.

Take a Regional Screenshot with a Shortcut

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Press the HarmonyOS key![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/keyboard/HM_keyboard_OH_1.png) + Shift + S key combination on the keyboard. The cursor changes to ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_screenshot_partial_cursor.png). Move it to the starting point of the area you want to capture, press and hold with one finger on the touchpad (or the left mouse button), and drag to select the area. Release your finger once the area is set.

# Marathon mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EV/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   To use this feature, first update the Huawei Health app to its latest version.
+   Make sure the goals set are reasonable and that you're in good condition before the event to get the most out of performance guidance.
+   Event information was obtained from the event's official website. For the most current event information, please refer to the event's official website.

## Marathon settings

1.  Enter the device app list and tap Workout.
2.  Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EV/en-US/en-us_image_0000002491299042.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon next to Marathon to access the Marathon settings screen. Here, you can customize the following options: Marathon guidance: Tap Marathon guidance and enable Marathon guidance.Once enabled, you'll get guidance during the marathon and reminders to refuel water and carbs. Tap Performance guidance and choose between Pace-based and HR-based according to your preferences. Pacer: Tap Pacer and enable Pacer.Once enabled, your watch will set a virtual pacer based on your goal time to help you during the event.

## Event management

Adding an event:

1.  Access the Health screen of Huawei Health and select Outdoor run.
2.  Scroll down to the Event assistant card, tap Search event, and choose your target event.
3.  Tap Next, select your desired Event distance and Goal, then tap Add to My events.
4.  Once added, the event will automatically sync to your watch. You can also manually sync an event to your watch by opening the My events screen, tapping <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EV/en-US/en-us_image_0000002500795264.png" width="24" height="24"/> in the upperright corner, and selecting Sync to device.

If you'd like to add a custom event, tap Custom event, and enter the event's name, date, and other details to create it.

Deleting an event:

1.  Access the Health screen of Huawei Health and select Outdoor run.
2.  On the Event assistant card, tap My events and then tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EV/en-US/en-us_image_0000002529469920.png" width="24" height="24"/> icon in the upperright corner, and select Delete event.
3.  Choose the event you want to delete, and tap Delete.

## Viewing an event

On your watch:

Enter the device app list, and choose Workout > Marathon to view your event information, such as days remaining until the event and your goal.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EV/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   Completed events will only be visible in the Huawei Health app, not on your watch.

On your phone:

1.  Access the Health screen of Huawei Health and select Outdoor run.
2.  On the Event assistant card, tap My events to view all added events. Tap In progress to see upcoming events. Tap an event to view details or make edits. Tap Ended to view completed events. When no events have been completed, all events will be displayed directly on the My events screen. The In progress and Ended options will not be available.

## Starting a run

1.  On your watch, enter the app list, and go to Workout > Marathon.
2.  If multiple races are scheduled for the day, choose the one you plan to participate in. If no races are available, you can choose Test run to practice.
3.  Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EV/en-US/en-us_image_0000002491476196.png"/> to start running. Once you finish, your marathon data will automatically sync to your phone, where you can review your performance.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EV/en-US/images/icon-note.svg" width="24" height="24"/>

+   During your run, you'll get personalized reminders to rehydrate or take in carbs to keep your pace up, and avoid dehydration and glycogen depletion. These reminders are based on your physical characteristics, goal result, athletic ability, race pace, weather conditions, and typical water station distributions.

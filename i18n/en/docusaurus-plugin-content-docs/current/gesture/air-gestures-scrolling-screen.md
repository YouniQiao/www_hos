# Swipe with Air Scroll

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_Swing_swipe.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Air scroll allows you to swipe up and down without having to tap the screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click System in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, go to **Shortcuts & gestures** > **Air scroll** and make sure that Air scroll is enabled.

3.  When the screen is on, hold your palm (with your fingertips facing up) or the back of your hand (with your fingertips facing down) parallel to the screen, at a distance of about half an arm's length away from the screen, and flick your palm downwards or upwards when the![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handup_blue.png) or![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handdown_blue.png) icon displays on the screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

To use this feature, your computer must run HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

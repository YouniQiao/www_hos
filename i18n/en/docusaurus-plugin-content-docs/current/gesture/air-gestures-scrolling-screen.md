# Air gesture screen scrolling

<video controls src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Swing_swipe.mp4" width="720" height="540"></video>

When you're browsing content and your hands aren't free to touch the screen, you can use air gestures to scroll up and down.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. Click ![Settings](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to open **Settings**, then click **System** in the left sidebar.
2. On the right, click **Quick access and gestures > Air scroll**, and make sure the **Air scroll** switch is turned on.
3. With the screen on, hold your hand about half an arm's length from the screen (40–70 cm is recommended), with your palm facing the screen (fingers pointing up) or the back of your hand facing the screen (fingers pointing down), and point it at the camera. Pause briefly until ![Gesture up](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handup_blue.png) or ![Gesture down](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handdown_blue.png) appears on the screen, then flick your wrist downward or upward repeatedly to scroll the page down or up.

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

This feature requires HarmonyOS 5.1 or later on your PC.

# Slide one finger up or down along the left edge to adjust brightness

<video controls src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_brightness_adjust.mp4" width="720" height="540"></video>

Use a touchpad gesture to quickly adjust the screen brightness.


![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

Slide one finger at a steady speed up or down along the left edge of the touchpad to increase or decrease the screen brightness.


![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

This feature requires PC system version 5.1.0.320 SP23 or later.

# Knuckle Screenshot and Screen Recording

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_knuckle_ScreenShot.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Capture content on your screen or record the screen, simply by knocking on the screen with your knuckles!

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Touchpad in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, click the More gestures tab and click to toggle on the Capture screen switch.

3.  Use your knuckles to take a screenshot or record the screen:

    +   Capture the full screen: Knock twice swiftly at one place on the touchpad with a single knuckle.

    +   Record the screen: Knock twice swiftly at one place on the touchpad with two knuckles to start screen recording. Knock twice swiftly with two knuckles at one place again to stop screen recording.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

You can go to **Gallery** > **Screenshots** to view screenshots, or go to **Gallery** > **Screen recordings** to view screen recordings by default.

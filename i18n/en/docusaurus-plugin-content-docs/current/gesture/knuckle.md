# Knuckle screenshots and screen recording

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_Knuckle.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

Use your knuckle to quickly take a full-screen screenshot or record the screen — fast and convenient.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then tap Touchpad in the left sidebar.
2.  On the right, tap the More gestures tab, then turn on the Knuckle gestures switch.
3.  As needed, use your knuckle to take a screenshot or record the screen:
    +   **Take a full-screen screenshot:** Tap twice in a row with one knuckle at the same spot on the touchpad.
    +   **Record the screen:** Tap twice in a row with two knuckles at the same spot on the touchpad to start recording; tap twice again with two knuckles at the same spot on the touchpad to stop recording.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

By default, screenshots are saved in Gallery under From apps > Screenshots, and screen recordings are saved in Gallery under From apps > Screen recording.

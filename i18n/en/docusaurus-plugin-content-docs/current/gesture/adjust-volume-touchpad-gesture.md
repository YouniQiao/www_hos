# Slide one finger up or down along the right edge to adjust the volume

<video controls src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_volume_adjust.mp4" width="720" height="540"></video>

Quickly adjust the system volume with a touchpad gesture.

![method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

Slide one finger at a steady speed up or down along the right edge of the touchpad to raise or lower the system volume.

![tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

This feature requires PC system version 5.1.0.320 SP23 or later.

# Take Grabshots from a Distance

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_Swing_ScreenShot.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Grabshot allows you to take screenshots of your device with easy air gestures, and without having to tap the screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click System in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, go to **Shortcuts & gestures** > **Grabshot** and make sure that Grabshot is enabled.

3.  When the screen is on, hold your palm about half an arm's length (about 40–70 cm) away from the screen, wait for the![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handup_blue.png) icon to appear, and clench your fist to take the grabshot.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

You can go to **Gallery** > **Screenshots** to view the screenshots by default.

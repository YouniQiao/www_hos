# Grab a screenshot with an air gesture

<video controls src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Swing_ScreenShot.mp4" width="720" height="540"></video>

Take a screenshot with an air gesture, without your hands ever touching the device — it's cool and fun.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. On the home screen, tap ![Settings](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the **dock** at the bottom to open **Settings**, then tap **System** in the left sidebar.
2. On the right, tap **Shortcuts & gestures** > **Air screenshot**, and make sure the **Air screenshot** switch is turned on.
3. While the screen is on, hold your open palm about half an arm's length from the screen (40–70 cm is recommended), aim it at the camera, and pause briefly. When ![gesture icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handup_blue.png) appears on the screen, clench your fist to take the screenshot.

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

- By default, screenshots are saved in **Gallery** under **From apps** > **Screenshots**.
- This feature requires HarmonyOS 5.1 or later on your PC.

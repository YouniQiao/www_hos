# Swipe up with three fingers to show the home screen

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_ShowHomeScreen.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

When you have many apps open, use a touchpad gesture to minimize all open app windows and quickly return to the home screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Swipe up with three fingers on the touchpad to minimize all open app windows; swipe down with three fingers to restore all open app windows.

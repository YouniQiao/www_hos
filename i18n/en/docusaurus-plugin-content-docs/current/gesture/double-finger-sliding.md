# Slide two fingers to browse more content

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_ScrollThrough.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>


When a document or web page spans beyond the screen or is cut off, use touchpad gestures to scroll the page up and down and browse more content.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take browsing a document as an example:

Tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_filemanager.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Files, then double-tap a document (such as a PDF) to open it. Slide two fingers up or down on the touchpad to scroll the page up or down in sync.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If your device supports touchscreen input, you can also place one or two fingers on the document or web page area of the screen, then slide up or down to scroll the page and browse more content.

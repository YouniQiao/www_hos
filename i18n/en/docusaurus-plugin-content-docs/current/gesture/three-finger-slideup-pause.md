# Slide up with three fingers, pause, then lift to view all running apps

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_TaskCenter.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

When you have many apps running at the same time, use a touchpad gesture to quickly open Task Center and view all running apps.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

On the touchpad, slide up with three fingers, pause, then lift to open Task Center. Slide down with three fingers to exit Task Center.

# Pinch to zoom images/pages

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_PinchToZoom.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>

On certain zoomable images/pages, you can use touchpad gestures to quickly zoom in and out.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take zooming in and out of images in Gallery as an example:

Tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery, then double-tap an image to open its details page. Spread two fingers apart on the touchpad to zoom in, or pinch them together to zoom out.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If your device supports touchscreen operations, you can also place two fingers on the image area of the screen, then spread them apart or pinch them together to zoom in or out.

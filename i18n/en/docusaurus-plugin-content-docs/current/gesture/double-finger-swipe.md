# Swipe with two fingers to switch pages

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Touchpad_Swipe.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="720" height="540"> </iframe>


On certain pages that can be switched left and right, you can use a touchpad gesture to switch pages quickly.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take switching between and viewing images in Gallery as an example:

Tap ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_gallery.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Gallery, then double-tap an image to open its details page. Swipe left or right with two fingers on the touchpad to switch to the next or previous image.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If your device supports touchscreen operations, you can also place one finger on the image area of the screen and swipe left or right to switch to the next or previous image.

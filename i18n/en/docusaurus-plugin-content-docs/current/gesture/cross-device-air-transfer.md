# Transfer Files Across Devices with Air Gestures

<video controls src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/video/vid_Swing_GrabDrop.mp4" width="720" height="540"></video>

Use air gestures to easily transfer photos or videos from this device's Gallery, music or videos that are playing, or a screenshot of the current screen to another device.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

1. On both devices, go to **Settings > System > Quick launch and gestures > Air transfer**, turn on **Air transfer**, and keep the screens on and unlocked.
2. On the sending device, open the content you want to share:
   - **Share a photo or video:** Open a single photo or video in **Gallery**.
   - **Share music:** Update Huawei **Music** to the latest version, then open the song's playback screen.
   - **Share a video:** Update Huawei **Video** to the latest version, then open the video's playback screen.
3. About half an arm's length from the sending device's screen (40–70 cm is recommended), hold your palm open facing the camera and pause briefly. When ![gesture icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handup_blue.png) appears on the screen, make a fist to grab the content, and the grab animation will appear.
4. Keeping your hand in a fist, move it in front of the receiving device's screen facing the camera and pause briefly. When ![fist icon](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_fist_blue.png) appears on the screen, open your palm to start the transfer.

![Tips](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png) Tips

- This feature requires HarmonyOS 5.1 or later on your PC.
- Only some phones/tablets support air transfer, and they must be running HarmonyOS 5 or later.
- Before transferring music or videos with air gestures, update Huawei **Music** and **Video** to the latest versions in AppGallery.

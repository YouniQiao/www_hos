# Cross-device File Transfers via Grab & drop

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_Swing_GrabDrop.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Enable Grab & drop to transfer images or videos from Gallery, the music or video that is being played, or screenshots to other devices using air gestures.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  On both devices, go to **Settings** > **System** > **Shortcuts & gestures** > **Grab & drop**, enable Grab & drop, and keep the screen unlocked and on.

2.  Open the content you wish to share on the sending device:

    +   Share an image or video: Open the image or video you wish to share in Gallery.

    +   Share a song: Update HUAWEI Music to the latest version and open the song playback screen.

    +   Share a video: Update HUAWEI Video to the latest version and open the video playback screen.

3.  Hold your palm about half an arm's length away from the screen of the sending device until![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_handup_blue.png) displays on the screen. Then clench your fist and wait for the sharing animation to display.

4.  Hold your fist and move it to the front of the camera of the receiving device. When![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_AirGesture_fist_blue.png) displays on the screen, stretch your palm out.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   To use this feature, your computer must run HarmonyOS 5.1 or later.

+   Only certain phones/tablets running HarmonyOS 5 or later support Grab & drop.

+   Before using Grab & drop to transfer music and videos, update HUAWEI Music and HUAWEI Video to their latest versions via AppGallery.

+   It is recommended that the distance between the sending and receiving devices be greater than 40 cm for better experience.

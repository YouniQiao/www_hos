# Set the screen auto-off duration

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_ScreenSleep_time.png" width="720" height=""/>

When you don't use your PC for a while, the screen turns off automatically. You can change the auto-off duration as needed.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Power and battery in the left sidebar.
2.  On the right pane, set the duration for Turn off screen when idle under both On battery power and Plugged in.

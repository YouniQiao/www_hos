# Font and Display Size Adjustments

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_settings_display_adjustsize.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Switch the font effects and display size with absolute ease for better readability.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Display & brightness in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, drag the sliders or click the icons (such as![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_settings_display_text.png) and![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/HM_public_plus.png)) near the sliders to adjust the settings, and click Apply.

    +   **Text size**: Adjusts the size of text within apps.

    +   **Text weight**: Adjusts the weight of text within apps.

    +   **Display size**: Zooms in or out on content such as images and text on the app screen.

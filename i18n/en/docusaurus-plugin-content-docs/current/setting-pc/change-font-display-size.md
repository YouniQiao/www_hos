# Adjust font and display size

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_display_adjustsize.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can set different font effects and display sizes based on your habits to make the content on your PC screen easier to view.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Display & brightness in the left sidebar.
2.  On the right pane, drag the slider for the desired setting or click the icons on either side of the slider (such as ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_settings_display_text.png), ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/HM_public_plus.png)) to make adjustments, then click Apply.
    +   **Font size:** Adjust the text size in app interfaces.
    +   **Font weight:** Adjust the text weight in app interfaces.
    +   **Display size:** Scale the content displayed in app interfaces (such as text and images) proportionally.

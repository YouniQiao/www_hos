# Smart charging for a longer battery lifespan

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_SmartCharging.png" width="720" height=""/>

Turn on Smart charging mode and the system will learn your charging habits and manage charging intelligently, so your battery doesn't sit at full charge for long periods. This slows battery aging and extends its lifespan.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Power and battery in the left sidebar.
2.  On the right, click Battery health, then click the switch to turn on Smart charging mode. You can also turn on Custom charging mode as needed and set the maximum charge level yourself.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

Smart charging mode and Custom charging mode are mutually exclusive. Choose the mode that best suits your needs.

# Display Battery Percentage in the Status bar

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_settings_BatteryPercent.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Stay plugged in to how your battery is doing, by having the battery percentage shown in the Status bar.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Click <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png" width="24" height="24"/> in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open **Settings**, click **Power & battery** in the left pane, and enable **Show battery percentage** in the right pane.

# Show Battery Percentage in the Status Bar

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_BatteryPercent.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can choose to show the battery percentage in the status bar at the bottom right of the home screen, so you can check the battery level at any time.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, click Power and battery in the left sidebar, and then turn on the Show battery percentage switch on the right.

# Give Your Eyes a Breather, with Dark Mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_settings_display_darkmode.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Enable Dark mode to apply a darker color scheme, and save power while sparing your eyes.

Enable Dark mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, and click Display & brightness in the left pane.

2.  In the right pane, click Dark mode, and toggle on the Scheduled or Enable all day switch as required.

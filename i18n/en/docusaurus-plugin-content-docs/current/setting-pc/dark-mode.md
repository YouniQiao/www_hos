# Dark Mode: Easier on the Eyes

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_display_darkmode.png" width="720" height=""/>

Set app backgrounds to a darker tone to reduce the strain bright screens put on your eyes — it's both comfortable and power-efficient.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the shortcut bar at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Display & brightness in the left sidebar.
2.  On the right, click Dark mode, then toggle on either Scheduled or All day, as needed.

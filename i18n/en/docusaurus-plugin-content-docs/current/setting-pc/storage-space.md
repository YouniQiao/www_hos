# View and Optimize Device Storage

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_StorageUsage.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

You can view the storage space usage of your computer and clear the cache data of apps or uninstall apps that are not frequently used to optimize the available storage space of your computer.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings. Click Storage in the left sidebar, and view the storage usage on the right.

2.  Click an app to access the details screen and view the storage space occupied by the app.

3.  Click Clear cache or Uninstall as required to free up the storage space occupied by the app.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Note

+   Certain apps cannot be uninstalled. Please refer to the actual usage.

+   Before uninstalling an app, remember to back up important data.

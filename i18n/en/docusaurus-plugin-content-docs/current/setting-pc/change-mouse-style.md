# Change the mouse cursor style

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_mouse.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can change the cursor speed, color, and size as needed to make the cursor easier to see and use.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Click ![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings, then click Mouse in the left sidebar. On the right side of the screen, you can change the following settings:

+   Drag the slider to adjust the cursor speed or scrolling speed.
+   Change the cursor fill color and cursor size.

# Change the wallpaper

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_wallpaper.png" width="720" height=""/>

You can pick your favorite images and set them as your home screen wallpaper and lock screen background to make your PC desktop stand out.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On the PC home screen, right-click a blank area and select Edit wallpaper.
2.  Click your favorite image from the existing wallpapers, or click Add image and select your favorite image from Gallery or Files to set it as the wallpaper.

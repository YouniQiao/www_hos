# Enable Minor Mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/figure/fig_settings_minors_mode.png" alt="Enable Minor Mode" width="720" height="540" />

When you enable **Minor Mode** on your device, some apps automatically switch to **Minor Mode**, guiding children toward appropriate content. You can also set device usage time limits for your child.

![Method](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png) Method

Before using this feature, sign in to your **HUAWEI ID**.

1. Click ![Settings](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to open **Settings**. You can also click ![App Center](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202511200rXkw5/zh-cn/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in the **dock** at the bottom of the home screen to enter **App Center**, then click to open **Settings**.
2. Click **Digital Balance** in the left sidebar, then select **Minor** on the right and click **Enable**.
3. You can select an existing user, or click **Create new user**, set your child's information (profile picture, name, age), and click **Agree and create**.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to set the password and security questions for Minor Mode.
5. On the Minor Mode page, you can set device and app usage time limits for your child, such as total screen time, time limits for apps and atomic services, and apps and atomic services that are always available.
6. After this feature is enabled, opening some video apps will enter **Minor Mode** by default, ensuring your child accesses content safely.

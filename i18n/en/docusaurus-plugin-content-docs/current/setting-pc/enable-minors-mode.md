# Enable Youth Mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/figure/fig_settings_minors_mode.png" alt="" width="720" height=""/>

Enabling **Youth mode** limits the kinds of content your kids can access and monitors the screen time, to create a curated, age-appropriate digital environment for your kids.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Try it out

Before using this feature, remember to log in to your **HUAWEI ID**.

1.  Click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_settings.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to open Settings. You can also click![](https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202605280pkqzz/en-us/image/common/icon/appicon_allapps.png) in Dock at the bottom of the home screen to go to App chest and click Settings to open it.

2.  Click Digital Balance in the left sidebar, select Enable for a child on the right, and click Enable.

3.  You can select an existing user, or click Create new user, set the child information (profile picture, name, and age), and click Agree & create.

4.  Follow the onscreen instructions to set the password and security question for Youth mode.

5.  On the Youth mode screen, you can set the device and app usage time limits for your child, such as the total screen time limit, usage limits for apps and services, and apps and services can be used at any time.

6.  Enabling this feature will automatically switch to Youth mode when certain video apps are opened, to ensure that your child only accesses age-appropriate content.

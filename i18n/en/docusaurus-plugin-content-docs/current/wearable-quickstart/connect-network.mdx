# Connect Your Watch to a Network

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.



> 💡 **Note:** Before using eSIM, confirm that your device supports eSIM. If it doesn't, eSIM services are unavailable.


    Your watch can connect to a network via Bluetooth through a paired phone or via WLAN. If eSIM service has been activated on your watch, it can also use an eSIM mobile network. Depending on the scenario, your watch intelligently switches between networks to select the most suitable one.
    Your watch uses networks in the following order of priority:

1. When your watch and phone are connected via Bluetooth, your watch uses the phone's current network (mobile data/WLAN) over Bluetooth to save power; > 💡 **Note:** In Power mode, iOS phones do not support sharing their network with your watch over Bluetooth. To use your watch's eSIM network while it is connected to your phone via Bluetooth, go to **Settings > ** on your watch and disable the **Auto-disable when Bluetooth is connected** switch.
2. When Bluetooth is disconnected or cannot connect, your watch uses the connected WLAN network.
3. When your watch and phone are disconnected via Bluetooth and WLAN is unavailable, your watch uses the eSIM mobile network.


## Connect to a network via Bluetooth

     Connect your watch and phone via the **HUAWEI Health** app, and keep the **HUAWEI Health** app running in the background. Your watch will then use your phone's current network (mobile data/WLAN) by default.


## Connect to a network via WLAN


> 💡 **Note:** Connecting to a network via WLAN is supported only in Power mode.


     After connecting your watch to a network via WLAN, you can use apps that require a network connection on your watch even when your phone isn't nearby.

1. Press the watch's Up button, select **Settings > WLAN** in the app list, and enable the WLAN switch. Your watch will automatically scan for available WLAN networks.
2. Tap the WLAN network you want to connect to, enter the password as prompted, and tap **Connect**.


## Ignore a connected WLAN network


1. Press the watch's Up button and select **Settings > WLAN** in the app list.
2. Select the connected WLAN network and tap **Don't save**.


## Connect to a network via eSIM


> 💡 **Note:** To connect to a network via eSIM, you must first activate eSIM service.


     Press the watch's Up button and select **Settings > Mobile network** > **Mobile data** in the app list, then enable the **Mobile data** switch.


## Airplane mode

     When traveling by plane, you can enable **Airplane mode** as required by the airline. Once **Airplane mode** is enabled, your watch turns off Bluetooth, WLAN, and mobile networks.
     You can enable or disable Airplane mode in the following ways:

- Swipe down from the top of the watch face to open the Control Panel, and tap the **Airplane mode** switch to enable or disable **Airplane mode**.
- Press the watch's Up button, select **Settings > Mobile network** in the app list, and enable or disable the **Airplane mode** switch.

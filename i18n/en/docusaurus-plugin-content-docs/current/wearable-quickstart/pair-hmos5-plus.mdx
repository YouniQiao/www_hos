# Pair and connect with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

## Before pairing


1. Install the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374851905.png" width="24" />**HUAWEI Health** app.
2. On your phone, go to **Settings > Log in to HUAWEI ID**. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, register one first, then log in. 
💡 **Tip:** To ensure a successful pairing between your wearable and your phone, we recommend logging in with an adult HUAWEI ID.

3. Power on the wearable, select a language, and get ready to pair.


## Pair and connect

**Add a device to connect**:

1. Bring the wearable close to your phone, open the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002340894070.png" width="24" /> in the upper right corner > Add device. Find your wearable in the scan results and tap **Connect**.
2. When a pairing request appears on the wearable's screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002341053842.png" width="24" />, and follow the on-screen instructions on your phone to finish pairing the wearable with the phone.

**Scan to connect:**

1. Bring the wearable close to your phone, open the home screen of **HUAWEI Health**, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374732085.png" width="24" /> in the upper right corner > Scan, then scan the QR code displayed on the wearable's screen.
2. When a pairing request appears on the wearable's screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374851909.png" width="24" />, and follow the on-screen instructions on your phone to finish pairing the wearable with the phone.

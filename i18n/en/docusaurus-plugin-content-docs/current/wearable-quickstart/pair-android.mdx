# Pair with an Android Phone

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.




1. Install **HUAWEI Health**<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002340603334.png" width="24" />. If it is already installed, open **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Me > Check for updates**, and update to the latest version.
2. Open **HUAWEI Health** and tap **Me > Log in with HUAWEI ID**. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, register one first and then log in. > 💡 **Tip:** To ensure successful pairing between your wearable device and phone, log in with an adult HUAWEI ID.
3. Power on the wearable device and select a language to get ready for pairing.
4. Bring the wearable device close to your phone, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374601185.png" width="24" /> in the upper right corner > Add device. Find your wearable device in the scan results and tap **Connect**. You can also tap **Scan** to scan the QR code displayed on the wearable device.
5. When a pairing request appears on the wearable device screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374481321.png" width="24" />, and follow the onscreen instructions on your phone to complete pairing between the wearable device and phone.
6. Tap **Go to settings** and complete the disconnection protection settings to prevent the wearable device from disconnecting from **HUAWEI Health**, which would prevent it from receiving phone messages and calls.


> 💡 **Tip:** To ensure that the wearable device receives notifications in a timely manner, check on your phone that message notification settings are correct.

# Set Up Custom Cards

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN**.

App card settings, such as adding cards or
## Add a Card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to go to the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002200805644.png" width="24" /> below the card.

3. Touch the Up button to save. On the watch face screen, swipe left to view the added card.
## Edit a Combo Card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to go to the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Touch the icon below the card to enter the customization screen, where you can perform the following operations as needed: Touch the target widget, then select the widget you want to replace it with on the feature screen.
3. Touch and hold the target widget and drag it to adjust its position. > 💡 **Tip:** Widgets in a combo card cannot be deleted, only replaced. To add a card for a workout type, you must first add that workout type to the workout list.
4. Touch the Up button to save.
## Move Cards
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to go to the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Touch and hold the card you want to move, and drag it left or right to the target position.
3. Touch the Up button to save.
## Remove a Card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to go to the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Touch and hold the card you want to remove, and drag it up to.
3. Touch the Up button to save.

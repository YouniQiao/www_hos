# Button and Screen Gesture Operations

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.


The watch features a color screen and supports full-screen touch, swipe up, swipe down, swipe left, swipe right, and touch and hold operations.


## Smart Sensing Window


| Operation | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Press | While wearing the watch, on the watch face screen, press the Smart Sensing Window for 3s to quickly launch features such as Micro Health Check.<br />On the watch face screen (only supported by some watch faces, such as **Ink Landscape**), tap the watch face to enter the watch face interaction state, then press the Smart Sensing Window. Different press strengths produce different watch face change effects. |
| Touch | When assessing high blood pressure risk, performing ECG analysis, or measuring fingertip SpO2, you need to touch the Smart Sensing Window. |

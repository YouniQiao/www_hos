# Dual-Mode Switching

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.


The watch supports switching between Full mode and Power Saving mode. In Full mode, the watch delivers the complete experience. In Power Saving mode, battery life is extended, but some features are unavailable, such as mobile network and video watch faces.
## Enabling Power Saving mode
In Full mode, swipe down from the top of the watch face screen, then tap to exit Full mode and enter Power Saving mode.
## Enabling Full mode
In Power Saving mode, swipe down from the top of the watch face screen, then tap to exit Power Saving mode and enter Full mode.
💡 **Tip:** When your watch's battery level drops below the minimum value, it automatically switches to Power Saving mode. When it is charged above the minimum value, it automatically switches back to Full mode.

# Button and Screen Operations

> This document applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

The watch features a color screen and supports full-screen touch, swipe up, swipe down, swipe left, swipe right, and touch and hold operations.


## Upper button


💡 **Tips:** Scenarios with special definitions, such as incoming calls and workouts, are exceptions.


| Operation | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Rotate | On the Star Ring home screen or list home screen, swipe up or down to browse apps. On the main Star Ring home screen, rotate the crown counterclockwise or swipe down on the screen to show or hide app names. After entering a specific app, scroll up or down on the screen. To switch between the Star Ring home screen and list home screen: On either home screen, swipe up to the last page of apps, then tap **Star Ring home screen** or **List home screen** to switch. Go to **Settings > Watch face and home screen** > **Home screen**, and select **Star Ring** or **List**. |
| Tap | When the watch screen is off, tap the upper button to turn on the screen. When the watch displays the watch face, tap the upper button to enter the app screen. When the watch displays a screen other than the watch face, tap the upper button to return to the watch face. When there is an incoming Bluetooth call, tap the upper button to mute the call on the watch. |
| Double-tap | When the watch screen is on, double-tap the upper button to view all running apps. For example, during a workout, double-tap the upper button to enter background running tasks, then tap the watch face card to return to the watch face. |
| Touch and hold | When the watch is powered off, touch and hold the upper button to power it on. When the watch is powered on, touch and hold the upper button to display the restart/power off options. When the watch is powered on, touch and hold the upper button for more than 12s to force a restart. |


## Lower button


| Operation | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Tap | Tap the lower button to directly enter the shortcut function screen. Customizing the lower button: Tap the watch's upper button, then in the apps, go to **Settings > Button settings** > **Tap lower button**. Select the target app to complete the lower button customization. Once set, tap the watch's lower button on the watch face to enter the shortcut function screen. |
| Double-tap | Open the Wallet app. |
| Touch and hold | Launch **Celia**. |


## Button combinations


| Operation | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Tap the upper and lower buttons at the same time | Take a screenshot. The image is uploaded to your phone's Gallery. |


## Basic screen gestures


| Operation | Function |
| --- | --- |
| Tap | Select and confirm. |
| Touch and hold | When the device is on the watch face, touch and hold to quickly change the watch face. When the device is on a card screen, touch and hold to display the card management screen. |
| Swipe up | View message notifications (swipe up on the watch face). |
| Swipe down | Enter the Control Panel (swipe down on the watch face). You can enable **Find phone**, set the **Sound mode**, and more. You can view the remaining battery level and Bluetooth connection status. |
| Swipe left | Swipe left on the watch face to view watch cards. |
| Swipe right | Swipe right on the watch face to enter the minus-one screen. Swipe right on an app sub-screen to return to the previous level. |

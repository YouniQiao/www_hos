# Home Screen Card Settings

> This document applies to the **WATCH GT 6 series**.

## Adding cards
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to open the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001993919273.png" width="24" /> and select the card you want to add.

3. Once added, swipe left on the watch face screen to view the cards you've added.
## Editing a combo card
1. On the watch face screen, swipe left to open the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
2. Swipe left or right across the cards, touch the icon below the combo card, then touch a widget to replace it.
💡 **Tip:** Widgets in a combo card cannot be deleted, only replaced. To add a card for a custom workout, first add the workout to the workout list.
## Moving/removing cards
On the watch face screen, swipe left to open the card screen, then touch and hold any card to enter the card editing screen.
- Move a card: Touch and hold a card and drag it to adjust its position.
- Remove a card: Touch <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000001993923985.png" width="24" /> below the card, or swipe up on the card, then touch it.

# Pair and connect with phones running HarmonyOS 4.3 or earlier

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, the WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.


1. Install **Huawei Health**<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374600597.png" width="24" />. If it is already installed, open **Huawei Health**, tap **Me > Check for updates**, and update to the latest version.
2. Open **Huawei Health** and tap **Me > Log in to HUAWEI ID**. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, register one first, then log in. > 💡 **Tip:** To ensure a successful pairing and connection between your wearable and phone, log in with an adult HUAWEI ID.
3. Power on the wearable, select a language, and get ready to pair.
4. Bring the wearable close to your phone, go to the **Huawei Health** home screen, and tap the icon in the upper right corner<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002340762538.png" width="24" /> > Add device. Find your wearable in the scan results and tap **Connect**. You can also tap **Scan** to scan the QR code displayed on the wearable screen directly.
5. When **Bluetooth message sync service** appears in **Huawei Health**, tap **Agree**.
6. When a pairing request appears on the wearable screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374480733.png" width="24" />, and follow the onscreen prompts on your phone to finish pairing the wearable with the phone. > 💡 **Tip:** To make sure your wearable receives notifications in a timely manner, check on your phone that message notification settings are configured correctly.

# Smart Gesture Controls

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH 5**.

When it's inconvenient to touch the watch screen, you can control the watch with gestures using the hand wearing it. For example, answer/reject calls, snooze/dismiss alarms, and more.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - When a gesture icon<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002261763885.png" width="24" />appears at the top of the screen, the current feature supports smart gestures.
>
> - To ensure smart gestures are recognized correctly, avoid wearing the watch over your wrist bone, and keep the back of the watch as close to your skin as possible.
>
> - During running, cycling, and other high-intensity workouts, or due to other factors (hair, skin tone, tattoos, how tight the watch is worn, temperature, humidity, etc.), recognition accuracy may decrease or gestures may not be recognized at all.


## Learn to use smart gestures

     Before using gestures, practice them by following the gesture tutorial to avoid accidental operations caused by improper gestures.

1. In the watch's app list, go to **Settings > Gestures** and practice the gestures: For the "tap" gesture, touch your thumb and index fingertip together twice in a row to confirm a selection.
2. For the "swipe" gesture, swipe your thumb from your index finger's knuckle to its tip twice in a row to scroll through options.
3. After practicing the gestures, turn on the **Smart gesture recognition** switch to use gestures with the corresponding features. When you don't want to use smart gestures, turn off the **Smart gesture recognition** switch.


## Answer/reject calls

     When a call comes in, use the "tap" gesture to answer it; once connected, "tap" to hang up. To reject the call, first "swipe" to switch to Reject, then "tap" to confirm.


## Snooze/dismiss alarms

     When an alarm goes off, use the "tap" gesture to snooze it. To dismiss it, first "swipe" to switch to Dismiss, then "tap" to confirm.


## Audio/video playback controls

     On the watch's audio/video playback control card, use the "tap" gesture to play/pause. "Swipe" to switch to the next or previous track, and "tap" to confirm the switch.


## Turn on remote camera

     Turn on the watch's remote camera, then use the "tap" gesture to take a photo instantly.


## Snooze/dismiss Nap mode


> 💡 **Tip:** Gesture control for Nap mode is only supported when the watch is connected to a Huawei phone running HarmonyOS 4.0 or later.


     On the watch's Nap mode screen, use the "tap" gesture to snooze the reminder. To dismiss it, first "swipe" to switch to Dismiss, then "tap" to confirm.


## Open Live Window

     When the watch receives live updates such as train or flight information, use the "tap" gesture to view the details.


## Control the timer

     Open the timer, then use the "tap" gesture to start or pause it. You can also "swipe" to switch to Reset, then "tap" to reset the timer.

# Pair with an iOS Phone

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.


1. Install **HUAWEI Health**<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002374480977.png" width="24" />. If it is already installed, update it to the latest version. If it is not installed, go to **App Store** on your phone, search for **HUAWEI Health**, and download and install it.
2. Open **HUAWEI Health** and go to **Me > Log in to HUAWEI ID**. If you don't have a HUAWEI ID, register one first and then log in. > 💡 **Tip:** To ensure successful pairing between your wearable device and phone, log in with an adult HUAWEI ID.
3. Power on the wearable device and select a language, then bring the device close to your phone. On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap the icon in the upper right corner<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002340603018.png" width="24" /> > Add device, find your device in the scan results, and tap **Connect**. You can also tap **Scan** to scan the QR code on the device directly.
4. When a pairing request appears on the wearable device screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002340602994.png" width="24" />. A Bluetooth pairing request dialog box appears on the **HUAWEI Health** screen on your phone. Tap **Pair**. When your phone asks whether to allow the device to display phone notifications, tap **Allow**. Follow the onscreen instructions.
5. Wait a few seconds. **HUAWEI Health** will indicate that pairing with the wearable device is successful.


> 💡 **Tip:**
>
> - Do not swipe up on your phone to manually close **HUAWEI Health**, as this may disconnect the wearable device from **HUAWEI Health**, preventing it from receiving phone messages and calls.
>
> - To ensure the wearable device receives notifications in a timely manner, check on your phone that message notification settings are correct.

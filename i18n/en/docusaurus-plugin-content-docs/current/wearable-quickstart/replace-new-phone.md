# Pair with a New Phone

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.

A wearable device cannot connect to multiple phones at the same time — each wearable can connect to only one phone. To connect to another phone, first enter the connect-to-new-phone mode.
1. On the wearable's app screen, tap **Settings > System & updates** > **Connect to new phone**. > 💡 **Tip:** If the wearable is connected to an iOS phone, go to Settings > on the currently connected iOS phone to find the wearable. Tap the i icon to ignore the device, then proceed to the next step.
2. Tap **Disconnect** to disconnect from the currently connected phone.
3. The wearable enters the pairing screen. Follow the onscreen instructions to complete pairing.

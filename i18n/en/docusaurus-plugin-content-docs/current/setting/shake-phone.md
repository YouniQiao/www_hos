# Shake your phone for a playful mood theme

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/video/10044753_f004_Theme.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


Set a Mood Bubble theme for a fun, interactive spatial experience. Rotate your phone to reveal a new glasses-free 3D visual effect; shake your phone and the ball's expression changes with the force of your movement.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & personalization, tap More themes, and under **Mood Bubble**, select **Playful mood**.
2.  Tap Customize to open the editing screen, where you can change the ball and the background color.
3.  Tap Apply to try out the Playful mood theme.


<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   On the lock screen, keep your face toward your phone and gaze at it for a moment. When the ball at the bottom bounces up, you can unlock the fun spatial interaction.
+   Before using this feature, update your device to the latest version.

# Weather themes that sense sun, clouds, rain, and snow in real time

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/video/10044753_f001_WeatherTheme.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Show your local weather in full screen on the lock screen and home screen, with real-time updates for sun, clouds, rain, and snow, so you always know what's coming.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & personalization, tap More themes, and select Weather.
2.  Tap Customize to adjust the weather shown (for example, Real-time weather) and the wallpaper animation duration.
3.  Tap Apply to try out the weather theme.

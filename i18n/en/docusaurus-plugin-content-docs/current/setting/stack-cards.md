# Stack cards to keep your home screen tidy

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/figure/10044757_f003_AddStack.png" width="360" height="486"/>


You can stack multiple cards to save more space on your home screen.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Stack cards**: Touch and hold a card, then drag it onto another card of the same size until they overlap completely. Swipe up or down to view the cards.

**Manage stacked cards**: Swipe down and hold to expand all cards. Touch and hold a card, then drag it to reposition it, or swipe left on a card to remove it.

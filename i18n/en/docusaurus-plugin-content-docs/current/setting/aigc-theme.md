# AIGC themes: a journey through time and space

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/video/10044753_f005_AIGC.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


Apply a time-travel theme and let AI turn your personal photos into wallpapers in a range of styles. Every time you pick up your phone, you get a different wallpaper style and background, keeping your screen feeling fresh.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & personalization, and tap More themes. On the Themes screen, select **Time Travel**.
2.  Tap Customize > Upload photo, upload a front-facing photo of one person, select the character information you want to generate, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_controlpanel_done.png" width="24" height="24"/>.
3.  Tap Apply to start generating. When generation is complete, tap Apply to set the AI-generated photo as your wallpaper.

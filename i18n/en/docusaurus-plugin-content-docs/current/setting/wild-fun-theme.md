# Country Field Fluffy, New Interactions

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044753_f012_countryfieldfluffy.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Dress up your Fluffy with a special set, from the lock screen to the home screen. Fluffy will charm you with its cuteness and accompany you, bringing a variety of interactions and surprises.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & style, tap **More themes**, and select **Country Field Fluffy** under **Cute Companions**.

2.  Tap **Customize** to enter the editing screen, and select your favorite costume, such as the Pineapple set.

3.  Tap **Apply** to interact with Fluffy:

    +   On the **home screen**: Fluffy will follow your swipes. Tap Fluffy to make it hop between app icons, widgets, or folders.

    +   On the **lock screen**, tap Fluffy for playful animations and expressions, or shake your phone to see it bounce and collide.

**Note**

+   When applying the theme for the first time on certain devices, tap **Go apply** to load the theme.

+   The Fluffy sets supported by different models may vary. Actual usage shall prevail.

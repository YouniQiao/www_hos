# Large Folders for Flexible Home Screen Layouts

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202511171jkrgl/zh-cn/image/video/10044757_f002_LargeFolder.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


With large folders, you can fit more apps on a single home screen and resize folders to arrange your home screen however you like. Tap an icon to open the app right away — convenient and practical.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Switch the display mode**: Touch and hold a small folder, then tap Display as large folder in the menu that appears.

**Resize a folder**: Touch and hold a large folder until the menu appears, then release and drag the edges to resize it.

**Open apps directly**: Tap an icon in a large folder to open the app directly. You can also tap the stacked icon in the lower right corner of a large folder to open the folder and view more apps.

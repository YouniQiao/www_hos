# Check screen usage time

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202406260lXnqu/zh-cn/image/figure/10044767_f002_AdultMode.png" width="360" height="486"/>

Reading the news, listening to music, chatting for a while... before you know it, hours have passed. Where did the time go?

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Settings > Digital Balance, and tap to enable Digital Balance.

Once enabled, you can tap Usage statistics to view detailed data on the time you spend on your phone. You can also set downtime, available duration, and limits for apps and atomic services as needed.

# Spatial Wallpaper, the Art of Space

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044758_f016_Spatial_wallpaper.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Set a Spatial wallpaper to experience the natural integration of the clock and wallpaper, bringing a brand-new perspective and making your lock screen more vivid and stylish.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & style > Wallpapers and you can:

    +   Select the preset **Spatial clock** wallpaper under **Spatial wallpaper** to automatically generate a Spatial clock style.

    +   Tap **Select from Gallery** to select a suitable wallpaper. A wallpaper with appropriately tilted, continuous, and straight edges will produce a better spatial clock effect.

2.  On the wallpaper editing screen, you can:

    +   Drag the wallpaper to adjust its position, and spread or pinch with two fingers to zoom in or out on the wallpaper.

    +   Touch and hold the clock and drag it to adjust its position, and drag the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_3dclock07.png" width="24" height="24"/> on the top or bottom edge of the clock to adjust its height.

3.  Go to Apply > OK to complete the settings.

**Note**

+   When you enable this feature for the first time, download the spatial computing model as prompted.

+   Currently, only static wallpapers support the generation of spatial clocks with depth of field or perspective effects (such as <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_3dclock04.png" width="24" height="24" alt="空间时钟图标1"/> and <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_3dclock05.png" width="24" height="24" alt="空间时钟图标2"/>). Actual experience shall prevail.

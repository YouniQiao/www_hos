With the enhanced Immersive light, enjoy a clearer interface, fluid light and shadow effects that follow your fingertips, and responsive dynamic visuals across large folders, Control Panel, and Notification Panel.

Go to Settings > Home screen & style > Immersive light and select your desired level. The stronger the effect, the more prominent the lighting effects.

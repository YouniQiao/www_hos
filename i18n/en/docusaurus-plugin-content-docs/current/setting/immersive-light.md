# All-New Immersive Light Effect for a More Transparent Interface

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/video/10044742_f001_Transparency.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

The immersive light effect makes interfaces such as Gallery, notifications, and Control Panel more transparent and vivid, delivering a refined, immersive visual experience while you use your device.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202612220zjZV2/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Go to Settings > Home screen & personalization > Immersive light effect, and select the desired intensity. The stronger the effect, the more pronounced the transparency and light effects.

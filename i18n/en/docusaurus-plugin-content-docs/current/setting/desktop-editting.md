# Edit the home screen your way

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202512031Znvtc/zh-cn/image/video/20005692_f002_HomeScreen.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


In the home screen editor, you can quickly change wallpapers and themes, adjust icon sizes, and add cards — all with just a few taps.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Pinch inward with two fingers on the home screen to open the home screen editor, then tap Wallpaper, Cards, Icons, or Themes to choose and apply what you want, making your home screen truly your own.

For example, tap Icons, turn off the Show names switch, and tap Apply to hide app names on your home screen icons.

# Adorable Chubby Hands: Play Rock-Paper-Scissors to Win Hand Accessory Blind Boxes

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/video/10044753_f010_fathands.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

The adorable Chubby Hands theme lets you play rock-paper-scissors and change outfits. A little tap brings out the cute side, and you can dress up your Chubby Hands differently every day.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Open the Themes app, enter "Chubby Hands" in the search box, go to the Chubby Hands theme, and tap Apply.
2.  Wake the screen. On the Chubby Hands lock screen, tap GAME to enter the rock-paper-scissors game.
3.  Tap the gesture buttons at the bottom to play your hand. Win 3 rounds in a row to earn a hand accessory, which you can wear right away.
4.  You can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031BETA/resource/card/202504201ngpds/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_fatdress2.png" width="24" height="24"/> to view the hand accessory sets you've won and change them.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

+   Double-tap the screen, and the hand accessory you're wearing will show fun animated effects.
+   Apply the Chubby Hands theme with a friend. When you wake the screen on the lock screen, tap to share and you'll see fun, playful interactive effects (requires a HUAWEI device that supports one-tap sharing).
+   If you use face unlock, for a better interactive experience, go to Settings > Biometrics & passwords > Face recognition > Unlock screen, and it's recommended to use swipe to unlock.

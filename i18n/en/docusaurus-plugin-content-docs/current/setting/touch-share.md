# Tap to Share, Fun Sharing

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508180uszcw/zh-cn/image/video/10044641_f019_TouchShare.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>


With just a tap, you can share photos, videos, WLAN, documents, and more directly with friends, making sharing simple and fun.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  On the sender's phone, enter the ready-to-share state (for example, open a single photo or video in Gallery, or select multiple photos or videos to share).
2.  Touch the top of the sender's phone to the top of the receiver's phone and wait for the sharing animation to appear.
3.  On the sender's phone, swipe up to share, and on the receiver's phone, tap to receive.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

This feature requires both phones to be upgraded to the latest version of HarmonyOS 5.

# Vitality Mood Theme: Fun and Stress Relief on the Lock Screen

<iframe src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202503171esvl6/zh-cn/image/video/10044753_f011_MoodMatch.mp4#toolbar=0" scrolling="no" border="0" frameborder="no" framespacing="0" allowfullscreen="true" width="360" height="486"> </iframe>

Set the Vitality Mood theme to interact with 3D emojis on the lock screen. Tap and press them for fun and stress relief.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & personalization, tap More themes, and select **Vitality Mood** under **Mood Box**.

2.  Tap Customize to enter the editing screen, where you can change the background style and visual style.

3.  Tap Apply to experience the Vitality Mood theme, then wake the screen on the lock screen:

    +   When you wake the screen, the system automatically switches to a fun animation.

    +   Tap or touch and hold an emoji to trigger a fun interaction.

    +   When charging, a dedicated charging animation appears, accompanied by a charging sound effect in Ring mode.

    +   Tap to share with another device using the Vitality Mood theme on the lock screen, and the two devices swap theme moods. There is also a chance of triggering a hidden animation.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B030/resource/card/202512281uswxk/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

You can adjust the volume of the theme's interaction sound effects by adjusting the system ringtone volume.

# Care for Your Family and Friends

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/20013690_f001_MyCircle.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Create a dedicated card for your family and friends in My circle to share health and fitness data and establish a remote guardian relationship.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Settings > HUAWEI ID and tap the **My circle** card.

2.  If you are using the feature for the first time, go to Add member > Add family member/friend and follow the onscreen instructions to add a member. If you have already added a member, you can tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_personbadge.png" width="24" height="24" alt="添加成员图标"/> to add another member.

3.  After setting the member information, you can customize the member card, for example, by adding services such as **Health data sharing** and **Mobile guardian**.

**Note**

+   To add a pet, select **Add pet** when adding a member account in **Add member** and follow the onscreen instructions.

+   Tap the profile picture of the member to access the details screen. Go to <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/common/buttons/ic_hiden_more.png" width="24" height="24" alt="更多选项图标"/> > Add widget to add the member widget to the home screen, or tap **Remove member** to remove the member as prompted.

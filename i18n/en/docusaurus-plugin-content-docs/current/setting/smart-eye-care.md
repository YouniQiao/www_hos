# Smart Eye Protection

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/20006187_f001_Smart_eye_protection.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Smart eye protection tracks your viewing habits and eye fatigue, and reminds you to take a break, helping you maintain eye health.

**Try it out**

**Settings** > **Digital Balance**

Smart eye protection

+   **Fatigue analysis**: Automatically detects eye fatigue, reminds you to take a break, and guides you through relaxation exercises.

+   **Viewing habit alerts**: Reminds you of poor eye habits in a timely manner, such as **Too close to screen** and **Dim light**.

+   **Eye comfort mode**: **Auto-enable** eye comfort mode, which automatically adjusts the screen display based on your eye fatigue status.

**Note**

+   When you enable **Digital Balance** for the first time, **Smart eye protection** will be enabled by default.

+   You can also enable this feature for your child by setting up a **Mobile guardian** relationship or enabling **Youth mode**.

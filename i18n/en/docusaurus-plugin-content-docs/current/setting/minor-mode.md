# Enable Minor Mode

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/figure/10044767_f001_ChildrenMode.png" width="360" height="486"/>


When you enable Minor Mode on your child's device, some apps automatically switch to Minor Mode to guide your child toward age-appropriate content. You can also set device usage time limits for your child.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Digital Balance, tap Enable Minor Mode, and set a password and security questions.

2.  On the Digital Balance screen, you can manage screen time and content access.

3.  When you open certain video apps, Minor Mode is enabled by default.

# Interactive Cute Animals

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202601231gjcjr/en/image/figure/10044758_f003_adorableanimals.png" alt="" width="360" height="486"/>

Choose our Interactive cute animals theme to have lovable cats and dogs by your side. You can also interact with them on your home screen or lock screen whenever you have a moment.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Settings > Home screen & style > More themes and select your favorite animal under **Interactive cute animals**, such as Felix.

2.  Tap **Customize** to change the pet's hair color, personality, and theme background.

3.  After tapping **Apply**, you can enjoy the following interactive features:

    +   **Home screen**: Tap the blank area on the home screen where the pet can fit. The pet will jump to the position where you have tapped. Tap the pet to play games with it.

    +   **Lock screen**: After turning on the screen, tap the pet or blank area, wave your hand at the pet (with your palm facing the screen and moving downwards), or make a heart gesture in the air. The pet will respond in a fun way. You can also trigger the pet animation when receiving a notification on the lock screen or swiping up on the music capsule.

**Note**

When applying the theme for the first time, tap **Go apply** to load the theme.

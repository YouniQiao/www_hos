   Cycling

    While cycling, you can use simulated power, slope linkage, and statistics to record every flat sprint or hill climb, and calculate simulated power based on your cycling speed, slope, and other data.


> 💡 **Tip:** Before use, update the HUAWEI Health app and your watch to the latest versions.


## Pairing and connecting with cycling accessories

     **Connecting an accessory for the first time**

1. Put the accessory into pairing mode.
2. In the watch's app list, go to **Settings > More connections** > **Workout device management**, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002542458449.png" width="24" /> to add a device.
3. Proceed to the next step or tap **Add manually**, then select a motion sensor, heart rate monitor, speed/cadence sensor, or power meter as needed, and connect to the corresponding device once it is found.

     **Disconnecting or reconnecting a paired accessory**

1. In the watch's app list, go to **Settings > More connections** > **Workout device management**.
2. Tap the name of a connected accessory to open its device management page, then tap **Disconnect**. Alternatively, tap the name of a paired but disconnected accessory to open its device management page, then tap **Reconnect**.


## Simulated power settings

     Simulated power is estimated based on your real-time cycling speed, altitude slope, body weight, and other information. After a ride, you can view the average simulated power for that session in the workout record, which can be used for cycling analysis and reference.
     Measurement is based on your weight, age, and other information. Before using this feature, go to **HUAWEI Health**, tap **Me > Profile**, and make sure your personal information is accurate.

1. In the watch's app list, tap **Workout**.
2. Swipe up or down on the screen and tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002359805390.png" width="24" />** icon next to **Outdoor cycling** to open the settings page.
3. Tap **Cycling management > Vehicle management**, and fill in the vehicle information (such as vehicle type and weight).


## Starting a ride

     Start a ride on your watch to display cycling data in real time on your phone, for richer data presentation.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Before use, make sure **View data on phone** is enabled: in the watch's app list, go to **Settings > Workout** > **View data on phone** and enable this feature.
>
> - To ensure that your phone displays cycling data during your workout, the Bluetooth connection must remain active.
>
> - Cadence data is displayed on your phone only when the watch is paired with a third-party cadence sensor.


1. In the watch's app list, tap **Workout**.
2. Swipe up or down on the screen and tap **Outdoor cycling** or **Indoor cycling**. If you select **Outdoor cycling**, on the outdoor cycling preparation page, swipe up or down on the right side of the screen to select a vehicle type; swipe up to the **More** page, select **Navigation route**, choose a route as needed, and then start the workout.
3. Tap the <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002521273486.png" width="560" /> icon to start the workout. Cycling data is displayed on your phone at the same time. On your phone, tap the displayed data type to switch as needed.


## Viewing cycling data


- Heart rate monitor data: After a heart rate monitor is connected, its heart rate data is displayed only during a workout (pool swimming and open water swimming are not supported); when you are not working out, your watch's heart rate data is displayed.
- Speed/cadence sensor/power meter data: In the app list, select **Workout**, tap **Outdoor cycling** or **Indoor cycling**, and after the workout starts, swipe up or down on the screen to view cycling data.
- If no power meter or cadence sensor is connected, after a ride, go to the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap the **Workout records** card, and view data such as the simulated power, slope linkage, and simulated cadence for that session.

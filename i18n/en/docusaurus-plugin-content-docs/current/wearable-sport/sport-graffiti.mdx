# Sport Graffiti

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="gt6" label="WATCH GT 6">

Professional Workout


|  | Sport Graffiti |
| --- | --- |


   Sport Graffiti

    Sport Graffiti is a workout that combines exercise and art. As you work out, passing through the required waypoints along a graffiti route lets you draw creative shapes, adding more fun to your workout.


## Add a graffiti route


1. On the **HUAWEI Health** **Workout** screen, select **Outdoor run**, tap the route icon to open the route overview screen, then go to the **Sport Graffiti** screen to view the graffiti routes supported in your current city.
2. Make sure Bluetooth is connected between your watch and phone, select a graffiti route to open its details screen, and tap **Transfer to wearable device** to sync the route to your watch.


## Start a Sport Graffiti workout


1. Press the watch's Up button, then in the app list, tap **Workout > Sport Graffiti**.
2. Select a graffiti route, and on the route details screen, tap **Go to workout > GO icon** to start the workout.
3. After the workout starts, check in at the required waypoints following the navigation prompts, and track your completion of the required waypoints. When the workout ends, your workout data is displayed on the watch.


## Share your Sport Graffiti results


1. On the **HUAWEI Health** home screen, tap **Workout records** to view detailed workout records.
2. Open a completed Sport Graffiti record, tap the share icon in the upper right corner, and select where to share it on social platforms.


## Delete a graffiti route


1. Press the watch's Up button, then in the app list, tap **Workout > Sport Graffiti**.
2. Touch and hold a graffiti route and tap **Delete** at the bottom to delete that single route. You can also touch and hold a graffiti route, tap **Select all** at the top, and tap **Delete** to delete all routes.

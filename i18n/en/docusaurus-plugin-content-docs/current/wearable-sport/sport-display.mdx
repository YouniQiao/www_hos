# Workout Screen Data Display Settings

> This article applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.


You can customize multiple data cards to view your workout data from various perspectives during exercise.
> 💡 **Note:** Some workout types do not support custom data settings. The actual behavior prevails.
1. In the app list on your wearable, tap , tap the icon next to a workout type (for example, ), and go to the settings screen.
2. Tap , select a data card, and turn on the switch.
3. Tap , select a display style (for example, four-panel style), and set the corresponding custom data (for example, heart rate, distance).
4. After setting up, when you start a workout, you can swipe up or down on the workout screen to view the enabled data cards and data. Touch and hold the data displayed on the screen to customize the data shown on the current screen.
- To restore the default configuration, go to the screen and tap .
- To turn off a data display (for example, training load), go to the screen, turn off the switch for the target item, and the data card will no longer appear on the workout screen.

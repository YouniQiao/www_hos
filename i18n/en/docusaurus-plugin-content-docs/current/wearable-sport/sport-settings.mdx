# Workout settings

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

You can configure workout types as needed, including **Goals**, **Reminders**, **Auto-pause**, **Data display**, **Pin workout**, and more.


> 💡 **Tip:** Settings vary by wearable device and workout type. If an option is not displayed, the workout does not support that setting.

1. On your wearable, tap **Workout** in the app list.
2. Tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EB/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002412909661.png" width="24" />** icon next to the workout type to open the workout settings screen. Goals
Tap **Goals** to set target values by **Distance**, **Time**, or **Total calories**.
3. Reminders
Tap **Reminders**, then tap **Interval reminders** to set interval reminders by **Distance** or **Time**. You can also turn **Heart rate reminders** and other options on or off.
4. **Workout touch prevention**
Tap **Workout touch prevention** and turn the switches for the corresponding features (such as **Auto-lock screen**) on or off as needed.
5. **Auto-pause**
Tap **Auto-pause** to turn the **Auto-pause** switch on or off. Once enabled, the workout pauses automatically when you stop and resumes when you start moving again.
6. **Remove this workout**
Tap **Remove this workout** to remove the workout type from the **Workout** app list.
7. **Pin workout**
Tap **Pin workout** to move the workout type to the top of the **Workout** app list.
In the **Workout** list, touch and hold the workout type you want to pin, then tap the pin icon.

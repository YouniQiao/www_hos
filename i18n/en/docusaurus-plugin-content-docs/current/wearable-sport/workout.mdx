# Start a workout

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="ultimate" label="WATCH Ultimate">


HUAWEI watches support a wide range of workout modes, including professional modes such as running and cycling, as well as fitness, equipment, and leisure modes. Choose the one you need and start your workout.


## Start on the watch

1. In the app list on your wearable device, tap **Workout**.
2. Swipe up or down to select a workout type. You can also swipe up to the last page and tap **Add workout** to add other workout types.
3. Tap the workout type icon to start your workout.
4. During a workout, touch and hold the Up button to end it, or press the Up button to pause. Tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002349389194.png" width="24" /> to resume, or tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002349229442.png" width="24" /> to end the workout.
During a workout, swipe up or down to view workout data. You can also swipe right to open the shortcut settings screen, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002551443975.png" width="24" /> to adjust the volume of workout prompts, and tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0EX/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002551563967.png" width="24" /> to open the workout settings screen and configure settings.

</TabItem>

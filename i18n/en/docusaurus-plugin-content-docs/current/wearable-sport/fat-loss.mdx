# Fat Loss and Shaping

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.

import Tabs from '@theme/Tabs';
import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="ultimate" label="WATCH Ultimate">


By combining data such as weight changes, calorie deficit, diet records, and workouts, you can set reasonable personalized training to achieve scientific weight loss and maintain your weight.


## Setting Initial Goals

1. In the watch's app list, tap **Fat Loss and Shaping**. On first use, the user authorization page appears; tap **Agree**.
2. Follow the onscreen instructions to complete the settings, then enter the **Fat Loss and Shaping** app home screen.


> 💡 **Tip:** If you haven't set your weight information, follow the onscreen instructions to complete the relevant settings, and the watch will generate a scientific weight loss plan or a weight maintenance plan. If you have already set your current weight and target weight in the **HUAWEI Health** app, they will be automatically synced to the watch, so you don't need to set them again on the watch.

</TabItem>

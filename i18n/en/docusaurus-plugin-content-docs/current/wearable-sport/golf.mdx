# Golf

> This document applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

   Golf


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - This feature is currently supported only on HUAWEI WATCH GT 6 Pro.
>
> - To use the automatic course map download feature, update your wearable device and **HUAWEI Health** to the latest versions.


    Practice mode supports detection of swing data such as backswing/downswing time, swing tempo, and swing speed, and provides visual demonstrations of swing motions and grip techniques along with key technique instructions to help you improve your game.
    Course mode helps you accurately track green locations, hazard distribution, and distance data on the course, so you can plan your shots and improve your performance.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - The watch's golf workout track records only swing tracks, not putt tracks.


## Practice mode


1. Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, tap **Workout > Golf**** **.
2. Select **Practice mode**, and tap the start icon (or press the lower button) to start your workout.
3. Swipe up and down on the screen to view grip technique demonstrations, swing motion demonstrations, and workout data.


## Course mode


> 💡 **Tips:** Golf course maps are not supported in some regions or on some courses. On the watch, tap **Golf > Course mode** > **Nearby courses**. If "No nearby courses found" is displayed, or in the **Workout** screen of **HUAWEI Health**, select **Golf** and tap a course that does not appear in **Course map**, this indicates that the country/course is not yet supported.


1. Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, tap **Workout > Golf**** **.
2. Select **Course mode**, tap **Nearby courses**, and after positioning succeeds, select the course you are at. If the course has already been downloaded, you can also select it from **Downloaded courses**. To download a course, refer to the **Downloading or deleting course maps** section below.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to select the venue, tee box, and other information. After positioning succeeds, tap the start icon (or press the lower button) to start your workout.
4. Swipe left on the screen to record your score for each hole on the scorecard screen. Swipe up and down on the screen to view the fairway overview, green view, green direction (must be enabled in **Data display**), data screens, and more. The fairway overview supports vector maps, and you can also do the following: On the home screen, tap the fairway map to enter the distance measurement view, and rotate the crown to zoom in or out; drag the map background to view the course layout outline.
5. Tap a hazard on the fairway map to enter the hazard distance measurement screen, where you can switch between viewing the hazard outline and the distances to its front and back edges.
6. Tap **Back** at the bottom to return to the home screen.
7. Touch and hold the watch's upper button to end the workout. Before ending, tap **Scorecard** to view your score, tap **Save for later** to temporarily save the round, or tap **End** to save this record and end the workout.


## Course mode settings


> 💡 **Tips:** Different devices support different course mode settings. If your device does not have a certain setting, it is not supported. Please refer to your actual device.


1. Press the watch's upper button, and in the app list, tap **Workout > Golf**** **.
2. Select **Course mode**, and tap the **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002382885633.png" width="24" />** icon to enter the settings screen. You can tap **Course management**; on the course management screen, touch and hold to select a course to delete, or select a specific course to enter and delete it. You can also tap **Check for updates** to check whether a new version of the course map is available.
3. You can turn the **Workout anti-mistouch** switch on or off.
4. You can tap **Unit settings** to change units.
5. You can set the **Tee shot reference line**.
6. You can configure the **Data display** screen switches for a round (such as green direction).


## More course mode information


1. If a course has two greens, how do I switch between green information? Update the watch to the latest version, then tap L R to switch greens.
2. How do I switch holes while playing golf? When you reach the tee box of a hole, the watch automatically switches to that hole. If it does not switch automatically, tap the hole number at the top of the home screen to select a hole.
3. How do I edit golf scoring data? During a golf workout, swipe left on the screen to enter the scorecard, and edit the scoring data. After the workout ends, scoring data cannot be edited on the watch.


## Downloading or deleting course maps

     **Manual download**:


> 💡 **Tips:** Before using this feature, update **HUAWEI Health** to the latest version. When the watch is properly paired and connected to your phone, downloaded course information is automatically synced to the watch.


1. In the **Workout** screen of **HUAWEI Health**, select **Golf**, and tap **Course map**.
2. On the **Discover courses** tab, select the target course and tap **Download**.
3. After the download is complete, tap the course to enter the course screen, and tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002382885641.png" width="24" /> > Sync to watch** in the upper right corner to sync the course to the watch. You can also choose to delete the downloaded course information.


      **Automatic download:**


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Automatic download is not supported when paired with an iOS phone.


1. In the **Workout** screen of **HUAWEI Health**, select **Golf**, and tap **Course map**.
2. On the **Watch maps** tab, tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002526500197.png" width="24" /> > Automatically cache nearby courses** in the upper right corner, and turn on the **Automatically cache nearby courses via WLAN** switch.
3. Once enabled, when the watch detects that you have arrived in a new city/region and your phone is connected to WLAN, it will automatically download nearby course maps and sync them to the watch.

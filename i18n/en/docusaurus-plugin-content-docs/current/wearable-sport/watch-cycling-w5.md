# Watch Cycling Sync

> This document applies to **WATCH 5**.

Professional sports


|  | Watch Cycling Sync |
| --- | --- |


   Watch Cycling Sync

    Start a cycling workout on your watch to sync real-time cycling data to your phone, for a richer data display.


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - Before starting a workout, enable **View data on phone**: go to the watch's app list, select **Workout > Workout settings** > **View data on phone**, and enable it.
>
> - When paired with a phone running HarmonyOS 5 or later, only Outdoor cycling supports phone-side sync. Indoor cycling is not supported.
>
> - To ensure cycling data stays visible on your phone during a workout, the Bluetooth connection must remain active.
>
> - Cadence data appears on your phone only when the watch is paired with a third-party cadence sensor.


1. Press the watch's Up button, then tap **Workout** in the app list.
2. Swipe up or down and tap **Outdoor cycling** or **Indoor cycling**. Tap the icon to start the workout; cycling data is displayed on your phone at the same time. On your phone, tap the displayed data type to switch as needed.

# Use offline maps

> This document applies to **WATCH Ultimate ULTIMATE DESIGN, WATCH GT 6 series, and WATCH 5**.

import TabItem from '@theme/TabItem';

   Use offline maps


    After downloading offline maps in **HUAWEI Health**, you can start map navigation directly from **Navigation routes** or **Outdoor workouts** on your watch. Offline maps can be used for map display in scenarios such as workouts (outdoor workouts, route import/export, route return, and more). After you start an outdoor workout on your watch, you can use offline maps only after positioning is successful.


## Download offline maps

     **Manual download**:

1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, select **Offline maps** to access the offline maps screen.
2. Go to the **Region list** screen, select the map for the region you need, and tap **Download**. Do not leave this screen during the download. After the download is complete, you can view it on the **Download management** screen.


     **Automatic download:**


> 💡 **Tips:**
>
> - This feature is only supported when the watch is paired with a Huawei phone running HarmonyOS 4.3 or earlier, or HarmonyOS 6 (system version 6.0.0.112 or later), or an Android phone.


1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, select **Offline maps** to access the offline maps screen.
2. On the **Download management** screen, turn on **Auto-cache over WLAN**.
3. Once enabled, when your watch detects that you have arrived in a new city/region and your phone is connected to WLAN, it will automatically cache the offline map for your current location and sync it to your watch.


## Delete offline maps


1. On the device details page in **HUAWEI Health**, select **Offline maps** to access the offline maps screen.
2. Go to the **Download management** screen, select the map you want to delete, and tap **<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/hwtips/topic/M0ER/zh-CN/zh-cn_image_0000002009689585.png" width="24" /> > Delete data** on the right to delete that offline map.


## Map settings


1. Press the watch's upper button and select **Settings > Offline maps** from the app list.
2. Set the map display information as needed. **Display mode**: **Auto switch**, **Light mode**, or **Dark mode**.
3. **Navigation view**: **Heading up** or **North up**.

## More information

     Offline maps add a map display to the workout track for outdoor workouts, drawing the workout track on top of the map layer; during a workout, the map shows the imported route track, markers, track direction, and other indicators; after a route is imported for navigation, you can preview the imported route track on the map.
     **Navigation routes:**
     In the device's app list, tap **Workout**, swipe up or down to select an outdoor workout such as **Trail running**, and on the workout preparation screen, swipe up to the **More** screen and select **Navigation routes**.

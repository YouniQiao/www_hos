# Track Back

> This document applies to **WATCH ULTIMATE DESIGN and WATCH GT 6 series**.

Your watch can record and navigate routes. After a workout starts or you reach your destination, enable Track Back to navigate back to a waypoint you passed or to your starting point.


On the watch's app list, touch **Workout** and start a single outdoor workout. During the workout, swipe up on the screen to access the track screen, where you can select **Track Back** or **Straight Line Back** to return to a waypoint you passed or to your starting point.


If **Track Back** or **Straight Line Back** is not displayed, touch the screen to make it appear.


> 💡 **Tip:** The track screen is not supported for outdoor track running. Track Back can only be used during a workout. Once the current outdoor workout ends, Track Back will no longer be available.

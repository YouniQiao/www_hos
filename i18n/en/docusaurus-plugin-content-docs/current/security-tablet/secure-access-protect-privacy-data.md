# Protect Your Data with Secure Access

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20005744_f002_PrivacyPicker.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

Your tablet comes equipped with built-in secure access, ensuring robust protection for your sensitive data. Whenever an app requests permission to view sensitive content—such as images, videos, or contacts—you retain full control, granting access only to what's necessary.

**Try it out**

For example, if you wish to send or upload an image in an app, the app can only access the content you have selected, and cannot access other unselected data or continuously access Gallery in the background.

**Note**

After an app accesses data, you can go to Settings > Privacy & security and tap **Images & videos** to view the app access records.

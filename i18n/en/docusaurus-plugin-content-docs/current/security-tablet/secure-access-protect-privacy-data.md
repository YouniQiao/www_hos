# Secure access, protecting your privacy data

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/20005744_f002_PrivacyPicker.png" alt="Secure access, protecting your privacy data" width="560" height="" />

Your tablet supports secure access by default. When an app needs to access sensitive data such as images, videos, and contacts, secure access can restrict the app to only the data you select, keeping your data safe.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Take sending or uploading an image in an app as an example. Under the restrictions of secure access, the app can only access the content you select. It cannot access any other data you have not selected, nor can it continuously access Gallery in the background.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

After an app accesses your data, you can go to **Settings > Privacy & security**, tap options such as **Images and videos**, and view the app's access records.

# Family Scam Guard

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/20012126_f002_SmartProtection.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

With Family Scam Guard activated, your tablet instantly alerts you if a loved one's account shows signs of exposing to a fraud risk.

**Try it out**

1.  On your tablet and your family member's device, log in to **HUAWEI ID**, go to Settings > Privacy & security > Call & message blocking > Family scam guard, tap **Enable**, and follow the onscreen instructions to set up a linkage.

2.  After the linkage, Family Scam Guard will automatically be enabled, and will protect your family in the following scenarios:

    +   **Risky calls**: You will be notified of the risk and can end the call for your family member.

    +   **Risky SMS messagesand web pages**: You can view risk records.

    +   **Risky apps**: You will be notified of the risk.

**Note**

On your tablet, go to Settings > Privacy & security > Call & message blocking > Family scam guard to view the records.

# Family Anti-Fraud: Safeguarding Your Family's Device Security

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/20012126_f002_SmartProtection.png" alt="Family Anti-Fraud" width="560" height="" />

Turn on Family Anti-Fraud, and when a family member may be at risk of a scam, your tablet will promptly send you a notification so you can step in right away and keep their device safe.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1. On your tablet and your family member's device, sign in to each **HUAWEI ID**, then go to **Settings > Privacy & security > Harassment and fraud blocking > Family Anti-Fraud**, tap **Enable protection**, and follow the prompts to set up the protection link.

2. Once linked, Family Anti-Fraud turns on automatically and protects against the following situations:
   - **Answering or making risky calls**: You'll receive a notification at the same time and can end the call for your family member.
   - **Receiving risky messages or visiting risky webpages**: You'll receive a risk record.
   - **Installing risky apps**: You'll receive a notification at the same time.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

On your tablet, go to **Settings > Privacy & security > Harassment and fraud blocking > Family Anti-Fraud** to view Family Anti-Fraud records.

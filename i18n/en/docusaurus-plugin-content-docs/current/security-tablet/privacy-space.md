# PrivateSpace, hide important information

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044538_f001_PrivateSpace.png" width="560" height=""/>


Turn on PrivateSpace to store important files, photos, and more in a separate space, keeping them safe from prying eyes and protecting your privacy.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

1.  Go to Settings > Privacy & security > PrivateSpace, and tap Turn on.
2.  Follow the onscreen instructions to set a PrivateSpace password. After setting up a security question, you can enroll a fingerprint.

Once PrivateSpace is turned on, enter it from the lock screen by entering the PrivateSpace password or using your enrolled PrivateSpace fingerprint.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

To switch between spaces using fingerprints, enroll different fingerprints in the MainSpace and PrivateSpace, and enable fingerprint unlock in both.

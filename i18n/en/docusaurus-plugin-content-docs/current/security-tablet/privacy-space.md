# Store Important Data in PrivateSpace

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044538_f001_PrivateSpace.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

PrivateSpace allows you to create a completely separate space on your device for storing personal data and protecting your privacy.

**Try it out**

1.  Go to Settings > Privacy & security > PrivateSpace, and tap **Enable**.

2.  Follow the onscreen instructions to set a PrivateSpace password.

After **PrivateSpace** is enabled, you can then access it using your password from the lock screen.

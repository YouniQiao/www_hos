# How to find a lost device

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10044536_f001_FindPhone.png" width="560" height=""/>


After you enable Find Device, you can locate your phone, tablet, and other devices to keep them and your data safe.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

Enable Find Device on your phone:

Go to Settings, sign in to your HUAWEI ID, go to Find Device, and turn on Find My Phone.

Find your lost phone on another device:

In the Utilities folder or on the home screen of the other device, tap Find Device, sign in to the same HUAWEI ID as the lost device, and tap the device you want to find. You can then locate it, lock it remotely, or erase its data.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

If your phone is offline because it isn't connected to the network or has been turned off, you can set an online notification, and you'll be notified once the phone comes back online.

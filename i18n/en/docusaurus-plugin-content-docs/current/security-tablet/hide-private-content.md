# Hide private photos and videos

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202502281vpXhm/zh-cn/image/figure/10044586_f008_galleryhide.png" width="560" height=""/>

If you don't want others to see private photos or videos in your album, you can hide them and access them through lock screen verification.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

+   **Hide items**: Go to Gallery, and on the Photos tab or in an album, tap Multi-select, select the photos or videos, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202502181Uo3yy/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Hide > OK.
+   **View hidden items**: In Gallery > Albums, tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202502181Uo3yy/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Hidden albums, then enter via lock screen verification.

+   **Unhide items**: In a hidden album, tap Multi-select, select the items you want to unhide, then tap <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202502181Uo3yy/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_public_password_unvisible.png" width="24" height="24"/> or <img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202502181Uo3yy/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/ic_more.png" width="24" height="24"/> > Unhide.

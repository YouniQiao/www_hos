# Facial Recognition and Face Unlock

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202508111clkwd/zh-cn/image/figure/10044531_f004_FaceUnlock_2D.png" width="560" height=""/>

Add your face to the tablet and unlock it securely with a glance.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Add facial data**: Go to Settings > Biometrics & passwords > Facial recognition, then follow the onscreen prompts to add your facial data.

**Unlock with your face**: Once your face is added, pick up the tablet and hold it up to your face. Turn on the screen and let it recognize your face to unlock the tablet.

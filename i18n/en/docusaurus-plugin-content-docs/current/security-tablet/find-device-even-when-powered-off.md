# Find Your Device Even When It's Powered Off

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202608192tbkfh/zh-cn/image/figure/10044536_f001_FindPhone.png" alt="Find Your Device Even When It's Powered Off" width="560" height="" />

Once Find Device is enabled, you can locate HUAWEI phones, tablets, and other devices remotely — even when they're powered off — to keep your device and data safe.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202503041becsx/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_method.png" width="24" height="24"/> Method

**Enable Find Device on your tablet and complete security verification:**

1. Go to **Settings**, sign in to your **HUAWEI ID**, open Cloud, and tap **Find Device**.
2. Turn on **Find My Tablet**.
3. Follow the onscreen instructions to complete security verification.

**Locate your lost tablet from another device:**

1. Open the **Find Device** app on another HUAWEI device.
2. Sign in with the same **HUAWEI ID** as the lost device.
3. Tap the device you want to find to locate it, lock it remotely, or erase its data.

Tablets that have completed security verification and meet the version requirements can be located even when powered off.

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/EMUI/C00B030/resource/card/202508300vZjQz/zh-cn/image/common/buttons/fig_tips.png" width="24" height="24"/> Tips

When your tablet isn't connected to the network or is powered off, it's offline. You can set an online notification, and you'll be notified once the tablet comes online.

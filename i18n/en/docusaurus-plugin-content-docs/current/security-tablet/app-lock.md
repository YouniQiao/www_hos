# Secured App Data with App Lock

<img src="https://tips-p01-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/MODEL/DOC/C00B031/resource/card/202606110qRije/en/image/figure/10044533_f001_AppLock.png" alt="" width="560" height=""/>

You can apply App Lock to any app, so that it can only be opened when your identity is verified.

**Try it out**

You can enable App Lock in either of the following ways:

+   On the home screen, hold down on the icon of the app you wish to lock, and tap **Add lock**.

+   Go to Settings > Privacy & security > App Lock. After your identity is verified, toggle on the app switch.

When enabling App Lock for the first time, you can set **Screen lock** or **Privacy lock** as the verification method.

After App Lock is enabled, tap the app and open it after your identity is verified.

**Note**

Go to Settings > Privacy & security > App Lock, and select **Lock apps on exit** or **Lock apps when screen locks** as the lock mode.
